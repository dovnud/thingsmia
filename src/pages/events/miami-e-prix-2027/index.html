Formula E Miami E-Prix 2027: race day at the Miami International Autodrome

Last reviewed: November 6, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The 2027 Google Cloud Miami E-Prix runs on Saturday, February 20, 2027 at the Miami International Autodrome at Hard Rock Stadium, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens, FL 33056. It's Round 5 of Formula E's Season 13, the fourth Formula E race in Miami and the second at this venue, and it marks the Miami debut of the GEN4 car. Grandstand tickets on the official event page are $82.45 for adults and $41.25 for children ages 3 to 15, checked November 6, 2026, and accessible seating is priced the same. One race day, one round, no second Miami race that weekend. This is not the Grand Prix. It's a different series, a different car and a much cheaper ticket, on the same stadium campus our Grand Prix guides cover in May.

What this race is, and what it isn't

Formula E is the electric single seater world championship. Season 13 is the GEN4 era, and the official event page bills Miami as the debut of the new car on this side of the calendar: in its words, the race marks the Miami debut of Formula E's biggest technological step yet, the GEN4. The calendar structure tells the format story. Miami sits on the Season 13 calendar as a single round on a single date, while the double header weekends list paired rounds across two days. So plan for one race day with the full program inside it, not a two day meeting. And hold one fact loosely: the official page had published no race session timetable when checked on November 6, 2026. Practice, qualifying and race times land on fiaformulae.com first. Any site printing session times now is filling a gap with a guess.

Tickets and the parking offer, from the official page

Grandstand prices from the official ticket table for February 20, 2027, checked November 6, 2026: adults $82.45, children 3 to 15 $41.25, and accessible tickets at the same two prices. Buy through the official event page at fiaformulae.com. The comparison that sells this race writes itself: a family grandstand day here costs less than a single high demand ticket at most stadium events in this city, and the under 16 price is a genuine half, not a token cut.

Parking has a real offer attached, with terms worth reading exactly. The official page states a limited time offer: complimentary parking with purchase, claimed during checkout, limited to one pass per transaction. In plain terms, the parking is free only if you claim it while you're buying, and one pass covers one car per order. Don't buy tickets planning to sort parking later and expect the offer to follow you. Claim it in the checkout or pay at the stadium.

Getting to the stadium, and where to stay

Hard Rock Stadium sits in Miami Gardens, inland from the beach, and every stadium trip on this site runs on the same logic: drive and park, rideshare to the geofence lots, or base near the stadium corridor and shorten the trip. The official E-Prix page publishes no shuttle plan, so this guide promises none. Our Dolphins game day guide covers the same campus on a football Sunday, parking and arrival patterns included, and the Grand Prix traffic and parking guide covers the road pattern around the autodrome on race weekends. Read the parking shape there, then check the E-Prix page again in February for anything the series adds.

For a bed, the stadium corridor and the airport area are the practical bases, and Aventura is the step up. The date does you no favors on price: February 20 lands inside Presidents Day week travel and on the closing weekend stretch of the Boat Show season, with the Boat Show stay guide covering the week just before. Miami hotel prices that week move early. If the race is the point of the trip, book the room when you book the grandstand, not after.

Race day shape, honestly stated

Here's what a planner can and can't tell you in November. The date, the venue, the ticket prices and the parking offer are published and checked. The session timetable is not published. No shuttle plan is published. Hospitality and fan zone details will fill in on the official page as February nears, and this guide gets a refresh when they land. What you can do now is the part that sells out: the grandstand ticket, the parking claim inside that same checkout, and the room. Do those three in one sitting. The rest of race day can wait for the series to publish it, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you their guess as a plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same event as the Miami Grand Prix?
No. The Grand Prix is Formula 1, in May, at the same autodrome. The E-Prix is Formula E, electric cars, on February 20, 2027, at a far lower ticket price. Our Grand Prix hub covers the May race.

How much are tickets?
$82.45 for adults and $41.25 for children 3 to 15 in the grandstand, checked on the official page on November 6, 2026. Prices can change as inventory sells, so the checkout total governs.

Is parking really free?
Under the offer's terms, yes: complimentary with a ticket purchase, claimed during checkout, one pass per transaction, for a limited time. Outside those terms, expect standard stadium parking.

When do session times come out?
The official page had none published in November 2026. They'll post at fiaformulae.com, and this guide refreshes when they do.
