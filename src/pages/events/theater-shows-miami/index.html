Theater Shows in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami theater has a clear center of gravity: the Adrienne Arsht Center downtown, where the Broadway in Miami series brings national touring musicals to the 2,400 seat Ziff Ballet Opera House, and where Miami City Ballet and Florida Grand Opera are resident companies. Around that anchor sit the Fillmore Miami Beach for touring stage shows and big comedy, the Arsht's own black box studio theater for smaller plays, and one beloved suburban stage, Actors' Playhouse at the Miracle Theatre in Coral Gables. The season runs October to June, subscriptions go first, and single tickets for the big musicals sell out their best seats early.

Broadway in Miami: the 2026-27 season

The Arsht Center's Broadway in Miami series is the city's main stage subscription, and the published 2026-27 season is a strong one. It opened with Buena Vista Social Club (September 29 to October 4, 2026). Still ahead: A Beautiful Noise, the Neil Diamond musical, December 1 to 6, 2026; The Sound of Music, February 9 to 14, 2027; Disney's Beauty and the Beast, March 30 to April 4, 2027; and Jersey Boys, June 8 to 13, 2027. Each title plays a one week stand of eight performances in the Ziff Ballet Opera House, and the pattern is the same every time: subscribers take the center orchestra first, the Tuesday and Wednesday evenings are the value performances, and weekend matinees are where families land. Buy through the Arsht Center's own box office channels; the center is the only primary seller, and resale markups on the hit titles are steep. Ballet and opera on the same stage

The Ziff Ballet Opera House is a shared home. Miami City Ballet fills it across the season, with George Balanchine's The Nutcracker running December 11 to 27, 2026 as the city's defining holiday production; the full season is in our Miami City Ballet guide. Florida Grand Opera mounts three full productions a year in the same hall, sung in Italian with English and Spanish translations projected above the stage; the 2026-27 season is Lucia di Lammermoor in November, Don Giovanni in January and February, and Nabucco in April, detailed in our Florida Grand Opera guide. A visitor with one free evening in December could do worse than the Nutcracker; a visitor in April gets Nabucco's famous chorus. The Christmas in Miami guide places the ballet in the full holiday picture.

Beyond downtown

The Fillmore Miami Beach at the Jackie Gleason Theater, 1700 Washington Avenue, puts touring productions, dance companies, and major comedy on a proscenium stage in a historic Art Deco room of 2,713 seats; its calendar is in the Fillmore guide. The Carnival Studio Theater, the Arsht's black box space of up to 250 seats inside the Ziff building, is where smaller plays, cabaret, and experimental work land, and it is the best room in the city for seeing something new at close range. Actors' Playhouse at the Miracle Theatre in Coral Gables runs a full season of musicals and plays in an intimate restored movie palace on Miracle Mile, and it is the longtime home of Miami's serious local theater audience. Finally, an honesty note: the region's second big stage is the Broward Center for the Performing Arts in Fort Lauderdale, about a 45 minute drive north. Florida Grand Opera performs there too, and its Broadway series sometimes books titles Miami does not. It is a Broward night out, not a Miami one, and worth the drive only for a title you cannot miss.

How theater tickets work here

Subscriptions are the system's backbone: the Arsht sells its Broadway, ballet, opera, and classical series as packages first, and single tickets open later against what remains. For a visitor, the practical rules are: buy from the venue or company box office only, because Miami's hit shows attract aggressive resale pricing; consider a weeknight performance, which is cheaper and easier to park for than a Saturday; and check the running time before you book dinner, because a three hour musical that starts at 8 p.m. ends close to 11. Late seating is at the ushers' discretion and usually means watching the first scene on a lobby monitor, so build in time for the Metromover or the garage. Dress is Miami theater dress: smart casual covers everything, jackets appear at opening nights and opera, and nobody will stop you at the door either way.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best theater in Miami?
By scale and programming, the Adrienne Arsht Center downtown: it holds the Broadway tours, the ballet, and the opera under one roof. For intimacy, Actors' Playhouse in Coral Gables and the Arsht's Carnival Studio Theater are the connoisseurs' rooms.

Do Broadway tours come to Miami?
Yes. The Broadway in Miami series at the Arsht Center brings five national tours a season, each for a one week stand. The 2026-27 titles and dates are listed above.

What should I wear to the theater in Miami?
Smart casual is the working standard. Opening nights and opera draw jackets and dresses; a Tuesday musical draws everything from blazers to clean sneakers.
