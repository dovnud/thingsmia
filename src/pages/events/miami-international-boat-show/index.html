Miami International Boat Show Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Discover Boating Miami International Boat Show returns for its 86th edition from Wednesday February 10 to Sunday February 14, 2027. One ticket covers four venues: the Miami Beach Convention Center, Pride Park beside it, Sailor's Cove at IGY Yacht Haven Grande Miami on the MacArthur Causeway, and the Miami Beach Yacht Collection on Collins Avenue. Adult one day general admission is listed at $45 and children five and under are free, with prices checked against the organizer on October 30, 2026. Go on a weekday if you can; Saturday is the crush.

When is the 2027 show, and what are the hours?

The organizer lists the 2027 show from February 10 to 14, 2027, over Presidents Day weekend. Hours are 12:00 pm to 7:00 pm on Wednesday February 10, 10:00 am to 7:00 pm from Thursday February 11 through Saturday February 13, and 10:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sunday February 14. Wednesday is the preview day, with its own higher priced ticket, and it is the calmest full look at the convention center halls. Sunday is the short day and the deal day, with a discounted Super Sunday adult ticket listed for 2027.

Where does it take place? The four venues, explained

The Miami Beach Convention Center is the central hub: boats up to 49 feet on land, engine manufacturers, electronics, and retail pavilions from fishing gear to accessories. Pride Park, directly beside the convention center, is the lifestyle and entertainment core, with interactive activations, exhibitors, seminars, and kid friendly activities, plus a new Entry Point area for boats at entry level price points. Sailor's Cove at IGY Yacht Haven Grande Miami, at 838 MacArthur Causeway on Watson Island, is the dedicated sailing enclave, with sailboats and sailing gear on the water. The Miami Beach Yacht Collection on Collins Avenue, between 44th and 47th streets near the Fontainebleau and Eden Roc, is the premium in water showcase: powerboats and motor yachts from 30 to 125 feet, plus tenders, and the Windward VIP Lounge for premium ticket holders. Complimentary shuttles and water taxis connect the venues, and one ticket grants access to all four.

What do tickets cost?

Per the organizer's August 14, 2026 announcement, checked October 30, 2026: an adult one day general admission ticket is $45, a two day general admission ticket is $78, and the Wednesday preview day ticket is $66, with a two day preview plus general admission combination at $105. Youth tickets for ages 6 to 15 are $17 for one day, and children five and under enter free. New for 2027 are a family four pack at $99, valid Thursday to Sunday, and a Super Sunday adult ticket at $30. Tickets are sold at MiamiBoatShow.com. Prices can change as the show approaches, so treat this list as the checked October 2026 position and confirm at purchase.

How do you work the show like a visitor, not an exhibitor?

Start at the convention center early, when the halls are walkable, then take the shuttle or water taxi to the Collins Avenue Yacht Collection while the light is good, because the in water displays are the part of this show no indoor hall can copy. Dozens of complimentary seminars run daily, covering topics from sailing and safety to fishing and boating basics, and they are the fastest free education in the building. Stay on Miami Beach if you can: our Fontainebleau guide and Eden Roc guide cover the two big resorts a short walk from the Yacht Collection, and the hotels near the convention center page rounds up the closest bases. Book early, because Boat Show week fills Miami Beach at the same time as the Coconut Grove Arts Festival across the bay.

The facts that matter

Discover Boating Miami International Boat Show, checked against miamiboatshow.com on October 30, 2026: 86th edition, February 10 to 14, 2027; hours 12:00 pm to 7:00 pm Wednesday, 10:00 am to 7:00 pm Thursday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 5:00 pm Sunday; four venues (Miami Beach Convention Center, Pride Park, Sailor's Cove at 838 MacArthur Causeway, and the Miami Beach Yacht Collection on Collins Avenue between 44th and 47th streets); one ticket covers all venues, connected by complimentary shuttles and water taxis; adult one day general admission $45, youth 6 to 15 $17, children five and under free. The show is owned by the National Marine Manufacturers Association in partnership with the International Yacht Brokers Association and produced by Informa Markets.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see all four venues in one day?
Yes, with an early start and the shuttle or water taxi between sites. Most visitors pair the convention center and Pride Park in the morning and one in water venue in the afternoon.

Is the Boat Show the same week as anything else?
Yes. It overlaps Presidents Day weekend and the Coconut Grove Arts Festival, February 13 to 15, 2027, so hotels and restaurants book out earlier than a normal February week.

Where should I stay for the Boat Show?
On Miami Beach, near the convention center or along Collins Avenue in Mid Beach. That puts three of the four venues within walking or short shuttle distance.
