Miami-Dade County Youth Fair 2027: tickets, the closed days and the family plan

Last reviewed: November 8, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami-Dade County Youth Fair runs Thursday, March 18 to Sunday, April 11, 2027 at the fairgrounds in Tamiami Park, 10901 Coral Way, Miami, FL 33165. That much the organizer, the Miami-Dade County Fair and Exposition, has published for 2027, and it is in the fair's own 2027 documents. What the fair has not published yet, as of this page's November 8, 2026 check: the daily opening hours, general admission prices, ride pass prices, and parking prices. Those numbers do not appear below, because printing last year's prices as this year's facts is how fair pages mislead families. What is published, and what actually shapes a family visit, is the field trip calendar. It is the most useful planning fact on this page.

The five field trip days: free mornings that change the fair

The fair sets aside five School Field Trip Days for kindergarten through eighth grade groups, each running 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. with free admission during those hours for the school groups. The Field Trip Unlimited Rides Wristband costs $15 per person and goes on sale November 27, 2026. The 2027 dates, from the fair's own field trips page: Friday, March 19 for elementary grades 3 to 5; Friday, April 2 for Pre-K, kindergarten, and grades 1 to 2; Wednesday, April 7 for middle school grades 6 to 8 in the Central area; Thursday, April 8 for middle school grades 6 to 8, North and South; and Friday, April 9 for exceptional students. Entertainment shows do not run during field trip hours, and the no-backpack, no-stroller, no-private-vehicle rules on that page bind the school groups, not visiting families.

Why a family should care about a school calendar: on those five mornings the grounds fill with bused-in classes until 1 p.m., and the ride lines and food courts carry that crowd into the early afternoon. If your family can pick its day freely, pick a non-field-trip weekday. If your only free day is a field trip day, arrive for the general session after the buses leave and work the fair toward the evening. One calendar, two very different fairs.

Closed days: what is listed, and what is confirmed

A fair that runs 25 days does not run all 25 days, and the closed weekdays matter when you are choosing between two open dates. Here is the honest state of the record. The Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau's event listing names March 29 and 30 and April 5 and 6, 2027 as closed days. That listing also warns that its details reflect the 2026 edition, and the organizer's own site had not posted a 2027 daily calendar when this page was checked. Treat those four dates as probable, not settled, and confirm them on fairexpo.com before you promise a child a specific day. The moment the fair posts its 2027 schedule, this page takes the organizer's version and drops the warning. Until then the warning stays, because a family that drives to a closed fair does not care which website the error came from.

Tickets and hours: the not-yet-published list

General admission tiers, ride wristbands for the public, and any unlimited-ride days will price on the fair's ticket pages when the 2027 sale opens, and not before. The one public price the organizer has set for 2027 is the field trip wristband above, and it belongs to school groups. Parking at the fairgrounds, entrances off Coral Way and the side streets, and the fair's transit notes will also publish with the schedule. Do not budget from a roundup's copied table. Fair pricing changes year to year, the 2026 table is already stale, and the fair's own pages are the only version worth planning money on. Check fairexpo.com in January, when fair searches start and the sale normally opens, and this page will carry the verified figures with their check date.

The family plan for a fair day

The fairgrounds sit in west Miami-Dade, closer to the airport and Doral hotel rows than to the beach, and a fair day pairs naturally with a west-side base. Families flying in for the fair or stacking it with a spring trip should price the airport corridor first, through Miami airport area hotels on Expedia, and our Doral guide covers the nearest neighborhood base in detail. Go late in the day if the schedule allows it: the Florida sun is the fair's real admission price in March, and the lights, the midway, and the food rows all improve after it drops. Pack for a full outdoor day: sun hats for the kids, a refillable bottle each, and shoes that can handle grass, gravel, and a long walk back to the car.

The facts that matter

Youth Fair facts checked November 8, 2026: the 2027 fair runs March 18 to April 11, 2027 at 10901 Coral Way (SW 24th Street), Miami, FL 33165, in Tamiami Park (organizer documents and field trips page, fairexpo.com). Five field trip days run 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on March 19, April 2, April 7, April 8, and April 9, 2027, with a $15 unlimited rides wristband for those groups, on sale from November 27, 2026. The organizer had not published 2027 daily hours, general admission, public ride prices, or parking prices at this check. Closed days of March 29 to 30 and April 5 to 6 are listed by the visitor bureau with a 2026-edition warning attached, not confirmed by the organizer.

Frequently asked questions

When do general tickets go on sale?
The organizer had not opened or dated the 2027 general sale at this check. The field trip wristband date, November 27, 2026, is the first 2027 sale date the fair has published.

Is the fair open every day from March 18 to April 11?
No fair runs its full span without closed weekdays. The bureau lists four closed dates, flagged above as unconfirmed by the organizer. Check the fair's calendar before fixing a day.

Where should an out-of-town family stay?
On the airport and Doral side of the county. The fairgrounds are a west Miami-Dade trip, and a beach hotel adds a cross-county drive at fair-closing time.
