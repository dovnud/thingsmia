Ultra Music Festival Miami Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Ultra Music Festival returns to Bayfront Park in downtown Miami on Friday March 26 to Sunday March 28, 2027, for its 27th edition. General admission is 18 and over, VIP is 21 and over, and tickets are sold through Ultra's own site, ultramusicfestival.com. The festival is the closing act of Miami Music Week: the Winter Music Conference runs March 24 to 26 at the Kimpton EPIC Hotel just across the river, and hundreds of club nights, pool parties, and label showcases fill the days around it. Stay downtown or in Brickell and you can walk or ride the free Metromover to the gate; driving to Bayfront Park on an Ultra day is the one plan that never works.

The festival in brief

Ultra is the flagship of the Ultra Worldwide festival family and the biggest electronic music weekend in North America. It takes over Bayfront Park, the waterfront park at 301 North Biscayne Boulevard, and rebuilds it into a small city of stages for three days. Ultra has already announced the first phase of the 2027 lineup (the announcement landed on October 15, 2026), and the full lineup rolls out in phases through the winter, which is the rhythm regulars plan around: tickets first, lineup arguments later. The crowd is international and young, the production is the point as much as the DJs, and the festival runs rain or shine in a month when Miami weather is usually at its best.

The stages and how a day flows

The park holds a family of stages that regulars navigate by sound. The Main Stage, at the park's south end facing the bay, is where the headliners close each night with the full fireworks and production show. The Live Stage hosts bands and live electronic acts. The Worldwide Stage carries the broader EDM program, and the RESISTANCE Megastructure, Ultra's techno and house brand, is a cavernous indoor style structure that feels like a warehouse party dropped into the park, with its own Carl Cox led following. Days start in the afternoon and build; the last two hours of each night are when the whole site converges on the closing sets, so plan your must see acts earlier and let the ending come to you. Set times publish in the Ultra app close to the weekend, and the honest advice from every veteran is the same: pick three acts a day you refuse to miss and let the rest be discovery.

Tickets, ages, and rules

Buy only through ultramusicfestival.com. Ultra tiers its tickets (general admission, GA+ and VIP in recent editions) and sells them in price tiers that step up as each allotment sells out, so the cheapest ticket is always the earliest one. General admission is strictly 18 and over and VIP areas are 21 and over, with ID checks at entry; there are no single day guarantees in the early sales phases, as the festival's core product is the three day pass. Entry runs on wristbands linked to your purchase, so screenshots and second hand printouts are not a plan. Florida sun plus three days outdoors is the real opponent: the festival allows sealed sunscreen and empty hydration gear per its published policies, and every experienced attendee treats water and shade as part of the set schedule. Ultra publishes its full item policy and bag rules ahead of the weekend; read them before you pack, because the gate enforces them without exceptions.

Getting there without a car

Bayfront Park sits on Biscayne Boulevard in the center of downtown, which makes Ultra one of the easiest big festivals in America to reach by transit. The Metrorail from the airport corridor and the southern suburbs stops at Government Center, a short walk or one free Metromover stop from the park. The Metromover's Omni and Inner loops circle downtown and Brickell and pass the park directly, and they are free. Coming from Miami Beach, buses and rideshares cross the causeways early in the day without drama and crawl by evening; go early. Brightline's MiamiCentral station is a few blocks away for anyone arriving from Fort Lauderdale or West Palm Beach. There is no meaningful festival parking: downtown garages fill by mid afternoon and surge in price. The closing hour is its own event, as tens of thousands leave at once; walking fifteen minutes toward Brickell or up Biscayne before ordering a car home saves real time.

Where to stay

Downtown and Brickell are the Ultra bases: the hotels there are a walk or one Metromover ride from the gate, and our hotel guides cover the main options. Miami Beach is the choice for anyone splitting the trip between the festival and Music Week pool parties, at the cost of a causeway trip each day. Either way, book early: Ultra weekend sits on top of the Miami Open finals and spring break season, and it is one of the most expensive hotel weekends of the Miami year.

Frequently asked questions

When is Ultra Music Festival 2027?
Friday March 26 to Sunday March 28, 2027, at Bayfront Park in downtown Miami. Source: Ultra Music Festival, ultramusicfestival.com.

How old do you have to be for Ultra Miami?
18 and over for general admission, 21 and over for VIP areas, with photo ID checked at entry.

Is Ultra part of Miami Music Week?
Ultra is the headline of the week. The Winter Music Conference (March 24 to 26, 2027) and hundreds of surrounding parties make up the rest; see our Miami Music Week guide.
