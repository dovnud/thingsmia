What the festival is

Jazz in the Gardens grew from a city concert series into one of the country's significant Black music festivals, and its name undersells its range. The lineups mix the genres its audience grew up with: classic and contemporary jazz, R&B and soul headliners, gospel touches, and hip hop legends, across two days on the grounds of Hard Rock Stadium at 347 Don Shula Drive in Miami Gardens. The crowd is grown, loyal, and dressed; this is a festival where the audience treats the weekend as an occasion. Around the music, the festival grounds carry food vendors with a strong soul food and Caribbean showing, market stalls, and the social, reunion atmosphere that makes the event feel as much like a family gathering as a concert. The 2026 edition was billed as the festival's 19th; the 2027 weekend will be its 20th, a milestone the city is expected to program accordingly.

Dates, tickets, and the lineup pattern

Here is the honest state of the facts. The official festival site, jazzinthegardens.com, currently announces "March 2027, Miami Gardens, FL" and nothing more specific. The 2026 edition played Saturday and Sunday, March 7 and 8, which suggests an early March weekend as the likely 2027 landing spot, but suggestion is not a date, and this page does not print one. When the city announces, expect the pattern of recent editions: a two day Saturday and Sunday program, one and two day tickets, premium and VIP tiers with lounge access, and a lineup released in waves through the winter. Tickets will sell through the festival's official channels and the stadium's ticketing partners. Getting to Hard Rock Stadium

Hard Rock Stadium sits in Miami Gardens, northwest of downtown Miami, and Jazz in the Gardens is a drive or rideshare event for most visitors. The stadium's lots open for festival parking, with premium and general tiers sold ahead in recent editions; arriving for the first sets rather than the headliners avoids the worst of the entry queues. Rideshare works well inbound and stretches thin at the close, when two days of crowd leave at once; the standard counter is to walk a few minutes from the gates before ordering, or to let the last encore crowd go while you finish the night at the food stalls. There is no rail station at the stadium. Stay nearby in Miami Gardens or Aventura if the festival is your whole trip; stay downtown or on the beach if it is one night of a bigger Miami week, and budget the drive both ways. Frequently asked questions

When is Jazz in the Gardens 2027?
In March 2027 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. The exact weekend has not been announced by the City of Miami Gardens yet; the 2026 edition ran March 7 and 8. Check jazzinthegardens.com for the announcement.

Where is it held?
Hard Rock Stadium, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens, the same stadium that hosts the Miami Dolphins, the Miami Open tennis, and the Miami Grand Prix.

What kind of music is it, really?
Jazz is the root, but the bills run across R&B, soul, gospel, and classic hip hop. Think legacy headliners and a grown audience rather than a club jazz program.
