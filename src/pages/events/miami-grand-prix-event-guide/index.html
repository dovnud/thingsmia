Miami Grand Prix Event Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix runs Friday April 30 to Sunday May 2, 2027, at the Miami International Autodrome, the temporary circuit built around Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. It is the sixth edition of the race, every previous edition has sold out according to the promoter, and general sale tickets opened on September 24, 2026 through the race's official channels at f1miamigp.com. The headline change for 2027: an expanded Paddock Club that extends toward Turn 1, with Paddock Club tickets offered on public sale for the first time. The race itself is on Sunday, May 2. This guide covers the weekend's shape and logistics; the full cluster, spectator guide, traffic and parking, costs, and hotels, is gathered in the Miami Grand Prix hub.

The weekend's shape

A Grand Prix weekend is a three day build. Friday is practice, the day the campus is loosest and the grandstands are easiest, and the connoisseurs' day for photography and wandering. Saturday brings qualifying, which sets Sunday's grid and is, for many fans, the most purely competitive session of the weekend. Sunday is race day: the full house, the ceremonies, and the main event in the afternoon. Around the on track program, the Miami campus runs like a festival: food and drink villages, sponsor exhibits, concerts and DJ sets, and the famous marina and beach club style hospitality zones that made this race's reputation as Formula 1's most lifestyle forward weekend. Support series and historic car demonstrations fill the gaps between sessions in the usual Grand Prix pattern. Gates open well before the first session each day, and the honest pacing advice is to arrive for the morning, not for the race: the campus is half the product.

Tickets: what sold, and what to do now

The promoter put 2027 tickets on general sale on September 24, 2026, and the structural fact of this race is demand: the promoter states that every previous edition, 2022 through 2026, has sold out. The inventory ladder runs from grounds passes and grandstands up through the hospitality tiers to the Paddock Club, which for 2027 grows into an expanded footprint reaching toward Turn 1 and, notably, is sold publicly for the first time rather than by invitation and allocation alone. Practical guidance: buy through f1miamigp.com or Formula 1's official channels only, because a sold out race is a magnet for fraudulent resale; consider Friday or Saturday if Sunday inventory is gone or priced beyond sense, since both days deliver the cars at full speed with a fraction of the crowd pressure; and read our Miami Grand Prix cost guide before assuming what a weekend here has to cost, because the range between a grounds pass weekend and a hospitality weekend is enormous.

The place: Miami Gardens, not Miami Beach

First timer's geography: the Autodrome is built in and around Hard Rock Stadium at 347 Don Shula Drive in Miami Gardens, inland in northwest Miami-Dade, roughly half an hour from Miami Beach and downtown in normal traffic, and longer on race weekend. The circuit uses the stadium's grounds and parking fields, and the stadium itself becomes part of the campus, with viewing and hospitality inside it. There is no rail station at the stadium. Race weekend runs on prebooked parking, rideshare zones, and shuttle operations, all detailed in the traffic and parking guide. Where you sleep is a real decision: the Grand Prix hotels guide weighs Miami Gardens and Aventura convenience against downtown, Brickell, and the Beach for atmosphere, and the spectator guide covers grandstand choices, what to bring, and how a race day flows hour by hour.

What makes Miami different

Every Grand Prix has a personality, and Miami's is the festival: this is the race that pairs the world's most technical sport with a South Florida weekend, concerts, food programming, and hospitality built for people who may watch half the laps. Purists once sniffed; the sellouts settled the argument. For a visitor, the synthesis is genuinely enjoyable: serious racing on a real street style circuit, wrapped in the best organized party on the Formula 1 calendar. Late April and early May also give the race Miami's kindest weather window, warm and mostly dry, after the March madness of the city's festival calendar and before the summer heat arrives.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Miami Grand Prix 2027?
Friday April 30 to Sunday May 2, 2027, with the race on Sunday May 2, at the Miami International Autodrome around Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. Source: the race promoter, f1miamigp.com.

Has it sold out before?
Yes. The promoter states every edition from 2022 through 2026 sold out. Buy through official channels early, and be skeptical of resale for a sold out event.

Where should I stay?
Miami Gardens and Aventura minimize the drive; downtown, Brickell, and Miami Beach maximize the trip around the race. The hotels guide weighs both, and the hub links the whole cluster.
