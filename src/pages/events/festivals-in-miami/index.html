Festivals in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami's year is a festival calendar with a city attached. December belongs to Art Basel. February stacks the Boat Show, the Coconut Grove Arts Festival, and SOBEWFF into three weeks. March is the mountain: Calle Ocho, the Miami Open, the Winter Music Conference, and Ultra in a single month. April adds the Film Festival, and the Grand Prix closes the spring on the first weekend of May. Every date below was checked against the organizer or venue for the 2026-27 season, and where an organizer has not yet published a 2027 date, this page says so instead of guessing.

December to February: art, boats, and food

Art Basel Miami Beach, December 4 to 6, 2026. The anchor fair of Miami Art Week at the Miami Beach Convention Center, with satellite fairs and museum openings across the city. Start with the Art Basel hub and the visitor guide.

Miami Marathon weekend, January 31, 2027. The Life Time Miami Marathon and Half finish at Bayfront Park and turn downtown into a race city for a weekend; see the Marathon visitor guide.

Discover Boating Miami International Boat Show, February 10 to 14, 2027. Five days across Miami Beach, from the convention center to the yacht collections on Collins Avenue; our Boat Show guide explains the multi venue layout.

Coconut Grove Arts Festival, February 13 to 15, 2027. The Presidents Day weekend outdoor art show on the Coconut Grove waterfront, running since the 1960s; see the festival guide.

South Beach Wine and Food Festival, February 25 to 28, 2027. Four days of ticketed tastings, dinners, and beach events in South Beach, produced for FIU's hospitality school; see the SOBEWFF guide.

March: the biggest month in Miami

Calle Ocho, March 14, 2027. Fifteen blocks of SW 8th Street in Little Havana close for the free finale of Carnaval Miami, produced by the Kiwanis Club of Little Havana. Our Calle Ocho guide has the plan for the day.

Miami Open tennis, March 14 to 28, 2027. Two weeks of ATP and WTA tennis at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, overlapping almost everything else in March. The sports hub links the visitor guide.

Jazz in the Gardens, March 2027. The City of Miami Gardens' two day festival of jazz, R&B, and soul at Hard Rock Stadium. The city has announced the month; the exact 2027 weekend had not been published when this page was checked on October 30, 2026. See the festival guide.

Winter Music Conference, March 24 to 26, 2027, and Ultra Music Festival, March 26 to 28, 2027. The conference at the Kimpton EPIC Hotel downtown hands off to Ultra at Bayfront Park, and the whole week between them is Miami Music Week. Guides: Miami Music Week and Ultra.

April and May: film and Formula 1

Miami Film Festival, April 1 to 11, 2027. Miami Dade College's 44th edition, the country's leading showcase for Ibero-American cinema; see the festival guide. Its fall sidebar, GEMS, runs October 29 to November 5, 2026.

Miami Grand Prix, April 30 to May 2, 2027. Formula 1 at the Miami International Autodrome around Hard Rock Stadium. The Grand Prix hub gathers the event guide, spectator, parking, cost, and hotel guides.

Miami Swim Week, late May (2027 dates pending). The swimwear industry takes over Miami Beach in the last week of May; the 2026 editions ran May 27 to 31, and neither major production had published 2027 dates when this page was checked. See the Swim Week guide.

How to pick, and how far ahead to book

Pick by what fills the rest of your days. Art Basel and SOBEWFF are walkable, hotel centric festivals where the base matters most. Ultra, Calle Ocho, and the Grand Prix are single site events where transport planning matters more than the room. The Boat Show and Coconut Grove reward early starts and punish late sleepers. On booking: for December, February, and March weekends, hotels should be booked months out, because those festivals stack on top of peak season rates; the shoulder festivals in April and May are where a spontaneous trip still works. Every festival above has a full ThingsMIA guide with its own hotel advice; the events hub keeps them all in one calendar.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest festival in Miami?
By attendance pressure on the city, Ultra and Miami Music Week in March and Art Basel week in December are the two peaks. By footprint, the Boat Show's five days across Miami Beach come close.

Which Miami festivals are free?
Calle Ocho is fully free. Art Basel's outdoor projects, the Coconut Grove festival's street scene, and much of Miami Art Week's public programming are free; the headline events themselves are ticketed.

When should I avoid Miami if I dislike crowds?
Mid March, when the Miami Open, Calle Ocho, and the run into Music Week overlap, and the first week of December, when Art Basel fills Miami Beach.
