Miami Swim Week Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami Swim Week is the swimwear industry's annual takeover of Miami Beach in late May, built around two main productions: Paraiso Miami Swim Week, which stages its runway tent in Collins Park by the beach, and Miami Swim Week The Shows, which headquartered at the Mondrian South Beach in 2026. The 2026 editions ran May 27 to 31. For 2027, neither production had published official dates when this page was checked on October 30, 2026, so plan around the last week of May and confirm on the organizers' sites before booking flights. It is a trade week first: much of it is industry and invitation only, but public passes, parties, and pop ups make it a real spectator event if you choose your moments.

Two productions, one week

The confusing thing about Miami Swim Week is that it is not a single organization. Paraiso Miami Swim Week is the established platform, 22 editions old in 2026, built around an oceanfront runway tent in Collins Park at 2100 Collins Avenue, with dozens of satellite events across South Beach: runway shows, wellness mornings, brand dinners, and pop up shops. Its designer list is the industry's working core, heavy on Latin American and resort labels. Miami Swim Week The Shows is the second production, a multi day schedule of runway shows that in 2026 headquartered at the Mondrian South Beach on West Avenue and presented more than 150 designers across its program, with a more open, ticketed feel. Around both, satellite producers, hotel parties, and sample sales fill the week. When people say "Swim Week," they mean the sum of all of it, and any single schedule you find covers only its own slice.

What a visitor can actually attend

Calibrate expectations first: the front rows are buyers, editors, and influencers, and many shows are invitation only because the week exists to sell next year's collections to stores. But the public layer is real and growing. Paraiso has sold public passes for its tent shows in recent editions (priced as a premium product), The Shows sells tickets to its runway blocks, and the satellite layer of launch parties, pop ups, fitness events, and hotel activations is largely open or ticketed to the public. The free version of Swim Week is the city itself that week: South Beach fills with the industry, hotel lobbies become casting calls, and the people watching on Collins Avenue and Lincoln Road is a spectator sport. Check both organizers' sites as May approaches; public ticket inventory typically releases in the spring, not the fall.

Where to stay and how the week feels

The week's center of gravity is Collins Park and the Collins Avenue corridor in the low 20s streets, with The Shows across the bay side of South Beach at the Mondrian on West Avenue. Staying in the Collins corridor puts Paraiso's tent, the convention center area events, and the beach in walking range; our Loews guide covers the big full service base just south of Collins Park, and the Mondrian South Beach guide covers The Shows' 2026 home. Late May is the edge of Miami's wet season: hot, humid, with afternoon storms that the whole week plans around, and hotel rates a step below the winter peak. It pairs naturally with a beach trip; stay for the shows, swim in the mornings, and treat the industry chaos as the entertainment it is.

Swim Week versus the other fashion moments

Visitors sometimes confuse Swim Week with Art Basel week in December; they are opposite poles. December is art, money, and winter formality. Swim Week is trade, sunshine, and the business of the beach itself, and it is the more Miami of the two. It also sits in a friendlier price window: flights and hotels in late May cost less than in December or March. If fashion is the draw and budget matters, this is the week. Frequently asked questions

When is Miami Swim Week 2027?
Not yet officially announced. The 2026 editions ran May 27 to 31, and the week traditionally falls in late May. Confirm dates on the Paraiso and Miami Swim Week The Shows sites before booking travel.

Can the public attend the runway shows?
Partly. Many shows are industry and invitation only, but both main productions have sold public passes or tickets in recent editions, and the satellite parties and pop ups are largely open. Public inventory usually releases in spring.

Where does it happen?
Paraiso's tent stands in Collins Park on Collins Avenue in Miami Beach; The Shows headquartered at the Mondrian South Beach in 2026, with events spread across South Beach hotels.
