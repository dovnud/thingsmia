South Beach Wine and Food Festival Guide

Last reviewed: November 2, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Food Network South Beach Wine and Food Festival, presented by Capital One and known everywhere as SOBEWFF, runs Thursday February 25 to Sunday February 28, 2027, in its 26th edition. It is not one event but a menu of separately ticketed ones: chef dinners, walk around tastings on the sand, brunches, demos, and late night parties spread across South Beach and beyond. Every ticket's net proceeds benefit the Chaplin School of Hospitality and Tourism Management at Florida International University, and the festival has raised more than $46 million for the school to date. Public tickets went on sale Monday November 2, 2026 at 9 a.m. Eastern at sobewff.org and are on sale now, and the signature events sell out, so booking early matters.

How the festival works

Think of SOBEWFF as a restaurant week crossed with a trade show, staged on a beach. You do not buy a festival pass and wander; you build a schedule event by event, each with its own ticket, venue, and time. The formats repeat every year: intimate chef dinners in restaurants and private spaces, big walk around tastings where dozens of chefs plate small bites in one tent or courtyard, cooking demos and panels with Food Network personalities, fitness and family side events, and parties that run late. Most events are 21 and over because alcohol is the second ingredient in everything. The production runs with Southern Glazer's Wine and Spirits alongside FIU, and hundreds of FIU hospitality students staff the weekend, which is the point: the festival is a working classroom that happens to feed thousands of guests.

The signature events

Two anchors organize most first timers' weekends. On opening night, Thursday February 25, Mike's Amazing Burger Bash, hosted by Rachael Ray, fills its beachside space with burger stands from chefs across the country, a judged competition, and the kind of crowd that treats a burger tasting as a formal occasion. On Saturday and Sunday, Goya Foods' Grand Tasting Village is the festival's big tent: a walk around village of tasting tents, demo stages, and brand pavilions on the sand in South Beach, and the single best ticket for anyone who wants the widest survey of the weekend in one afternoon. Around those two, the festival's November 2, 2026 lineup announcement names Coca-Cola presents Tournament of Champions LIVE, hosted by Guy Fieri, on Friday February 26, Tacos and Tequila, hosted by Aaron Sanchez, on Saturday February 27, and two Sunday closers on February 28: Overtown EatUp, hosted by Marcus Samuelsson, and Classic American Eats, hosted by Andrew Zimmern with a special performance by Rev Run. The announcement counts more than 500 chefs, culinary personalities, and tastemakers across the weekend, with events spanning from Homestead to Miami Beach and as far north as Fort Lauderdale. The full event list is live at sobewff.org; pick two or three anchors, leave air between them, and resist the urge to stack two tastings back to back, because the portions are small but the count is not.

Tickets and timing

Capital One cardholders got a presale window from October 26, 2026; the public on sale opened Monday November 2, 2026 at 9 a.m. Eastern on sobewff.org, and tickets are on sale now. Prices vary by format, from modest sums for demos and family events to several hundred dollars for the marquee dinners and the Grand Tasting Village's premium tiers, and the festival's own page for each event states exactly what the ticket includes. Two rules from experience: the named chef dinners go first, because each seats a roomful of people once; and Sunday events are the value play, when locals with Monday jobs thin out. If an event you want sells out, the festival sometimes releases small additional runs closer to February, so joining the waitlist and the mailing list is worth the two minutes.

Where to stay, and how to pace the weekend

The festival is centered on South Beach, with the Grand Tasting Village on the sand and many events within the beach's walkable core, so a South Beach hotel turns the weekend into a walking trip. Our best hotels in South Beach guide and the Loews and Betsy guides cover the practical bases at different budgets. Events also spill to the mainland, to Wynwood, the Design District, and Coral Gables, so keep one rideshare budget line for the weekend. Pacing advice that veterans repeat: one big tasting a day is a full program, hydrate like it is a sport, and schedule nothing important for the morning after Burger Bash.

Frequently asked questions

When do SOBEWFF 2027 tickets go on sale?
To the public on Monday November 2, 2026 at 9 a.m. Eastern at sobewff.org, and tickets are on sale now. A Capital One cardholder presale ran from October 26, 2026.

What should a first timer book?
The Grand Tasting Village on Saturday or Sunday for breadth, plus one smaller event, a dinner or Burger Bash on Thursday, for depth. Two anchors and air between them beat a stacked schedule.

Where does the money go?
All net proceeds benefit the Chaplin School of Hospitality and Tourism Management at Florida International University, which co-produces the festival with Southern Glazer's. More than $46 million has been raised to date.
