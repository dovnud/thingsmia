Miami Events Guide 2027

Miami's event year runs on a handful of huge weekends: Art Basel in December, the Boat Show and the South Beach Wine and Food Festival in February, Calle Ocho and Ultra in March, and the Miami Grand Prix at the end of April. This hub links every event, festival, and venue guide on the site. Every date printed here was checked against the event's own organizer before publication.

Next editions, dates verified with the organizers

  • Art Basel Miami Beach: public days December 4 to 6, 2026, at the Miami Beach Convention Center (artbasel.com).
  • Miami International Boat Show: February 10 to 14, 2027, across Miami Beach venues (miamiboatshow.com).
  • South Beach Wine and Food Festival: February 25 to 28, 2027 (sobewff.org).
  • Calle Ocho: March 14, 2027, on SW 8th Street in Little Havana (carnavalmiami.com).
  • Ultra Music Festival: March 26 to 28, 2027, at Bayfront Park (ultramusicfestival.com).
  • Miami Grand Prix: April 30 to May 2, 2027, at the Miami International Autodrome, Miami Gardens (f1miamigp.com).
  • Art Deco Weekend: usually held in January on Ocean Drive; the organizer, the Miami Design Preservation League, announces each edition's dates.

Frequently asked questions

When is Art Basel Miami Beach?
The next edition's public days are Friday December 4 to Sunday December 6, 2026, at the Miami Beach Convention Center, with invitation only preview days on December 2 and 3. Source: Art Basel, artbasel.com.

When is Ultra Music Festival in 2027?
Ultra returns to Bayfront Park in downtown Miami on March 26 to 28, 2027, per the festival's own announcement on ultramusicfestival.com. General admission is 18 and over; VIP is 21 and over.

When is the Miami Grand Prix?
The Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix runs April 30 to May 2, 2027, at the Miami International Autodrome around Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. Source: the race promoter, f1miamigp.com.

When are the Boat Show and the South Beach Wine and Food Festival?
The Discover Boating Miami International Boat Show runs February 10 to 14, 2027 (miamiboatshow.com), and SOBEWFF runs February 25 to 28, 2027 (sobewff.org). February is the most crowded month on the Miami visitor calendar.

How far ahead should I book a hotel for a big event weekend?
As early as you reasonably can once your dates are set. Miami hotel prices climb steeply as the big weekends fill, and the areas closest to each event go first. Our event guides link to the hotel areas that work best for each weekend.
