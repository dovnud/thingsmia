Miami Music Week Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami Music Week is the week electronic music takes the whole city: in 2027 it builds around the Winter Music Conference, March 24 to 26 at the Kimpton EPIC Hotel downtown, and closes with Ultra Music Festival at Bayfront Park, March 26 to 28. Between and around those anchors, labels, promoters, and hotels stage a rolling program of club nights, pool parties, boat parties, and showcases across Miami Beach, downtown, and Wynwood, from the Tuesday before Ultra through the Sunday night after. There is no single ticket and no single schedule; you build your week event by event. The strategy that works: book your two or three headline nights early, leave the days loose, and stay somewhere that splits the difference between the beach parties and downtown.

The Winter Music Conference, the week's industry core

The Winter Music Conference, WMC, is the world's longest running electronic music conference, and its 37th edition takes over the Kimpton EPIC Hotel at 270 Biscayne Boulevard Way in downtown Miami from Wednesday March 24 to Friday March 26, 2027. Per the conference's own program, the week mixes panels, keynote interviews, workshops, a gear exhibition, and networking sessions across two tracks, Industry and Creators, plus two rooftop pool parties at the EPIC, which is also the official Miami Music Week hotel. One Pro Badge covers the conference programming and the pool parties, and the badge also carries entry to sanctioned Miami Music Week events across the city, subject to each venue's capacity. Conference programming is 18 and over; the pool parties are 21 and over. If you work in the industry, or want to, WMC is the reason to arrive on Tuesday; if you are purely a fan, its pool parties and the hotel's orbit are the accessible layer.

The party map

Music Week spreads across three territories, each with its own hours. Miami Beach owns the days and the glamour nights: hotel pool parties through the afternoons, and the big clubs after dark. Downtown owns Ultra itself at Bayfront Park and the warehouse scale afterparties near the park. Wynwood owns the open air and underground end, with garden venues and art district spaces hosting label showcases that run long. Events announce in waves from January onward, and the full picture only exists in the final weeks, aggregated by the week's listing sites and the promoters themselves. Two disciplines save a Music Week: buy the nights you cannot miss as soon as they announce, because headline rooms sell out and resale is brutal; and never stack a pool party, a festival day, and a 4 a.m. club set in the same 24 hours more than once. The week is a marathon run at sprint pace, and the veterans pace it.

How Ultra fits, and how to split your time

Ultra, Friday to Sunday at Bayfront Park, is the week's sun. Many visitors do Ultra only and treat Music Week as the frame; others skip the festival and run a pure club week at half the cost. The hybrid that satisfies most people: pool party or WMC session on Thursday, Ultra on Friday and Sunday, one great club night on Saturday after the festival closes, and a slow Monday. Our Ultra guide covers the festival itself: tickets, ages, stages, and the no car rule for Bayfront Park. Note the pileup around the week: the Miami Open tennis finals run the same weekend (March 27 and 28, 2027) at Hard Rock Stadium, and spring break is still in the air, so flights and hotels for this week are among the most contested of the Miami year.

Where to stay

Downtown and Brickell are the efficient base: walking distance to the EPIC and Bayfront Park, on the free Metromover, and a short causeway ride to the Beach. Miami Beach is the immersive base: pool parties at your door, clubs around you, and a daily trip downtown for Ultra. Wynwood suits the showcase crowd. Wherever you land, book it the moment your dates are set; this week does not get cheaper as it approaches. The hotel guides cover the districts, and the festivals guide shows what else March throws at the city.

Frequently asked questions

When is Miami Music Week 2027?
The core dates are the Winter Music Conference, March 24 to 26, 2027, and Ultra, March 26 to 28, 2027, with parties running from the days before the conference through the Sunday night after Ultra.

Do I need a badge or pass for the whole week?
No. Music Week is a loose umbrella of separately ticketed events. The WMC Pro Badge covers the conference and its pool parties and adds entry to sanctioned events subject to capacity; everything else is bought night by night.

Where should I stay for Music Week?
Downtown or Brickell for efficiency (WMC and Ultra on foot), Miami Beach for the pool party circuit. Book as early as you can; it is one of the most expensive hotel weeks of the year.
