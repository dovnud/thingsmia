Miami Book Fair Guide

Last reviewed: November 9, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami Book Fair runs Sunday November 15 to Sunday November 22, 2026, at Miami Dade College's Wolfson Campus in Downtown Miami, 300 NE Second Avenue. It is the Fair's 43rd edition, presented by Miami Dade College, with nearly 500 authors on the announced lineup. The three day Street Fair closes the week, running Friday November 20 to Sunday November 22, and Street Fair admission is free on the Friday. Buy tickets through the Fair's own site at miamibookfair.com.

What is the Miami Book Fair, exactly?

It is an eight day literary festival run by Miami Dade College, founded in 1984 by the college and partners and held each November in downtown Miami. The 2026 lineup announced by the college includes Margaret Atwood, Colson Whitehead, Marlon James, Tony Shalhoub, and Bonnie Garmus, alongside Ani DiFranco, Susan Orlean, Edwidge Danticat, Hernan Diaz, and Yann Martel, among many others. Programming runs in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole, which is part of what makes this fair feel like Miami rather than a touring book event that happens to stop here. Alongside the headline fiction and nonfiction, the program strands cover poetry, romance, Caribbean and IberoAmerican writing, Jewish life and culture, art and design, and business and technology.

The week has two distinct halves. The first half is built around author talks inside the campus buildings, including the Evenings With series. The second half opens the streets around the campus for the Street Fair, when the festival turns into an outdoor market of booksellers and publishers with stages, food, and family programming. Which half you choose depends on whether you came to hear one writer speak in a room or to wander, browse, and discover writers you had not planned on.

What happens during the week?

The Fair opens on Sunday November 15 with an Opening Day Party, described by the college as a free street party on the campus with live music, food vendors, a full bar, and books for sale. The Evenings With series runs November 15 to 20 and is the Fair's signature set of ticketed evening conversations; the announced names include Tony Shalhoub with Josie Adams, Salman Rushdie with Rachel Eliza Griffiths, Colson Whitehead, Elena Armas in conversation with Meg Cabot, Jeff Garlin, and Victoria Aveyard. On weeknights from November 16 to 19, the Lost Chapter Lounge in the atrium of Building 1 opens from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. before and after the evening events. A practical warning from the program itself: individual sessions are ticketed separately, some talks fill early, and the full schedule sits on miamibookfair.com, so pick your must see authors first and build the rest of the week around them.

How does the Street Fair work?

The Street Fair runs Friday November 20 to Sunday November 22, 2026, on the streets around the Wolfson Campus. Hours are 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Friday and Saturday and 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday, with gates opening at 9 a.m. each day. Per Miami Dade College, it brings together hundreds of booksellers and publishers, with author presentations, live music on the Off the Shelf stage, and food vendors through the weekend. Inside it, Children's Alley runs on the same three days with interactive storytelling, live performances, hands on science, and creative activities for families, plus a children's and teen author program that this year includes Meg Cabot, Sabaa Tahir, and Gail Carson Levine. If you only have one day for the Fair, make it a Street Fair day: it is the one part of the week where talks, browsing, music, and family programming all happen within a few downtown blocks at the same time.

How do tickets work?

Tickets are sold at MiamiBookFair.com/tickets, per the college. Street Fair admission is free on Friday November 20. For Saturday and Sunday, the college lists single day tickets at $12, with discounted admission of $5 for ages 13 to 18 and $7 for seniors over 62, and children 12 and under are always free. Multi day passes are listed at $18, with discounts for seniors and teens. Evenings With tickets are sold per event, and the college notes that they include admission and often a copy of the featured book, so read the listing for the session you want before you buy, because what is included varies by event. Prices on this page were checked against the college's announcement on October 29, 2026; if a session matters to you, confirm its current price and availability on the Fair's site before you travel.

Getting there and parking

The Wolfson Campus sits in the middle of Downtown Miami at 300 NE Second Avenue, a few blocks from Biscayne Bay. Miami Dade College lists free parking at the MDC Wolfson Campus Parking Garage from Sunday November 15 to Sunday November 22; the garage is at 500 NE Second Avenue, with its entrance on NE Fifth Street between First and Second avenues. The college also lists $5 flat rate parking at the Miami Parking Authority's College Station Garage for Book Fair events across the same dates. Downtown is the part of Miami where driving is most optional: our getting around Miami guide explains the transit and rideshare options for the downtown core, and during Street Fair weekend the streets immediately around the campus are given over to the Fair, so expect closures and plan to walk the last few blocks either way.

Where should you stay?

Stay downtown if the Fair is the point of your trip. A Downtown Miami or Brickell base puts you within walking distance of the campus, which matters when you are carrying a bag of signed books back at night and returning for a morning session. Frequently asked questions

Is the Miami Book Fair free?
Parts of it are. The Opening Day Party on November 15 and Street Fair admission on Friday November 20 are free, and children 12 and under enter the Street Fair free all weekend. Author sessions and the Saturday and Sunday Street Fair are ticketed, with the prices listed above as checked on October 29, 2026.

Where exactly is the Fair held?
At Miami Dade College's Wolfson Campus, 300 NE Second Avenue in Downtown Miami. The Street Fair spreads onto the streets around the campus for its final three days, November 20 to 22.

Do I need tickets for individual author talks?
Yes. Evenings With and many individual sessions are ticketed separately through miamibookfair.com, and popular names can sell out. Book the sessions you care about first, then fill the gaps with the Street Fair and free programming.

Is the Fair good for children?
Yes, particularly on Street Fair weekend. Children's Alley runs November 20 to 22 with storytelling, performances, and hands on activities, and the wider program includes children's and teen authors. Our Miami with kids guide helps plan the rest of a family trip around it.
