Hard Rock Live Hollywood Visitor Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first, with the geography stated plainly. Hard Rock Live is not in Miami. It is the 7,000 seat indoor theater inside the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel and Casino at 1 Seminole Way, Hollywood, FL 33314, in Broward County, roughly 20 miles north of downtown Miami and 30 to 45 minutes by car depending on traffic. Miami visitors go anyway, constantly, because the room is superb and its calendar is stacked: A list concerts, superstar stand up, award shows, and televised events in a theater that feels half its size. The current version of the venue opened on October 25, 2019, part of the property's $1.5 billion expansion that also built the famous Guitar Hotel, and it cost more than $125 million on its own. Ed Sheeran played two nights here on October 29 and 30, 2026, the week this guide was written; that is the tier of act this room exists for.

The room: a 7,000 seat theater that feels smaller

Hard Rock Live's trick is its shape. The 225,000 square foot venue, designed by Sceno Plus, wraps its seats in a clamshell arc around the stage, so the farthest seat is far closer to the performer than the capacity number implies, and the house systems, sound, lighting, and giant HD screens, are built in rather than trucked in. The result is the region's best seat to capacity ratio for major acts, and Pollstar named it Casino and Resort Venue of the Year at its 2025 awards. Programming leans into that advantage: legacy rock and pop residencies, Latin superstars, country, major comedy, boxing and mixed martial arts, and televised award and pageant nights. The calendar is on the property's site, seminolehardrockhollywood.com, and tickets sell through its official channels; for the biggest names, the room's modest size means sellouts come fast.

The trip up: plan it like a small journey

From Miami, this is a driving event. The property sits on the Hollywood Seminole Reservation along State Road 7 (US 441), west of the beaches, and the practical routes are I-95 or Florida's Turnpike north, then west. In light traffic, downtown Miami and Miami Beach are both roughly half an hour; on a Friday evening with a sold out show, budget the better part of an hour and park once, in the property's garages, which are free and built for exactly this surge. Rideshare is easy on the way in and expensive on the way out at the final encore, when several thousand people order at once; walking ten minutes into the property for a drink while the surge passes is the local counter. There is no useful transit option from Miami for a late show. If the concert is the centerpiece of your trip, staying on property or in Hollywood or Fort Lauderdale removes the whole problem; our hotel advice below covers both plays.

The resort around the show

The venue sits inside a full resort, which changes the evening's shape. The Guitar Hotel, the 450 foot tower shaped like its namesake, lights the sky for miles and anchors a property with a large casino floor, a deep restaurant bench from fine dining to a food hall, lounges and clubs, pools, and the Shoppes retail row. The honest framing for a Miami visitor: this is a casino resort night, with the concert as its headline act. Arrive early, eat on property, play or wander after the show, and leave when the garages thin. It is also, notably, a place where the whole evening can happen indoors in air conditioning, which in a South Florida summer is a genuine feature. Families do attend matinee and early shows, but the property's center of gravity after dark is adult.

Where to stay

Two strategies work. Stay on property or nearby in Hollywood or Fort Lauderdale and make the show a five minute walk or drive; the beach towns also give you a different, quieter South Florida base for a few days. Or stay in Miami and treat the concert as a night expedition, with a designated driver or a rideshare budget. The first is easier; the second keeps your trip centered on Miami. Either way, compare current prices before you commit, because event nights move the Hollywood market as surely as they move Miami's.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hard Rock Live in Miami?
No. It is in Hollywood, Florida, in Broward County, about 20 miles north of downtown Miami. Allow 30 to 45 minutes by car, more on a sold out Friday night.

How many seats does it have?
7,000, arranged in a clamshell arc that keeps seats unusually close to the stage for a room of that size.

Can I get there without a car?
Practically, no, not for a late show. Drive, rideshare, or stay on property or nearby in Hollywood or Fort Lauderdale.
