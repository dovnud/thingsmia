Coconut Grove Arts Festival Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Coconut Grove Arts Festival returns for its 63rd edition over Presidents Day weekend, Saturday February 13 to Monday February 15, 2027. It takes over the Coconut Grove waterfront along McFarlane Road, South Bayshore Drive, and Pan American Drive, with the festival address listed at 2700 South Bayshore Drive. Hours are 10:00 am to 6:00 pm on Saturday and Sunday and 10:00 am to 5:00 pm on Monday. It is an outdoor, juried fine art show: up to 285 artists, selected through a blind jury process, selling work you can actually take home.

What is the Coconut Grove Arts Festival?

It is one of the anchor events of the Miami winter: a three day outdoor fine arts festival presented by the Coconut Grove Arts and Historical Association, a nonprofit that runs the festival with a professional staff and more than 600 volunteers, per the association. Artists apply through a juried process and must attend all three days in person, so the person selling the work is the person who made it, which changes the conversations you have at every booth. The setting does half the work: the booths line the streets of Coconut Grove, Miami's oldest village neighbourhood, along the Biscayne Bay waterfront by Dinner Key and Regatta Park.

When and where, exactly?

The 2027 festival runs Saturday February 13, Sunday February 14, and Monday February 15, 2027, which is Presidents Day weekend. The organizer's information page lists the festival address as 2700 South Bayshore Drive, Miami, FL 33133, just south of downtown, with the footprint running along McFarlane Road, South Bayshore Drive, and Pan American Drive. Hours are 10:00 am to 6:00 pm on Saturday and Sunday, closing an hour earlier at 5:00 pm on Monday. The same weekend brings the Miami International Boat Show to Miami Beach, February 10 to 14, so the whole city runs at festival capacity: book rooms and dinner reservations earlier than you think you need to.

Tickets, artists, and what to expect on the ground

The organizer's artist FAQ states that the festival selects up to 285 artists through a blind jurying process, and the Greater Miami visitors bureau describes more than 275 artists on display for 2027, so plan on roughly 285 booths of painting, sculpture, photography, jewellery, and mixed media along nearly a mile of waterfront streets. On tickets, the position at the time of checking, October 30, 2026, is that tickets will go on sale in advance and will also be sold at the festival; the organizer had not published 2027 admission prices on the pages checked, and this page prints no price rather than guess one. Go early in the day for the serious buying, because the crowds build by midday, and give yourself a full half day at minimum. Coconut Grove itself is the lunch plan: the village's cafes and waterfront restaurants sit inside and around the festival footprint, and our Coconut Grove visitor guide covers the neighbourhood beyond festival weekend.

Where do you stay, and how do you get there?

Stay in Coconut Grove or Coral Gables for the shortest trip to the gates, or downtown and Brickell for a wider hotel choice a short drive south. Our Miami hotels hub and the family hotels page are the starting points, and February is peak season, so the earlier the booking the better the choice. Driving in means festival weekend traffic on South Bayshore Drive and full garages; rideshare, the Metrorail to Coconut Grove station with a walk or shuttle at the end, or a hotel base you can walk from will all beat circling for parking. If you are combining the weekend, the Boat Show guide covers the other half of the city's double header.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Coconut Grove Arts Festival free?
It is a ticketed festival. For 2027, the visitors bureau states tickets will be sold in advance and at the festival; prices were not published on the pages checked on October 30, 2026, so check cgaf.com before you go.

Can I buy art and take it home the same day?
Yes. Artists sell from their booths and must attend all three days in person. Larger pieces can usually be arranged for shipping with the artist at the booth.

What else is on the same weekend?
The Miami International Boat Show runs February 10 to 14, 2027 on Miami Beach, and Monday February 15 is Presidents Day. Expect peak February hotel pricing across Miami.
