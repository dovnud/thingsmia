Miami City Ballet Visitor Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami City Ballet is one of America's major ballet companies and the resident dance company of the Adrienne Arsht Center, where it performs in the Ziff Ballet Opera House in downtown Miami; each season it also plays Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach. The 2026-27 season is the first under artistic director Gonzalo Garcia, and its shape is classic: the season opened with American Rhythms in October, George Balanchine's The Nutcracker runs December 11 to 27, 2026 as the city's defining holiday production, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland follows February 25 to March 7, 2027, and Swan Lake closes the main season April 29 to May 2, 2027. Tickets sell through the Arsht Center and the company's own site, miamicityballet.org.

The company

Miami City Ballet built its name on a combination few companies can claim: a serious Balanchine inheritance, the great choreographer's works are the spine of the repertory, danced by a company with a distinctly Miami energy, in a city whose light and Latin pulse get credited by critics for the troupe's attack and musicality. The company is based in Miami Beach, where its studios sit a short walk from Lincoln Road, and its school is one of the country's significant ballet academies. In Miami it performs at the Arsht Center's Ziff Ballet Opera House, a 2,400 seat house built with dance in mind; the same productions then travel to the Broward Center in Fort Lauderdale and the Kravis Center in West Palm Beach, which is worth knowing if your trip is based up the coast. The 2026-27 season opens a new chapter: Gonzalo Garcia, a former principal with New York City Ballet and San Francisco Ballet, begins as artistic director, and the Arsht bills the season as a blockbuster mix of classics and new work.

The 2026-27 season, date by date

American Rhythms opened the Miami run in October 2026 (October 16 to 25 across Miami and Fort Lauderdale), a mixed bill in the American idiom. George Balanchine's The Nutcracker is the season's popular peak: December 11 to 27, 2026 at the Arsht Center, with matinees and evening performances across the run, Tchaikovsky's score played live by the Miami City Ballet Orchestra, and the company's opulent production, which reimagines the classic through a Miami lens of color and light. It is the single best performing arts ticket in Miami at Christmas, and the centerpiece of our Christmas in Miami guide. Alice's Adventures in Wonderland runs February 25 to March 7, 2027, the big story ballet of the spring. Swan Lake closes the mainstage season April 29 to May 2, 2027. Dates above are the Arsht Center's published Miami dates; Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach dates bracket them, and the company's site lists the full calendar venue by venue.

Tickets and the shape of a ballet night

Subscriptions sell first and carry the discount; single tickets for The Nutcracker move early, especially the weekend matinees, which are where Miami families build the tradition. For a first ballet, choose a full length story ballet over a mixed bill: The Nutcracker in December, Alice in February and March, or Swan Lake in the spring are narratives you can follow without a program synopsis, and each fills the stage. Seating: dance reads best with some elevation, so the first tier of the Ziff is the connoisseur's value, close enough for faces, high enough for the patterns that are the point of Balanchine. Running times vary by program, typically around two hours with an intermission for the story ballets. Dress is Miami smart casual; The Nutcracker draws dressed up children and grandparents in equal number, which is part of its charm. Arrive for the pre show atmosphere in the Thomson Plaza and read the program notes; ballet rewards five minutes of preparation more than almost any art form.

Pairing ballet with the trip

The Arsht sits in downtown's Arts and Entertainment District, minutes from Brickell and the waterfront, and the Metromover station at the door makes a downtown or Brickell hotel the natural base; our Arsht Center guide covers the building, and the hotel guides cover the districts. December visitors should treat The Nutcracker and the rest of the holiday city as one itinerary: the ballet, the Christmas lights and events, and a beach day or two between them is Miami's best winter week.

Frequently asked questions

When is The Nutcracker in Miami in 2026?
Miami City Ballet performs George Balanchine's The Nutcracker at the Arsht Center from December 11 to 27, 2026, with matinees and evening performances and a live orchestra.

Where does Miami City Ballet perform?
At the Adrienne Arsht Center in downtown Miami, its home stage, and each season in Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach as well.

What should a first timer see?
A full length story ballet: The Nutcracker in December, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland in late February and March, or Swan Lake in late April and May 2027.
