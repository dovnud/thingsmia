Checked facts

Miami-Dade County's Parks department describes 17 miles of county beaches from Government Cut to the Broward County line, including Haulover Beach Park and Crandon Park Beach.

The same county page describes Haulover as a 1.4 mile beach park between Bal Harbour and Sunny Isles Beach.

The City of Miami Beach prohibits alcohol and smoking on City of Miami Beach beaches. That rule is stated here for the City of Miami Beach only.

Miami-Dade County says its beach cleanup crews work seven days a week and remove sargassum buildup at the water line.

Common questions

Can I drink alcohol on the beach in Miami Beach?

No. The City of Miami Beach prohibits alcohol and smoking on its beaches. Rules differ in other beach cities, so check the specific beach guide above for where you are going.

What should I do if I am caught in a rip current?

The National Weather Service guidance is to swim parallel to the shoreline to escape the current rather than swimming against it toward shore. The beach safety guide above covers flags and jellyfish as well.

What is sargassum and should it change my plans?

Sargassum is seaweed that washes onto Atlantic facing beaches in variable amounts. Miami-Dade County crews clean the water line daily, and conditions change beach by beach and day by day, so check the sargassum guide above close to your trip rather than months ahead.

Is Hollywood Beach part of Miami?

No. Hollywood Beach is in Broward County, north of Miami-Dade. It is included in this section as a nearby option and labeled that way.
