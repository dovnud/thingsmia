Sunny Isles Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Sunny Isles Beach is its own small city in northeast Miami-Dade County, about two and a half miles of Collins Avenue lined with high rise resorts and condominiums, sitting between Haulover Inlet to the south and Golden Beach to the north. The beach is public, and the city states it plainly: the public beach is open sunrise to sunset. The City of Sunny Isles Beach runs its own Ocean Rescue lifeguards, its own beach rules, and the Newport Fishing Pier at 16501 Collins Avenue, which is open to the public for walking and fishing. Come here for a resort beach day with a full service city behind it.

Where is Sunny Isles Beach?

Sunny Isles Beach occupies the northern end of the same barrier island chain as Miami Beach, but it is not part of the City of Miami Beach. It is a separate municipality with its own government center at 18070 Collins Avenue, its own police and Ocean Rescue, and its own beach ordinances. That distinction explains why the rules, the lifeguard hours, and the parking system all differ from the beaches a few miles south. If you are staying in a tower here, the beach is effectively your front yard. If you are visiting for the day, the pier area and the city parks are your practical bases.

The Newport Fishing Pier: is it open?

Yes. The Newport Fishing Pier, at 16501 Collins Avenue in Pier Park, is open as the city fishing pier and the landmark of this beach. The pier was first built in 1936 and the current structure reopened in 2013 after Hurricane Wilma destroyed the earlier pier. It runs about 776 feet into the Atlantic and is described by the city and county sources as the only public fishing pier in Miami-Dade County. Walking the pier and fishing from it are handled differently: fishing carries a fee, and pier hours and current fees change, so check the City of Sunny Isles Beach pier information for the amounts on the day you go rather than relying on a price printed here. Two city safety rules from the beach regulations matter at the pier: no swimming under the pier, and no jumping off the pier. Pier Park at its base gives you beach access, restrooms, and a main public entry point on this shoreline.

Beach rules and lifeguard hours

The City of Sunny Isles Beach publishes its beach rules, and they are specific. Items not allowed on the beach under city ordinance include glass containers, domestic animals except service animals, fishing from the beach itself, camping, cooking or open fires, fireworks, and smoking. Swimmers must stay inside the swim zone buoys, umbrellas and tents must not block the lifeguards view of the water, and directions from Ocean Rescue lifeguards must be followed at all times. The city Ocean Rescue division is based at 18070 Collins Avenue and posts a current beach conditions line at 305.792.1940. Lifeguard on duty hours listed by the city are 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. during Daylight Saving Time and 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. during Standard Time. The city also posts its daily warning flag: green for calm conditions, yellow for medium hazard, red for high hazard, double red for water closed, and purple for dangerous marine life. Swim near a lifeguard tower, inside the buoys, and within those hours.

Parking and planning your day

Day trip parking is the scarce resource here. The city runs metered municipal lots and garages, including parking near Pier Park, and rates and time limits are set by the city and change, so check the City of Sunny Isles Beach parking information for current amounts instead of budgeting from an old figure. The city also runs a free local shuttle that circulates inside Sunny Isles Beach, the practical way to reach the sand from a hotel or lot a few blocks inland. Shape the day simply: swim in the morning inside the guarded zone, walk the pier late in the day, and use the restaurants along Collins Avenue rather than cooking, since open fires and cooking are prohibited on the beach itself. Frequently asked questions

Is Sunny Isles Beach part of Miami Beach?
No. It is its own city in Miami-Dade County, north of Haulover Inlet, with its own beach rules and lifeguards. Miami Beach rules do not apply here; Sunny Isles Beach ordinances do.

Is the Newport Fishing Pier open?
Yes, the pier at 16501 Collins Avenue is open to the public as the city fishing pier. Fishing carries a fee and pier hours vary, so confirm the current fee and hours with the City of Sunny Isles Beach before you go.

Can I bring a dog onto Sunny Isles Beach?
The city ordinance list prohibits domestic animals on the beach, with an exception for service animals. Glass containers, camping, cooking, and smoking are also on the city prohibited list.

When are lifeguards on duty?
The city lists 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. during Daylight Saving Time and 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. during Standard Time. The beach itself is open sunrise to sunset, so early and late swims fall outside guarded hours.
