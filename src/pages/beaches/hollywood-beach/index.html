Hollywood Beach Guide (nearby, in Broward County)

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first, with the label up front. Hollywood Beach is not in Miami and not in Miami-Dade County. It is the beach of the City of Hollywood in Broward County, north of Miami, roughly halfway between Miami and Fort Lauderdale. ThingsMIA includes it as a nearby option, labeled as such, because Miami visitors day trip to it and because its Broadwalk, a brick paved promenade that the City of Hollywood says stretches nearly 2.5 miles along the Atlantic, offers something Miami Beach does not: a low rise, old Florida beach town strip where the cafes and small inns face the sand directly. Broward rules, Broward lifeguards, Broward parking. Plan it as a Broward day, not a Miami one.

First, the geography: this is Broward County, not Miami

It matters because everything else follows from it. Hollywood is a Broward County city. Its beach ordinances come from the City of Hollywood, its lifeguards are Hollywood Fire Rescue and Beach Safety, and its parking is run by the city, not by Miami Beach or Miami-Dade County. If you are based in Miami, reaching Hollywood Beach means a drive north into Broward, typically via I-95 or A1A. The payoff is a different product: where Miami Beach stacks towers and scene, Hollywood Beach keeps a lower, older profile along much of the Broadwalk, with small hotels and rental bikes moving at walking pace.

The Broadwalk is the main event

The City of Hollywood describes its Broadwalk, spelled with an a, as a one of a kind promenade stretching nearly 2.5 miles along the Atlantic Ocean, a brick paved thoroughfare used by pedestrians, joggers, bicyclists, and rollerbladers. The city facility listing for the beach and Broadwalk includes ADA accessibility, a bike trail, a parking garage, restaurants and cafes, restrooms, and a walking path, which sums the place up well: the Broadwalk is both the beach access and the town center. Along it you will find the Hollywood Beach Theatre for live music, public art, and Charnow Park at Garfield Street, a beachfront park with shaded seating, picnic pavilions, a playground, a splash fountain for kids, and paddleball courts. Accessible beach access points listed by the city include Carolina, Connecticut, Johnson, New York, Tyler, Harrison, and Oregon Streets. A simple, good day here is a slow Broadwalk loop with swim stops, rather than a single fixed spot on the sand.

Beach rules, lifeguards, and the dog beach

Hollywood runs a guarded beach: lifeguard towers and first aid stations line the sand, and the city directs anyone who needs help to the nearest first aid lifeguard tower during guarded hours. The City of Hollywood publishes current tower information and beach rules on its own site, so check there for the hours that apply to your visit rather than relying on a timetable printed here. City guidance for beach days is consistent on the prohibitions: alcohol, glass containers, smoking, and fires are prohibited on the sandy beach. Keep canopies within the city size limits and keep coolers small, as the city enforces both on busy days. Dogs are not permitted on the Broadwalk, but Hollywood has a designated Dog Beach between the Pershing and Custer Street lifeguard stands, listed by the city at 4999 N Surf Road, open from sunrise to sunset with no passes required, which is a genuinely useful option for travelers with a dog in Broward.

Parking and planning a nearby day trip

Because this is a nearby Broward trip, transport decides the day. The City of Hollywood lists public parking at beach garages and lots, including the Hollywood Beach Garage at A1A and Hollywood Boulevard and the Garfield Parking Garage, plus additional city lots behind the Broadwalk; rates are set by the city and change, so check the City of Hollywood parking information for current amounts. Once you are on the barrier island, the Hollywood Sun Shuttle, the city on demand service for the beach and downtown, removes most in town driving; the city facility page lists it at $1 per person per ride, checked October 30, 2026. If Hollywood wins the day, stay for the Broadwalk evening, when the theatre music and the cafe lights give this Broward strip its best hour. If you decide the drive is not worth it, the closest Miami-Dade equivalents are the Sunny Isles Beach guide to the south and the full beaches section, and the getting around Miami guide helps you weigh the trip honestly.

The facts that matter

Hollywood Beach is in the City of Hollywood, Broward County, Florida, not Miami and not Miami-Dade County. The City of Hollywood describes the Broadwalk as a brick paved promenade stretching nearly 2.5 miles along the Atlantic, used by pedestrians, joggers, bicyclists, and rollerbladers, with the Hollywood Beach Theatre and Charnow Park on it. City rules prohibit alcohol, glass containers, smoking, and fires on the sandy beach. The city Dog Beach sits between the Pershing and Custer Street lifeguard stands, open sunrise to sunset, no passes required. City garages include the Hollywood Beach Garage at A1A and Hollywood Boulevard.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hollywood Beach part of Miami?
No. Hollywood Beach is in Broward County, in the City of Hollywood, north of Miami-Dade County. This guide includes it as a nearby day trip option and labels it that way throughout.

What is the Broadwalk?
It is Hollywood Beach's oceanfront promenade, spelled Broadwalk: nearly 2.5 miles of brick paving along the Atlantic, per the City of Hollywood, shared by walkers, joggers, cyclists, and rollerbladers, with cafes, the beach theatre, and Charnow Park along it.

Can I drink alcohol on Hollywood Beach?
No. City of Hollywood guidance prohibits alcohol, glass containers, smoking, and fires on the sandy beach. Drinks belong at the Broadwalk restaurants and bars behind the sand.

Can I bring my dog?
Not on the Broadwalk, but yes at the city's Dog Beach between the Pershing and Custer Street lifeguard stands, which the city lists as open from sunrise to sunset with no passes required.
