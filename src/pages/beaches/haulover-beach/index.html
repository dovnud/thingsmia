Haulover Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Haulover Beach Park is a Miami-Dade County park at 10800 Collins Avenue, between Bal Harbour and Sunny Isles Beach, a few miles north of Miami Beach. The county calls it the largest remaining stretch of undeveloped beachfront in Miami-Dade County, at 1.4 miles, known for white sand, open ocean surf, and coastal vegetation. County signage at the beach marks a clothing optional section in the northern part of the beach. This guide covers the beach areas, parking, and facilities, so you can choose your stretch before you arrive.

Where is Haulover, and what kind of beach is it?

Haulover sits on the barrier island strip north of Miami Beach, with the Atlantic on one side and Biscayne Bay, the Intracoastal Waterway, and Baker's Haulover Cut on the other, per Miami-Dade County Parks. The ocean side is a wide, open beach park rather than a hotel strip. The bay and cut side holds the marina and fishing access. The county lists park hours as sunrise to sunset, with park office hours seven days a week from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Because this is a county park, not a City of Miami Beach beach, read the county's posted signs at the entrance for the stretch you use.

The beach areas, including the clothing optional section

Most visitors use the main ocean beach for a standard beach day in swimwear, and that is the larger part of the shoreline. County signage marks a clothing optional section in the northern part of the beach. Stated in the county's terms: within the signed section, clothing is optional, and outside the signed section it is not. The signs are the authority on the day, because they mark the boundary in place. If you want that section, follow the signs to the northern part. If you do not, stay in the main beach areas to the south of it, near the concessions, rentals, and picnic areas. Choose your area deliberately when you arrive, rather than drifting past a change in signage.

Facilities, concessions, and the marina side

The county's beaches page lists the offer: food concessions, a kite store, beach chair and umbrella rentals, and picnic areas with tables and grills, plus a full service marina, a bark park, and a beach area for dogs. With access to the Atlantic, the Intracoastal Waterway, and Baker's Haulover Cut, the county also points to onshore and offshore fishing. That mix makes Haulover a full day park rather than a quick swim stop: rent shade, eat from the concessions or grill at a table, and end the afternoon watching boats use the cut. The Bill Bird Marina office is open seven days a week from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., at 305-947-3525, per the county park page.

Parking and getting in

Parking is paid, and the county park page posts the schedule. For cars, Monday through Thursday is $7 in all lots, with a $1 additional surcharge for the North Lot and Lot #1. On weekends and holidays, cars pay $10. Buses and RVs pay $15. Lots are open from sunrise until one hour before sunset. Your lot choice sets your walk to the part of the beach you intend to use, so arrive earlier on busy weekends, and confirm the posted rate at the entrance, since the county can change its schedule.

How to plan a Haulover day

Start with the water. The county describes open ocean surf here, a different swim from the calm, sandbar protected water at Crandon Park. Confident swimmers will read that as a plus; families with small children will usually prefer our family beaches guide. Whatever your group, read our beach safety guide, check the flags at the beach, and follow lifeguard directions where a stand is staffed. Then plan around the park, not just the sand: a picnic lunch, a kite from the kite store, and time on the marina side. Set your return around sunset, when the park day ends.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the clothing optional section at Haulover?
County signage marks a clothing optional section in the northern part of the beach. Follow the signs at the beach itself for the boundary, and stay in the signed section if that is the area you are using.

How much is parking at Haulover Beach Park?
The county park page lists $7 for cars Monday through Thursday, with a $1 additional surcharge for the North Lot and Lot #1, $10 for cars on weekends and holidays, and $15 for buses and RVs. Lots are open from sunrise until one hour before sunset.

Is Haulover good for young children?
It depends on the swim you want. The county describes open ocean surf here, not calm water. For small children, the calmer picks in our family guide, led by Crandon Park, are usually the better first choice.

What else is in the park besides the beach?
Per the county, a marina, a bark park and dog beach area, picnic areas, food concessions, a kite store, and fishing access to the Atlantic, the Intracoastal, and Baker's Haulover Cut.
