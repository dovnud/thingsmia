The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 29, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach is the formal, oceanfront classic at 1 Lincoln Road, where Lincoln Road meets the beach: more than 370 rooms and suites inside a landmark 1953 building, an elevated pool over the Atlantic, dining by the Jose Andres Group, a full service spa, and an optional Club Lounge. Book it for polished service at the center of South Beach. Skip it if you want a boutique, a party scene, or a low all in total.

What is The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach, and where exactly is it?

The address does a lot of the work: 1 Lincoln Road, at the eastern end of Lincoln Road, directly on the sand at Collins Avenue. The hotel's own site traces the building to architect Morris Lapidus, the designer behind much of classic Miami Beach, and says it opened as The DiLido Hotel in 1953. That history is not decoration here; the restored building, the lobby bar named for Lapidus himself, and the beachfront setting are the product, updated with current Ritz-Carlton service and interiors. From the front door, Lincoln Road runs west, Ocean Drive is a walk to the south, and the Miami Beach Convention Center is a walk to the north west, which makes this a natural base for Art Basel week and for a first, central South Beach stay.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel describes its rooms and suites in a palette of ocean blue, warm gold, and rich coffee, with plush bedding in high thread count linens and marble bathrooms with Diptyque bath amenities, and it notes that select rooms and suites add private balconies or poolside patios. With more than 370 rooms and suites, this is a large hotel, and the honest booking question is outlook: oceanfront categories face the water over the pool deck, while city and partial view rooms face the South Beach rooftops. That view gap, more than square footage, is what the rate steps buy here. Club level rooms add access to The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge, with dedicated concierge service and daily culinary presentations; the hotel is clear that Club access comes with a Club booking, and that Marriott Bonvoy elite status alone does not include it.

What is there to do without leaving?

The elevated outdoor pool is the resort's center, set above the beach with views over the Atlantic, personalized pool and beach service, and cabanas and daybeds, with a whirlpool and a 24 hour fitness center in the same wellness picture. The Ritz-Carlton Spa, South Beach is the quiet half of the offer, described by the hotel as a retreat with tranquil treatment rooms, steam and sauna areas, and relaxation lounges. Dining is by the Jose Andres Group, per the hotel's own site: Zaytinya, a mezze style restaurant drawing on Turkish, Greek, and Lebanese cuisines; DiLido Beach Bar for ocean to table food and cocktails with direct beach access; and Lapidus Bar, named for the building's architect, with craft cocktails and live music. The hotel also lists Ritz Kids programming for children ages 5 to 11. Fees apply on top of the rate, including for valet parking and cabanas, and this page prints no fee amounts because they change; check the all in total before you pay.

What surrounds The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach?

Few Miami Beach hotels sit at a more useful crossing. Step west and Lincoln Road gives you an evening of shops and restaurants without a car. Step south and Collins Avenue and then Ocean Drive take you through the Art Deco Historic District. Step onto the sand and the beach and the Beachwalk run in both directions, north toward the convention center area and south toward South Pointe. The hotel's own experiences list leans on the same geography, naming The Wolfsonian, The Bass Museum, and Miami City Ballet among the cultural stops near the property, with Wynwood, the Design District, and Little Havana as short drives across the causeway. In practice, a stay here needs very little transport planning: beach by day, Lincoln Road by night, and one cultural outing a day on foot or by a short ride.

Who does The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach suit, and who should skip it?

It suits travelers who want classic, staffed, formal luxury at the center of the map: couples marking an occasion, families who will use the pool, beach service, and kids programming, and Art Basel and convention visitors who want to walk to the fair and come back to a full service resort. Skip it if your taste runs to design led informality or a see and be seen pool scene; the W, a few blocks north, does that job on purpose. Skip it, too, if the budget is tight: between the room rate, Club upgrades, valet parking, and resort charges, the all in total sits in the top tier of South Beach, so compare totals, not headline rates. Check in is at 4:00 pm and check out is at 11:00 am, per the hotel's own site.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach really on the beach?
Yes. The hotel is oceanfront at 1 Lincoln Road, with an elevated pool overlooking the Atlantic and personalized beach service on the sand in front of the property, per its own site.

Does Marriott Bonvoy status get me into the Club Lounge?
No, not on its own. The hotel states that Club access requires an upgraded Club booking, and that Marriott Bonvoy elite status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.

Is The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach good for Art Basel?
Yes. The convention center is a walk away and Lincoln Road is at the door. Our Art Basel visitor guide covers the fair itself. Expect fair week pricing.
