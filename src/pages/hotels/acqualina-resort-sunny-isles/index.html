Acqualina Resort and Residences Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Acqualina is the quiet, formal luxury resort of Sunny Isles Beach: 98 hotel rooms and suites at 17875 Collins Avenue, on 4.5 beachfront acres with about 400 feet of Atlantic shoreline, three oceanfront pools, a 20,000 square foot spa, and four restaurants. Book it for service, space, and calm north of Miami Beach. Skip it if you want nightlife at the door, a scene by the pool, or a rate that leaves room in the budget for much else.

What is Acqualina, and where exactly is it?

The resort stands at 17875 Collins Avenue in Sunny Isles Beach, the separate beach city north of Miami Beach and south of Fort Lauderdale, in a tower the hotel describes in Mediterranean villa terms, with lawns, red umbrellas, and red couches set above the sand. The hotel part is deliberately small. Alongside the 98 rooms and suites sit 188 residences, so the building mixes guests with owners, and the grounds feel more like a private estate than a resort complex. Aventura Mall is a short drive west, Bal Harbour Shops is a short drive south, and Oleta River State Park is close by for kayaking and trails. What you do not get is a walkable nightlife strip: Collins Avenue here is a resort corridor, and evenings mostly happen inside the resort or by car.

What are the rooms like?

Rooms and suites are large by Miami standards, with private terraces facing either the Atlantic or the Intracoastal Waterway, marble bathrooms, and, in the suites, separate living areas and full kitchens with dining space, which is why longer family stays work well here. Listings split the count as 54 guest rooms and 44 suites, and the honest booking choice is outlook and floor: oceanfront categories put the water directly in front of you, while Intracoastal rooms face the sunset and the city lights for less money. Service is the resort's calling card, with a staffed, attentive style that repeat guests cite more than any single design feature. If a standard hotel room elsewhere feels tight for a week, an Acqualina suite is the counterpoint, priced accordingly.

What is there to eat and do without leaving?

Dining runs to four oceanfront restaurants. Il Mulino New York is the Italian anchor, a formal dinner room with a terrace over the ocean. Avra Miami serves Greek and Mediterranean cooking. Ke-uH serves Japanese fusion at dinner. Costa Grill is the casual, guests and residents only beachside spot for lunch by the pools. The Acqualina Spa is the second anchor: 20,000 square feet over two floors, built around ESPA treatments, with treatment rooms, relaxation lounges with Himalayan salt walls, crystal steam rooms, a Finnish sauna, and an outdoor spa pool terrace with a heated jet pool. Outside, three oceanfront pools split the crowd, including an adults only pool and family pools, with cabanas and serviced beach loungers on the sand. Children get the AcquaMarine program, a marine biology based kids program the resort runs for younger guests, and there is an oceanfront fitness center for the routine you meant to keep.

Who does Acqualina suit, and who should skip it?

It suits couples who want a resort that runs quietly and precisely, families who will actually use the suites, the kids program, and the three pools, and spa travelers who treat the spa as a destination rather than an add on. It also suits anyone who has done South Beach and now wants the beach without the noise. Skip it if your trip is built around restaurants and bars you walk to, because Sunny Isles is a drive to almost everything off property. Skip it, too, if the budget matters: rates sit at the top of the Miami area range, and dining, cabanas, spa treatments, parking, and resort charges all add to the total. Check the all in price, not the headline rate, before you decide it is out of reach or within it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Acqualina in Miami Beach?
No. It is in Sunny Isles Beach, a separate city north of Miami Beach on the same barrier island strip. South Beach is a drive south, roughly half an hour depending on traffic.

Is Acqualina good for families?
Yes, by design. Suites have kitchens and living space, there are family pools alongside an adults only pool, and the AcquaMarine program gives children structured beach and ocean activities.

Do you need to leave the resort to eat well?
No. Four restaurants cover Italian, Greek, Japanese, and casual beachside dining on site, which matters in Sunny Isles, where off property dining usually means a drive.
