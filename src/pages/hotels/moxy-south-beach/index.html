Moxy South Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Moxy Miami South Beach is the social, design led Marriott at 915 Washington Avenue in the Art Deco District, two blocks from the beach. Its 202 rooms include quad bunk rooms built for groups, and the stay revolves around two pools: a 72 foot pool on the second floor terrace and a shallow rooftop pool at The Upside bar on the eighth floor. Book it for location, rooftops, and a group friendly room mix at a price below the big Collins Avenue resorts. Skip it if you want quiet, formal, or directly on the sand.

What is Moxy South Beach, and where exactly is it?

The hotel stands on Washington Avenue at 9th Street, inside the Art Deco District and two blocks west of the beach. Marriott places it steps from Ocean Drive and Espanola Way, with Lincoln Road a walk north, which puts the whole South Beach circuit of beach, dining, and nightlife within walking distance and makes a car more liability than asset here. It is a Moxy, Marriott's playful select service brand, run as a resort style property: the public spaces, rooftops, and pool decks carry the experience, and the rooms are compact and clever rather than large.

What are the rooms like?

The house has 202 guestrooms. The mix runs from king and two queen rooms, some with ocean views, up to the format groups come for: quad bunk rooms sleeping four in twin bunks, including a deluxe version with its own sitting room. That bunk format is rare in South Beach and it changes the maths for bachelorette weekends, festival crews, and friend groups who would otherwise book two or three separate rooms. Trade offs are the Moxy standard: streamlined storage, a social lobby instead of a private living room feel, and a house style that puts energy ahead of hush. Check the exact room name and outlook before you pay, because view and size vary widely across the 202.

What is there to do without leaving?

Two pools anchor the stay. Down on the second floor terrace, a 72 foot pool runs through a planted deck with lounge seating and cabanas. Up on the eighth floor, The Upside rooftop bar pairs a shallow lounging pool with daybeds, in water loungers, panoramic views over South Beach and the downtown skyline, and space used for screenings and events. Serena, the open air rooftop restaurant and bar, channels Oaxaca and Mexico City patio dining and is the sunset reservation of the house. At lobby level, Tiendita is the cafe and bodega for Cuban coffee and pastries, and Bar Moxy doubles as the social hub and check in point. The hotel also lists private event lounges, an indoor outdoor gym, and free fitness classes, plus a Beach Hut for guests heading to the sand; the hotel description notes two beach lounge chairs are included. The dining and bar lineup at this hotel changes more often than most, so confirm the current venue list on Marriott's site when you book.

Who does Moxy South Beach suit, and who should skip it?

It suits groups first: the bunk rooms, two rooftops, and a lobby built for meeting people make it one of the easiest South Beach bases for a crew travelling together. It suits younger travellers and design fans who will spend their days at the pool decks and their nights within walking distance, and anyone who wants a Marriott points stay in the middle of South Beach without Collins Avenue resort pricing. Skip it if you need direct beach frontage, a spa, or a quiet early night; the rooftops run late by design, and light sleepers should ask for a higher floor away from the venues. A resort style fee structure and paid parking are part of the South Beach pattern here too, so compare the all in total, not the headline rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is Moxy South Beach on the beach?
No. It is on Washington Avenue, two blocks from the beach, and runs a Beach Hut for guests at the sand. The pools and rooftops are the on site water.

Can four friends share one room at Moxy South Beach?
Yes. Quad bunk rooms sleep four in twin bunks, and a deluxe version adds a sitting room. Confirm the exact layout on the room you book.

Is Moxy South Beach good for Art Basel and convention weeks?
Yes. It is a walk from the Miami Beach Convention Center end of South Beach, and the group rooms help when fair week rates climb. Book early for December.
