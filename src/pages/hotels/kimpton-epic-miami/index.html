Kimpton EPIC Hotel Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Kimpton EPIC is downtown Miami's waterfront boutique leaning tower: 411 rooms and suites at 270 Biscayne Blvd Way, where the Miami River meets Biscayne Bay, with a private balcony in every room, two pools and a seafood restaurant up on the 16th floor, and Zuma at ground level. Book it for balconies, bay views and a lively pool deck between downtown and Brickell. Skip it if you want sand at the door, because EPIC is river and bayfront, not beachfront.

What is Kimpton EPIC, and where exactly is it?

EPIC stands at 270 Biscayne Blvd Way, Miami, FL 33131, on the south bank of the Miami River at the edge of downtown, with Brickell starting just across the bridge. It is a useful in between address: Bayfront Park and Bayside Marketplace are a short walk north, the Kaseya Center is close by, Brickell's offices and restaurants are minutes south, and PortMiami is a short drive across the causeway, which makes EPIC a regular pick for cruise nights with more personality than the big convention hotels. The building mixes hotel rooms with residences, but the hotel keeps its own entrance and lobby, so guests are not wandering through someone else's apartment building. The position on the river adds something the pure bayfront hotels do not have: boat traffic, drawbridges and a marina feel at the base of the tower, with the bay opening out beyond. South Beach, to be clear, is a drive away. Stay here for the city, the water views and the food, and treat the beach as an excursion.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel lists 411 rooms and suites. The feature that defines them is simple and rare downtown: every room has a private balcony, behind floor to ceiling windows, with views either over the city or over the water toward the bay and the port. Rooms sit high in the tower, so even city facing categories look out over streets rather than into a wall. Inside, the style is calm and contemporary, with warm natural materials, spa style bathrooms and work friendly tables, and suites add separate living and dining space, with some categories carrying a second balcony. Water view rooms are the upgrade most guests rate, especially on the bay side at sunrise. If you are sensitive to noise, ask about floor and side at booking: the pool deck scene on the 16th floor and the river bridges are part of the location, and higher floors sit further from both.

What is there to eat, drink and do on site?

The 16th floor is the social centre of the hotel. Area 31, the signature restaurant up there, serves a chef driven, seafood led menu with an open air terrace facing the bay, and it handles everything from breakfast to sunset drinks. Beside it, the 16th floor pool deck holds two rooftop pools with private cabanas, a pool bar and wide views over the river, the bay and the downtown skyline. Down at lobby level, Zuma serves modern Japanese izakaya dining, including its robata grill, and draws a strong local crowd as well as guests, so dinner reservations matter. Wellness is handled by Privai Spa and Fitness, the current spa operator listed for the hotel, offering treatments alongside a fitness centre with classes. Kimpton touches run through the stay, including the brand's pet friendly stance and hosted evening social hour traditions, though the exact daily programme is worth confirming at check in. There is also a marina at the property, which suits travellers arriving or chartering by boat and adds to the waterfront feel even if you never step on a deck.

Who does Kimpton EPIC suit, and who should skip it?

It suits couples who want a balcony and a view without a mega resort, food led travellers who will actually book Zuma and Area 31, cruise guests who want one stylish night near the port, and business travellers who prefer Brickell access with a less corporate mood. The pet friendly policy and the social pool deck give it a relaxed streak that the more formal towers nearby do not chase. It does not suit travellers who want a quiet, retreat style pool: the 16th floor deck is a scene on weekends, with music and a crowd. It does not suit beach first trips either, and it is not a budget pick, since dining, cabanas and valet parking all carry downtown luxury pricing. Families can do well in the suites, but the overall energy leans adult, especially around the restaurants and pool at night. Choose EPIC when the city is the trip. Choose the beach when the beach is the trip.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kimpton EPIC on the beach?
No. EPIC sits on the Miami River where it meets Biscayne Bay in downtown Miami. You get pools, a marina and bay views, but the nearest ocean beach is across the causeway in Miami Beach.

Which floor are the pools on?
The 16th floor. The deck holds two pools, cabanas and a pool bar, and Area 31 restaurant shares the floor, so you can move from a swim to dinner without changing buildings.

Does every room have a balcony?
Yes, according to the hotel's own room descriptions. Every room and suite carries a private balcony, with either city or water views. If the view matters, book the water view category by name rather than hoping for an upgrade.
