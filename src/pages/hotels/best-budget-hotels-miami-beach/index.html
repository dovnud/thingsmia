Best Budget Hotels in Miami Beach

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Budget in Miami Beach means trade offs: smaller rooms, shared bathrooms in hostel picks, a walk instead of a beachfront door, and resort fees that can still apply. Every stay below was verified as operating on its own official site in October 2026. Generator Miami rebranded as Hotel Rendale in March 2026, and Bikini Hostel is listed as permanently closed, so neither appears under its old name.

Which Miami Beach budget stays are actually worth booking?

Freehand Miami

The design led hostel hotel at 2727 Indian Creek Drive, in the historic Indian Creek Hotel building one block from the beach in Mid Beach, home to the Broken Shaker cocktail bar and 27 Restaurant around a tropical pool courtyard. The important current fact is in its own FAQ: Freehand has shifted away from shared rooms and by the bed bookings, and rooms are now private, booked for your own group. That suits friends traveling together who want hostel social energy without a dorm, and solo travelers who value the bar and pool scene. Rooms are compact and the courtyard gets lively, so light sleepers should ask for a room away from the pool. See our Freehand guide.

Hotel Rendale Miami Beach (formerly Generator Miami)

The Mid Beach social hotel at 3120 Collins Avenue, which traded as Generator Miami until March 2026 and now operates under its own name, Hotel Rendale, verified on its official site. Its FAQ lists king rooms, junior and superior suites and Squad Rooms in a bunk bed style for families or groups, which keeps a hostel idea alive inside a hotel frame: groups can still sleep together cheaply, while couples get a normal private room. There is a pool, a restaurant and bar program and the beach a short walk east. It suits groups and younger travelers basing in Mid Beach to save versus South Beach resort pricing. Expect a social lobby rather than quiet, and confirm your room type: the bunk rooms are the budget end of the building.

Viajero Miami

A boutique hostel at 1120 Collins Avenue in the South Beach Art Deco district, a short walk from Espanola Way and Ocean Drive, run by the Latin American Viajero hostel group. The model is mixed: shared dorms for the lowest bed price in central South Beach and private rooms for travelers who want the social base without sharing a room, with a bar, a restaurant listed as Delirio and courtyard pool space behind the Collins Avenue frontage. Listings describe it as adults only, and the atmosphere matches that: social, programmed and nightlife friendly. It suits solo travelers and backpackers who want to meet people in the middle of South Beach. Trade offs are the usual hostel ones: shared dorm bathrooms, limited storage, and quiet is not the product. Book a private room if you like the location but not the dorm.

Hotel Gaythering

A small adults oriented boutique hotel at 1409 Lincoln Road, at the west end of the Lincoln Road shopping district, describing itself as a gay hotel where all adults are welcome, with a lively bar that runs nightly events such as karaoke and trivia. State it plainly before booking: this is an adults only stay, its self service spa with steam room, dry sauna and hot tub is men only and clothing optional, and some of its lowest priced crate style rooms share bathrooms, so read the room description carefully. In return you get a famously friendly small hotel bar, a walkable position for Lincoln Road and the convention center, and rates below the Collins Avenue beachfront. There is no pool and the beach is a walk east: it suits social travelers, not resort seekers.

Circa 39 Hotel

An eclectic boutique hotel at 3900 Collins Avenue in Mid Beach, across the street from the sand, with a pool, the leafy WunderGarden courtyard and a rum bar, priced well below its Mid Beach resort neighbors. Rooms are cheerful rather than luxurious, and Mid Beach itself is the value play: quieter than South Beach, on the boardwalk, with the beach as the main event and restaurants a trolley ride away. Two honest flags. A resort fee still applies, so compare the all in total. And the hotel listing notes renovation work from August 1 to November 30, 2026, so ask what is affected on your dates before paying. It suits couples and beach first travelers who want Collins Avenue without Fontainebleau pricing.

The Tony Hotel South Beach

A historic Art Deco boutique at 801 Collins Avenue, with a second building at 800 Ocean Drive, built in 1939 and restored under developer Tony Goldman, whose name the hotel now carries. It is not hostel cheap, and this page will not pretend otherwise: it is the budget pick for travelers who want a real hotel with a rooftop pool, the News Cafe corner and Wish Cafe, in the middle of the 8th Street action, for notably less than the big luxury names nearby. Confirm which building your category sits in. The trade off is South Beach itself: 8th Street is loud and rooms in historic buildings run small. It suits couples and groups who came for the neighborhood and will use the rooftop between beach and nightlife.

Tropics Hotel

A simple hotel and hostel at 1550 Collins Avenue, a block west of Lincoln Road, with a pool, a sun deck and a patio, listed with about 70 rooms. This is the no frills end of the list: expect basic rooms, a social hostel streak in the common areas and a location strategy, paying for Collins Avenue at 16th Street rather than for design or service. For travelers spending days on the beach two blocks east and evenings on Lincoln Road, that trade can be right. Check recent reviews for your room type, because quality varies room by room at this price, and confirm whether your rate is private or shared. It suits backpackers and short stays where the room is a base, not the trip.

How do you keep a Miami Beach stay genuinely cheap?

Compare the all in total: a low rate plus a daily resort fee, parking and taxes can approach a mid range hotel that includes more. Dorm beds are the cheapest sleep in town, but a private room in the same building often costs little more per person for two travelers. Mid Beach and west South Beach price lower than the beachfront, and spring break and event weekends lift even hostel prices sharply.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to sleep in Miami Beach?
A dorm bed at Viajero, or a bunk style Squad Room at Hotel Rendale for a group. Also price a private room at Freehand, which no longer sells by the bed: the gap is sometimes small.

Do budget Miami Beach hotels still charge resort fees?
Some do, including Circa 39, and hostels may add smaller facility or deposit charges. Always compare the all in total at checkout, not the first rate you see.

Which of these are adults only?
Hotel Gaythering is adults only and gay focused, welcoming all adults, with a men only clothing optional spa. Viajero is also listed as adults only. Neither suits a family trip.
