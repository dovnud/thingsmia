Best Hotels for Cruise Passengers in Miami

Last reviewed: October 25, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Stay downtown or in Brickell the night before your sailing. The one hotel on this list with a shuttle we could verify on the hotel's own site is the Comfort Inn and Suites Downtown Brickell, Port of Miami, which also includes a hot breakfast. The Hampton Inn Brickell includes breakfast but runs no port shuttle, a first hand review confirms, so plan a rideshare. Every other hotel below earns its place on location and breakfast, with shuttle arrangements to confirm when you book, because offerings change.

Which hotels work best before or after a cruise?

Comfort Inn and Suites Downtown Brickell, Port of Miami

The practical cruiser pick. Choice Hotels' own page for this property, checked October 25, 2026, lists a free hot breakfast and a one way shuttle to the Port of Miami for a fee of 12 dollars per person, with registration at check in. It also states there is no airport shuttle, so plan that leg separately. The hotel is on the Miami River in Brickell, with restaurants a short walk away.

Hampton Inn and Suites Miami, Brickell Downtown

Hilton's property description includes its hot breakfast in the rate pitch, which is exactly what embarkation morning needs. Be clear on the transfer: a detailed first hand review from January 2026 states this hotel does not offer a shuttle to the cruise port or the airport, and measured the drive at just under three miles by road. Book it for breakfast and Brickell convenience, and order a rideshare to your terminal.

InterContinental Miami

The bayfront landmark in downtown, which its own site places near the Port of Miami, with bay view rooms that watch the ships come and go. Breakfast is served at the hotel. This is the upgrade pick: a resort scale downtown hotel the night before, a short drive across the causeway on sailing morning, and Bayside Marketplace next door for the evening before.

Kimpton EPIC Hotel

A riverfront luxury hotel where downtown meets Brickell, with two rooftop pools and breakfast served on site. Choose it if the pre cruise night is part of the holiday rather than a stopover, and arrange the port transfer at booking, since this page could not verify a hotel shuttle on the property's own channels.

Hyatt Regency Miami

A dependable downtown full service hotel on the Miami River, walkable to Bayside Marketplace and the downtown restaurant scene. Cruise guides consistently use it as a default pre cruise base for its location. Confirm breakfast arrangements and the port transfer when you book, as both sit in the rate details rather than the hotel's headline offers.

JW Marriott Marquis Miami

A large luxury hotel on Biscayne Boulevard Way in downtown, with bay views and big hotel facilities including an indoor sports and entertainment level. Suits cruisers traveling as a group or celebrating the night before, who want a full service base a short drive from the terminals.

citizenM Miami Brickell

The smart budget leaning option. Marriott's listing describes compact, design led rooms and a canteen that serves coffee and breakfast around the clock, which helps early port mornings. Rooms are small by design, so it suits a one night stay with cruise luggage kept simple, or couples traveling light.

Four Seasons Hotel Miami

The Brickell luxury option, a calm, high service hotel on Brickell Avenue. Booking sources note breakfast is available at a surcharge here rather than included. Choose it to start the trip in comfort, and have the concierge arrange the port car, which is the normal pattern at this level.

The facts that matter

Only one shuttle claim on this page is stated as fact, because only one could be verified on a hotel's own site in October 2026: the Comfort Inn and Suites Downtown Brickell runs a one way shuttle to PortMiami for 12 dollars per person, booked at check in, and its hot breakfast is included. Fees and shuttle schedules change, so recheck on the hotel page when you book. Luggage hold before check in or after check out is common at full service hotels, but it is a policy detail, not a promise: ask when you reserve, especially if your flight home is late on disembarkation day. No hotel on the downtown or Brickell mainland is a comfortable walk to a cruise terminal with luggage; plan a car for every option here.

Frequently asked questions

Should I stay near the port or in Miami Beach before a cruise?
Near the port, meaning downtown or Brickell, for a one night pre cruise stay. You trade beach time for a short, predictable embarkation morning. Stay in Miami Beach if you have two or more nights and the beach is part of the trip.

Do Miami hotels offer free shuttles to PortMiami?
Some hotels offer port shuttles and many charge per person; one verified example is on this page. Treat any free shuttle claim you see on a third party list with suspicion and confirm it on the hotel's own site or by phone before you rely on it.

What time should I leave the hotel on embarkation day?
Work back from your cruise line's assigned arrival window, and add buffer for causeway traffic, which stacks up when several ships are boarding. Staying downtown keeps the drive short, but it does not make it instant.
