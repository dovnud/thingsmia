Mondrian South Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Mondrian South Beach is the bay side exception: a Marcel Wanders designed condo hotel at 1100 West Avenue on Biscayne Bay, not on the ocean, with sunset views over the downtown skyline, a bayside pool deck, and kitchen equipped suites. It is the South Beach pick for longer stays and sunset watchers. It is not the pick if your first question is "how far to the sand," because the answer is about a mile.

What is Mondrian South Beach, and where exactly is it?

The Mondrian opened in 2008 as a condo hotel on the western edge of South Beach, fronting Biscayne Bay at 1100 West Avenue, and it was recently renovated across the property. The design, by Marcel Wanders, is the hotel's identity: a "Sleeping Beauty's Castle" theme in white and gold, anchored by a floating staircase in the lobby that is the most photographed object in the building. The position is the trade off. Lincoln Road, the convention center, and the beach are all roughly a mile away, which is a short drive or a long walk; what you get in return is bay water, downtown skyline views, and quieter nights than the ocean side addresses.

What are the rooms like?

Rooms are studios and one and two bedroom suites, many with kitchenettes or full kitchens and bay view balconies. Because this is a condo hotel, individual units are privately owned, so interiors vary by owner even within the same category: ask for a recent photo or a renovated unit if the listing does not confirm it. The suite tier is the real product here, and it is what separates Mondrian from the pure room hotels: a kitchen, a balcony, and a separate sleeping space make a five night stay affordable in a way a standard room never does.

What is there to do without leaving?

The bayside pool deck faces west for sunsets over the bay and the skyline, and Baia Beach Club serves the pool and bay side hours with Mediterranean cooking. A small spa handles treatments, and the fitness center covers the basics. The pool deck's social energy peaks on weekends, which suits the party adjacent crowd and not the early to bed set. There is no ocean beach here, and the hotel does not pretend otherwise: guests reach the sand by car, rideshare, or the free trolley loop, and the hotel's own beach shuttle arrangements vary by season, so confirm at booking if that matters to you.

What is the West Avenue side of South Beach like?

Different from the postcard side, and that is the appeal. West Avenue is a largely residential corridor of towers and marinas along the bay, with a local rhythm of dog walkers, paddleboarders, and neighborhood cafes rather than tour groups. Mornings here are quiet, the bay is flat and bright, and the day's event is the sunset, which the whole neighborhood seems to schedule around. Lincoln Road's west end and the convention center sit about a mile northeast, Ocean Drive a mile east across the island, and the free Miami Beach trolley stitches the bay side into the rest of South Beach for anyone who does not want to drive it. Travelers who have done the Ocean Drive trip and want the locals' version of the island tend to end up on this side.

Who does Mondrian suit, and who should skip it?

It suits longer stays that need a kitchen, sunset lovers, groups splitting a suite, and travelers who like South Beach access without Ocean Drive's noise at night. The design also earns it a loyal creative crowd. Skip it if the beach is the reason you came, if unit to unit variation will bother you, or if you hate walking a mile to Lincoln Road and the convention center. A daily resort fee applies on top of the rate, as at most Miami Beach hotels, so compare the all in total.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mondrian South Beach on the beach?
No, and this matters: it fronts Biscayne Bay on the west side of the island, about a mile from the Atlantic sand. If you want to walk to the beach, look at our Loews or 1 Hotel guides.

Is Mondrian good for longer stays?
Yes, that is its strength: kitchen equipped suites, balconies, and a residential feel make it one of the more livable South Beach stays for five nights or more.

What is the Mondrian known for?
Marcel Wanders' castle themed design, the floating staircase in the lobby, and sunsets over Biscayne Bay from the pool deck.
