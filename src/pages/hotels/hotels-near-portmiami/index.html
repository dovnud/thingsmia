Hotels Near PortMiami

Last reviewed: October 25, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. PortMiami sits on Dodge Island, and no hotel stands at the terminals themselves. The closest places to stay are across the causeway in downtown Miami and Brickell. Hotels.com listed citizenM Miami Brickell at 1.3 miles from the port in October 2026, the closest listed distance on this page, with the Comfort Inn Brickell, Kimpton EPIC, and Hampton Inn Brickell all listed around 1.8 to 1.9 miles. Treat every figure as a listed distance to the port area: the road route to your specific terminal runs longer.

Which hotels are closest to the cruise terminals?

citizenM Miami Brickell

Listed by Hotels.com at 1.3 miles from PortMiami, the shortest listed distance here. A compact, tech led Brickell hotel with a canteen serving coffee and breakfast around the clock. Best for a short pre cruise night when you want to be close, fed early, and gone.

Comfort Inn and Suites Downtown Brickell, Port of Miami

Listed at 1.8 miles from the port. It earns its full name honestly: Choice Hotels' own page lists an included hot breakfast and a one way shuttle to PortMiami for 12 dollars per person, registered at check in (checked October 25, 2026). Of the hotels on this page, it is the one built most directly around cruise passengers.

Kimpton EPIC Hotel

Listed at 1.9 miles from the port. A luxury riverfront hotel where downtown meets Brickell, so the causeway onto Dodge Island starts almost at the door. Choose it for the closest luxury feel with a real evening scene around you.

Hampton Inn and Suites Miami, Brickell Downtown

Listed at 1.9 miles from the port, with one honest complication: a first hand review in January 2026 measured the road route to the cruise port at just under three miles using Google Maps. Both can be true, listed distance and road distance measure different things. Expect a short drive, not a two minute hop, and know this hotel does not run a port shuttle; breakfast is included.

InterContinental Miami

Listed at 2.1 miles from the port. The downtown bayfront landmark, and the hotel whose rooms look back at the ships. It suits cruisers who want a full service, upscale night with Bayside Marketplace next door, and do not mind being a few minutes further than the Brickell cluster.

Hyatt Regency Miami

A downtown riverfront staple, listed at 2.7 kilometres, about 1.7 miles, from the port on Hotels.com's Canadian site. A steady full service choice for cruisers who prefer a familiar brand and a walkable downtown evening.

Holiday Inn Port of Miami, Downtown

A long standing cruise passenger address on Biscayne Boulevard facing Bayside Marketplace, counted among the closest downtown hotels to the port in cruise hotel guides. This page could not verify a current shuttle or package offer on the hotel's own site, so book it for position and confirm any transfer at reservation.

JW Marriott Marquis Miami

A big downtown luxury hotel on Biscayne Boulevard Way, in the same downtown cluster as the hotels above: across the bay from the terminals, a short causeway drive away. Pick it for facilities and group travel rather than for the last few hundred metres of distance.

How far is too far on embarkation morning?

Downtown and Brickell are the near port neighborhoods: across the causeway from the terminals, minutes by car outside peak boarding surges. Miami Beach is the alternative people often consider, and it is further: a causeway crossing of its own, then the port causeway, with event traffic on top. It works for a beach first trip, but for a plain pre cruise night it adds risk for no benefit. Airport area hotels sit further west again and make sense only for very late arrivals, and even then downtown is usually the calmer morning.

The facts that matter

The distances above are the figures Hotels.com listed for each property against PortMiami, checked October 2026, and they describe distance to the port area, not a promised drive time to your terminal door. PortMiami's terminals spread along Dodge Island, and your cruise line's terminal may sit at either end of it. Road distance is longer than listed straight line distance, and causeway traffic on multi ship mornings is the real variable. Build buffer into embarkation morning regardless of which hotel you choose, and follow your cruise line's arrival window.

Frequently asked questions

Can I walk from a hotel to my cruise terminal?
No hotel on this page is a realistic walk to a terminal with luggage. The port is on an island reached by a causeway built for vehicles. Plan a shuttle, taxi, or rideshare for every downtown and Brickell hotel.

Which is the closest hotel to PortMiami?
By listed distance, citizenM Miami Brickell at 1.3 miles on Hotels.com. By cruise specific setup, the Comfort Inn and Suites Downtown Brickell, Port of Miami, with its port shuttle and included breakfast, is the closest thing to a purpose built answer.

Downtown or Brickell: which side is better?
They blend into each other along the river. Downtown puts Bayside and the bayfront at your door; Brickell adds a denser restaurant scene. For port distance the difference between them is small compared with the difference between both and Miami Beach.
