Hotels Near Miami International Airport

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami International Airport has exactly one hotel inside the terminal itself, the Miami International Airport Hotel on Concourse E, and it is the right pick for a tight connection or a very early departure. For everyone else, the cluster just outside the airport roads does the job well: the Sheraton sits under a mile away with a shuttle every 15 minutes around the clock, Embassy Suites gives families a proper suite and breakfast, and the Blue Lagoon district a few miles south adds the Hilton and the Pullman on a quiet lagoon peninsula. Every property below was verified on its own official site in October 2026.

Which hotels near Miami Airport should you look at first?

Miami International Airport Hotel

The only hotel located directly inside Miami International Airport, on the second level of Concourse E at the departures level, with its entrance by Door 11. The address is listed as Le Jeune Road, Concourse E, 2nd Floor, NW 20th Street, Miami, FL 33122, but in practice you never go outside: you walk from check in to the ticketing counters and security inside the terminal. The hotel itself describes soundproofed rooms, which matters in a building that operates all night. There is no shuttle because there is nothing to shuttle to, and no parking decision to make. Choose it for a long layover, a missed connection, a cruise morning with zero transfer risk, or a departure so early that any road time feels like a gamble. Skip it if you want a pool deck day or a neighborhood to walk around, because the airport is the neighborhood here.

Sheraton Miami Airport Hotel and Executive Meeting Center

The closest full service hotel outside the terminal, at 3900 NW 21st Street, Miami, FL 33142. Marriott lists Miami International Airport at about 0.6 miles from the property, and the drive is roughly five minutes in normal traffic. The detail that sells this hotel is on its own Marriott page: a complimentary airport shuttle that runs every 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, picking up on the arrivals level at the hotel shuttle stops. That frequency removes the usual airport hotel anxiety about a 4 am departure or a midnight arrival. The property is built for transit and meetings, with a large block of event rooms, an outdoor pool, and a setting on the Miami River side of the airport roads. Choose it if your flight times are awkward, you are attending a meeting at the airport, or you simply want the shortest possible hop between bed and baggage claim with a major brand behind it.

Embassy Suites by Hilton Miami International Airport

An all suite hotel at 3974 NW South River Drive, Miami, FL 33142, on the north side of the airport. Hilton places it about half a mile from the airport and within two miles by its own location summary, and it offers a free airport shuttle on a schedule, plus a scheduled shuttle service toward PortMiami that the hotel describes on its own site. The format is the appeal: every unit is a suite with a separate living area, which suits families, groups sharing, and anyone spreading work out the night before a flight. The stay includes the two Embassy signatures, a made to order breakfast and a complimentary evening reception, plus an outdoor pool and the Grill 305 restaurant. Choose it for a family night before a cruise, or a longer airport stay where a standard room would feel tight. Confirm the current shuttle hours with the hotel before you rely on them for a very early flight.

Hilton Miami Airport Blue Lagoon

A large resort style Hilton at 5101 Blue Lagoon Drive, Miami, FL 33126, set on a peninsula in the Blue Lagoon lake district south of the airport. Hilton states the hotel is four miles from Miami International Airport, and the drive is usually about 10 to 15 minutes. Its own site lists a complimentary airport shuttle running every 30 minutes from 5 am to 12:30 am, with pickup and drop off on the departures level at the hotel shuttle zone signs. This is the airport hotel for people who want the layover to feel like a break: a big outdoor pool, lagoon views, and a quieter corporate park setting rather than a runway fence. Choose it for a day long layover, a business stay in the Blue Lagoon or Waterford districts, or a family that wants pool time before a cruise. The tradeoff is distance: at four miles you plan around a shuttle timetable.

Pullman Miami Airport

The design led option in the same Blue Lagoon area, at 5800 Waterford District Drive, Miami, FL 33126, operated by Accor under its Pullman brand and still trading under that name on its official site. Accor places Miami International Airport at approximately 3.3 miles from the hotel and lists a complimentary shuttle service to and from the airport, and the property overlooks the lagoon with an outdoor pool and a fitness center. The feel is more boutique than the big transit hotels: fewer rooms, a calmer lobby, and a restaurant and bar aimed at business travelers who use the Waterford district as a base. Choose it if you want the Blue Lagoon calm with a more polished, quieter interior, or if your meetings are in the Waterford offices. As with every shuttle on this page, check the current timetable when you book, because frequencies change.

How to choose, and how the airport run actually works

Start with your flight time. Departing before 7 am or landing after 11 pm points you to the in terminal hotel or the Sheraton and its 24 hour shuttle. Traveling with children or a group points you to Embassy Suites for space and breakfast. Want the layover itself to be pleasant points you south to Blue Lagoon. For the off airport hotels the pattern is the same: collect your bags, follow the hotel shuttle signs on the arrivals level, and confirm your pickup zone before you land, because the shuttle stops are spread along a big terminal. If you are driving, most airport area hotels charge for parking and rates change, so check the all in cost rather than the room rate alone.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a hotel inside Miami International Airport?
Yes, one. The Miami International Airport Hotel is inside the terminal on the second level of Concourse E, before security, so you can walk between the lobby, ticketing, and the concourses without going outside or taking a shuttle.

Which airport hotel is best before a cruise?
Embassy Suites, for space, breakfast, and the scheduled PortMiami shuttle service the hotel describes on its own site. Confirm any port transfer directly with your hotel, because port shuttles are separate from airport shuttles and may carry a fee.

How far are the airport hotels from the terminal, really?
The Sheraton is under a mile, Embassy Suites is roughly a mile by road, and the Blue Lagoon hotels are about three to four miles, 10 to 15 minutes by shuttle in normal traffic. None of the off airport hotels is a walk you should attempt with luggage.
