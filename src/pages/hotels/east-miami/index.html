EAST Miami Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. EAST Miami is Brickell's playful lifestyle hotel: 352 rooms, suites and residence suites at 788 Brickell Plaza, sitting directly on top of Brickell City Centre, with a Uruguayan grill called Quinto, a garden rooftop bar called Sugar on the 40th floor, and a large pool deck with four pools. Book it for shopping at the door, a social rooftop and apartment style stays. Skip it if you want the beach outside, or a hushed, formal luxury mood.

What is EAST Miami, and where exactly is it?

EAST Miami stands at 788 Brickell Plaza, Miami, FL 33131, inside Brickell City Centre, the large open air shopping and dining complex in the middle of Brickell. The hotel is Swire Hotels' first property in North America, following EAST hotels in Hong Kong and Beijing, and that origin explains the Asian touches in the design and in its bars. The location is the argument for the hotel: you step out of the lobby into Brickell City Centre's shops, cinema and restaurants, the Brickell financial district surrounds you, and downtown, Wynwood and the Design District are short drives or rides north. The hotel says Miami International Airport is within a 15 minute drive, though real times depend heavily on traffic. PortMiami is also close, which puts EAST in the mix for cruise stays with better dining and shopping at the door than most port hotels. What it is not is a beach hotel. Biscayne Bay is visible from high rooms, but the ocean beach is across the causeway, and beach days need planning like any other downtown stay.

What are the rooms like?

Swire's own description counts 352 guest rooms in total, including eight suites and 89 residence suites. Standard rooms run from about 300 square feet upward in that description, while the largest residence suites reach about 1,800 square feet. Every category leans on the same formula: floor to ceiling windows, a private balcony, a walk in rain shower and views over the Brickell skyline or toward Biscayne Bay. Corner categories add wraparound balcony space and are the ones regulars ask for. The residence suites are the practical difference here, with one, two and three bedroom layouts, fully equipped kitchens and laundry facilities, plus access to hotel services. That makes EAST a credible base for longer work stays, family trips where a kitchen matters, and groups who want hotel housekeeping with apartment space. Book the exact layout by name, because the range from a compact urban king to a three bedroom residence is wide, and photos of one category do not describe another.

What is there to eat, drink and do on site?

Quinto, the hotel restaurant on the fifth floor, cooks in the Uruguayan parrilla tradition, built around an open flame grill, with indoor and outdoor seating. It is the hotel's signature dining room and the one to book for a proper dinner in house. Sugar, on the 40th floor, is the rooftop bar the hotel is known for: a garden style space with an Asian influenced design, a hand carved teak bar and wide views over the city, busy at sunset and into the night. Through a hidden door by Sugar sits Tea Room, the hotel's small speakeasy style lounge with Asian inspired cocktails. Down at lobby level, Domain works as a cafe, bar and informal co working space through the day. The pool deck is a major asset: about 20,000 square feet with four distinct pool experiences, described by the hotel as a lap pool, a spa pool, a cold plunge and a hot tub, plus a poolside bar. The gym, branded BEAST, runs 24 hours. There is no separate full service spa listed in the hotel's own current descriptions, so travellers who want a spa day should plan to book one elsewhere in Brickell or Miami Beach.

Who does EAST Miami suit, and who should skip it?

It suits shoppers and diners who will use Brickell City Centre daily, younger and design led travellers who want a social rooftop rather than a quiet bar, remote workers who like a lively lobby, and longer stay guests who need a kitchen and laundry without giving up hotel service. Cruise travellers who value restaurants and shops over a port view also do well here. It does not suit travellers chasing calm. Sugar is popular with locals, the lobby hums, and the whole property leans energetic, which is the point for most guests and a drawback for light sleepers who retire early. Ask for a higher floor away from the rooftop if noise is a concern. It does not suit beach first trips, spa focused trips, or travellers who want traditional formality. Rates sit in the upscale Brickell band and the rooftop, parking and extras add up, so compare the all in total with the quieter towers nearby before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Is EAST Miami on the beach?
No. It is in Brickell, on top of Brickell City Centre, with skyline and bay views from high floors. The ocean beach is in Miami Beach, a drive across the causeway.

Which floor is Sugar on?
The 40th floor. Sugar is EAST's rooftop garden bar, and the Tea Room speakeasy style lounge sits through a hidden door beside it. Both are popular with locals, so expect a crowd at sunset and on weekend nights.

Is EAST Miami good for a longer stay?
It can be. The 89 residence suites include one to three bedroom layouts with full kitchens and laundry, plus hotel services. Confirm the exact layout, floor and view when you book, because the residence range is wide.
