The Standard Spa, Miami Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 29, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Standard Spa, Miami Beach describes itself as less a hotel and more an adults only spa with guest rooms, and that is the right way to set expectations. It sits at 40 Island Avenue on Belle Isle, in the Venetian Islands off the Venetian Causeway, wrapped in tropical gardens on Biscayne Bay. The stay is built around the spa and its hydrotherapy playground, a bayside infinity pool, and slow garden days, not around the ocean beach, which is about a mile away. Book it to soak, steam, and do very little.

What is The Standard Spa, and where exactly is it?

The hotel occupies Belle Isle, which the property notes is the only natural island among the Venetian Islands, reached by the Venetian Causeway between mainland Miami and Miami Beach. That position is the hotel's whole personality: close to South Beach action, as the hotel puts it, but separated from it by water and gardens, with downtown Miami across the bay in the other direction. The grounds are low rise and green rather than tower and concrete, with rooms spread through tropical planting and the spa at the center of daily life.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel is small by Miami Beach standards, at around 100 rooms, and the categories are named by size and setting. Standard rooms come in Medium and Large, Garden rooms face the planting, Bath Terrace rooms add a private terrace with an outdoor soaking tub, and the Bay View Suites top the range with views over Biscayne Bay. The official descriptions stress breezy, soothing rooms with private terraces on many categories and spa style rain showers. Every room sleeps up to three guests on one queen or king bed plus a daybed, and there are no connecting rooms. If the soaking tub is the feature you are paying for, book a Bath Terrace category by name.

What is there to do without leaving?

The spa is the main event, and it is included for hotel guests. The Standard calls it a hydrotherapy playground, indoors and outdoors. The circuit runs through a Turkish style hammam, an aroma steam room, a sauna, an ice room, a Roman waterfall hot tub, an arctic plunge, and treatment baths, with the spa open daily from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm on the hotel FAQ. Treatments are the paid layer on top, and the hotel runs yoga and wellness classes with visiting teachers. Outdoors, the bayside infinity pool is reserved for hotel guests and members, and non guests can buy day passes on weekdays, subject to availability. The gym is open daily from 6:00 am to 10:00 pm.

Where do you eat and drink?

Lido Bayside Grill is the main restaurant, serving a global inspired menu on the bay from 8:00 am to midnight daily, with breakfast on weekday mornings and brunch on weekends. Café Standard is the all day indoor café, open daily from 7:00 am to 5:00 pm, and Monterrey Bar is the intimate indoor bar, open daily from 5:00 pm to midnight. The hotel has also added Belle, a dining spot for hotel guests and members named for the island. One policy worth knowing: the hotel states that Lido Bayside Grill and Café Standard are the only dining areas where guests under 18 are allowed.

What surrounds The Standard on Belle Isle?

Belle Isle is a small, mostly residential island, and the surroundings are water, causeway, and skyline rather than shops and clubs. The hotel faces across Biscayne Bay toward the downtown Miami skyline, which fills its sunsets. South Beach is close in distance and separate in feel: the hotel puts the ocean beach at about a mile away, roughly ten minutes by car or about twenty minutes on foot, so a beach day is an outing, not an elevator ride.

Who does The Standard suit, and who should skip it?

It suits adults who genuinely like spas, couples on a do little weekend, solo travelers recharging, and Miami regulars who want the locals' membership scene rather than a resort bubble. Skip it if you are traveling with children, full stop: the hotel is adults only, guests must be 18 or older to stay and 21 or older to check in. Skip it, too, if daily ocean swimming is the point of Miami for you, or if you need a low all in price. The hotel FAQ lists a daily resort fee of $55 plus tax, covering the hydrotherapy playground, classes, bikes, gym, and wifi, and valet is the only parking, so price the stay on its total. Pets, for the record, are welcome and stay free.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Standard Spa adults only?
Yes. The hotel describes itself as an adults only spa with guest rooms. Its FAQ states that guests must be 18 or older to stay and 21 or older to check in, and that guests under 18 are only allowed in Lido Bayside Grill and Café Standard.

Is The Standard on the beach?
No. It sits on Biscayne Bay on Belle Isle. The hotel puts the ocean beach at about a mile away, roughly ten minutes by car or about twenty minutes on foot. If walking to the sand matters most, compare our best beachfront hotels page.

Can non guests use the spa and pool?
Yes, within limits. The hotel sells day passes online for Mondays to Thursdays, based on availability, covering the pool and the spa amenities. Hotel guests use the hydrotherapy playground as part of their stay.

What does the resort fee cover?
The hotel FAQ lists a daily resort fee of $55 plus tax, including the hydrotherapy playground, health and wellness classes, bikes, gym access, and wifi. Our Miami resort fees guide explains how these charges work across the city.
