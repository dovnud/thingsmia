Faena Hotel Miami Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Faena is Miami Beach's most theatrical luxury hotel: 179 rooms and suites at 3201 Collins Avenue in Mid Beach, wrapped in red, gold, and animal print, with a 22,000 square foot spa, fire driven Argentine dining, a cabaret theater, and butler serviced beach. Book it for a splurge where the hotel itself is an event. Skip it if you want understatement, or if the rate makes you wince, because everything on property is priced to match.

What is Faena, and where exactly is it?

The hotel anchors the Faena District, a Mid Beach enclave built by Alan Faena in and around the former Saxony Hotel building at 3201 Collins Avenue. Filmmaker Baz Luhrmann and designer Catherine Martin shaped the interiors, and it shows: a mural lined lobby the hotel calls the Cathedral, a gold mammoth skeleton by Damien Hirst standing in the garden, and staff costumed like a production. The district wraps around it, with the Faena Forum cultural center across Collins Avenue, the Faena Theater in the building, and Casa Faena, the smaller sister inn, a few blocks north at 3500 Collins. Mid Beach puts you between worlds: South Beach starts ten minutes south, and the convention center is close enough to make Faena a genuine Art Week base, which the district leans into every December.

What are the rooms like?

With 179 rooms and suites, Faena is a fraction of the size of its Mid Beach neighbors, and the rooms are the point: deco inflected, saturated with color, turquoise and red velvet, Carrara marble bathrooms carrying products from the hotel's Tierra Santa spa, and in the higher categories, proper terraces facing the ocean. Service is old school in structure, with butler attention woven into the stay rather than sold as an add on. Corner and oceanfront categories carry the premium; bay and city view rooms are the value play if the design is what you came for.

What is there to do without leaving?

Tierra Santa Healing House, the spa, takes the entire third floor at 22,000 square feet, built around South American healing traditions with one of the largest hammams on the East Coast, treatment suites, and a thermal circuit of steam, sauna, and ice. Dining is a short, strong list: Los Fuegos, where Francis Mallmann cooks over open fire in the Argentine asado tradition; Pao, Paul Qui's Asian dining room; and the Veranda for the beach facing hours. The Living Room and the Saxony Bar carry the evenings, and the Faena Theater stages the hotel's own cabaret productions. Outside, the pool deck and the beach run on red and white stripes and butler service, closer to a private club than a public resort scene.

What happens in the district during Art Week?

Faena treats Miami Art Week as its home fixture. The Faena Forum, the Rem Koolhaas designed cultural center directly across Collins Avenue from the hotel, runs exhibitions and performances through the year and leans hardest into December, when the district around it fills with installations and the fair crowd drifts between the convention center and 32nd Street all week. Staying here that week means the art world is in your lobby and your restaurants whether you hold a fair ticket or not, which is either the entire point or a reason to pick another week, depending on the trip you want. Outside Art Week the district keeps a gentler version of the same rhythm: theater nights at the Faena Theater, spa days at Tierra Santa, and dinners at Los Fuegos that locals book without staying over.

Who does Faena suit, and who should skip it?

It suits design lovers, couples marking something, art week travelers who want the fair's social center of gravity in the lobby, and anyone who has done the glass tower luxury thing and wants a hotel with a point of view. It does not suit minimalists, families looking for kids club infrastructure, or travelers who plan to spend their days away from the hotel: at these rates, the property is the itinerary. Rates sit at the very top of the Miami Beach range and the extras follow, so this is a deliberate splurge, not an upgrade you talk yourself into at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Is Faena on the beach?
Yes. The hotel is oceanfront at 3201 Collins Avenue with its own serviced stretch of sand, red and white umbrellas, and attendants. The pool deck sits between the building and the beach.

Is Faena good for Art Basel week?
It is one of the week's social centers: the Faena Forum across the street hosts art week programming, and the convention center is a short drive south. Read our Art Basel hub for the full picture.

What is the difference between Faena Hotel and Casa Faena?
The hotel at 3201 Collins is the full luxury flagship. Casa Faena at 3500 Collins is the smaller, gentler priced boutique inn in the same district, sharing the beach and the neighborhood but not the scale.
