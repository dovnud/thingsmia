The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort is the full scale formal luxury resort of Bal Harbour: 213 rooms, studios, and suites in a 27 story oceanfront tower at 9703 Collins Avenue, directly across from Bal Harbour Shops, with St. Regis butler service, a 14,000 square foot spa, two outdoor pools, and four places to eat and drink. Book it for a complete, staffed resort that never requires leaving. Skip it if you want boutique scale, informality, or a rate anywhere near moderate.

What is The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort, and where exactly is it?

The resort rises 27 stories at 9703 Collins Avenue in Bal Harbour, on the ocean side of Collins with the beach in front and Bal Harbour Shops directly across the street, which is the defining convenience of the address. Marriott, which owns the St. Regis brand, presents the hotel as a luxury beach resort at the meeting point of art, fashion, and design, and the property pairs the hotel with private residences and condominium style suites in the same complex. The village around it is small, manicured, and quiet: Surfside begins just south with more everyday restaurants, Haulover Park and its inlet are just north, and South Beach is a drive of about twenty to thirty minutes. Art Basel week fills the hotel every December, but for most of the year this is the calm, wealthy end of the island.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel lists 213 ocean view rooms, studios, and suites, all with direct ocean views from private balconies, which removes the usual view lottery at booking. Interiors by the design firm Yabu Pushelberg use a soft, pale palette meant to carry the beach colors indoors, and bathrooms run to deep soaking tubs and rainfall showers. Suites step up through one bedroom layouts to large multi bedroom residences, including Royal and Presidential suites, so groups and longer stays are properly covered. The brand signature is St. Regis Butler Service, offered to guests as part of the stay, covering the small logistics of unpacking, pressing, coffee, and reservations that formal luxury guests actually use. Check in is at 4:00 pm and check out is at 11:00 am, per the hotel's Marriott page.

What is there to eat and do without leaving?

Dining has real range for a single resort. Atlantikos serves Greek cooking on a terrace by the pools, open for breakfast and lunch. BH Prime is the dinner steakhouse, a newer addition to the lineup. La Gourmandise is the all day cafe for French pastries, afternoon tea, and light meals. The St. Regis Bar and Wine Vault carries the evening, with the brand's champagne sabering ritual at dusk. The Spa at St. Regis covers 14,000 square feet of treatment rooms and relaxation space, next to the hotel's athletic club and fitness program. Outside, two outdoor pools split the mood, a tranquillity pool kept adults only and a family pool near the children's club, plus Oceanfront Day Villas and a staffed beach with cabanas and loungers across a long stretch of white sand. In other words, the resort is built so a guest can eat, swim, spa, and shop across the street for days without a car.

Who does The St. Regis Bal Harbour Resort suit, and who should skip it?

It suits travelers who want the traditional grand resort pattern at its most polished: butler service, a serious spa, several restaurants, and the beach and Bal Harbour Shops both at the door. It suits multigenerational families better than most formal hotels, because the family pool, children's programming, and big suites sit alongside the adults only tranquillity pool rather than replacing it. It also suits Art Basel and shopping trips where the hotel is the base and the splurge at once. Skip it if formality is not your style, because the service pattern here is deliberate and ceremonial. Skip it, too, if you plan to spend days exploring Miami itself, since Bal Harbour is the far north of the beach strip and every city outing is a drive. Rates are among the highest on this coast, and day villas, cabanas, spa treatments, parking, and resort charges add to them, so judge the all in total.

Frequently asked questions

Is The St. Regis Bal Harbour really across from Bal Harbour Shops?
Yes. The resort at 9703 Collins Avenue faces the mall across the street, which makes it the natural hotel for a shopping focused stay in Bal Harbour.

Does every room have an ocean view?
The hotel presents its rooms, studios, and suites as ocean view with private balconies facing the Atlantic. Confirm the exact category outlook at booking, since view wording varies by room type.

Is it good for families?
Yes, for a formal hotel. There is a dedicated family pool alongside the adults only tranquillity pool, children's programming, and large multi bedroom suites, alongside butler service for the adults.
