Hotels Near Hard Rock Stadium

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Be honest before you book: Hard Rock Stadium at 347 Don Shula Drive in Miami Gardens, home of the Miami Dolphins, the University of Miami Hurricanes, the Orange Bowl, the Miami Open, and the biggest concerts in South Florida, has almost no walkable hotel supply. One hotel, the Stadium Hotel, sits about a mile away, and even that is a drive, not a stroll, because the roads around the stadium are built for cars. Everyone else drives, rideshares, or rides a shuttle. The realistic choices are the Stadium Hotel for pure closeness, Miami Lakes Hotel for a proper stay about 15 minutes west, and the Aventura hotels about 20 minutes east, near the Brightline station the stadium itself recommends. Most visitors stay in Fort Lauderdale, Doral, or Miami Beach and drive in.

Which hotels near Hard Rock Stadium should you look at first?

Stadium Hotel

The closest hotel to the gates, full stop. Stadium Hotel stands at 21485 NW 27th Avenue, Miami Gardens, FL 33056, and the hotel itself describes its position as just a mile from Hard Rock Stadium and Calder Casino. It is a large, independent, sports themed property of 217 rooms, each with a walk out balcony, plus an outdoor pool, a mini golf course, and the Legends Sports Grill on site. For a fan whose entire trip is the event, waking up a five minute drive from the parking lots, with no surge priced ride home, is a genuine advantage. Set expectations honestly: this is a practical fan base, not a luxury resort, and the surrounding blocks are a highway corridor, so do not plan to walk to the stadium along NW 27th Avenue. Drive, or confirm what event day transport the hotel is running when you book.

Miami Lakes Hotel on Main

The best proper hotel within a short drive, west of the stadium in the planned town of Miami Lakes. The property at 6842 Main Street, Miami Lakes, FL 33014 was long known as Shula's Hotel and Golf Club, a name with its own Dolphins history, and now trades as Miami Lakes Hotel on Main on its official site, miamilakeshotel.com. It is roughly seven miles from the stadium, about a 15 minute drive in normal traffic, longer on event days. What you get is a real stay rather than a crash pad: around 205 rooms on a golf course, a pool, and Main Street's restaurants and cinema a short walk from the lobby. Choose it if the game is one part of a longer trip, or you are traveling with family. Event night still means a drive back west after the crowds clear.

AC Hotel Miami Aventura

The modern pick on the east side, at 20805 Biscayne Boulevard, Aventura, FL 33180. Marriott's AC brand is European in style: clean lined rooms, a rooftop pool deck, and a lounge built for a nightcap rather than a tailgate. The stadium is roughly nine miles west, about a 20 minute drive in normal traffic. The strategic reason to stay in Aventura is on the stadium's own site: for Dolphins games, Hard Rock Stadium promotes Brightline trains to Aventura station with complimentary stadium shuttles, and the station sits a short hop from this hotel. That pairing turns game day into a train ride instead of a parking lot negotiation. Choose the AC for a polished base near Aventura Mall and the Sunny Isles beaches a few minutes east.

Courtyard Miami Aventura Mall

The dependable alternative a few blocks from the AC, at 2825 NE 191st Street, Aventura, FL 33180, directly by Aventura Mall. Courtyard is Marriott's workhorse brand, and this property does what the flag promises: modern rooms, an outdoor pool, the Bistro for breakfast and evening food, and easy parking. The stadium run matches the AC's, roughly eight to nine miles west, about 20 minutes by car outside event traffic, and the same Brightline plus stadium shuttle option applies if you would rather not drive. One honest flag from Marriott's own page: exterior renovation work has been listed through late November 2026, described as limited to the building exterior, so ask about current status when you book. Choose the Courtyard for a predictable stay where the mall, the beach, and the game are all an easy drive.

The framing most visitors actually need

Here is the part other guides skip: many stadium visitors never stay near the stadium at all, and they are not making a mistake. Fort Lauderdale, roughly 30 to 40 minutes north in normal traffic, pairs the game with a beach vacation and often better value. Doral, west of the airport, works for travelers flying in and out of MIA who want newer hotels and an easy Turnpike run north. Miami Beach is the longest run, around 40 minutes or more on event days. The stadium's own guidance supports the stay elsewhere strategy: prepaid parking, free park and ride shuttles, and a rideshare lot that is itself a long walk from the gates. Pick your base for the trip you want, then your transport on top of it.

Event day traffic and parking, from the stadium's own guidance

Hard Rock Stadium publishes its parking and transport rules at hardrockstadium.com, and three points shape every hotel decision. First, stadium parking is prepaid: buy the pass with the ticket, not at the gate. Second, the free GEICO HRS Express shuttles run from Lot 70 at the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel (5700 S SR 7, Fort Lauderdale) and Lot 95 at the Golden Glades Parking Garage (16000 NW 7th Avenue, Miami), from three hours before kickoff until about 75 minutes after the game, and the stadium steers rideshare users to those lots for an easier pickup. Third, official rideshare pickup sits in Lot 44 at the Betty T. Ferguson Recreational Complex, which the stadium describes as an estimated 30 minute walk from the entrance. Leave earlier than feels necessary: the last two miles are where event day time goes to die.

Frequently asked questions

Can you walk from a hotel to Hard Rock Stadium?
Realistically, no. The closest hotel is about a mile away along major roads with heavy event traffic, in an area built around parking lots and highways. Plan to drive, take a shuttle where offered, or use the Brightline and park and ride options the stadium publishes.

Where do most visitors stay for a Dolphins game?
Split three ways: a minority stay close in Miami Gardens, Miami Lakes, or Aventura for the shortest drive, and the rest stay in Fort Lauderdale, Doral, or Miami Beach and drive or shuttle in. The tradeoff is game day convenience against what you do with the rest of the trip.

What is the best way to reach the stadium without a car?
For Dolphins games, the stadium promotes Brightline to Aventura station with complimentary stadium shuttles. Otherwise, use the free GEICO HRS Express shuttles from Lot 70 and Lot 95, and treat a direct rideshare as the last resort, because official rideshare pickup is a long walk from the gates.
