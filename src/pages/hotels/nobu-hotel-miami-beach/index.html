Nobu Hotel Miami Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 29, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Nobu Hotel Miami Beach is a hotel inside a hotel: a separate 206 room hotel set within the Eden Roc property at 4525 Collins Avenue in Mid Beach, with its own rooms, rates, and mood, built around the Nobu Miami restaurant. Guests share the Eden Roc resort setting, including four oceanfront pools, an adults only Nobu pool, and the 22,000 square foot Esencia Wellness Spa. Book it if dinner matters as much as the beach.

What is Nobu Hotel, and where exactly is it?

Eden Roc is the historic resort at 4525 Collins Avenue, and Nobu Hotel Miami Beach operates inside that same property, sharing its space, pools, spa, and beachfront while running as its own hotel. The Nobu site describes the concept as conceived by chef Nobu Matsuhisa, Robert De Niro, and Meir Teper, as a contemporary Japanese beach house inside a classic Miami Modern resort shell. Your reservation name matters: an Eden Roc booking and a Nobu booking put you in different rooms at different rates at one address. The location is oceanfront Mid Beach. The hotel puts the South Beach area at about three miles away, close enough for a dinner run by car, far enough that evenings at home base stay comparatively quiet.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel counts 206 guest rooms and three penthouse suites. Standard rooms come in Resort View and Bay View versions at 380 to 420 square feet, with king or two queen beds, and the Ocean View Room with Balcony steps up to 440 square feet. Suites add real space: Bay View Junior Suites at 539 to 627 square feet, the Zen Ocean View Suite at 593 square feet, and the Umi Ocean View Suite at up to 725 square feet. At the top sit one bedroom villas, the Yuhi, Asahi, and Nobu villas, running up to 1,900 square feet. The style is calm and textural, wood furniture and Japanese detailing.

What is there to do without leaving?

The Nobu guest inherits the full Eden Roc resort plant. There are four outdoor pools, including the exclusive adults only Nobu pool set above the ocean, plus cabanas for rent and serviced beach in front of the hotel. Esencia Wellness Spa covers 22,000 square feet, with treatment rooms, salon services, and hydrotherapy amenities including a sauna, cold plunge, whirlpool, steam room, and relaxation lounge, with complimentary hydrotherapy access for Nobu Hotel guests. The Esencia fitness center is open 24 hours to hotel guests, with Technogym equipment and classes from HIIT and kickboxing to yoga. A daily Teas of the World ceremony is listed among the resort fee inclusions.

Where do you eat and drink?

Dining is the reason this hotel exists. Nobu Miami serves chef Nobu Matsuhisa's Japanese cooking with South American influences, and the menu leads with the dishes that built the name, Black Cod Miso, Yellowtail Sashimi, and Rock Shrimp Tempura, in the dining room, the lounge, or on the oceanfront patio at dinner. Ocean Social covers the Floridian side with ocean views and locally sourced dishes, and Café Inez on the lobby level handles coffee, pastries, salads, and sandwiches. The Nobu Lobby Bar runs Tanoshi Hour specials daily, and Aquatica Pool and Beach Bar covers poolside drinks. One accuracy note for readers of older guides: Malibu Farm, long associated with Eden Roc, does not appear on the hotel's current dining list, so do not plan a meal around it without checking first.

What surrounds Nobu on Collins Avenue?

Mid Beach is the grown up middle of the island. The Beachwalk runs along the sand in front of the hotel, the Fontainebleau, at 4441 Collins Avenue, is the next big resort address to the south, and the Faena District sits a short ride down Collins Avenue. Days settle into a resort rhythm, pool and beach in the morning, spa in the afternoon, and a serious dinner downstairs, with South Beach nightlife, Lincoln Road, and the convention center all a drive south rather than a walk. You trade the Ocean Drive buzz for quieter nights and a less crowded stretch of sand.

Who does Nobu suit, and who should skip it?

It suits food first travelers who want the restaurant in the building, couples who will use the adults only pool and the spa, and anyone who prefers Mid Beach resort scale without the Fontainebleau's event energy next door. Skip it if you want to walk to South Beach bars and clubs, if a straightforward single hotel check in matters to you, because the two hotels at one address still confuse arrivals, or if you are hunting the lowest rate on Collins Avenue. This is a premium priced stay, and a daily resort fee and paid valet parking apply on top of the rate, so compare the all in total before you pay. Families are welcome, but the Nobu pool is adults only.

Frequently asked questions

Are Nobu Hotel and Eden Roc the same hotel?
No. They share the address at 4525 Collins Avenue and the resort setting, but they are separately branded hotels with different rooms and rates. Our Eden Roc guide covers the host resort.

Is there an adults only pool?
Yes. Of the four pools on the property, the Nobu pool is reserved for adults and sits above the ocean, while the other pools serve all resort guests.

Do Nobu guests get spa access?
Nobu Hotel guests receive complimentary access to the hydrotherapy amenities at Esencia, including the sauna, cold plunge, whirlpool, and steam room. Treatments are paid extras.

How far is Nobu from South Beach?
The hotel puts the South Beach area at about three miles south. Plan on driving or taking a rideshare rather than walking.
