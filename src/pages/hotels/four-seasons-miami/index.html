Four Seasons Hotel Miami Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Four Seasons Hotel Miami is the polished Brickell choice: 221 rooms and suites at 1435 Brickell Avenue, set above the avenue in a tall tower, with the lobby, restaurants and a two acre pool terrace all gathered on the seventh floor. Book it for calm service, bay views and a resort sized pool deck without leaving the business district. Skip it if you came for the beach, because this is not a beachfront hotel, and the sand is a drive across the causeway.

What is Four Seasons Hotel Miami, and where exactly is it?

The hotel sits at 1435 Brickell Avenue, Miami, FL 33131, in the heart of Brickell, the financial district south of the Miami River. The setup is unusual and worth understanding before you arrive. The lobby is on the seventh floor, and hotel rooms run from the 8th floor to the 29th floor of the tower, with residences above. That raised position is the whole trick: from the seventh floor up, the building opens to views over Biscayne Bay, Brickell Key and the downtown skyline, and street noise drops away. Brickell City Centre, with its shops and restaurants, is a short walk north, Mary Brickell Village is close by, and downtown, the Kaseya Center and PortMiami sit across or along the bay to the north. Miami International Airport is about a 20 minute drive in normal traffic, according to the hotel. What you trade for all this city access is the beach. Miami Beach is a separate drive, so plan beach days as outings rather than stepping out of the lobby onto sand.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel lists 221 rooms and suites in total, made up of 182 rooms and 39 suites. Rooms face either the city or the bay, and the bay side is the one most guests remember, with water, Brickell Key and passing boats in the frame. Interiors were recently redesigned, in the hotel's own description, and the feel is quiet and residential rather than theatrical: light woods, calm colours and marble bathrooms. Higher floors and corner categories add space and wider views, and the suites add separate living areas that suit longer business stays or families who want a door between beds. Because rooms start on the eighth floor, even lower categories sit above the avenue. If view matters to you, say so at booking, because city view and bay view rooms at the same size can feel like different hotels by morning.

What is there to eat, drink and do on site?

The seventh floor does most of the work. EDGE Brasserie and its cocktail bar serve breakfast, lunch and dinner there in a relaxed brasserie style built around regional cooking, and the hotel promotes its Sunday brunch with chef stations and a seafood raw bar. Out by the water, Bahia is the poolside restaurant, serving a northern Mediterranean menu at lunch, with dinner on Thursday and Friday evenings at the time we checked, plus cocktails and rose wines beside the pool. The pool terrace itself is the headline: nearly two acres on the seventh floor, with a grand main pool, a palm grove, over water hammocks, cabanas and a separate shallow pool area that families tend to claim. Wellness runs through Equinox, the fitness club in the building. Hotel guests get access to the club and its classes, and spa treatments are offered through the Equinox spa setup, so book treatments as you would at a standalone spa rather than expecting a separate hotel spa wing. A multi million dollar art collection with a strong Latin American focus runs through the public spaces, which gives the corridors more character than the usual corporate tower.

Who does Four Seasons Miami suit, and who should skip it?

It suits business travellers who want the Brickell address without a bland business hotel, couples adding a calm city stay before or after a cruise, and anyone who values a large, quiet pool deck and consistent service over a scene. Families do well here too, thanks to the pool layout and suite options, as long as everyone understands the beach is a planned trip. It does not suit beach first travellers, nightlife hunters who want to walk back from South Beach, or bargain hunters. Rates and extras sit at the luxury end of the downtown market, parking is valet led, and food and drink on site are priced like a flagship hotel. If your days will be spent in Miami Beach, stay in Miami Beach and visit Brickell. If your days are meetings, dinners and one good pool afternoon, this is a strong base.

Frequently asked questions

Is Four Seasons Hotel Miami on the beach?
No. It is on Brickell Avenue overlooking Biscayne Bay. The water in the view is the bay, not the ocean beach. Miami Beach is a drive across the causeway, so treat beach time as a day trip.

Which floor is the pool on?
The pool terrace is on the seventh floor, the same level as the lobby and EDGE Brasserie. It covers nearly two acres and includes the main pool, a palm grove with hammocks over the water, and cabanas.

Is Four Seasons Miami good before a cruise?
Yes, for travellers who want a quiet luxury night downtown. PortMiami is a short drive north across the bay. See our best hotels for cruise passengers guide for the full comparison.
