1 Hotel South Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. 1 Hotel South Beach is the nature led luxury resort: a full city block at 2341 Collins Avenue with more than 400 rooms and suites, 600 feet of beachfront, four pools including an adults only rooftop, a Bamford spa, and a serious fitness center, all wrapped in reclaimed wood and living greenery. Book it if wellness and design matter as much as the beach. Skip it if you dislike big footprints or big bills.

What is 1 Hotel South Beach, and where exactly is it?

The hotel opened in 2015 as the flagship of the 1 Hotels brand and occupies the entire block between 23rd and 24th Streets at 2341 Collins Avenue, at the northern edge of South Beach proper. That position splits the difference nicely: the convention center and the Art Basel fair are a short walk south, Lincoln Road is close, and the neighborhood immediately around the hotel is calmer than the Ocean Drive end. The brand's sustainability pitch is built into the fabric rather than printed on a card: reclaimed materials throughout, a farm stand in the lobby, triple filtered water instead of bottled, and a design language of wood, stone, and plants that has been widely copied since.

What are the rooms like?

Rooms and suites run large by South Beach standards, with the 168 suites including residence style units with kitchens that suit longer stays and families. The look is consistent across categories: pale wood, linen, living plants, and big windows on the water. Oceanview and oceanfront categories command the premium; city view rooms face the skyline and the sunset, which is not a bad consolation. Families should look at the residence layouts first, because the ability to do breakfast and laundry inside a resort room changes the economics of a week here.

What is there to do without leaving?

Four pools structure the property: the Center Pool as the social middle, the quieter Cabana and South pools, and Watr at the 1 Rooftop, an adults only pool and lounge on the roof with the widest views in the house. The Bamford Wellness Spa handles treatments, and Anatomy, the fitness center, is a genuine gym at roughly 14,000 square feet rather than a treadmill closet. Dining runs deep: Watr on the rooftop, Habitat for Mexican inspired dining, Tala Beach for the outdoor hours, PLNTHOUSE for plant based food, The Sandbox at the pools, Neighbors Cafe for coffee, and Drift as the lobby bar. Kids get the Seedlings club. The beach itself is 600 feet of serviced frontage, the widest private stretch most guests here will have used.

How green is it, really?

More than the marketing minimum. The brand was founded on the idea, and at this flagship it shows up in the fabric of the building: reclaimed and natural materials in the rooms, a living wall presence in the public spaces, a farm stand selling local produce in the lobby, filtered water stations in place of plastic bottles, and a design that works with daylight and sea air rather than against them. It is still a large luxury resort with four pools and a rooftop scene, so nobody should book it as an eco lodge; the honest reading is that it is the luxury option for travelers who want their hotel's footprint considered rather than ignored, and who like the calm, natural aesthetic that comes with it.

Who does 1 Hotel suit, and who should skip it?

It suits wellness minded travelers, design conscious couples, families who want space without giving up a luxury badge, and anyone staying long enough for the residence layouts to pay off. It is also a strong Art Basel base, close to the fair with a calmer home to return to. The downsides are scale and spend: the block is big enough that walks from room to roof to beach are real, the rooftop scene gets lively on weekends which cuts against the wellness pitch, and rates plus the daily resort fee sit firmly in the top tier. If you want a small hotel where the staff know your name by day two, this is not it.

Frequently asked questions

How many pools does 1 Hotel South Beach have?
Four: the Center Pool, Cabana Pool, South Pool, and the adults only rooftop pool at Watr. Most big Miami Beach resorts run fewer; the spread means one of them is usually calm.

Is 1 Hotel good for families?
Yes, especially in the residence style suites with kitchens, and the Seedlings kids club covers the programming side. Rates are luxury tier, so it is a splurge family pick rather than a value one; compare our family hotels page.

Is 1 Hotel walkable to Art Basel?
Yes. The convention center is a short walk south along Collins Avenue, which makes it one of the most practical fair bases in the luxury tier. See the Art Basel hub.
