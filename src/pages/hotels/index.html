Checked facts

The Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau describes Art Deco style hotels lining Collins Avenue in South Beach, and Mid Beach resorts including Fontainebleau Miami Beach, Eden Roc, and Nobu Hotel as beachfront resorts welcoming guests since the 1950s.

Brickell and Downtown hotel towers named by the Bureau include Four Seasons Hotel Miami and EAST Miami.

In Coral Gables, the Bureau describes The Biltmore as a historic landmark dating to the 1920s.

Common questions

Which Miami area should a first time visitor choose?

For a beach first trip, look at South Beach or Mid Beach. For restaurants, business, and cruise access, look at Brickell and Downtown. For a quieter, residential feel, look at Coral Gables or Bal Harbour. The area guides above split the picks by area so you can compare like with like.

What should I check besides the nightly rate?

Check the resort fee, parking cost, and what the rate actually includes, such as beach chairs or breakfast. Miami resort fees change, which is why the no resort fee guide above is rechecked before it is updated.

Which area works best the night before a cruise?

Downtown and Brickell put you on the city side of the causeway, close to PortMiami, and the airport area suits very late arrivals. Use the cruise passenger and PortMiami hotel guides above, and confirm any shuttle directly with the hotel.

Is beachfront the same as being in Miami Beach?

Not always. Beachfront describes the property setting, while Miami Beach is the city on the barrier island. Sunny Isles Beach and Bal Harbour are separate beach cities north of Miami Beach, and their guides above say so.
