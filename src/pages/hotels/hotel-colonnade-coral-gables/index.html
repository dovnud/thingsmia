Hotel Colonnade Coral Gables Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Hotel Colonnade is the boutique Autograph Collection hotel of downtown Coral Gables: 157 rooms and suites at 180 Aragon Avenue, a short walk from Miracle Mile, inside a grand 1926 building with a rotunda lobby, a rooftop pool, and its own cafe and lobby bar. Book it for weddings, events, business, and a walkable, leafy base away from the beach crowds. Skip it if your trip is about the sand, because the beach is a drive across the city.

What is Hotel Colonnade, and where exactly is it?

The hotel occupies a historic 1926 building at 180 Aragon Avenue, at Ponce de Leon Boulevard in downtown Coral Gables, and Marriott sells it, accurately, on position: Miracle Mile, the Gables' main shopping and dining street, is steps away, with the Shops at Merrick Park a short drive south and the University of Miami and Little Havana within easy reach. The public rooms are the first impression and the lasting one, a grand corridor and lobby with vaulted ceilings, a striking staircase under a rotunda, marble, and chandeliers, kept from the building's first life and now shared with restaurants and event spaces inside the same complex. Coral Gables itself is the other half of the product: a planned, Mediterranean styled city of tree lined streets, plazas, and canals, calmer and more residential than anywhere on the beach strip, with a free trolley running past the hotel.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel's own FAQ counts 157 rooms and suites, including 11 accessible rooms, split across Deluxe and Premier rooms and larger suites with separate living space. Where the lobby is historic, the rooms are modern: wood floors, contemporary decor, plush bedding, and large flat screen televisions, with some categories adding private balconies and a set of Ponce de Leon balcony rooms, plus second floor rooms with direct pool access for guests who want to step straight out to the water. City views here mean rooftops, palms, and the Gables skyline rather than ocean, and that is a fair trade for the neighborhood, but it is worth knowing before you picture a beach hotel. For a wedding block, a business trip, or a University of Miami visit, the layout is practical in a way the grand lobby does not suggest.

What is there to eat and do without leaving?

Breakfast and lunch run through Aragon Cafe, the hotel's cafe, with the lobby bar taking the evening with handcrafted cocktails under the stained glass and chandeliers, and in room dining available when you would rather stay put. Because the hotel shares its historic building with other restaurants, dinner choice inside the complex runs wider than one hotel restaurant, and Miracle Mile adds a full street of restaurants a short walk away, which is the real dining answer in the Gables. The rooftop is the leisure center: a heated outdoor pool and hot tub on a rooftop terrace with views over downtown Coral Gables, open during the day into the early evening. Add a fitness center, a game room with billiards, and extensive meeting and wedding space, and the pattern is clear. This is a social, event led hotel with a good pool, not a resort, and it does that job well.

Who does Hotel Colonnade suit, and who should skip it?

It suits wedding guests and couples marrying in the Gables, business travelers who want character instead of an airport box, University of Miami families, and anyone building a Miami trip around restaurants, shopping, and neighborhoods rather than beach days. It also suits a split stay: beach hotel first, Colonnade for the city half. Skip it if the ocean is the point of the trip, because South Beach is a drive of roughly twenty to thirty minutes each way and there is no beach service, shuttle promise, or sand at the door to soften that. Skip it, too, if you want resort scale facilities, since the pool is a rooftop amenity rather than a resort deck. Valet parking is charged per day, at a rate the hotel publishes and changes, so check the current fee and the all in total before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hotel Colonnade on Miracle Mile?
It stands at 180 Aragon Avenue, just off Miracle Mile at Ponce de Leon Boulevard. Marriott describes it as on Miracle Mile and the hotel's own site says near it; either way, the Mile's shops and restaurants are a short walk.

Is Hotel Colonnade a beach hotel?
No. Coral Gables is an inland, planned city. The hotel has a rooftop pool, but the nearest beaches are a drive away in Miami Beach or Key Biscayne.

Is Hotel Colonnade good for weddings and events?
Yes. The 1926 public rooms, the rotunda, and the event and meeting space are core to the hotel, and it is a common Coral Gables wedding venue. Confirm current event capacity directly with the hotel.
