The Betsy South Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Betsy is Ocean Drive's culture hotel: a family owned boutique at 1440 Ocean Drive, two historic buildings joined by a giant orb shaped skybridge, with a rooftop pool, a writer in residence, live jazz nightly, and a library. With about 130 rooms it is the personal counterweight to the Collins Avenue megaresorts. Book it for books, art, and quiet luxury on the liveliest street in Miami.

What is The Betsy, and where exactly is it?

The hotel joins two buildings on Ocean Drive at 14th Street: the Georgian Revival original, built as the Betsy Ross Hotel, and an Art Deco wing, the former Carlton Hotel, connected at the upper floors by The Orb, the egg shaped skybridge that has become a piece of Ocean Drive public art. It is family owned by the Plutzik family, and the ownership shows in the programming: a writer in residence scheme, curated exhibitions rotating through the public spaces, a working library, poetry and chamber music on the calendar, and photography and art woven through the corridors. The setting is classic South Beach, Lummus Park and the beach directly across the street, with the convention center a walk of about twenty minutes north, a fact that makes The Betsy a thinking person's Art Basel base.

What are the rooms like?

With about 130 rooms and suites split between two historic buildings, expect character over uniformity: rooms in the original building run smaller with classic bones; the Art Deco wing's suites are the larger, lighter option, and the top categories add terraces and ocean views. The style throughout is coastal colonial, cool colors, good art on the walls, and supremely comfortable beds. What you do not get is the resort standard issue everything: no room here is a warehouse, and if square footage is your luxury metric, check the stated room sizes carefully before choosing a category.

What is there to do without leaving?

The rooftop is the hotel's stage: a pool deck suspended between the two buildings with ocean views, a wellness garden, sunrise yoga, and daybeds above the Ocean Drive hum. A second, courtyard pool serves the quieter hours. Dining is Laurent Tourondel's LT Steak & Seafood, the chef's second empire restaurant, with The Alley for Italian in the passage between the buildings, the Carlton Room Cafe pouring Panther Coffee, and a lobby bar with live jazz every night. The beach is across Ocean Drive, and the resort fee covers the chairs, towels, and umbrella service on the sand, one of the cleaner value arrangements on the street.

What surrounds The Betsy on Ocean Drive?

The hotel fronts the best kept stretch of the Art Deco Historic District: Lummus Park and the beach directly across the street, the pastel facades of Ocean Drive running in both directions, and the Art Deco Welcome Center a few blocks south for walking tour departures. Days here take a natural shape: the rooftop pool in the morning, the park and the beach across the street in the afternoon, and the hotel's own cultural calendar, an exhibition opening, a chamber performance, a writer's reading, in the evening before the lobby bar's jazz set. It is the rare Ocean Drive address where the literary program is as real as the location, and regulars book it the way they would a favorite small hotel in any city, by name and by room.

Who does The Betsy suit, and who should skip it?

It suits culture first travelers, writers and readers, couples who want Ocean Drive without the party, and Art Basel visitors who prefer a boutique with an art program of its own to a fair adjacent tower. Skip it if you want a huge pool scene, a kids club, or the cheapest room on Ocean Drive; historic boutique space at this address prices in the upper middle of the South Beach range, and weekend nights bring Ocean Drive's street energy right up to the sidewalk cafe. For quiet, ask for a courtyard facing room.

Frequently asked questions

What is the big egg on the side of the building?
That is The Orb, the skybridge joining The Betsy's two buildings, designed as a work of art in its own right and now one of the most photographed details on Ocean Drive.

Is The Betsy good for Art Basel?
Yes. It is a comfortable walk from the convention center and the hotel runs its own visual art and literary programming during the week. Our Art Basel visitor guide covers the fair itself.

Is The Betsy quiet?
Inside, yes, by design. Outside, it fronts Ocean Drive, so street life is part of the address; courtyard rooms are the quiet pick.
