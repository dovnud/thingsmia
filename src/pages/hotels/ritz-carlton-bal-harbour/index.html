Short answer first. The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour is the private, low key luxury option at the north end of the Miami Beach barrier island: about 100 rooms and suites at 10295 Collins Avenue, with just two rooms on each floor, a heated oceanfront pool, the 10,000 square foot Exhale Spa, and dining at Artisan Beach House. Book it for quiet, space, and Bal Harbour calm. Skip it if you want a large resort's facilities, a social scene, or South Beach at the door.

What is The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, and where exactly is it?

The hotel stands at 10295 Collins Avenue in Bal Harbour, near the northern tip of the barrier island, where Collins Avenue meets the Haulover Inlet. The hotel describes itself as a coastal sanctuary overlooking the Atlantic Ocean and the Intracoastal Waterway, and the setting backs that up: beach in front, inlet and waterway around, and the village of Bal Harbour behind. Bal Harbour Shops, the open air luxury mall, is a short drive or a longer walk south, and Surfside's restaurants sit just beyond it. South Beach is a drive of roughly twenty to thirty minutes depending on traffic, which is exactly the point of staying here. You trade immediacy for quiet, and the guests who book this hotel tend to make that trade on purpose.

What are the rooms like?

Privacy is the structural fact here. The hotel lays out just two rooms or suites per floor, served by semi private elevators, so corridors are empty and the building never feels full. Published room counts vary between listings, from the mid 90s to just over 100, so this page describes it as about 100 rooms and suites rather than printing a single figure. Every room or suite has a private terrace, and the signature bathroom detail is a freestanding tub set by floor to ceiling windows with water views. Suites add full kitchens, which changes the stay for families and longer visits. Outlook splits between the Atlantic and the Intracoastal, and both are genuinely good here because the inlet gives the waterway side moving boats and open sky instead of a wall of towers.

What is there to eat and do without leaving?

Artisan Beach House is the main restaurant, serving globally inspired, seafood led cooking for breakfast, lunch, and dinner with indoor and outdoor seating facing the water. Water's Edge covers the poolside hours with lighter fare and drinks. Exhale Spa takes 10,000 square feet, with private therapy rooms, sauna and steam areas, hydrotherapy, and a fitness program built around barre, yoga, and strength classes, with daily classes listed for guests. Outside, the heated outdoor pool is the resort center, with private cabanas that listings describe with day beds and personal service, and the beach in front of the hotel is set up with loungers for guests. There is also a fitness center and an art collection through the public spaces, with pieces created for this location. None of this is on a grand resort scale, and that is the appeal: everything is close, staffed, and uncrowded.

Who does The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour suit, and who should skip it?

It suits couples who want the Ritz-Carlton service pattern without a big resort around it, travelers who value a quiet floor and a private terrace over facilities, and Bal Harbour shoppers who want to stay near the mall without staying in it. It can suit small families in suites with kitchens, as long as nobody expects a kids club or a water park. Skip it if you want choice: with one main restaurant and one pool, a week here leans on the room, the beach, and the spa rather than on variety. Skip it, too, for a first Miami trip built around South Beach sights, because every outing is a drive. Rates are in the top tier for the area, and cabanas, spa treatments, parking, and resort charges add up on top, so compare the all in total. One practical note: third party listings showed temporary closure notices during 2026, so confirm the hotel is accepting reservations for your exact dates on its own site before you plan around it.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour on the beach?
Yes. The hotel is oceanfront at the northern tip of the barrier island, with its pool and serviced beach facing the Atlantic and views of the Intracoastal Waterway from the other side.

How is it different from The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach?
Scale and mood. The South Beach hotel is a large, central resort on Lincoln Road. Bal Harbour is about a quarter of the size, with two rooms per floor and a quiet, residential setting. Our South Beach guide covers the other property.

Is Bal Harbour good for shopping?
Yes, of one specific kind. Bal Harbour Shops is the area's luxury mall, a short drive south, with designer stores and well known restaurants. Everyday shopping is better in Surfside or Aventura.
