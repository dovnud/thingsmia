Best Miami Hotels for Families

Last reviewed: October 25, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For a beach holiday with kids, start with Loews Miami Beach for its kids club, Fontainebleau and Eden Roc for big pool complexes, and The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne for a calm, self contained resort. If you would rather skip the beach scene entirely, the Biltmore in Coral Gables and the Aventura and Doral resorts trade sand for space. Every hotel below was verified as operating in October 2026.

Which Miami hotels work best with kids?

Loews Miami Beach Hotel

The most family ready of the South Beach resorts. The hotel's own site names its SoBe Kids Club, and the property pairs that with a large pool deck, direct beach access, and several on site restaurants, so a family can run a whole day without leaving. The Lincoln Road location also puts casual dining and shops in walking distance.

Fontainebleau Miami Beach

The big resort answer: an extensive multi pool poolscape on the Mid-Beach sand, with pools at different energy levels, so families can find a quieter corner. Family guides also point to its children's pool and organized activities. Choose it when the resort is the holiday and the kids are old enough to roam a large property.

Eden Roc Miami Beach

Next door to Fontainebleau, with three beachfront pools by the hotel's own description and a calmer feel than its neighbor. Family coverage highlights its pool complex and suite options. A practical pick for families who want Mid-Beach resort facilities with a slightly slower pulse.

The Ritz-Carlton Key Biscayne, Miami

A self contained beach resort on Key Biscayne, a quiet barrier island a short drive from downtown. The hotel runs the Ritz Kids program and markets its residences to families, and the beach here is calmer than Miami Beach. Best for families who want the resort to be the destination and plan one or two city outings rather than daily sightseeing.

Acqualina Resort and Residences, Sunny Isles Beach

A luxury residential resort on a wide Sunny Isles beach, with multi room residences that give families separate sleeping space and kitchens. Suits longer stays and multigenerational trips where the room setup matters as much as the beach, and where a quieter, more residential neighborhood is a plus.

Marriott Stanton South Beach

Marriott describes this South of Fifth hotel as family friendly, and the setting supports it: oceanfront pools, a residential neighborhood feel, and South Pointe Park with its playground and wide lawns nearby. A good choice for families who want South Beach without the Ocean Drive noise.

Biltmore Hotel, Coral Gables

The inland alternative. A historic Coral Gables resort famous for one of the largest hotel pools in the country, by the hotel's own description, with grounds to explore and a culinary academy that runs classes for children as well as adults. There is no beach here, so pick it for a pool and gardens holiday base, or pair it with beach day trips.

JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort and Spa, Aventura

A large resort in Aventura, north of Miami Beach, built around golf courses and extensive grounds. Families use it for space and a resort bubble away from the beach crowds, with the Aventura Mall area nearby for rainy day options. You will drive to the beach from here, so it fits car based trips.

Beach base or resort base: which suits your family?

Young children usually do best where the pool, the room, and food are within a short walk, which favors Fontainebleau, Eden Roc, Loews, and Key Biscayne. Older kids and teens often prefer the walkable energy of South Beach. If naps, early bedtimes, and parking a car matter more than nightlife outside the window, the calmer areas, Sunny Isles, Key Biscayne, Coral Gables, and Aventura, will feel easier than the Collins Avenue strip.

What should families check before booking?

The facts that matter

Kids club ages, hours, and whether the club is included or paid vary by hotel and season, and this page does not print them because they change. Confirm them on the hotel's own site when you book. The same applies to cribs, rollaway beds, and connecting rooms: request them in writing at booking, not at check in. Resort fees and daily parking charges are common in Miami and vary by property, so compare all in totals.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area of Miami for families?
Mid-Beach for big resort facilities, Key Biscayne and Sunny Isles for calm beaches, and South of Fifth for a walkable South Beach stay without the strip noise.

Do Miami hotels charge for kids?
Policies vary by hotel and by the child's age. Check the occupancy and extra person rules on the booking page for the exact room you choose, especially for teens.

Is South Beach suitable for children?
Yes, with the right base. The beach is wide, the water is the main event by day, and family friendly hotels cluster around Lincoln Road and South of Fifth. Our South Beach page marks the calmest picks.
