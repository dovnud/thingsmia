Loews Miami Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Loews is South Beach's full service anchor: 790 rooms at 1601 Collins Avenue, directly on the ocean, one block south of Lincoln Road, with a palm lined pool deck, a kids club, a spa, and the Historic District at the door. It is the default recommendation for families and groups who want everything handled in one big, well run resort. Choose somewhere smaller if a boutique feel matters more than facilities.

What is Loews Miami Beach, and where exactly is it?

The hotel pairs a modern oceanfront tower with the historic St. Moritz tower, an Art Deco era building folded into the resort, at 1601 Collins Avenue in the heart of South Beach. That address is the practical center of everything: the beach behind the pool deck, Lincoln Road's shops and restaurants one block north, Ocean Drive a short walk south, and the Miami Beach Convention Center an easy walk up Collins Avenue, close enough that Loews is one of the natural Art Basel bases. The hotel's 790 rooms, including 65 suites, were refreshed in a $55 million guestroom renovation completed in 2025, so the interiors are newer than the classic name suggests.

What are the rooms like?

Rooms run in relaxing earth tones and pastels with either Atlantic or bay and city views, and the honest split is between the main tower's scale and the St. Moritz tower's character. Suites climb to two dual story presidential suites with panoramic views. For families, the math that matters is floor space and bedding: standard rooms are workable for a couple and a small child, and the suite tier is where a family of four stops tripping over luggage. Ask for the highest floor your category allows; at 16th and Collins the view improves fast with height.

What is there to do without leaving?

The oceanfront pool deck is the resort's living room, palm lined, with direct access through to the sand and the hotel's beach service. SOAK Cabanas and Daybeds are the paid upgrade tier on the deck, enclosed cabanas with their own terraces and attendant service. Dining runs to five or more restaurants and lounges, including Preston's Market for breakfast, Bar Collins and Miami Joe Coffee Co. for the casual hours, Lure Fishbar for seafood, and Rao's Miami Beach, the Italian American name installed in the historic St. Moritz tower. The spa sits in the St. Moritz tower as well. Families get the SoBe Kids Club, and the hotel leans into family programming year round, which is the clearest line between Loews and its design hotel neighbors.

What is the St. Moritz tower, and should you book it?

The resort's second act is built into its site: alongside the modern oceanfront tower stands the historic St. Moritz building, an Art Deco era tower that Loews folded into the resort rather than demolishing. Today it holds the spa, Rao's Miami Beach restaurant, and a set of rooms and suites with a different character from the main tower, lower, older bones, more boutique in scale. Booking the St. Moritz side suits travelers who like historic fabric and a slightly quieter corridor; booking the main tower suits anyone prioritizing height, view, and the newest renovation finishes. Either way you share the same pool deck, beach, and restaurants, so the choice is about the room you sleep in, not the resort you get.

Who does Loews suit, and who should skip it?

It suits families first, then groups, conferences, and Art Basel visitors who want a full service base a walk from the fair. It also suits first time South Beach visitors generally: the location forgives a lot of planning mistakes. Skip it if you want hush; 790 rooms and 65,000 square feet of event space mean conventions and weddings share your pool deck, and the resort runs at full energy on weekends. The extras follow the big resort pattern too, a daily resort fee and priced parking, so compare all in totals, not headline rates.

Frequently asked questions

Is Loews good for families?
It is one of the strongest family picks in South Beach: the SoBe Kids Club, a big pool deck, direct beach access, and a central position that keeps every outing short. Our family hotels page compares it with the alternatives.

How close is Loews to the convention center?
A short walk north up Collins Avenue, close enough to make it a working Art Basel base without a car. See the Art Basel traffic and parking guide for the full logistics picture.

Is Loews on the beach?
Yes, directly. The pool deck opens onto the sand at 16th Street, with the hotel's beach service on it.
