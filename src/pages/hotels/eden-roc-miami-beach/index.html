Eden Roc Miami Beach Guide

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Eden Roc is the other Lapidus landmark on Millionaire's Row: a 1950s icon at 4525 Collins Avenue, directly beside the Fontainebleau, with three beachfront pools, a 22,000 square foot spa, big balconied rooms, and Nobu's Miami restaurant inside the building. It usually prices below its famous neighbor for a similar stretch of sand. Book it for Mid Beach glamour with a little more breathing room.

What is Eden Roc, and where exactly is it?

Eden Roc opened in 1956 to a design by Morris Lapidus, two years after his Fontainebleau next door, and the pair of them made this stretch of Collins Avenue the postcard of American resort glamour. The hotel sits at 4525 Collins Avenue, oceanfront in Mid Beach, with the Beachwalk running past its pools to the sand. Know the setup before you book: the address holds two hotels. Eden Roc runs the main resort, and Nobu Hotel Miami Beach, a separate 206 room hotel with its own lobby feel and rates, occupies part of the same complex and shares the setting. They are different products at one address, so check which name is on your reservation.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel's own description leads with the right detail: bright, spacious rooms with oversized balconies, and dazzling ocean views from most of them. The style is modern beach house rather than period piece, light woods and blues under the historic shell. Oceanfront categories are the ones to pay for here, because the building angles toward the water and the balcony is where a Mid Beach room earns its rate. Suites widen out for families and longer stays, and the Nobu rooms inside the complex run moodier and more minimal if that is your taste.

What is there to do without leaving?

Three beachfront pools spread the crowds better than a single resort deck, and the Esencia Wellness spa covers 22,000 square feet with treatment rooms, a salon, and a fitness center. Dining is five restaurants and bars deep: Ocean Social, the ocean facing dining room led by chef Tristen Epps; Nobu Miami, the Japanese restaurant that draws diners from across the city; Aquatica at the poolside; and Cafe Inez for coffee and grab and go. The beach service runs chairs and umbrellas onto the sand in front, and the Beachwalk connects you north and south for runs and rides.

Why are there two Lapidus landmarks side by side?

The rivalry is the history of this beach. Morris Lapidus designed the Fontainebleau in 1954 and was then brought in to design Eden Roc, opened in 1956, immediately next door for competing owners, and the pair spent the 1950s and 1960s out hosting each other as the twin capitals of Miami Beach glamour. You can still read the competition in the architecture: both buildings curve and gesture at the ocean, and Eden Roc's lobby and pool layout keep more of the intimate, clubby feel of the era than its bigger neighbor. For architecture minded guests, booking one and dining in the other is the full story, and the Beachwalk between their pool decks makes the comparison a five minute stroll.

Who does Eden Roc suit, and who should skip it?

It suits travelers who want the historic Mid Beach strip without paying the Fontainebleau's peak rates, couples who will actually use the spa, and diners who like having Nobu downstairs. Families do fine here too, with the caveat that this is a grown up resort in tone. Skip it if you want to walk to South Beach nightlife, because Mid Beach evenings are quiet and the strip is a drive or trolley ride south, and skip it if event scale bothers you: the complex hosts weddings and conferences, and the lobby can feel like a venue on weekends. The two hotels one address arrangement also confuses first timers at check in, so know your hotel's name before you arrive.

The facts that matter

Eden Roc Miami Beach, checked against the hotel's own site on October 28, 2026: 4525 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL 33140; designed by Morris Lapidus and opened in 1956; three beachfront pools; Esencia Wellness spa of 22,000 square feet; five restaurants and bars including Ocean Social and Nobu Miami; Nobu Hotel Miami Beach (206 rooms) shares the address. A daily resort fee and parking charges apply on top of the rate and vary by date, so check the all in total before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Are Eden Roc and Nobu Hotel the same hotel?
They share the address at 4525 Collins Avenue and the resort setting, but they are separately branded hotels with different rooms, rates, and check in. Your reservation name decides which one you are staying in.

Is Eden Roc next to the Fontainebleau?
Yes, directly beside it on Collins Avenue. Our Fontainebleau guide covers the neighbor; between the two, Eden Roc is usually the calmer and slightly cheaper stay.

Is Eden Roc good for a beach day?
Yes. Three pools, a serviced beach out front, and the Beachwalk at the door. The trade off is distance: South Beach's action is a ride south, not a stroll.
