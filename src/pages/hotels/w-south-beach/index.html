W South Beach Guide

Last reviewed: November 8, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. W South Beach is the design led oceanfront hotel at 2201 Collins Avenue, at the northern edge of South Beach, built around large rooms with private balconies and ocean views, two heated pools, the AWAY Spa, and a program of tastings, classes, and live music included in the resort fee. With about 350 rooms and suites, it is a full scale resort, not a boutique. Book it for space, balconies, and a pool and beach day that runs itself. Skip it if you want quiet formality.

What is W South Beach, and where exactly is it?

The hotel stands on Collins Avenue at 22nd Street, directly on the sand, where South Beach starts to shade into Mid Beach. Lincoln Road and the Miami Beach Convention Center are both a walk to the south, and the Bass Museum sits across the street, with the hotel offering guests a private tour of the museum on selected days when they show a room key. The building opened in 2009 and its rooms and spa were redesigned in a renovation completed in 2020, so the interiors read calmer and lighter than the party hotel reputation the W brand carried in its first Miami decade.

What are the rooms like?

Space is the selling point. The hotel's own rooms page describes contemporary suites in soft tones with clean design and natural light, beach house style millwork, Italian marbling, and herringbone floors in natural oak, with floor to ceiling windows opening onto private balconies. The overview makes the same promise for the whole house: guest rooms with ocean views and oversized balconies, and suites and penthouses above that tier with larger layouts. In practice, the category you pick matters more than the brand promise. Ocean facing categories are the reason to pay W prices; lower or side facing rooms give you the design and the resort, but not the view that fills the photographs. Check the stated size and outlook of the exact room name before you pay, because the spread between a base room and a top suite here is wide.

What is there to do without leaving?

Two heated pools anchor the resort day: WET, the main outdoor heated pool with its bar and grille serving to chaise lounges and rentable cabanas, and Mini WET, a second, adult heated pool for a quieter swim. The hotel also runs rooftop basketball, tennis, and pickleball courts, an unusual extra in South Beach, and the FIT fitness center covers the indoor workout. AWAY Spa is the wellness center of the house, described by the hotel as a luxury wellness and beauty sanctuary offering sensory treatments and massages, with limestone steam showers and an infrared sauna. Dining on the current official list is WET Bar and Grille by the pool, The Grove for all day coastal New American dining with a courtyard, Living Room Bar for cocktails and weekly live music, and in room dining. A daily program of guest activities, from mixology and wine and cheese tastings to fitness sessions, is included in the resort fee. A resort fee applies on top of the rate, and this page prints no amount because fees change; check the all in total before you pay.

What surrounds W South Beach on Collins Avenue?

Days here split naturally between the serviced beach in front of the hotel and the cultural strip behind it. The Bass Museum across Collins Avenue is the closest serious attraction, and the hotel's own art programming leans on that neighbor. Collins Avenue itself is the hotel row: a Beachwalk walk north takes you toward Mid Beach, while a walk south passes the other big South Beach resorts toward Lincoln Road and the convention center, which is why the W fills with Art Basel visitors in December; our Art Basel visitor guide covers the fair itself.

Who does W South Beach suit, and who should skip it?

It suits travelers who want a big, serviced resort and will actually use it: pool days, spa time, rooftop courts, programmed activities, and a balcony over the ocean at the end of it. It suits couples and groups who like design and music in the mix, and Art Basel and convention visitors who want a resort base a walk from the hall. Skip it if you want hushed, formal luxury; the service style here is informal and the pool scene has energy by design. Skip it, too, if a low headline rate is the plan: rooms, cabanas, valet parking, and the resort fee all sit in the upper tier of South Beach pricing, so compare all in totals. Check in is at 4:00 pm and check out is at 11:00 am, per the hotel's own site.

Frequently asked questions

Does every room at W South Beach have a balcony?
The hotel's own rooms page describes floor to ceiling windows opening onto private balconies and its overview promises ocean views and oversized balconies across the guest rooms. Confirm the outlook on the exact category you book, because view quality varies by floor and angle.

Is W South Beach good for Art Basel?
Yes. The convention center is a walk to the south, the Bass Museum is across the street, and the hotel runs its own art linked programming. Expect fair week pricing and a full house.

Can I bring a dog to W South Beach?
The hotel's pet policy welcomes pets, with up to two pets per room, each up to 40 pounds, and a non refundable pet fee per stay. Confirm the current fee when you book.
