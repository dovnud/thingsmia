Hotels Near Miami Beach Convention Center

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami Beach Convention Center at 1901 Convention Center Drive sits in the best hotel grid in South Beach, and five strong hotels are a genuine walk from its doors. The Setai is the closest luxury stay, the Ritz-Carlton and Loews are the full service pair a few blocks south, the W is a short walk north, and Moxy trades a longer walk for a lighter rate. The Standard and Fontainebleau are not walks at all: they are short drives, stated plainly below. The list is ordered by walking practicality, because during Art Basel week a walkable hotel beats a famous one across traffic.

Which hotels near the convention center should you look at first?

The Setai, Miami Beach

The closest luxury hotel to the convention center doors. The Setai stands at 2001 Collins Avenue, roughly two blocks north of the center, about a five minute walk along Collins Avenue. It is the calmest address in South Beach: a hushed courtyard, three temperature controlled pools, and rooms that feel like a retreat rather than a scene. For an Art Basel exhibitor or collector crossing between the fair and the hotel several times a day, that short, quiet walk is the whole argument. Dining and Lincoln Road sit just south when you want them. Choose The Setai for privacy, service, and the shortest luxury commute to the fair. Our full Setai guide covers rooms, dining, and the spa in detail.

The Ritz-Carlton, South Beach

At 1 Lincoln Road, where Lincoln Road meets Collins Avenue, roughly a third of a mile south of the convention center, about a seven to ten minute walk. The position is the most useful in South Beach: the convention center north, Lincoln Road shopping at the door, the beach behind the elevated oceanfront pool. Art Basel lists the Ritz-Carlton, South Beach as an official hotel partner for the fair, which tells you how the art world treats this address during fair week. It suits delegates and fair visitors who want classic full service luxury and expect to entertain or be entertained around Lincoln Road in the evenings. See our Ritz-Carlton South Beach guide for the full room and dining detail.

Loews Miami Beach Hotel

The full service anchor at 1601 Collins Avenue, about half a mile south of the convention center, an easy 10 minute walk up Collins Avenue. Loews is the practical convention pick: 790 rooms, a large pool deck, several restaurants, and the depth to absorb a conference crowd without strain. It sits one block south of Lincoln Road, so dinners are handled, and the beach is behind the pool deck for the hours between sessions. Groups, families traveling with a delegate, and exhibitors who need meeting space of their own tend to land here. The tradeoff is scale: this is a big resort, not a boutique hideaway. Our Loews Miami Beach guide walks through the towers, rooms, dining, and kids club.

W South Beach

At 2201 Collins Avenue, roughly half a mile north of the convention center, about a 10 minute walk south along Collins Avenue or the beachwalk. The W is the design led, see and be seen option: large rooms with balconies, a strong pool scene, and a long association with the Art Basel social calendar. For fair week visitors whose evenings matter as much as the show floor, the walk home is short and the lobby is part of the event. Families do stay here, but the energy is grown up, and standard convention travelers who want quiet corridors may prefer Loews or the Ritz-Carlton. Read our W South Beach guide before you book for the honest room and noise picture.

Moxy South Beach

The lighter budget walk, at 915 Washington Avenue in the Art Deco District, roughly a mile south of the convention center. That is about a 20 minute walk, or a short drive or rideshare when the December sun or a schedule argues against walking. Moxy is a playful Marriott brand built around social spaces, a rooftop, and smaller, smart rooms, and the rate usually undercuts the Collins Avenue luxury row by a wide margin. Choose it if you are attending the fair or a conference on a real budget and would rather spend on dinners and art than on the room. Expect a social lobby and a younger crowd rather than hush. Our Moxy South Beach guide covers the rooms and rooftop.

The Standard Spa, Miami Beach

Be clear about this one: The Standard is not a walk to the convention center. It sits at 40 Island Avenue on Belle Isle, across Biscayne Bay on the Venetian Causeway, roughly a mile and a half away, about a 10 minute drive in normal traffic and a long, exposed walk few people enjoy twice. What you get for the drive is a different stay: a spa led, adults leaning hideaway with a hydrotherapy circuit, a bay front pool, and a calm the Collins Avenue strip never has. Choose it for a wellness focused trip where the convention center is one stop among several, not the whole itinerary. Our Standard Miami Beach guide explains the spa, the rooms, and the Belle Isle setting.

Fontainebleau Miami Beach

Also not a walk, and this page will not pretend otherwise. Fontainebleau stands at 4441 Collins Avenue in Mid Beach, roughly two and a half miles north of the convention center, about a 10 to 15 minute drive in normal traffic, longer at fair week peaks. People still book it for convention center events because it is a destination in itself: around 1,500 rooms, a huge pool complex, a serious restaurant and nightlife lineup, and meeting infrastructure of its own. Choose it if the hotel is half the trip and a daily drive is an acceptable cost. For a pure Art Basel base where you walk to the fair, pick from the five above. Our Fontainebleau guide covers the resort in full.

How to choose, and getting there during a big show

Walk if you can. During Art Basel and other major conventions, the streets around 17th Street slow to a crawl, parking fills early, and a 10 minute walk beats a 25 minute rideshare. Pick The Setai or the Ritz-Carlton for the shortest luxury walks, Loews for group practicality, the W for the social version, and Moxy for the budget version. If you drive in, the center runs its own parking and the city garages around 17th Street are the fallback; our Art Basel traffic and parking guide maps fair week street by street. Book early: the walkable hotels sell December inventory months ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the closest hotel to the Miami Beach Convention Center?
The Setai at 2001 Collins Avenue, about two blocks north. The Ritz-Carlton and Loews are the closest full service alternatives, under half a mile south.

Where should I stay for Art Basel?
In one of the five walkable hotels above, booked early. Fair week traffic makes even short drives slow. Start with our Art Basel hub for dates and the week around the fair.

Is Fontainebleau walkable to the convention center?
No. It is about two and a half miles north in Mid Beach, a short drive but not a walk. If walking matters, stay in South Beach.
