Freehand Miami Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Freehand Miami is the budget friendly outlier of Miami Beach: a boutique hotel with both private and shared rooms, set in the historic Indian Creek Hotel building at 2727 Indian Creek Drive, one block from the beach in Mid Beach. People book it for the garden pool scene and the Broken Shaker, its award winning cocktail bar, and stay for prices the big resorts do not offer. Book it if you travel light and social. Skip it if you want a full service resort or a silent corridor.

What is Freehand Miami, and where exactly is it?

The Freehand is a reinvention of the historic Indian Creek Hotel, one of Miami Beach's classic Art Deco buildings, on Indian Creek Drive at 27th Street. The hotel itself calls the location a five minute walk from the beach and less than a mile from the nightlife of South Beach, which is the honest shape of it: Mid Beach quiet at the door, South Beach energy a bike ride or a short drive south, and the Miami Beach Boardwalk right at the beach end of the street. It was created for a newer generation of travellers, with handcrafted design and a community atmosphere, and it still runs that way.

What are the rooms like?

The range is the point. Private rooms and suites sit alongside shared rooms, including a six bed shared room the hotel shows on its own site, which is how solo travellers and groups keep a Miami Beach address at a shared room price. Rooms were designed by Roman and Williams and carry custom commissioned artwork by Bard College students and alumni, so even the shared end of the house looks considered rather than cut rate. The trade is privacy and quiet: shared rooms and a social pool scene mean this house hums, and rooms near the pool hear it. Light sleepers should say so at booking and ask what is available away from the garden.

What is there to do without leaving?

The backyard is the hotel: an outdoor terrace and pool in a garden setting, with the Broken Shaker pouring at the poolside. The hotel describes Broken Shaker as the Tales of the Cocktail award winning Best American Hotel Bar, and it draws locals as well as guests, which is rare for a hotel bar at this price point. Ray's, a hometown style bar with a disco ball and pool table that joined the hotel in December 2024, serves bites, beers, and cocktails on Fridays to Sundays. The practical list is long for a small hotel: complimentary bicycles, complimentary Tesla rides within a two mile radius, fast free WiFi, a 24 hour front desk, daily housekeeping, and free Saturday morning fitness and wellness classes. Coffee and pastry service runs Fridays to Sundays. Dogs up to 75 pounds are welcome for a pet fee per stay; the hotel listed that fee at $75 when checked on October 30, 2026, so confirm it when you book. One honest gap to know before you arrive: the long running 27 Restaurant does not appear on the hotel's current official page, so do not plan dinners around it without checking what is serving now.

Who does Freehand Miami suit, and who should skip it?

It suits solo travellers, backpackers upgrading one notch, friend groups splitting a shared room, and anyone whose Miami plan is beach, boardwalk, and bars rather than spa appointments and valet stands. It is also a smart Art Basel and Boat Show base: Mid Beach sits between the convention center and the Collins Avenue venues, at a fraction of fair week resort rates. Skip it if you need guaranteed quiet, a big resort pool deck, or on site parking simplicity; parking is offsite or limited valet through a neighbouring hotel at a listed special rate, and both options carry a charge.

Frequently asked questions

Is Freehand Miami a hostel?
It began as a hostel hotel hybrid and now presents itself as a boutique hotel. Shared rooms remain part of the mix alongside private rooms and suites, and every category is bookable the normal hotel way.

How far is Freehand Miami from the beach?
The hotel lists itself one block from the beach, about a five minute walk, with the Boardwalk at the end of the street.

Can I park at Freehand Miami?
Not in a hotel garage. The hotel lists offsite parking nearby and a limited valet option through the AC Hotel by Marriott at a special rate for Freehand guests. Both cost extra; confirm the current rate before you drive.
