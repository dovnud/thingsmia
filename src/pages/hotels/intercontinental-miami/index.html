InterContinental Miami Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. InterContinental Miami is the big bayfront downtown landmark: 652 rooms and suites at 100 Chopin Plaza, right on Biscayne Bay beside Bayfront Park, with a Pan Latin steakhouse, a spa with 10 treatment rooms, a rooftop pool and some of the widest bay and port views in the city. Book it for cruise nights, events at the Kaseya Center and classic full service scale. Skip it if you want a small boutique feel, or a beach outside the door, because the shoreline here is bayfront, not sand.

What is InterContinental Miami, and where exactly is it?

The hotel stands at 100 Chopin Plaza, Miami, FL 33131, on the eastern edge of downtown where the city meets Biscayne Bay. Bayfront Park wraps around it, Bayside Marketplace is a short walk north, and the Kaseya Center and the Perez Art Museum are close by along the bay. PortMiami sits across the water, close enough that cruise ships fill many room windows, which is a large part of the appeal for pre cruise and post cruise stays. Brickell, with its offices and restaurants, starts just south across the Miami River. The tower rises 34 stories, and the hotel has been a fixture of this corner of the skyline for more than four decades. That history shows in the scale: this is a convention capable, wedding hosting, big lobby hotel, not an intimate hideaway. It also means facilities are broad, staffing is deep, and there is usually something happening in the public spaces, from conferences to cruise groups gathering for transfers.

What are the rooms like?

The hotel lists 652 rooms and suites, including 22 suites in the materials we checked. Rooms were redesigned in a recent renovation and start at about 403 square feet according to IHG, which is generous for downtown. Expect floor to ceiling windows, pillow top bedding, Nespresso machines and bathrooms with premium amenities, with the real choice being the view: bay and port facing rooms look over the water and the cruise terminals, while city view rooms face the downtown skyline. Club InterContinental rooms add access to the Club lounge, which the hotel describes as offering breakfast, afternoon tea and evening drinks with dedicated service. For cruise travellers, a high bay facing room is the signature experience here, since you can watch ships arrive and leave without leaving bed. Families and groups do well with the sheer number of room configurations, though connecting options and bed types should be confirmed at booking in a hotel of this size.

What is there to eat, drink and do on site?

Toro Toro, by chef Richard Sandoval, is the headline restaurant, a Pan Latin steakhouse in the lobby known for dry aged steaks cooked over wood fire. Ole handles breakfast, with a buffet and a la carte dishes in a bright lobby level room. Bluewater is the rooftop poolside spot for drinks and light bites with views over the port, and there is an on site Starbucks in the lobby for early starts. Freddy's Speakeasy, a small hidden style bar with handcrafted cocktails, appears in the hotel's current dining descriptions, so check on the night whether it is open during your stay. The spa is mySpa, offering massages, facials including HydraFacial treatments and body therapies across 10 treatment rooms, with a couples suite in the mix. The rooftop pool deck has cabanas and bay views, the fitness studio runs 24 hours, and up on the fifth floor, SkyLawn gives the hotel about 35,000 square feet of rooftop green space beside the pool deck, a rare patch of lawn in this part of downtown and a popular event setting.

Who does InterContinental Miami suit, and who should skip it?

It suits cruise passengers who want a proper hotel night with a port view, conference and event travellers, couples who like a grand lobby and a spa under the same roof, and anyone attending a game or concert at the Kaseya Center who wants to walk back. The pet friendly programme is a genuine part of the current offer, which will matter to some travellers and not at all to others. It does not suit travellers chasing a quiet, small scale stay: with 652 rooms, big events and cruise turnover, the lobby and elevators can feel busy at peak times. It also does not suit beach first trips. Bayfront Park is lovely for a morning walk, but there is no swimming beach at the door, and Miami Beach is a causeway drive away. Prices for food, parking and extras follow the big flagship pattern, so check the full total, not just the room rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is InterContinental Miami on the beach?
No. It is on Biscayne Bay in downtown Miami, next to Bayfront Park. You get water views, a pool deck and passing cruise ships, but no sand at the hotel. Miami Beach is a drive across the causeway.

How close is InterContinental Miami to PortMiami?
Very close by downtown standards. The port is across the bay and visible from bay facing rooms, and the drive to the terminals is short in normal traffic. See our PortMiami guide for terminal details.

What is the spa called?
mySpa. IHG lists 10 treatment rooms with massages, skincare and HydraFacial treatments, plus a couples suite. Book ahead on cruise weekends and event nights, when demand rises.
