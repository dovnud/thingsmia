Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix 2027: tickets and where to stay

Last reviewed: November 9, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The race runs Friday, April 30 to Sunday, May 2, 2027, at the Miami International Autodrome inside the Hard Rock Stadium complex in Miami Gardens, and it carries a new name: the Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix, with Hard Rock replacing the previous title sponsor for 2027. Tickets are on general sale now, not on a waitlist. Grandstands start at $685 for a three day ticket, hospitality at $1,500, and the Paddock Club at $16,500. Two facts to lock before you plan around anyone else's summary: Miami is not a Sprint weekend in 2027, and no session start times are published yet. Every time on the official race page reads TBC.

The weekend, as the calendar states it

The official Formula 1 race page lists Friday, April 30 for Practice 1 and Practice 2, Saturday, May 1 for Practice 3 and Qualifying, and Sunday, May 2 for the race, with every session time marked TBC. The circuit is a 19 turn, 3.36 mile lap on the stadium campus at 347 Don Shula Drive. The Sprint question deserves its own paragraph because 2026 habits die hard: Formula 1's 2027 calendar announcement of September 16, 2026 raised the Sprint count to ten, naming Bahrain, Australia, Japan, Monaco, and Abu Dhabi as new Sprint venues and Canada, Silverstone, Monza, Sao Paulo, and Qatar as returning ones. Miami is not on that list. Plan a standard weekend: two Friday practice sessions, a Saturday that builds to qualifying, and a Sunday race. Anyone selling you a Saturday sprint race in Miami for 2027 is selling you last year's format.

Grandstands: the 2027 prices from the promoter

All grandstand prices here are three day tickets from the promoter's own grandstand page, checked November 9, 2026. The Team Grandstands are $685, with eight team stands for 2027: Ferrari, Williams, Audi, McLaren, Red Bull, Haas, Cadillac, and Racing Bulls. Beach is $695. The Marina grandstands, presented by MSC Cruises and covering Turns 6, 7, and 8, are $730. The Grandstand Pass is $845 and puts you in a different grandstand each day, which suits a first visit better than loyalty to one corner. Turn 18 is $935, Turn 1 North is $945, and Start/Finish, presented by Gainbridge, tops the list at $1,125. Grandstand tickets include access to the wider campus and to the 300 level of the stadium and Team Village viewing. One add-on to understand before it surprises you at renewal time: Grandstand+ presented by Heineken is a benefits add-on for three day grandstand buyers who enroll in auto-renewal, not a separate seat. And the honest gap: no Campus Pass general admission product appeared on the promoter's homepage, grandstand, or hospitality pages at this check. Secondary sellers describe it as not yet on sale or deposit only. This page prints no Campus Pass price, and neither should anyone else until the promoter lists one.

Hospitality: where the money goes

Hospitality starts with the Hard Rock Beach Club Deck at $1,500. The Turn 18 Club is $3,800. The MSC Yacht Club, a five level structure at Turns 5 to 9 in the Marina, is $4,250. The 72 Club is $8,000. Above those sit the Paddock Club at $16,500, which went on public sale for the first time on September 24, 2026, and suites listed from $67,000 on the promoter's homepage. The Marina is real for 2027 on the ticket pages: Marina grandstands and the Yacht Club both sell against it. What is not published yet is a 2027 campus map; the detailed neighborhood layout in circulation, with its themed campus zones, comes from the promoter's release for the 2026 race, and this page doesn't present it as the 2027 layout.

Where to stay, matched to the ticket

No hotel sits at the circuit gates, so every stay is a strategy. The convenience play is Miami Gardens and Aventura: short drives, early arrivals, easy exits, and the Grand Prix hotels guide weighs that stock area by area. The trip play is downtown, Brickell, or Miami Beach, a 30 to 45 minute run in race traffic, repaid with the full Miami weekend around the sessions. Match the base to the spend. A $685 grandstand weekend leaks its value if the hotel bill doubles it; a hospitality weekend wastes its evenings in a stadium suburb. Book the room when you book the seat, because race weekend is a peak pricing event and the near stadium stock goes first. The traffic and parking guide is the transport half of the plan; book parking or your shuttle with the ticket, not after.

When to buy, and what happens next on this page

Buy the seat and the room in the same week. General sale is open now, the near stadium hotel stock is the first thing the weekend consumes, and the prices printed here are the promoter's list prices at the November 9, 2026 check, not a promise they'll hold to April. Two things will change on this page before the race: session start times, which land here in a refresh the week Formula 1 publishes them, and the Campus Pass question, which this page rechecks against the promoter's site rather than the resale market. The rest of the month around the race, concerts, festivals, and the start of the wet season, is in the Miami in May coverage.
