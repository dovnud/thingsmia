Miami Grand Prix 2027

The Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix is the loudest weekend of Miami's spring: a world championship race on a purpose built circuit around Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, wrapped in a festival of concerts, food, and hospitality. The 2027 race runs Friday April 30 to Sunday May 2. This hub gathers everything ThingsMIA publishes on the Grand Prix in one place: the event guide, the spectator guide, traffic and parking, an honest cost guide, and the hotel guide. Every date below was checked against the race promoter.

The 2027 race at a glance

  • Dates: Friday April 30 to Sunday May 2, 2027. Race day is Sunday, May 2.
  • Venue: Miami International Autodrome at Hard Rock Stadium, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens.
  • Edition: the sixth Miami Grand Prix. The promoter states every previous edition has sold out.
  • Tickets: general sale opened September 24, 2026, through f1miamigp.com and Formula 1's official channels.
  • New for 2027: an expanded Paddock Club extending toward Turn 1, with Paddock Club tickets on public sale for the first time.
  • Geography: Miami Gardens is inland, northwest Miami-Dade, roughly 30 minutes from Miami Beach and downtown in normal traffic.

Source: Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix promoter, f1miamigp.com, checked October 30, 2026.

Where to stay for the race

The Autodrome sits in Miami Gardens, an inland suburb, so no hotel is at the circuit gates and every stay involves a planned trip in. The convenience play is Miami Gardens itself and nearby Aventura: short drives, early arrivals, easy exits. The trip play is downtown, Brickell, or Miami Beach: a 30 to 45 minute run to the stadium in race traffic, repaid with the full Miami weekend around the race, dinners, beaches, and nightlife the Gardens cannot offer. Race weekend is a peak pricing event, and the stock near the stadium goes first; book as soon as your tickets are in hand. The hotel guide above weighs each area honestly, including the race day timeline each one implies. Race fans stretching the trip into a full Florida week will find the season's other anchors in the festivals guide, from February's Miami International Boat Show to the Coconut Grove Arts Festival.

Make a full weekend of it

The three days reward different plans. Friday is the value day: practice sessions, a campus still finding its rhythm, the shortest lines, and the best light for photographs of the cars. Treat it as a scouting day, walk the whole site, and learn your gates and grandstands before the crowds arrive. Saturday pairs qualifying, the most tactical hour in the sport, with a free evening: head back toward the city for dinner (the restaurant guides cover Brickell, Wynwood, and the Beach) and an early night. Sunday is the long day: arrive in the morning for the build up and the full campus program, settle in well before the start, and let the crowd leave first when it ends; the hour after the checkered flag is the slowest traffic of the year in Miami Gardens, and the campus keeps feeding and entertaining people who are in no hurry. If your flights allow it, take Monday slow: a beach morning is the correct decompression from a Grand Prix weekend, and the season guides will argue early May is one of the finest weeks to be in this city anyway.

Getting there, in one paragraph

There is no train to Hard Rock Stadium, so race weekend runs on wheels: prebooked parking in the stadium lots, organized rideshare zones, and shuttle services from hubs around the county, all of which the promoter details as the weekend approaches. The one rule that matters is to decide your plan before you arrive and book the parking or shuttle with the ticket, not after. The traffic and parking guide is the deep version, timelines included.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Miami Grand Prix 2027?
Friday April 30 to Sunday May 2, 2027, with the race on Sunday, May 2, at the Miami International Autodrome in Miami Gardens. Source: f1miamigp.com.

Where is the race held?
On a temporary circuit built around Hard Rock Stadium, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens, in northwest Miami-Dade. It is not in Miami Beach or downtown.

Do tickets sell out?
The promoter states every edition from 2022 through 2026 sold out. General sale for 2027 opened September 24, 2026. Buy through official channels only.

What is new for 2027?
An expanded Paddock Club extending toward Turn 1, offered on public sale for the first time, per the promoter.
