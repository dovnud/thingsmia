Miami Art Week News

Last reviewed: November 1, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026. Updated November 1, 2026.

The confirmed core. Art Basel Miami Beach runs December 4 to 6, 2026 at the Miami Beach Convention Center (artbasel.com), and the citywide Art Week builds around it. This post tracks what is officially set for the 2026 edition and what is still pending, as of November 1, 2026.

Art Basel Miami Beach: December 4 to 6, 2026

The fair's published dates are December 4 to 6, 2026, with the preview days for invited guests ahead of the public run, following the fair's established structure (artbasel.com). The 2026 edition again anchors at the Miami Beach Convention Center in the Collins Park cultural district, steps from The Bass and the public library, which makes the beach side walkable in a way the mainland sprawl is not. Our Art Basel hub carries the visitor program, and the fair guide covers tickets and timing.

The satellite fairs: same week, own dates

Art Week's gravity pulls in the established satellite fairs, including Art Miami, Design Miami, Untitled Art, NADA Miami, and the debuting InScope, from the team behind the former Scope fair, which cluster in the same first week of December. Each fair has now published its own 2026 dates and venues, and our Miami Art Week fair planner compares them fair by fair, with ticket notes and three walkable day routes. The pattern that does not change: Wynwood and the Design District run their own gallery and brand programming the same week, and the causeways between the beach and the mainland become the week's bottleneck, which is why our traffic and parking guide exists.

Hotel and flight pressure: the standing warning

Art Week is the tightest inventory week of Miami's year. The confirmed fair dates have been public long enough that the best located rooms at sane rates are already the scarce resource; our South Beach guide and the Art Basel hub lay out the base options from Collins Park outward. Book the room before the fair program drops; the program never changes which bed you need.

What is not yet published

Updated November 1, 2026: Art Basel has published its full 2026 exhibitor list, 272 exhibitors with the sector counts carried in our fair guide, including Zero 10's Generation presentation of 19 projects by 21 galleries and platforms, and the satellite fairs have confirmed their dates and venues, compared in the fair planner. Still to come: the city's Art Week traffic and parking plan, published closer to the week, and the museums' extended hours. Items land here and in the weekly roundup as they are officially posted, not before.

The museum layer times itself to the week

Miami's museums build their year around Art Week: PAMM, the Institute of Contemporary Art in the Design District, The Bass on the beach, and the Wolfsonian time major exhibitions and extended hours to the fair week, following the city's established Art Week pattern. For visitors, that means the museum day you might skip in another month becomes a headline act in December, and it is the cheapest ticket in the week's economy. Our museums guide maps the institutions; the fairs' and museums' own pages carry the 2026 exhibition details as they publish them.
