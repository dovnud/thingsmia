Miami Weekly Roundup

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

What this post is. A standing weekly roundup of the Miami visitor news that can be verified against an official source: city, county, airport, port, venue, or organizer. Items are dated, and anything we could not verify is left out rather than repeated. This edition covers the state of play as of October 30, 2026.

Stone crab season is open (October 15, 2026)

Florida's stone crab season opened October 15 and runs to May 1, 2027 (Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission). The first claws are on menus across the county, with the famous rooms at their busiest in opening weeks. Our food culture post explains the one claw harvest rule that keeps the fishery sustainable.

Next up: Miami Book Fair, November 15 to 22, 2026

Miami Dade College's Book Fair is the next major date on the calendar (miamibookfair.com), a week of author events ending in the downtown street fair. Our Book Fair guide has the logistics, and the November post places it in the month's trip math.

Art Week dates are set: Art Basel, December 4 to 6, 2026

Art Basel Miami Beach runs December 4 to 6, 2026 at the Miami Beach Convention Center (artbasel.com), with the satellite fairs and the citywide Art Week around it. Hotel inventory for that week is the tightest of the year; our Art Basel hub and traffic and parking guide are the planning tools.

PortMiami posts a record year

PortMiami handled 8,564,151 cruise passengers in fiscal year 2025, a port record (Miami Dade County, PortMiami). The winter season now building is the port's busiest, and Royal Caribbean ships are temporarily using the new MSC Terminal AA while Terminal G is rebuilt. Details in our PortMiami cruise news post and the port guide.

Standing notice: New Year's Eve at Bayfront Park

As of October 30, 2026, the City of Miami has not officially announced its Bayfront Park New Year's Eve program for December 31, 2026. Our New Year's Eve guide says so plainly and will be updated the moment the city publishes the program. We flag this because older listings online describe past years as if they were this year's plan.

Stadium calendar: January is loaded

Hard Rock Stadium hosts the Orange Bowl College Football Playoff semifinal on January 14, 2027 (orangebowl.org), and the Miami Open returns March 14 to 28, 2027 (miamiopen.com). The full stadium picture is in our stadium event news post.

Inter Miami's first Freedom Park season is under way

Inter Miami CF is playing its first season at Nu Stadium at Miami Freedom Park, the club's permanent home next to Miami International Airport, which opened on April 4, 2026 with the inaugural match against Austin FC (Inter Miami CF). The stadium seats 26,700, and the surrounding district continues to build out in phases. Visiting supporters should use the club's match day transport guidance; the full stadium picture is in our stadium event news post.

How this roundup works

Every item above traces to an official source named in the text, checked October 30, 2026. Rumors, unsourced openings, and recycled listings from prior years are excluded by rule. The roundup refreshes weekly; dated items stay dated.
