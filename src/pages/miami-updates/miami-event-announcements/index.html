Miami Event Announcements

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

What this post tracks. Officially announced Miami event dates for the season ahead, each traced to its organizer. When an organizer has not announced, this post says so rather than repeating last year's dates. Current as of October 30, 2026.


The season ahead runs from downtown street fairs to beachfront festival weeks. Photo: Pexels.

Miami Book Fair: November 15 to 22, 2026 (announced)

Miami Dade College has published the 2026 Book Fair dates (miamibookfair.com): a week of ticketed author events downtown closing with the free street fair weekend. Our Book Fair guide covers the program structure and downtown logistics.

Art Basel Miami Beach: December 4 to 6, 2026 (announced)

Art Basel has published December 4 to 6, 2026 for the Miami Beach fair at the Convention Center (artbasel.com), with preview days for invited guests ahead of the public dates, per the fair's usual structure. The satellite fairs cluster in the same week; each posts its own dates. Plan with the Art Basel hub and the traffic and parking guide.

Orange Bowl, CFP semifinal: January 14, 2027 (announced)

The Orange Bowl's 2027 game is a College Football Playoff semifinal on Thursday, January 14, 2027 at Hard Rock Stadium (orangebowl.org), with the bowl's festival events around it. Our Orange Bowl guide covers tickets, parking, and game week.

Winter sports and shows: February and March 2027 (announced)

The Miami International Boat Show runs February 10 to 14, 2027 (NMMA), the Coconut Grove Arts Festival February 13 to 15, 2027, SOBEWFF February 25 to 28, 2027, and the Miami Open March 14 to 28, 2027 at Hard Rock Stadium (miamiopen.com). Each has a guide on the events hub.

Not yet announced: Bayfront Park New Year's Eve 2026

The City of Miami has not published its December 31, 2026 Bayfront Park program as of this post. Our New Year's Eve guide states that plainly and will update on the city's announcement. Listings elsewhere that present a 2026 Bayfront lineup are recycling earlier years.

Announced by the festival, flagged for confirmation: Ultra 2027

Ultra Music Festival's announced 2027 dates are March 26 to 28 at Bayfront Park; the festival's own site is the confirming source and Music Week programming builds around it. See the March post for the month's full stack.

Miami Marathon: January 31, 2027 (announced)

The Life Time Miami Marathon and Half Marathon runs Sunday, January 31, 2027 (organizer, Life Time), on the sunrise course across downtown, the causeways, and Miami Beach. Road closures run through the morning across the course; spectators and airport bound drivers should plan around them. Our Marathon guide covers vantage points and the closure pattern.

Also in the January cluster: Art Deco Weekend

The Miami Design Preservation League's Art Deco Weekend takes over Ocean Drive each January with tours, classic cars, and street programming. The league publishes the edition's exact dates on its site; the festival belongs on any January itinerary built around the district, alongside our Art Deco history post. January's full stack, with the Orange Bowl semifinal on January 14, is laid out in the January post.
