Miami Stadium Event News

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

The picture. Miami's stadium calendar for the season ahead is anchored by three confirmed dates: the Orange Bowl semifinal and the Miami Open at Hard Rock Stadium, and Inter Miami's first full season at its new home, Nu Stadium at Miami Freedom Park. Here is the verified stadium state as of October 30, 2026.

Nu Stadium at Miami Freedom Park: Inter Miami's new home

Inter Miami CF opened its permanent home on April 4, 2026, with the inaugural match against Austin FC at the new stadium in Miami Freedom Park, adjacent to Miami International Airport (Inter Miami CF). The stadium seats 26,700 and carries the Nu Stadium name under a naming rights deal with Nubank announced in March 2026. The wider Freedom Park district (retail, offices, and parkland) continues to build out in phases through 2028, so match day surroundings are still evolving; allow time, and use the club's published transport guidance. For visiting fans, our getting around guide covers the airport area logistics.

Orange Bowl CFP semifinal: January 14, 2027, Hard Rock Stadium

The Capital One Orange Bowl is a College Football Playoff semifinal in the 2026 season cycle, played Thursday, January 14, 2027 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens (orangebowl.org). Playoff status makes this the biggest college football date in the stadium's year: tickets, parking, and hotel demand behave like a national final. Our Orange Bowl guide is the planning document.

Miami Open: March 14 to 28, 2027, Hard Rock Stadium

The Miami Open presented by Itau returns to the Hard Rock Stadium campus March 14 to 28, 2027 (miamiopen.com). The tennis campus built into and around the football stadium remains the tournament's long term home. Day sessions are the value play; the March post prices the fortnight against the month's other festivals.

Miami Grand Prix: Hard Rock campus, spring 2027

Formula 1's Miami Grand Prix runs on the Miami International Autodrome, the temporary circuit built around Hard Rock Stadium. Recent editions ran on the first weekend of May; Formula 1 and the organizer publish the confirmed 2027 date, and this post will carry it when they do. The stadium campus's ability to host a playoff semifinal, a tennis fortnight, and a grand prix in five months is the story of Miami's stadium era.

loanDepot park: the baseball anchor

The Marlins' loanDepot park in Little Havana remains the summer stadium, with the retractable roof that guarantees start times through the rainy season. It hosted World Baseball Classic games in March 2026, the tournament's championship rounds having made Miami their recurring home in recent editions (MLB and the tournament). Regular season planning runs through our events hub rather than this post.

The World Cup summer just past

Hard Rock Stadium comes into this season off the biggest summer in its history: it hosted FIFA World Cup matches in June and July 2026 as one of the tournament's US venues (FIFA). The tournament run is the reason the stadium's operations, from transport to crowd flow, are as polished as they currently are, and it sets up the venue's next cycle: the January semifinal, the March tennis fortnight, and the spring Grand Prix covered above, all on the same campus in Miami Gardens.
