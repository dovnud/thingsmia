Miami Updates: What Changed for Visitors

Guides tell you how Miami works; this section tells you what just changed. Miami Updates tracks openings, closures, new event dates, cruise and airport news, and City of Miami Beach notices, on running pages that stay current instead of expiring. The section begins publishing as the site launches, and every item that appears here will be dated and linked to its source, so you can always tell what is current and what has already moved on.

The running pages, ready for launch

  • Miami Weekly Roundup: The series home for the weekly roundup: the week's openings, changes, and notices that affect a Miami trip, in one dated post.
  • New Restaurant Openings in Miami: A running page tracking the notable restaurant openings visitors will actually hear about.
  • New Hotel Openings in Miami: New and newly renovated hotels visitors can book, tracked as they open.
  • Miami Event Announcements: New dates, on sale announcements, and lineup news for Miami's big event weekends.
  • Miami Closures and Changes Visitors Hit: Closures, moves, and renamings that catch visitors out, tracked on one page.
  • PortMiami and Cruise News: Terminal changes, new ships homeporting in Miami, and sailing schedule news from PortMiami.
  • MIA Airport News for Travelers: Changes at Miami International Airport that matter to arriving and departing visitors.
  • Miami Beach Conditions and City Notices: City of Miami Beach notices, beach conditions, and seasonal rule changes.
  • Miami Stadium and Arena Event News: New event announcements for Hard Rock Stadium, Kaseya Center, and loanDepot Park.
  • Miami Art Week News: Art Basel and Miami Art Week announcements as they land through the year.
  • Spring Break City Notices, Miami Beach: The City of Miami Beach's spring break rules and notices for the current season, updated each year.

Frequently asked questions

When does Miami Updates start publishing?
As the site launches. The running pages above are the section's structure; dated entries begin appearing on them at launch, starting with the weekly roundup.

Do you publish rumors about openings and closures?
No. An update appears here when the change is confirmed by the business, the organizer, or the city, and the entry links to that source. That is the same standard as the rest of the site, set out in our editorial policy.

How do I report a change you have missed?
Email info@thingsmia.com or use the contact page. Verified corrections and changes are exactly what this section exists for.

Where can I check Miami Beach rules right now?
The beach conditions and city notices page in this section tracks the City of Miami Beach's current rules, and the spring break notices page carries the seasonal measures each year.
