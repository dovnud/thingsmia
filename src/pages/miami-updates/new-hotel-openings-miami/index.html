New Hotel Openings in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

Short version. Miami's next hotel wave lands in 2027, not this quarter: the Shore Club's return as an Auberge resort and Aman's Faena District debut are the two confirmed anchors, both officially slated for 2027. This post separates confirmed openings from announced intentions, and recaps the notable 2025 opening that changed the mid beach market.


The next openings cluster on the beach: two luxury restorations headline the confirmed 2027 list. Photo: Pexels.

Confirmed: The Shore Club, Auberge Resorts Collection (2027)

Witkoff and Monroe Capital's redevelopment of the Shore Club site at 1901 Collins Avenue pairs a roughly 140 room Auberge hotel with branded residences, in a project whose completion is slated for 2027 (developer announcements, 2025). The design team announced for the project includes Robert A.M. Stern Architects for the new residential tower and Kobi Karp for the restoration of the historic hotel buildings, including the 1949 Cromwell. When it opens, it returns one of Collins Avenue's landmark names to the market. Our hotels hub will profile it when rates and dates are bookable.

Confirmed: Aman Miami Beach (2027)

Aman's first Florida property restores the Versailles building in the Faena District at 3425 Collins Avenue, pairing hotel suites with private residences. Aman's official materials carry a 2027 expected opening. It is the ultra luxury end of the market: expect the smallest room count and the highest rates on this list, and expect details to stay scarce until the brand publishes them.

Status flagged: Delano Miami Beach (Ennismore)

The Delano, closed since 2020, is being restored under Ennismore, which announced a reopening targeted at early 2026. As of October 30, 2026, we have not verified a completed opening or bookable inventory, so this post lists it as in progress rather than open. The distinction matters for anyone planning around it: do not build a trip on the Delano until the hotel itself sells the rooms.

Opened in 2025: Andaz Miami Beach

The year's notable beach opening was Hyatt's conversion of The Confidante into Andaz Miami Beach at 4041 Collins Avenue, which opened in 2025 (Hyatt). It brought a lifestyle brand rate tier to mid beach below the Faena and Setai level, and it is the freshest hardware in that stretch. It is profiled across the South Beach area guides context of the mid beach market.

What this means for booking now

For trips before 2027, the confirmed news is that nothing on this list changes your booking: the current market is the market. For Art Week and peak season trips that book a year out, the 2027 openings matter for late 2027 travel, not before. The beachfront guide ranks what is actually bookable today.

The Delano, in context

The Delano's long closure explains the caution in the item above. The hotel, built in 1947 and relaunched in 1995 as the property that arguably invented the modern boutique hotel lobby scene, closed in March 2020 and has been dark since, an entire pandemic era gap on one of Collins Avenue's most famous blocks. Its return under Ennismore, an Accor lifestyle brand operator, is one of the most watched restorations in American hotels. Watched is the operative word: until the property publishes rates and takes reservations, this post keeps it in the pipeline column, and the weekly roundup will carry the opening when it is real.
