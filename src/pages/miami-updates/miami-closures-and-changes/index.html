Miami Closures and Changes

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

What this post tracks. Confirmed Miami closures and material changes that affect a visitor's plans, each with its source. Permanently closed means confirmed closed, not rumored. Current as of October 30, 2026.


Most of Miami's closures this year are not on the beach. The sand is fine; two famous names are gone. Photo: Pexels.

Closed permanently: Miami Seaquarium (October 2025)

The Miami Seaquarium on Virginia Key closed permanently in October 2025, ending a 70 year run that began in 1955. Miami Dade County terminated the operator's lease after years of animal welfare findings, and the county has been evaluating the site's future. Any listing, ticket seller, or itinerary still offering the Seaquarium is out of date. Families should route to Frost Science's aquarium instead; our museums guide makes the swap, and the kids guide has been updated to match.

Closed permanently: Santa's Enchanted Forest

Santa's Enchanted Forest, the vast Christmas carnival that ran for four decades, most famously at Tropical Park, will not return; its permanent closure was confirmed in 2025 after its final seasons at a Medley site. Holiday planning that still points families there is recycling old listings. Miami's working Christmas lights anchor is now Christmas Wonderland at Tropical Park, covered in our Christmas in Miami guide.

Renamed and moving: HistoryMiami is now the Museum of Miami

The county's history museum, long known as HistoryMiami Museum, now presents itself as the Museum of Miami (formerly HistoryMiami Museum), with relocation plans beyond its downtown cultural center home discussed in its announcements. Practical effect for visitors: search results and maps under either name point to the same institution, and hours and location should be confirmed on the museum's current site before a visit, as our museums guide flags.

Under construction: Royal Caribbean's Terminal G, PortMiami

PortMiami's Terminal G has been demolished for a full rebuild as a new Royal Caribbean terminal, expected in 2027 (PortMiami and Royal Caribbean announcements). In the meantime, Royal Caribbean ships are using the new MSC Terminal AA on sailing days. Passengers should follow their cruise line's terminal instructions rather than any printed guide, including ours; the port guide carries the current terminal map logic.

Seasonal closure: Venetian Pool, Coral Gables

A standing seasonal notice, not news: the City of Coral Gables closes the Venetian Pool for the cooler part of the year and on weather calls in season. It is the closure most likely to ambush a winter itinerary built from summer articles. Check the city's pool page before planning a Gables day around it; our hidden gems post builds in the check.

What happened to the Seaquarium's animals and site

Under the county's transition after the lease termination, the Seaquarium's remaining animals were transferred to other accredited facilities, a process the county and federal inspectors oversaw through the 2025 closure. The Virginia Key site's long term future, including public proposals for its reuse, remains undecided in county process as of this post; no replacement attraction has been approved or announced. Visitors driving the Rickenbacker Causeway will still see the property and its landmark dome; it is closed, fenced, and not bookable at any price, whatever older ticketing pages suggest.
