New Restaurant Openings in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

The honest headline. As of October 30, 2026, no new Miami restaurant opening with an officially published opening date has been announced for the coming weeks that we can verify. Miami restaurant news runs on rumor and soft openings; this post only prints openings confirmed by the restaurant, its group, or its landlord, and it is updated as confirmations land. Here is what is verifiably in the pipeline, and what just changed on the scene.


Most of the season's dining energy is in Miami Beach, where two major hotel projects bring new restaurants in 2027. Photo: Pexels.

Confirmed pipeline: The Shore Club's restaurant program (2027)

The redevelopment of the Shore Club on Collins Avenue into an Auberge Resorts Collection hotel and residences, a project of Witkoff and Monroe Capital, is slated for completion in 2027 (developer and Auberge announcements). The project includes a restaurant and beach club program on the historic site. No restaurant name or chef has been officially announced as of this post, and we will not guess at one.

Confirmed pipeline: Aman Miami Beach (2027)

Aman's Miami Beach property in the Faena District, in the restored Versailles building, carries a 2027 expected opening on the brand's official materials. Aman properties anchor their dining around the brand's restaurants; the Miami venues have not been officially detailed yet. This is a hotel opening with dining attached; see the companion post on hotel openings.

The season's real opening: stone crab rooms, from October 15

The closest thing Miami has to a scheduled restaurant season is stone crab: from October 15, 2026 (FWC), the city's seafood rooms run their crab menus through May 1, 2027. It is not a new opening, and labeling it honestly matters more than a thin list. Our restaurants hub tracks where the claws are served.

Recent era anchor: Elastika at The Moore, Design District

Not new this season, and labeled as such: The Moore's opening in the Miami Design District (2024) brought Elastika, the restaurant under the Zaha Hadid sculpture that gives it its name, plus a private club and small hotel. It remains the district's dining anchor and the reference point for what a confirmed, operating opening looks like versus a rendering and a promise.

What we are watching, without dates

Several announced projects carry restaurants without official dates, including the Delano Miami Beach restoration under Ennismore, whose reopening timeline has been stated as early 2026 by the brand and has not been confirmed as complete as of this post. When a date is officially published, the item moves into the confirmed list above with its source. Until then, it stays here, clearly labeled.

How an opening becomes confirmed here

Miami openings arrive in stages: a signed lease in a trade paper, a liquor license application, a friends and family service, a soft opening with a limited menu, and finally a published opening date with full hours. Only the last stage counts as confirmed on this page, because the earlier stages slip by weeks or die quietly, and a visitor planning around them gets burned. When a restaurant on your list announces its date through its own channels, it will appear here with that date and that source; until then, the restaurants hub lists rooms that are open and serving today, which is the list a trip can actually use.
