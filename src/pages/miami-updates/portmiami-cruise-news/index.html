PortMiami Cruise News

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

By the ThingsMIA Desk, October 30, 2026.

The headline. PortMiami closed fiscal year 2025 with 8,564,151 cruise passengers, the most in its history (Miami Dade County, PortMiami). The winter season now under way is the port's peak, with the world's largest cruise terminal in operation and a second mega terminal under construction. Here is the verified state of the port for the season ahead.


PortMiami in its record era: the ships are bigger, the terminals newer, and the passenger counts keep setting records. Photo: Pexels.

Record year: FY2025 passenger numbers

PortMiami's fiscal 2025 count of 8,564,151 passengers rose about 4 percent over the prior year (PortMiami, October 2025 release). The port regularly describes itself as the Cruise Capital of the World, and the 2025 numbers support the claim: more passengers embarked here than at any other port. For travelers, the record translates to full ships and busy terminals on winter weekends; our pre cruise guide is built for exactly that.

Terminal AA: the world's largest cruise terminal

MSC Cruises' Terminal AA, which opened in April 2025, is the largest cruise terminal in the world at about 490,000 square feet, able to process up to 36,000 passengers a day across as many as three ships (Miami Dade County). Its biometric embarkation flow is the fastest at the port. If your sailing is on MSC or, this season, on Royal Caribbean, this is likely your terminal; the port guide maps the terminals and the parking.

Terminal G rebuild: Royal Caribbean's temporary home

Royal Caribbean's old Terminal G has been demolished and is being rebuilt as a new terminal expected in 2027 (PortMiami and Royal Caribbean). During construction, Royal Caribbean ships berth at Terminal AA. The practical instruction is simple and comes from the port: follow your cruise line's embarkation documents for terminal and parking, because assignments during the rebuild are operational, not permanent.

The season ahead

Winter is PortMiami's peak: the newest ships in the industry homeport here for Caribbean seasons, and Saturday and Sunday turnarounds stack up. Two standing recommendations from the port's own guidance and ours: arrive in Miami the day before your sailing (the cruise passenger hotels guide ranks the port adjacent options), and use the MIA to PortMiami transfer guide rather than improvising on embarkation morning.

Shore power and the port's upgrades

PortMiami's shore power program, a county initiative that lets docked ships plug into the grid instead of running engines, is in operation at multiple berths and continues to expand under the port's capital program (Miami Dade County). It does not change a passenger's day, but it is the port's headline environmental project and the reason construction fencing appears around working berths.

The ships behind the numbers

The record counts ride on the industry's newest hardware. MSC World America, MSC's largest ship serving the US market, began sailing from Miami in April 2025 and was purpose matched to Terminal AA (MSC Cruises). Royal Caribbean's Icon class, the largest cruise ships in the world, also homeports in Miami. The pattern for the season ahead is continuity: the big ships stay, the terminals keep filling, and the port's constraint is landside traffic on peak turnaround days, which is a planning note, not a problem, if you arrive the day before as the pre cruise guide insists.
