Checked facts

Miami-Dade County's PortMiami cruise terminals page lists Terminal AA for MSC Cruises, Terminal A for Royal Caribbean International, Terminal B for Norwegian Cruise Line, and Terminal V for Virgin Voyages.

The same county page lists Terminal C for ships from several cruise lines, Terminals D, E, and F for Carnival Cruise Line, and Terminal J for small vessels and luxury cruises. Confirm your terminal with your cruise line, because assignments can change by sailing.

The county's PortMiami directions page states that parking is available for each cruise terminal on the north and south sides of the port, and that most cruise lines offer shuttle service from Miami International Airport.

Terminal G appears on the county page as a future Royal Caribbean terminal, so it is not listed as a current sailing terminal on this hub.

Common questions

How do I know which PortMiami terminal my ship uses?

Start with the terminals by cruise line guide above, then confirm with your cruise line close to sailing. The county lists terminal homes by line, but individual sailings can be reassigned.

Is there parking at PortMiami?

Yes. Miami-Dade County states that parking is available for each cruise terminal on the north and south sides of the port. This hub does not print a rate, because the county text reviewed for this page did not publish one simple rate. The parking guide above is where rates are checked and dated.

How do I get from Miami airport to PortMiami?

The county notes that most cruise lines offer shuttle service from Miami International Airport, and ground transport including shuttles, taxis, and rideshare runs from the airport. The transfer guide above compares the options.

Can I sail from Fort Lauderdale on a Miami trip?

Yes, and many travelers do. Brightline serves MiamiCentral and Fort Lauderdale stations, and the Brightline to Port Everglades guide above covers the station to port leg for a Port Everglades sailing.
