Luggage Storage for Cruise Passengers in Miami

Last reviewed: October 26, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Cruise passengers with hours between disembarking and a flight home store their bags through an online luggage storage network. The two big ones, Bounce and LuggageHero, both keep bags inside local businesses near the port and downtown: Bounce charges a flat daily rate and includes up to $10,000 of bag protection per booking, while LuggageHero lets you pay by the hour with a timer you start yourself and includes a $500 guarantee. Book online before you disembark; neither model works as a walk up with cash.

What are my options on disembarkation day?

Three, realistically. A storage network location near the port or downtown, booked online in a few minutes. A hotel bell desk, if you stayed there the night before the cruise or are staying after; full service hotels commonly hold bags for guests, but it is a policy detail to confirm when you reserve, not a promise. Or your own car, if you drove and parked. What not to plan on is leaving bags at the cruise terminal or the airport: neither publishes a storage service for cruise passengers that this page could verify, so build the day around one of the three options above. The post cruise guide shows how the freed up day can look.

How does Bounce work?

Bounce is the flat rate model. You book and pay online, choose a location, which may be a partner local business such as a shop or hotel, or a locker, and check in by showing your booking QR code at drop off. Bounce's homepage describes a flat daily rate with no limit on bag size, free cancellation any time before your drop off time, and bag protection of up to $10,000 included in every booking. Its homepage advertises daily rates from $6.95 (checked October 26, 2026); that is a global starting figure, and the price for your Miami location and date is the one shown when you book. One detail from its own terms matters: pay online. Bookings paid in cash at the location are not covered by the protection.

How does LuggageHero work?

LuggageHero is the pay for the hours you use model. Its locations sit inside existing businesses, retail shops, hotels, and cafes, which the company says are vetted by a local employee with regular quality checks, and bags are stored away from public access. Rates come in hourly, daily, and multiday forms, and you start the timer yourself when you drop off, so a three hour gap costs three hours rather than a full day. Booking ahead online is required, and payment is online by card only; the shops take no cash, and the coverage only applies when you pay LuggageHero directly. Every booking includes a $500 guarantee, with optional premium insurance up to $3,000 offered through First Marine Insurance, a Lloyd's coverholder. There is no fixed drop off time beyond the location's opening hours on your booked day, and cancellation is free before 9 a.m. on the drop off date.

Which one should I choose?

Match the model to your gap. For a full day out, from morning disembarkation to an evening flight, Bounce's flat daily rate and higher included protection are the simpler fit, and one booking covers the day whatever your timing does. For a short gap of two or three hours, LuggageHero's hourly timer usually wins because you stop paying when you pick up. Either way, choose the location by the shape of your day, near the port if you are heading straight out, or near where you will spend the afternoon if you are finishing downtown or in Miami Beach, and check the location's closing time against your pickup. Both networks live or die on the individual shop's hours.

Frequently asked questions

Can I store bags at PortMiami itself?
This page could not verify any storage service at the terminals, so do not plan on one. Book a network location near the port or downtown instead; both providers list locations by neighborhood at booking.

Are my bags insured while stored?
Covered, within limits, if you follow the model. Bounce includes up to $10,000 of protection per booking, and LuggageHero includes a $500 guarantee with optional premium cover to $3,000. Both require the booking and payment to run through their own platforms; paying a shop in cash steps outside the coverage on both.

Do I have to book in advance?
Yes for LuggageHero, which requires an online booking, and in practice for Bounce too, since the QR check in and the protection both come from the online booking. It takes a few minutes; do it from the ship before you disembark.

What if my plans change?
Both allow free cancellation inside a window: Bounce any time before your drop off time, LuggageHero before 9 a.m. on the drop off date, per their homepages checked October 26, 2026.
