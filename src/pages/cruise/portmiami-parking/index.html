PortMiami Parking Guide: Garages, Rates and Rules

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Parking at PortMiami costs $28 per day in Garages C, D, F and G and in Surface Lot E, and $35 per day in Garages AA and B, at the rates Miami-Dade County published and we checked on October 26, 2026. You pay on exit, the garages are staffed from 6 a.m. to 5 p.m. on cruise days, and debit cards are not accepted, which is the detail that catches more drivers than any other. Match the garage to your terminal before you leave home and the whole job takes ten minutes.

Which garage for which terminal

The port pairs each terminal with parking on the north and south sides of Dodge Island, and the county lists the garages against the terminals they serve. Garage AA serves the MSC terminal it was built with, at the $35 rate. Garage B serves Norwegian's Terminal B, also $35, and it is the one exception to the no reservations rule at the port: Garage B takes reservations through ParkWhiz for Norwegian sailings. Garage A serves Royal Caribbean's Terminal A, and its pricing lives on Royal Caribbean's own parking site rather than in the county table, so check there if you are sailing Royal Caribbean. The $28 garages, C, D, F and G plus Surface Lot E, cover the Carnival terminals and the multi line terminals between them. Whichever you use, write the garage letter and level on your phone before you walk away; the port is bigger than it looks from the curb and every returning passenger is tired.

How payment works, and the debit card trap

PortMiami parking is paid on exit, not on entry. The county's parking page is blunt about one thing: debit cards are not accepted at the port garages. Bring cash or a Visa, MasterCard, American Express or Discover card, and if your one card is a debit card, sort that out before embarkation day rather than at a barrier at 8 a.m. with a line behind you. Oversized vehicles and special event days can be priced differently from the standard daily table, and the county updates its rates page when numbers change, so treat the figures here as checked on October 26, 2026 and glance at the county page again in the week you sail.

Hours, staffing and the free shuttle

The county states the garages are staffed from 6 a.m. to 5 p.m. on cruise days, and a free shuttle runs between port facilities on cruise days only. Arrive inside the arrival window your cruise line assigns rather than at dawn; the garage flow is built around staggered boarding, and the busiest pinch is mid morning when departing passengers are still leaving. If your sailing returns on a morning when several ships turn around together, build extra time into the exit rather than a tight flight, because the barrier lines, not the drive, are what slow people down.

Driving in: the tunnel and the address

Program your navigation for 1015 North America Way, Miami, FL 33132, then follow signs for your terminal letter once you are on Dodge Island. From downtown and the highways, the route in uses the dedicated PortMiami tunnel, which is toll free and keeps cruise traffic off the downtown street grid. From Miami International Airport the port is about 9 miles, typically 15 to 25 minutes by road. Port parking or off port parking?

The honest trade is convenience against price. Parking at the port puts you a short walk or free shuttle ride from your terminal with the county running the facility, and for a seven day cruise the $28 garages total $196 before taxes and fees. Off port lots near the airport and downtown advertise lower daily rates and run their own shuttles, which can save real money on a long sailing and cost you time and certainty on both ends. Our cruise parking alternatives guide works through that comparison properly. Whichever you pick, screenshot your booking or your garage ticket stub; disputes are much easier with the paperwork in hand.

The facts that matter

Frequently asked questions

Can I reserve PortMiami parking in advance?
Only in one case. Garage B, serving Norwegian's Terminal B, takes reservations through ParkWhiz. Everything else at the port is first come, paid on exit.

Why was my card declined at the garage barrier?
Most likely it was a debit card. Miami-Dade County states debit cards are not accepted at PortMiami garages. Cash and the major credit networks listed on the county page are.

How much is a week of parking at PortMiami?
At the rates checked on October 26, 2026, seven days is $196 in the $28 garages and $245 in Garages AA and B, before any taxes or special event pricing.

Is there EV or accessible parking at the port?
The county's parking information page covers accessible spaces and facility details by garage. Check it for your specific garage in the week you sail, because assignments can change.
