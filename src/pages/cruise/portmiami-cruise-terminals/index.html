PortMiami Cruise Terminals by Cruise Line

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami-Dade County assigns each PortMiami terminal a home cruise line: AA is MSC, A is Royal Caribbean, B is Norwegian, C serves several lines, D, E and F are Carnival, J handles small and luxury ships, and V is Virgin Voyages. Terminal G is a future Royal Caribbean terminal expected in 2027, not a sailing terminal yet. Use this page to aim your driver and pick your garage, then confirm against your boarding documents in the final week, because individual sailings do get reassigned.

The newest: Terminal AA (MSC) and Terminal A (Royal Caribbean)

Terminal AA, opened in 2025 for MSC Cruises, is the port's flagship: the county describes it as the largest cruise terminal in the world, 490,000 square feet, able to handle up to three ships and 36,000 passengers a day, with its own Garage AA at the $35 daily rate. Next door in spirit, Terminal A is Royal Caribbean's Crown of Miami, opened in 2018 and built for the Oasis class ships that made Miami the cruise capital. Royal Caribbean parking at Garage A is priced through Royal Caribbean's own site rather than the county table. If your ship is one of the biggest in the world, you are almost certainly in this pair.

Terminal B (Norwegian) and Terminal C (several lines)

Terminal B, the Pearl of Miami, is Norwegian Cruise Line's home, a glass heavy terminal opened for Norwegian's newest ships, with Garage B at $35 a day and the port's only reservable parking, booked through ParkWhiz for Norwegian sailings. Terminal C is the port's flexible space, serving ships from several lines depending on the day's schedule, which makes the boarding document check doubly important for anyone assigned there: your line's app and documents name the terminal, and the port's signage finishes the job once you are on Dodge Island.

Terminals D, E and F (Carnival) and the small ship end

Carnival's three terminals, D, E and F, total 471,000 square feet combined per the county and handle the highest frequency of sailings in the port, which is why the $28 garages cluster around them. At the other end of the scale, Terminal J is the boutique terminal for small vessels and luxury lines, where boarding is a smaller, calmer affair. Terminal V, opened in 2022, is Virgin Voyages' home, styled to match the brand and serving its adults only ships. Between them, the port can turn around a remarkable number of ships on a Saturday, which is exactly when its roads and garages feel it.

What your terminal changes in practice

Three things follow from the letter. Your garage: the parking pairings and rates are in our PortMiami parking guide. Your drop off curb: each terminal has its own, so a driver needs the letter, not just the port name, and rideshare apps ask for it. And your timing: the mega terminals process thousands of people through staggered arrival windows, so your assigned window matters more at A, AA and the Carnival complex than anywhere else in the port. Arrive in your window and even the biggest terminal feels orderly.

Confirming your terminal, the reliable way

Guides like this one give you the home assignments. Your sailing's truth lives in two places: your cruise line's app or boarding documents after online check in, and the electronic signage on Dodge Island on the day. Check the documents in the final week, again the night before, and tell your driver the letter as well as the line. If a reassignment happens, it is an inconvenience measured in a five minute drive along the port road, not a crisis, as long as you learn it before you unload the bags.

The facts that matter

Terminal homes per Miami-Dade County, checked October 26, 2026: AA MSC Cruises (opened 2025; 490,000 square feet; up to three ships and 36,000 passengers a day), A Royal Caribbean (opened 2018), B Norwegian Cruise Line, C several lines, D, E and F Carnival (471,000 square feet combined), J small and luxury vessels, V Virgin Voyages (opened 2022). Terminal G is a future Royal Caribbean terminal, expected 2027. Parking: $28 per day Garages C, D, F, G and Lot E; $35 Garages AA and B; Garage A priced via Royal Caribbean.

Frequently asked questions

Which terminal does Carnival use at PortMiami?
Terminals D, E and F are Carnival's home terminals at PortMiami. Your specific sailing's terminal is confirmed in your boarding documents.

Is Terminal G open yet?
No. The county lists Terminal G as a future Royal Caribbean terminal expected to open in 2027. Royal Caribbean sails from Terminal A today.

Can my sailing be moved to a different terminal?
Yes, assignments can change by sailing. The county list gives home terminals; your line's documents in the final week are the authority.

Where do I park for Terminal AA?
Garage AA serves the MSC terminal at $35 per day, at county rates checked October 26, 2026. The full garage pairings are in our parking guide.
