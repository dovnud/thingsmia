Things to Do Near PortMiami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The port sits on Dodge Island directly across the water from downtown, so the near port map is the downtown map. Bayside Marketplace and Bayfront Park are the easy walk off the ship options once you are landside, the Frost Science museum and the Pérez Art Museum anchor Museum Park a few minutes north, the Kaseya Center sits on the bayfront, and Jungle Island and the Miami Children's Museum are on the causeway you cross to Miami Beach. With a full post cruise day, Wynwood and Little Havana are each a short drive. Match the plan to your hours and your luggage situation, which our luggage storage guide solves first.

Right at the bayfront: Bayside and Bayfront Park

Once you are off the island and downtown, Bayside Marketplace is the default cruise passenger landing zone: open air shops, casual restaurants, boat tour docks and a steady supply of mojitos, a few minutes' drive from the terminals. It is tourist infrastructure and knows it, and for a two hour gap with bags stored it does the job well. Bayfront Park next door is the greener version of the same hour: bay views, the Torch of Friendship, and space for kids to burn off ship energy. Both sit on Biscayne Boulevard directly facing the port, so the taxi back is short and predictable.

Museum Park: Frost Science and PAMM

Ten minutes north up the boulevard, Maurice A. Ferré Park holds the two museums that turn a post cruise day into a genuinely good day. Frost Science combines a planetarium, an aquarium built over multiple levels and rotating exhibitions, and it absorbs families for three hours without effort. The Pérez Art Museum Miami next door is the calm version: modern and contemporary art in a Herzog and de Meuron building with hanging gardens and bay terraces. Either one is a better use of a late flight day than an airport lounge, and both are air conditioned in the way only Miami museums understand.

Watson Island: Jungle Island and the Children's Museum

The MacArthur Causeway toward Miami Beach crosses Watson Island, where Jungle Island and the Miami Children's Museum sit minutes from the port. Jungle Island is the animal park in a tropical garden setting, good for a half day with younger kids. The Children's Museum is the rainy day insurance policy: two floors of hands on exhibits aimed squarely at under tens. If your post cruise day ends at Miami Beach, both are literally on your way.

With four or more hours: Wynwood and Little Havana

A half day opens the two neighborhoods that define Miami beyond the beach. Wynwood, about a 10 to 15 minute drive from the port, is the mural district: the Wynwood Walls compound and streets of large scale work around NW 2nd Avenue. Little Havana, a similar drive west, is Calle Ocho: cigar rollers, cafecito windows, domino park and the food our Little Havana food tour guide walks through. Both pair well with a late flight, and both are planned properly in our Wynwood and Little Havana guides. Guided versions of all of it, including Everglades runs for long days, are bookable through the tour link below.

What not to attempt on a port day

Geography is the limit. The Everglades airboat corridor is a half day commitment with real drive time; do it on a proper day, not between a ship and a flight. Miami Beach is close in miles and far in causeway traffic on turnaround mornings; go only with hours in hand. And anything that needs your passport or your ship's worth of luggage stays off the list until both are sorted. The facts that matter

Everything on this page sits within roughly a 15 minute drive of the port in normal traffic: Bayside Marketplace and Bayfront Park on the downtown bayfront, Frost Science and the Pérez Art Museum at Museum Park, the Kaseya Center on Biscayne Boulevard, Jungle Island and the Miami Children's Museum on Watson Island, and Wynwood and Little Havana just beyond. Causeway and turnaround day traffic can double those times; the estimates here are planning figures, not promises.

Frequently asked questions

Can I walk from the cruise terminal to Bayside?
Not sensibly. The port is on an island built for vehicles. Take a taxi or rideshare off Dodge Island; the drive is only a few minutes.

Where do I leave my bags for a post cruise day?
Cruise lines, storage services near the port and downtown, and some hotels all take bags for a fee. Our luggage storage guide compares the options.

Is there enough near the port for a full day?
Yes. A museum in the morning, lunch at Bayside, and Wynwood or Little Havana in the afternoon fills a late flight day without rushing.

What is closest with small children?
The Miami Children's Museum and Jungle Island on Watson Island, both minutes away, with Frost Science close behind for mixed ages.
