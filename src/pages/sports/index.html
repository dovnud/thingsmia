Miami Sports for Visitors

Miami is a full spectrum sports city, and a visitor can build a trip around it in any month: the Heat downtown, the Dolphins and Hurricanes in Miami Gardens, Inter Miami in their new home by the airport, the Marlins under a roof in Little Havana, tennis at the Miami Open, and Formula 1 each spring. This hub is the visitor's map: every team, its stadium, and the ThingsMIA guide that gets you to your seat. One honesty rule runs through all of it: the Florida Panthers play in Sunrise, in Broward County, not in Miami, and every Panthers reference on this site says so, because visitors have driven to the wrong county on game night before.

Miami's teams and homes at a glance

  • Miami Heat (NBA): Kaseya Center, 601 Biscayne Boulevard, downtown Miami. 19,600 seats.
  • Miami Dolphins (NFL) and Miami Hurricanes (college football): Hard Rock Stadium, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens.
  • Inter Miami CF (MLS): Nu Stadium at Miami Freedom Park, by Miami International Airport. Opened April 4, 2026; 26,700 seats.
  • Miami Marlins (MLB): loanDepot park, Little Havana, a retractable roof ballpark west of downtown.
  • Florida Panthers (NHL): Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise, Broward County, roughly a 45 minute drive northwest of downtown Miami. Not in Miami.
  • Miami Open tennis: Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens, March 14 to 28, 2027 (dates listed as subject to change).
  • Miami Grand Prix (Formula 1): Miami International Autodrome at Hard Rock Stadium, April 30 to May 2, 2027.

Sources: team and venue publications, checked October 30, 2026.

How Miami sports fit a trip

The seasons interlock kindly for visitors. Fall and winter bring the full house: Dolphins and Hurricanes football, Heat basketball, Panthers hockey up in Sunrise, and the Orange Bowl in January. Spring is the showcase season: the Miami Open and spring training trips up the coast in March, the Grand Prix closing April into May. Summer belongs to the Marlins' air conditioned roof and Inter Miami under the lights. The great new fact of Miami sports is Nu Stadium: Inter Miami's permanent home at Miami Freedom Park opened on April 4, 2026, a 26,700 seat soccer stadium beside Miami International Airport, replacing the club's temporary years in Fort Lauderdale. It is now genuinely easy to pair a match with a flight. If you are building a sports trip, the pattern is to anchor on one stadium event, add a second team in the same week, and let the festival calendar fill the days between. February stacks especially well: a game weekend can share the trip with the Miami International Boat Show and the Coconut Grove Arts Festival. Tickets, the honest version

Every Miami team sells through its own official channels and the major primary sellers, and the rules that protect you are the same as for concerts: start from the team's own site, use the official resale exchange when a game sells out, and never trust a screenshot of a mobile ticket, because every Miami stadium and arena runs on live mobile entry. Prices are dynamic across the board now, so a Tuesday Marlins game and a Saturday Heat game are different species of purchase; the value in Miami sports is real if you hunt it, in weekday baseball under the roof, the first rows of the Heat's upper bowl, and early season Inter Miami fixtures before the bandwagon prices them. Frequently asked questions

Where do the Florida Panthers play?
In Sunrise, Florida, at Amerant Bank Arena, in Broward County, roughly a 45 minute drive northwest of downtown Miami. Not in Miami, despite the name; plan the drive.

Where does Inter Miami play now?
At Nu Stadium in Miami Freedom Park, beside Miami International Airport. The 26,700 seat stadium opened on April 4, 2026 and replaced the club's temporary home in Fort Lauderdale.

Which stadium hosts the most events?
Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens: Dolphins and Hurricanes football, the Miami Open tennis, the Orange Bowl, and the Miami Grand Prix circuit around it.

What is the easiest ticket in Miami sports?
Marlins baseball at loanDepot park, with the roof guaranteeing the game plays, and Heat basketball for the best arena night out downtown.
