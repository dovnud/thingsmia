About ThingsMIA

ThingsMIA is an independent Miami travel guide, built by the team behind ThingsVegas. We cover Miami hotels, tours, restaurants, beaches, cruises from PortMiami, events, and neighborhoods, with practical guides written for visitors planning a real trip.

ThingsMIA is not affiliated with the City of Miami, Miami-Dade County, the Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau, PortMiami, or any hotel, venue, cruise line, or tour company we write about.

How we work

Every guide is researched from primary sources, checked before publication, and carries a last reviewed date. When we earn a commission from a booking or a purchase made through a link on this site, the price you pay does not change. See our Affiliate Disclosure and Editorial Policy for the full details.
