Frequently asked questions

What is different about packing for Miami versus another beach city?
Three things: the rain, which can arrive fast in the summer months; the cruise factor, since many Miami trips start or end at PortMiami and need an embarkation day bag; and the heat, which rules out half the fabrics that work elsewhere. The packing guides here are built around those three.

How do you choose the products on these pages?
By comparing specifications, materials, sizes, and verified owner feedback for the Miami use case, and by saying plainly who a pick does not suit. Where a buying guide has limits, the page states them rather than pretending every product was tested in person.

Are the prices on these pages exact?
No, and treat any site that promises exact prices with suspicion. Prices move; each guide was checked on its stated review date, and the live price is always the one at checkout.
