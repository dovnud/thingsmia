Miami Bachelorette Essentials

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. A Miami bachelorette runs on three stages, the beach or boat day, the big dinner, and the club night, and the kit that matters is the one that survives all three: sun protection that gets reapplied, footwear for both sand and dance floors, a shared waterproof pouch system for the boat, and the morning recovery supplies that save day two. This guide organizes the shopping by stage, in the order the weekend actually happens.

Stage one: the beach and boat day

Matching swimsuits and sashes are the photograph; the kit underneath is the substance: reef considerate sunscreen from our sunscreen guide in enough quantity for a group, a big mesh beach bag per our bags guide, waterproof phone pouches for every attendee, and a cooler stocked beyond water into electrolytes. Boat days add motion sickness supplies for the one friend who swears she never gets seasick. The group that packs collectively, one person on medical and recovery, one on sun, one on logistics, never duplicates and never lacks.

Stage two and three: dinner and the club

Miami dinners run late and clubs later; our LIV guide and beach clubs guide set the venues. The kit: a mini flat iron or styling tool where the hotel lacks one, blister care because Miami heels meet Miami walking distances, a portable charger shared on rotation from our chargers guide, and small crossbody bags that satisfy both club dress codes and the security logic in our anti theft guide. Matching outfits photograph best in one strong color against Miami's pastel Deco backdrop; the bride in white is the classic for a reason.

Recovery: the day two system

The groups that enjoy day two pre bought it: electrolyte packets, real breakfast plans, ibuprofen and the rest of a basic kit, eye masks and earplugs for the light sleepers in a shared suite, and a slow plan, brunch and the pool, rather than a second expedition. Build recovery into the itinerary when you build the itinerary, not at 3 a.m. Our spa guide is the upgraded version: a Standard Spa morning has saved more bachelorette Sundays than any brunch.

Group logistics nobody glamorous

One shared note with the hotel address, the dinner reservation and the boat meeting point. One person holding the group fund in a payment app. A rideshare plan for club closing time, when surge pricing meets low batteries. And a small emergency kit in the bride's bag: safety pins, fashion tape, a spare phone cable. Unsexy, and exactly the difference between a smooth weekend and a group chat full of apologies.

The facts that matter

Buying guide only: no brands, prices or ratings. Venue and booking advice reflects the nightlife and beach club guides on this site; check dress codes and reservation terms with the venue for your dates. Sunscreen and recovery supplies are comfort goods, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most forgotten bachelorette essential?
Recovery supplies and a second day plan. Everyone packs for night one; the groups that thrive packed for the morning after.

Matching outfits: yes or no?
One coordinated moment, usually the boat or beach day, photographs best. Three days of matching is a logistics tax.

How do we handle money in a group?
One fund holder, one payment app, reservations prepaid where possible. Settle nightly in the group chat to avoid the airport reckoning.

Beach club or boat for the big day?
Both work; the boat gives privacy and photos, the club gives service and zero planning. Our beach clubs guide compares the club side honestly.
