Waterproof phone pouch and dry bag minis

The single most used beach gift on this page. A waterproof phone pouch keeps a phone shooting photos on the boat and safe on the sand, and the small dry bag versions swallow keys, cards, and cash for kayak and airboat days. They pack to nothing, they suit every age, and they get used on day one of the trip. Our beach packing list shows where they sit in a well packed Miami beach bag.

Reef safe sunscreen, in stick and travel sizes

Sunscreen is a boring gift until it is the one thing between a Miami December and a burned first day. Stick formats and small tubes are the stocking friendly versions: no leaks in a carry on, easy reapplication on the beach, and reef safe formulas are the responsible default for Biscayne Bay snorkelling and Key Largo day trips. Pair it with the beach safety guide for the sun and water rules that matter in Miami.

Portable charger and cable kit

Travel days eat batteries: boarding passes, rideshares, photos, and a phone doubling as the tour ticket. A slim portable charger and a short braided cable are the pocket sized fix, and they are the stuffer most likely to be borrowed by the whole family before the flight home. For cruise travelers sailing from PortMiami, this pairs with the kit in our cruise packing list.

Shop portable chargers on Amazon

Microfiber beach towel and sand free mat minis

A packable microfiber towel is the upgrade every beach bag needs: it dries fast, sheds sand, and rolls down to a fraction of a hotel towel, which is why it works as a stocking stuffer at all. It is also the rare stuffer that earns its place on boat days, pool days, and the flight home as a spare layer.

Shop microfiber beach towels on Amazon

Sunglasses, straps, and the small stuff that saves a day

Polarized sunglasses, a floating strap for boat days, a hard case that survives a beach bag, and luggage tags in colours you can spot on the MIA carousel: individually small, together they are the difference between a smooth beach week and a string of small losses. This is the category to fill the last space in the stocking.

The Miami flavored finish: cafecito and key lime

End the stocking with Miami itself: small bags of Cuban style coffee for the home cafecito ritual, and key lime candies or cookies for the person counting down to a Florida trip. Both travel well, both are unmistakably South Florida, and both point at the bigger gift in our Christmas gifts for Miami lovers guide: the trip itself.
