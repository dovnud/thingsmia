Beach gear for the Miami regular

The useful end of the gift market: a serious beach towel that shakes sand off, a dry bag for boat days on Biscayne Bay, reef safe sunscreen for the winter swim, and a tote that survives both the beach and the flight home. If your person has a Miami trip booked, this is the category that gets used first and thanked longest. Our beach packing list shows what a well packed Miami beach bag actually holds.

Cuban coffee for the cafecito devotee

Miami runs on cafecito, and the home version is a small ritual: a stovetop cafetera, Cuban style coffee ground fine, demitasse cups, and the patience to whip the first drops with sugar into espuma. As a gift it lands twice, once when it is opened and again every morning after. Pair it with a read through our Little Havana food tour guide if a trip is part of the plan.

Miami art and photography books

The coffee table category: photography books of the Art Deco district and Ocean Drive, Wynwood street art collections, aerial Miami and Everglades photography, and architecture books on the Miami Modern style. These are the gifts that keep a Miami obsession visible in the house all year, and they suit the person who already owns enough stuff. The Art Basel hub is the companion read for the art minded recipient.
