Perez Art Museum Miami Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Perez Art Museum Miami, known as PAMM, is Miami's flagship modern and contemporary art museum, in a striking waterfront building by Herzog and de Meuron at 1103 Biscayne Boulevard in Museum Park. Come for international art from the 20th and 21st centuries, the hanging gardens and the Biscayne Bay terraces, and pair it with Frost Science next door if you have a full day.

What kind of museum is PAMM?

PAMM collects and shows modern and contemporary international art, with exhibitions that highlight Miami's position at the crossroads of the Americas and the city's diverse communities. That means the galleries mix painting, sculpture, installation and video, with a permanent collection alongside rotating special exhibitions, so two visits in different seasons can feel quite different. The building is part of the visit in its own right: deep shaded verandas, hanging gardens suspended from the canopy, and wide steps down toward the bay make the museum function as a public living room for Museum Park as much as a gallery box. The Verde restaurant gives you waterfront dining on site, and the shop leans toward art books and design objects rather than generic souvenirs.

Tickets, hours and the free evening

PAMM is at 1103 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami, FL 33132. Current visitor information checked October 30, 2026 lists the museum open Monday 11 am to 6 pm, Thursday 11 am to 9 pm, and Friday to Sunday 11 am to 6 pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday. General admission is $18 for adults and $14 for seniors age 62 and up, students with ID, and youth age 7 to 18; children age 6 and under enter free. Admission is free for everyone on Thursdays after 5 pm, which is the single best value tip on this page: the late Thursday opening until 9 pm turns PAMM into an easy early evening plan. Tickets can sell out at busy times, so book ahead for weekends and major exhibition openings. The Museum Park Metromover station puts you steps from the entrance.

How to visit the galleries

Start with any special exhibition, because those are the shows with closing dates and the longest lines later in the day, then work through the collection galleries at a slower pace. Give yourself permission to sit: the terraces and bay views are part of the museum's rhythm, and a coffee break overlooking Biscayne Bay resets your attention better than pushing through every room at once. If you are visiting with children, ask at the desk about current family programming rather than expecting a children's museum; PAMM is an art museum first, and younger visitors do best with a shorter, focused route plus time outside on the steps and in the park. Allow about two hours for a satisfying visit, longer during a major exhibition.

PAMM or Frost Science: how to choose

The two Museum Park museums share a park and a transit stop but do different jobs. PAMM is quieter, visual and idea driven, best for adults, teens with an interest in art, and travelers who want a cultural anchor to a downtown day. Frost Science is louder, hands on and built around families, with an aquarium and a planetarium show included in its ticket. With one day and mixed ages, many groups split the difference: Frost Science in the morning while energy is high, PAMM in the afternoon, and the free Thursday evening at PAMM if your trip includes a Thursday. Our separate Frost Science guide covers that plan from the other side.

Building a downtown day around PAMM

Museum Park sits between downtown and the bay, so PAMM anchors a walkable cluster: the park itself, Bayside Marketplace a short walk south, and the downtown and Brickell restaurants beyond. Arrive by Metromover and you avoid downtown parking prices entirely; if you do drive, the museum lists on site garage parking at a flat rate, with a member discount. Do not schedule PAMM on a Tuesday or Wednesday out of habit, because it will be closed, and do not leave the Thursday free evening unused if your dates allow it. It is the rare case where the cheapest way to visit is also one of the most enjoyable.

Frequently asked questions

When is PAMM free?
Admission is free for everyone on Thursdays after 5 pm, during the late opening until 9 pm. Members and several other categories, including active military and veterans with ID, also receive free admission.

How long does PAMM take?
About two hours for the galleries at a comfortable pace, plus time on the terraces. Major special exhibitions can add another half hour or more.

Is PAMM good for young children?
It can work with a short, focused route and time in Museum Park outside, but it is not a children's museum. Families with younger children usually get more from Frost Science next door.
