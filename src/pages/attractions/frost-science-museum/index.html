Frost Science Museum Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Phillip and Patricia Frost Museum of Science, known as Frost Science, is a hands on science museum, aquarium and planetarium in one building at 1101 Biscayne Boulevard in Museum Park. One Explorer Ticket covers the exhibitions, the aquarium and one planetarium show. It is the strongest indoor family attraction in central Miami, and it needs three to four hours done properly.

What is inside Frost Science?

The museum stacks several attractions into one waterfront building on multiple levels. The aquarium is built over three levels, viewed from above, at the surface and from below, and it centers on a large Gulf Stream tank holding sharks, rays and other open water fish; visitor guides put that tank at 500,000 gallons. Around it sit hands on science exhibitions covering the human body, engineering, light, flight and South Florida ecosystems, plus changing special exhibitions. The Frost Planetarium runs immersive full dome shows, and one show is included with your Explorer Ticket, reserved on site on the day on a first come, first served basis. That last detail shapes your whole visit plan, so read the timing section below before you go.

Tickets, hours and booking ahead

Frost Science is at 1101 Biscayne Boulevard, Miami, FL 33132, in Maurice A. Ferre Park, also known as Museum Park. The official Plan Your Day page checked October 30, 2026 lists Explorer Tickets starting at $29.95 for adults and $24.95 for youth age 4 to 11, with children age 3 and under free and members free. Pricing is dynamic and varies by the day you choose, so the price for your date is the one shown on the official ticket calendar, not a fixed number printed anywhere else. The museum opens daily at 10 am; current guides list closing at 5 pm Monday to Thursday and 6 pm Friday to Sunday, so confirm your date on the official site. Seniors age 62 and up receive $5 off Explorer Tickets at the ticket center, and first responders, active military and veterans receive free general admission with ID, per the official visitor tips. Buy online in advance, especially for weekends and school holidays.

The visit order that works

Arrive at opening. Your first job inside is not an exhibition; it is reserving your planetarium show at the planetarium kiosk or on your phone, because show seats are allocated on site and popular times fill. Then ride up and work the aquarium from the top level down, so you see the Gulf Stream habitat from the surface before you stand beneath it. Save the hands on floors for after the aquarium, when crowds have spread out, and take the outdoor terraces as your breaks: the building's bay views are genuinely good and give children reset time between galleries. The museum itself advises that a full visit runs three to four hours, which matches reality. A two hour visit means choosing: aquarium plus planetarium, or exhibitions plus planetarium, but not everything.

Frost Science with children, by age

For toddlers, the value is in the aquarium viewing windows, the water and nature exhibits and the planetarium visuals, in short bursts with terrace breaks. Primary school children are the museum's sweet spot, with enough reading, building and experimenting to fill the full visit. Teens do best when you let them lead on the science floors and the special exhibition rather than dragging them through every gallery in order. Whoever you bring, pack light: you will walk and stand for hours indoors, and downtown parking and transit both reward a small bag. The Museum Park Metromover station is steps away, and on site garage parking is listed at an $18 flat rate, with a member discount, on the official page.

Frost or PAMM, and rainy day value

Frost Science shares Museum Park with PAMM, and the honest split is simple: Frost for families and hands on science, PAMM for art and a quieter visit. Because Frost is almost entirely indoors, it is also the best bad weather insurance in downtown Miami, which is exactly why it gets busy on rainy days; book ahead when the forecast turns. Pair it with Bayside Marketplace for an easy meal afterward, or with PAMM on a Thursday, when PAMM opens late and entry is free after 5 pm. Do not pair Frost with Zoo Miami on the same day. Both deserve a full, fresh visit, and the cross town drive in between helps neither.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Frost ticket include the planetarium?
Yes. The Explorer Ticket includes museum exhibitions, the aquarium and one Frost Planetarium show, subject to availability and reserved on site on the day of your visit.

How long does Frost Science take?
Three to four hours for the full museum, per the museum's own visitor guidance. Shorter visits should prioritize the aquarium and one planetarium show.

Why does the ticket price change by date?
The museum uses dynamic pricing. The official Plan Your Day page lists starting prices, and the exact price for your visit appears when you select a date on the official ticket calendar.
