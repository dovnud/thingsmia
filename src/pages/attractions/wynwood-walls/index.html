Wynwood Walls Visitor Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Wynwood Walls is Miami's original outdoor street art museum, a ticketed, landscaped campus of large murals, sculptures and indoor galleries at 2516 NW 2nd Ave in the Wynwood district. It was founded in 2009 by Tony Goldman. Allow one to two hours, buy the simple ticket, and then keep walking, because the surrounding Wynwood streets are part of the experience.

What exactly is Wynwood Walls?

Think of it as a curated outdoor museum rather than a random graffiti stop. Inside the gates you get a rotating set of large scale murals by artists from around the world, sculptures set into planted courtyards, and indoor gallery space showing contemporary urban art. The official site describes general admission as self guided, and each ticket includes a complimentary spray painting experience, which is a useful detail for families: there is something to do with your hands, not only walls to photograph. The campus is compact and flat, so the visit itself is easy walking. The harder part is the Miami sun, because most of the art is outdoors with limited shade at midday.

Tickets, hours and the address

Wynwood Walls is at 2516 NW 2nd Ave, Miami, FL 33127. On the official admissions page checked October 30, 2026, hours are listed as 10:30 am to 6:30 pm daily, and admission is $12 for adults age 18 and up, $5 for youth age 13 to 17, $10 for seniors age 65 and up, and free for children age 12 and under. Hours can shift for private events and seasonal programs, so check the official site on the morning of your visit. Metered street parking fills fast on weekends; the Wynwood Garage on NW 26th Street is the calmer option if you are driving, and rideshares drop you right at the Welcome Center entrance.

How to do the visit well

Go early or late in the day. Morning light is kinder for photos, crowds are thinner, and the heat is manageable. Work the outdoor walls first, then step into the galleries when you need air conditioning, then finish with the spray painting experience so paint and timing do not rush the art. The free Wynwood Walls app, noted on the official FAQ page, adds an interactive map and guided tour material in three languages, and it helps because murals change: a wall you saw online last year may have been painted over, which is the point of a living street art museum. If you want context rather than just color, book the official guided tour upgrade at the Welcome Center.

What is around it in Wynwood?

Do not treat the Walls as a drive in, drive out stop. The blocks around NW 2nd Avenue carry more murals on warehouse walls, plus galleries, breweries, bakeries and design shops that make Wynwood a half day district rather than a one hour attraction. A sensible route is Walls in the morning, lunch in the neighborhood, then a slow walk up NW 2nd Avenue and across to NW 5th Avenue to see the uncurated street work. Keep expectations straight: the art outside the gates is free and rougher, the art inside is paid, maintained and interpreted. Most visitors who enjoy street art want both halves.

Who should skip it, and common mistakes

Skip it if you need a fully indoor, air conditioned museum day; PAMM and Frost Science in Museum Park do that job better. The common mistakes are arriving at noon in summer with no water, assuming children will only look (use the spray experience and the app), and budgeting thirty minutes. Thirty minutes buys you photos, not a visit. Also note that Wynwood Walls is a separate venue from the nearby Museum of Graffiti, and the official site states they are not affiliated, so buy the ticket for the place you actually intend to enter.

Frequently asked questions

How long does Wynwood Walls take?
One to two hours for the ticketed campus alone. Add at least another hour if you walk the surrounding Wynwood streets, which most visitors should.

Is Wynwood Walls worth paying for when street art outside is free?
For most visitors, yes. You pay for the curated large murals, the galleries, upkeep and the spray painting experience. The free streets around it complement the visit rather than replacing it.

Is Wynwood Walls good for children?
Yes, especially older children. Children 12 and under enter free as listed on October 30, 2026, and the spray painting experience gives younger visitors something active to do.
