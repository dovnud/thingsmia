Checked facts

Wynwood Walls describes itself as an open air street art museum founded in 2009 by Tony Goldman, with murals and sculptures by more than 130 artists from over 35 countries. Its address is 2516 NW 2nd Ave, Miami, FL 33127.

Vizcaya was built between 1914 and 1922 as the winter home of James Deering, opened as a public museum in 1953, and includes 10 acres of formal gardens. It is a National Historic Landmark at 3251 South Miami Avenue.

The City of Coral Gables states that the Venetian Pool was created in 1923 from a coral rock quarry and opened in 1924, and holds 820,000 gallons of spring water fed from an underground aquifer.

Common questions

Which attractions should first time visitors start with?

Wynwood Walls for art, Vizcaya for history and gardens, and one Museum Park stop, PAMM or Frost Science, covers a strong first trip. The guides above group naturally into those three days.

Where is Wynwood Walls?

At 2516 NW 2nd Ave, Miami, FL 33127, in the Wynwood district. Check the official site for current hours before you go, since hours change by season and event.

Is the Venetian Pool a hotel pool?

No. It is a historic public pool run by the City of Coral Gables, created from a coral rock quarry and fed by spring water from an aquifer. Entry rules and capacity limits apply, so read the guide before you go.

How many big attractions fit in one day?

Two is realistic, three is a stretch once Miami traffic is counted. Pair sights in the same area, such as PAMM and Frost Science in Museum Park, and give Zoo Miami or Vizcaya the space they need.
