Miami in January: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. January is Miami at its best and its most expensive: the driest, most comfortable weather of the year, the Orange Bowl semifinal and the Miami Marathon on the calendar, and peak season hotel rates. If budget matters more than perfection, come in the quieter weeks between the events. If weather matters most, this is your month.

The January events that matter

Capital One Orange Bowl, College Football Playoff semifinal, Thursday, January 14, 2027, Hard Rock Stadium (orangebowl.org). The 2027 game is a playoff semifinal, which lifts it from a bowl trip to a national event: book flights and rooms early, and expect game week pricing near the stadium and downtown. Our Orange Bowl guide covers tickets and game day logistics.

Miami Marathon and Half Marathon, Sunday, January 31, 2027 (organizer, Life Time). The course runs downtown, the causeways, and the beach at sunrise; road closures ripple across the morning, so spectators should plan vantage points and drivers should plan around it. See the Marathon guide.

Art Deco Weekend, Ocean Drive (Miami Design Preservation League). The district's own festival of tours, classic cars, and street programming runs each January; the league posts the edition's dates on its site, and our Art Deco history post is the primer. The South Beach Jazz Festival and the Pegasus World Cup at Gulfstream Park in Hallandale Beach also land in January in recent editions; check the organizers for the 2027 dates.

January weather in Miami

Long term National Weather Service averages for Miami in January: highs around 76 degrees, lows around 60, and the lowest rainfall of any month, under two inches on average. Cold fronts pass every week or so and can drop a morning into the 50s; locals call it winter and visitors call it perfect. The ocean is at its coolest of the year, swimmable for most visitors and brisk for thin blooded ones. Pack one real layer for evenings and for the marathon start line.

Hotel prices and availability in January

January is peak season: the year's highest baseline rates, with hard spikes around the Orange Bowl and marathon weekend. The softest January value sits in the first week after the New Year holiday and in the gap weeks between the big events. The resort fees guide matters most this month, because peak rates plus fees plus parking stack quickly.

Best January trip shapes

Event trip (3 to 4 days). Base downtown or in Miami Beach for the game or the race, add a museum day and a beach morning, and book everything early. Weather escape (5 to 7 days). The classic snowbird week: beach mornings, the Everglades on the coolest day (mosquitoes are at their lowest in the dry season), and one day trip from the day trips guide. Shoulder steal (midweek). Tuesday to Thursday between events is as cheap as January gets.

One perfect January day

Sunrise on Miami Beach while the marathon crowds are still a week away, coffee at a ventanita, then the Art Deco District mid morning when the light rakes across the stucco and the tour groups are thin. Cross the causeway for a museum afternoon at PAMM, where the terrace catches the bay breeze, and time the Rickenbacker Causeway for sunset with the Brickell skyline turning gold. Dinner in the Grove or the Gables, where January patios are the whole point of the month. If your day is race Sunday instead, flip it: watch the leaders on the MacArthur Causeway at dawn, brunch while the course clears, and take the beach in the afternoon when the front runners are home and the roads reopen. January's gift is that every hour of that plan works in short sleeves, with the single layer you packed riding along for the evening.

Frequently asked questions

Is January a good month for Miami?
It is the best weather month of the year and one of the priciest. If you can only come once and weather is the priority, January is the answer.

Can you swim in Miami in January?
Yes. The Atlantic is at its coolest, around the low 70s most years, and beach days are the norm between fronts.

What should I pack for January?
Beach kit plus one warm layer for front mornings and air conditioned interiors. Rain is unlikely but a light shell covers it.
