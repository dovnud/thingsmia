Miami Museums Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami has two museums a visitor should treat as anchors, the Perez Art Museum Miami (PAMM) for art and Frost Science for families, and then a long tail of specific, characterful museums that reward picking by interest rather than by fame. This guide compares the real options, says who each one suits, and is honest about which ones are a half day and which ones are an hour.

The two anchors

Perez Art Museum Miami (PAMM), 1103 Biscayne Blvd. The city's flagship art museum sits on Biscayne Bay in Museum Park, in a Herzog and de Meuron building whose hanging gardens and shaded verandas are half the experience. Inside, the collection covers modern and contemporary art of the Americas, the Caribbean, and the African diaspora, which makes it the one museum that feels specifically like Miami rather than anywhere. Plan two to three hours, and step out on the terrace for the bay view. It pairs naturally with a bayfront day downtown.

Frost Science (Phillip and Patricia Frost Museum of Science), 1101 Biscayne Blvd. Directly next door, Frost Science combines an aquarium built around a 500,000 gallon Gulf Stream tank you view from below, a planetarium, and hands-on science floors. It is the strongest rainy day or hot day option in the city and the automatic pick with children. Book timed tickets ahead on weekends. Our Miami with kids guide works it into a family day.

The houses and the history

Vizcaya Museum and Gardens, 3251 S Miami Ave. A 1916 Italian Renaissance style villa on the bay in Coconut Grove, with formal gardens, a stone barge in the water, and interiors preserved as the estate of industrialist James Deering. Vizcaya is as much architecture and garden as museum, and it is the single most beautiful ticket in Miami. Go in the morning before the heat and the wedding shoots build. It anchors the attractions side of the site for a reason; see the attractions hub for how it compares.

HistoryMiami Museum, downtown. The Smithsonian affiliate covering South Florida history, from Tequesta artifacts to the Pan Am era to the Mariel boatlift. It is the museum that explains how this improbable city got here, and its guided tours of Little Havana and the Miami River are led by historians. If you read one history museum, this is it. Note that the museum has been planning a move and rebrand in recent years, so check its official site for the current location and hours before you set out.

The Wolfsonian FIU, 1001 Washington Ave, Miami Beach. A design museum in the heart of the Art Deco District, built around the decorative arts and propaganda of 1850 to 1950. It is the thinking visitor's Art Deco stop, and it pairs perfectly with our Art Deco history post and the Art Deco walking tour.

The specialists

The Bass, 2100 Collins Ave, Miami Beach. Contemporary art in a 1930s Art Deco former library, small enough to do well in ninety minutes, with a park and the beach outside. Superblue Miami, Allapattah. Large scale immersive installations you walk through; book ahead, it sells out on weekends, and combine it with the Wynwood art day on the Wynwood art tour. Coral Gables Museum. Architecture and the planned city story of Coral Gables, in the old police and fire station. The Black Archives Historic Lyric Theater, Overtown. The cultural anchor of historic Overtown, worth the stop for anyone tracing Black Miami. Gold Coast Railroad Museum and the Dezer Collection suit transport obsessives and families respectively, and both sit south, near Zoo Miami.

How to choose, honestly

One museum day: PAMM plus Frost Science share a park and a Metromover stop, so they are the efficient pairing. Art lovers: PAMM, The Bass, and Wynwood Walls across two days. History lovers: HistoryMiami and Vizcaya. Families: Frost Science first, then the Children's Museum on Watson Island. Rain plan: Frost Science or PAMM, both downtown and transit served. Prices move and free or discounted days rotate (several museums run monthly free family days), so treat any price printed anywhere as a check the official site before you go figure rather than a promise.

The facts that matter

Frequently asked questions

What is the one museum not to miss?
For art, PAMM. For the pure Miami experience, Vizcaya. They answer different questions, and both earn it.

Are Miami museums good for kids?
Frost Science is built for them, and the Miami Children's Museum on Watson Island is the under ten specialist. PAMM runs family programming on select Saturdays.

How many museums can you do in a day?
Two comfortably, if they are neighbors. Museum fatigue is real by the third, and Miami rewards an afternoon reset by the water.
