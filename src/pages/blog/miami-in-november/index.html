Miami in November: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. November is the season's soft opening: the Miami Book Fair, the first reliably dry and pleasant weather since April, Thanksgiving week demand, and Art Basel close enough to book around. Rates sit between summer value and winter peak, which is exactly the point.

The November events that matter

Miami Book Fair, November 15 to 22, 2026, Miami Dade College, downtown (miamibookfair.com). One of the largest literary festivals in the country: a week of author events closing with the weekend street fair. It fills downtown hotels with readers and makes the neighborhood feel like a campus. Our Book Fair guide covers the program and the logistics.

Thanksgiving week, November 23 to 29, 2026 (Thanksgiving Day is Thursday, November 26, 2026). The first big family travel week of the season: beach weather returns, rates spike for the long weekend, and the holiday lights switch on across the county, the start of the season our Christmas in Miami guide maps. Our Thanksgiving in Miami guide lists the verified Thanksgiving dinners, the Turkey Trot and the North Miami parade for the day itself.

The pre Art Week runway. Galleries and museums open their winter shows through November, the Arsht season hits its stride, and the Sunny Isles Beach Jazz Fest has run in November in recent editions. The Art Basel hub is the forward planner for December's main event.

November weather in Miami

Long term averages: highs around 81 degrees, lows around 67, and rainfall down near three inches as the dry season establishes. Fronts bring the year's most pleasant pattern: a breezy day, a cool morning, then warmth. Hurricane season ends November 30, and November storms affecting Miami are rare in the record. The ocean cools a touch but stays swimmable.

Hotel prices and availability in November

Shoulder pricing with two spikes: Book Fair week lifts downtown, and Thanksgiving week lifts everywhere, especially family sized inventory on the beach. The soft spot is the window between the fair and the holiday, and the first week of the month is the last of the near summer rates. Book Thanksgiving early through the family hotels guide.

Best November trip shapes

Book Fair trip (3 to 4 days). Downtown base, author sessions by day, and the Grove and the Gables in the new dry air. Thanksgiving family week (5 to 7 days). Beach base, a holiday lights evening, and one Everglades day now that the trails are drying; our kids guide sequences it. Pre season steal (4 days, early November). The last cheap week before the calendar turns, with summer rates meeting autumn weather.

One perfect November day

Build it downtown, where November belongs. Coffee and the Book Fair's morning sessions while the streets are still cool, then the street fair itself when it opens, with a bag filling faster than planned. Lunch on the river or in Brickell, then a museum hour at PAMM on the terrace in the first dry air since spring. The late afternoon belongs to the Grove: the Barnacle's hammock and the bay at Coconut Grove in the month's clean light, and dinner in the Grove or the Gables patios that November exists to justify. If your day is a Thanksgiving week day instead, trade the fair for the beach in the morning and the holiday lights after dinner, and forgive the crowds: everyone at the next table is running the same grateful math. November's days are shorter and better, and Miami knows it.

November packing and travel notes

Pack the beach kit and one layer for front mornings; November's swings are gentle but real, and the first dry cold snap can put a sweater on the whole beach for a day. Thanksgiving week travelers should treat MIA like a holiday airport in any American city: earlier arrivals, patience at the international halls, and no tight cruise connections, per the airport guide. Everyone else gets the month's gift: a city dressed for the season at shoulder effort.

Frequently asked questions

Is November hurricane season?
Technically until November 30, but November is historically one of the quietest months for Miami impacts. Standard flexible booking is enough.

How busy is Thanksgiving week?
It is the season's first peak week: book flights and family rooms early, and expect holiday pricing.

Is November good beach weather?
Yes. Highs near 80, low humidity after fronts, and warm enough water. It is the start of the winter beach pattern.
