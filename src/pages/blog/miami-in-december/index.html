Miami in December: Events, Weather, Prices

Last reviewed: November 3, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. December is Miami's best month and its most expensive one. The weather settles into the driest, mildest pattern of the year (daytime highs averaging around 78 degrees), and the calendar stacks Art Basel in the first week, the holiday light season all month, and New Year's Eve at the end. Prices follow the calendar: Art Week and the Christmas to New Year stretch are the peaks, and the days between them are the relative value window. This post walks the month in order, then covers weather and money honestly.

How does the month unfold?

Late November into week one: the season opens. The Miami Book Fair closes out November, and the light season switches on right behind it: Christmas Wonderland at Tropical Park and the NightGarden at Fairchild Garden both open November 12, Luminosa at Jungle Island opens November 13, and Zoo Lights starts November 27 at Zoo Miami.

December 4 to 6: Art Week. Art Basel Miami Beach anchors the biggest week of the Miami year, with traffic to match. If art is not your reason, know that Art Week pricing and crowds touch everything from hotels to restaurants; if it is your reason, the Art Basel hub is the planning start. The same weekend closes with a hometown tradition: the Junior Orange Bowl Parade rolls down Miracle Mile in Coral Gables on Sunday December 6, free to watch along the route.

Mid month: the holiday core. Miami City Ballet's Nutcracker runs December 11 to 27 at the Arsht Center. The Winterfest Boat Parade sails in Fort Lauderdale on Saturday December 12 (a day trip, and labelled as Fort Lauderdale wherever we mention it). Zoo Lights, Christmas Wonderland, Luminosa, NightGarden, and Pinecrest Gardens' Nights of Lights (select nights from December 4) fill the evenings between. One mid month item is not for visitors at all, but it moves hotel prices: the G20 Leaders Summit meets in Doral on December 14 and 15, and its delegations take a block of airport corridor rooms out of the market that week, which is why our G20 summit travel guide tells December visitors to book early and stay east. The full rundown is the Christmas in Miami guide.

December 24 to 31: the peak. Nochebuena on the 24th, Christmas Day on the beach for many visitors, then the run into New Year's Eve: the Bayfront Park countdown tradition downtown, hotel galas on the beach, and bay cruises under the fireworks. One status note we will keep repeating because it matters: the Bayfront Park Trust had not published the December 31, 2026 lineup as of November 3, 2026. The New Year's Eve guide tracks what is known and what is still to be announced.

The January lookahead. Two dates shape December booking decisions. The Orange Bowl is a College Football Playoff semifinal this cycle, played Thursday January 14, 2027 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, so it is a January event, not a New Year one, whatever older guides tell you. And the Miami Marathon runs Sunday January 31, 2027, which pulls runner hotel demand into late January.

What is the weather actually like?

December is when Miami's rainy season is firmly over. The long term climate normals for the month: average high about 78 degrees, average low in the low 60s, and about 2 inches of rain across the month, making it one of the driest months of the year. Read those as averages. Most December days are beach days; a passing cold front can still deliver a windy, genuinely cool day or two, especially late in the month, and locals treat 65 degrees as a winter event. Pack swimwear and one real layer, and you have covered the realistic range. The best time to visit Miami guide puts December in the context of the full year, and the beach packing list covers what to bring.

What do prices do in December?

They peak, twice. Art Week (the days around December 4 to 6) and the Christmas to New Year stretch are the two most expensive windows of the Miami year for hotels, and December 31 is the single priciest night. The relative value sits between the peaks: the weekdays after Art Week crowds leave and before the holiday arrivals land, roughly the second week of the month, still carry peak season weather with somewhat less pressure on rates. Restaurants in the popular districts book out for the holiday weeks, and the bookable experiences (bay cruises on the 31st, the Nutcracker near Christmas, Zoo Lights on weekend nights) sell their best dates early. The working rules are simple: book refundable where you can, lock the dated things first, and do not wait for December prices to drop, because in Miami they do not.

Three ways to shape a December trip

The Art Week trip (4 to 5 days, early month). Base in Miami Beach or Midtown, do the fair and the satellite exhibitions, and add one light display night at Fairchild or Jungle Island once the fairs close. Book through the Art Basel hub.

The family holiday trip (5 to 7 days, mid to late month). Base downtown, in Brickell, or in Coral Gables for the light displays, and run Zoo Lights, Christmas Wonderland, Pinecrest, and a beach day off one hotel. Start with the Christmas guide and our guides to the Loews Miami Beach and Fontainebleau if you prefer the sand.

The New Year trip (3 to 4 days, end of month). Pick your side of the bay and stay there: downtown for Bayfront Park, Miami Beach for the hotel parties. The New Year's Eve guide has the transport logic, and a bay cruise on the 31st is the booking to make first.

Frequently asked questions

Is December a good time to visit Miami?
It is the best weather month of the year and the fullest events month, at peak prices. If budget leads, the same weather pattern usually holds into January outside event weekends.

How warm is Miami in December?
Average highs run about 78 degrees with lows in the low 60s. Most days suit the beach; a cold front can bring a cooler spell, so bring one layer.

What is the most expensive week?
The Christmas to New Year stretch, with December 31 the priciest single night. Art Week in early December is the other peak.

Is the Orange Bowl on New Year's Day?
Not this cycle. The Orange Bowl is a playoff semifinal on Thursday January 14, 2027 at Hard Rock Stadium.
