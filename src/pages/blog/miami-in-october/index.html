Miami in October: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. October is Miami's hinge: stone crab season opens October 15, the heat finally breaks late in the month, rates start climbing out of the summer floor, and Art Week is close enough to feel. The first half is still hurricane season thinking; the second half is the season turning. Time it to the second half if you can.

The October events that matter

Stone crab season opens October 15 (Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission; the season runs to May 1). Opening week is a local festival in all but name: the first claws hit Joe's Stone Crab and the seafood rooms across the city, and our food culture post tells the one claw story behind the season.

III Points, Wynwood. The underground leaning electronic and alternative festival has anchored an October weekend in Wynwood in recent editions (the festival confirms dates each year). It is the thinking counterpart to Ultra's March maximalism.

Miami Carnival. The Caribbean carnival's parade and concerts run over the Columbus Day weekend period in recent editions (Miami Carnival confirms the year's program), a major weekend for the Caribbean diaspora and the most colorful street party of the fall. Halloween closes the month with the Lincoln Road and Wynwood street scenes.

October weather in Miami

Long term averages: highs around 86 degrees, lows around 73, and rainfall around six inches, falling through the month as the dry season approaches. Hurricane risk drops sharply after mid October but the season officially runs to November 30 (NOAA). The first real cold front usually arrives late in the month, and locals treat the first dry, breezy morning like a holiday. The Everglades are still wet and buggy early; by month's end the trails improve fast.

Hotel prices and availability in October

Shoulder pricing, rising through the month: early October still trades near summer value, while the last week starts to price the season ahead, especially on weekends. Stone crab opening and the festival weekends create small, bookable spikes rather than sellouts. It is a good month for the South Beach guide's better addresses at humane rates.

Best October trip shapes

Stone crab trip (3 days, after October 15). Opening week claws, a Grove morning, and the beach in the turning weather. Festival weekend (3 to 4 days). III Points or Carnival as the spine, Wynwood and Little Haiti around it. Season opener week (5 to 7 days, late month). The first dry air, an Everglades day as the water starts to fall, and Art Week anticipation without Art Week prices.

One perfect October day

Late October offers the year's most satisfying Miami day. Start with the beach in the new, drier air, when the summer crowds are gone and the water is still warm; cross to Little Havana for a ventanita lunch and the dominoes at their afternoon best; then take the Everglades' Shark Valley in the golden hours, when the water is dropping, the birds are gathering, and the tram ride runs cool. End where the month began: at a seafood table with the season's first stone crabs, cracked and cold with mustard sauce, eaten within two weeks of the October 15 opener while the whole city is doing the same. That day does not exist in any other month, because it needs the crabs, the front, and the falling water at once, and October is when Miami's three calendars agree.

Halloween, briefly

The month closes in costume: Lincoln Road and Wynwood fill with street Halloween crowds on the night itself, the bars and clubs run their biggest themed weekend of the fall, and family programming runs at Zoo Miami and the gardens in the weekends before. It is a local's holiday more than a visitor draw, which is its charm: no tickets to chase, just a city dressing up as the days finally cool.

Frequently asked questions

Is early October still hurricane season?
Yes, officially until November 30, though the risk curve falls steeply after mid October. Early October trips should still book flexibly.

When do stone crabs start?
October 15, every year, statewide. Opening week at the famous rooms is busy; weekday lunches are the insider move.

Does October feel like fall?
Not until the first front, usually late month. Before that it is late summer with better intentions.

Keep planning

Neighbors: September and November. Then the best time to visit guide and the restaurants hub for the crab run.
