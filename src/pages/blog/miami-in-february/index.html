Miami in February: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. February stacks three of Miami's signature events into four weeks: the International Boat Show, the Coconut Grove Arts Festival, and the South Beach Wine and Food Festival, on top of dry, warm weather. It is the connoisseur's peak month: full prices, full calendar, and the city showing off.

The February events that matter

Miami International Boat Show, February 10 to 14, 2027 (NMMA). One of the largest boat shows in the world spreads across the Miami Beach Convention Center and the marinas, with in water displays at Museum Park and elsewhere. Even non boaters feel it: hotel demand, restaurant waits, and causeway traffic all peak. Our boat show guide has the layout and the workarounds.

Coconut Grove Arts Festival, February 13 to 15, 2027 (festival association). The Grove's art filled Presidents Day weekend: hundreds of artists along the bayfront streets of the city's oldest neighborhood. It overlaps the boat show's final days, so the second weekend of February is the single busiest of the winter. See the festival guide.

South Beach Wine and Food Festival (SOBEWFF), February 25 to 28, 2027 (festival). Four days of chef events, tastings, and beachfront grand tastings that anchor the national food calendar. Tickets for the headline events sell early; the events hub tracks the program.

February weather in Miami

Long term averages: highs around 78 degrees, lows around 62, and rainfall still low, a little over two inches for the month. Fronts still pass but lose their bite as the month goes on. By late February the ocean is warming and full beach days are the default. It is, with January, the safest weather bet on the Miami calendar.

Hotel prices and availability in February

Peak season, and the event stack makes mid February the priciest stretch of the entire year in Miami Beach and downtown: boat show week plus Presidents Day weekend plus the arts festival is a perfect demand storm. The relative value sits in the first week of the month and in Grove and Gables properties once the beach sells through. Book the festival weekends months ahead through the hotels hub guidance.

Best February trip shapes

Food trip (4 days). SOBEWFF as the spine, with ventanita breakfasts and one fritanga lunch from our food culture post between the chef events. Art and boats (3 days). Arts festival mornings in the Grove, boat show afternoons, dinners in Coral Gables. Romance (2 to 3 days). Valentine's Day falls on the boat show's last day in 2027, so the couples play is the week after: our couples itinerary at shoulder crowding.

One perfect February day, and the traffic truth

Start at the Coconut Grove Arts Festival gates at opening, when the artists are fresh and the bayfront is cool, then walk the Grove for lunch before the festival streets peak. Boat show afternoons reward the in water displays at Museum Park, where the same ticket of an afternoon shows you the fleet against the skyline. The traffic truth for the middle weekend: the causeways between the Grove, downtown, and the beach carry festival, boat show, and Presidents Day loads at once, and rides that take twelve minutes in January take forty. Build the day on one side of the bay, cross once, and eat on the side you end on. February forgives the planner and punishes the zigzag, and the month is too good to spend in the back of a car on the MacArthur.

February packing note

Pack for two seasons in one bag: full beach kit for the afternoons, and one real layer for the mornings a front passes, when the same beach reads twenty degrees cooler. Festival days are walking days (the Grove's streets and the boat show's docks both punish dress shoes), and the boat show's in water displays mean boat shoes or clean sneakers if you plan to step aboard. February's sun is stronger than it feels through the dry air; the packing list has the full kit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest February weekend?
The middle one: the boat show, the Coconut Grove Arts Festival, and Presidents Day weekend collide, February 13 to 15 in 2027.

Is February too crowded?
On event weekends, yes, in the best way and the worst way. The first week of February is peak weather without the crush.

Does February book out?
Beachfront and downtown hotels for the event weekends sell early. Grove, Gables, and airport area stock lasts longer.
