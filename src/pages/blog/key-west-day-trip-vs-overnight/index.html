Key West: Day Trip or Overnight?

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Key West is about 160 miles and roughly three and a half to four hours each way from Miami by road, which makes the day trip a long, scenic, doable sprint and the overnight the better trip if you can spare it. The drive down the Overseas Highway is half the experience either way. Decide by your total days: with four or fewer in Miami, day trip it or skip it; with five or more, sleep there.

The day trip, honestly

The math: leave Miami by 7 a.m., cross the 42 bridges of the Overseas Highway (including the Seven Mile Bridge), and roll into Key West late morning. You get the greatest hits: Duval Street, the Southernmost Point buoy photo, the Hemingway Home and its cats, a conch fritter lunch, and Mallory Square's sunset if you stretch it, with the drive back in the dark. Organized tours run this exact loop with the driving done for you; our Key West day trip page covers the options and the timing. The honest cost: eight hours of your day in transit or on a bus, and Key West at its most crowded midday hours, when the cruise ships are in.

The overnight, honestly

Sleeping in Key West buys the island's two best shifts: the evening, when Duval Street turns into the famous, rowdy, wonderful bar crawl and the day trippers are gone; and the early morning, when the roosters own the quiet streets and the light on the conch houses is the photograph everyone wants. It also buys time for the water: a snorkel or sunset sail to the reef, Dry Tortugas by ferry or seaplane if you book the full day, and a proper Key lime pie comparison across at least three bakeries. The honest cost: Key West lodging is expensive, parking is scarce and priced like it, and you spend two half days on the highway instead of one very long one.

The middle options

One night in the Middle Keys. Base in Marathon or Islamorada, the sportfishing capital, and day trip the last hour into Key West. You get the Seven Mile Bridge both ways in daylight, tarpon at Robbie's, and lower room rates. The ferry or flight. The Key West Express sails from Fort Myers, not Miami, so it does not solve a Miami base; flights from MIA to Key West (EYW) run about an hour and price like it. Split the stay. For a week plus trip, three nights Miami and two in Key West is the connoisseur's split, ending the trip in whichever city your flight leaves from. The decision table

Day trip if

You have four or fewer days, you mainly want the postcard sights and the highway, and an early start does not scare you. Book the tour version unless you love driving.

Overnight if

You have five or more days, evenings and mornings matter to you, or the Dry Tortugas is on your list. Book the room before the tour bus books the town.

Skip it if

You have three days and it is your first Miami trip. The Everglades and the bay give you more Miami per hour; Key West deserves its own trip.

The drive, segment by segment

The highway is a chain of keys with personalities: Key Largo for diving and the first conch fritters, Islamorada for sportfishing and tarpon feeding at Robbie's, Marathon for the Turtle Hospital and the Seven Mile Bridge's start, Big Pine for the tiny Key deer, and then the final causeway run into Key West. Even the day trip version should stop twice; the road is the attraction it is because every segment gives you a reason.

The facts that matter

Miami to Key West is about 160 miles via US 1, the Overseas Highway, with 42 bridges including the Seven Mile Bridge (Florida Keys tourism and FDOT materials). Drive time runs three and a half to four hours each way in normal traffic. The Key West Express operates from Fort Myers and Marco Island, not Miami. Details checked October 30, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is the drive itself worth it?
Yes. The Overseas Highway is one of America's great drives, ocean on both sides for long stretches. It is the rare case where the transit is an attraction.

Can you do Dry Tortugas on a day trip from Miami?
Not sensibly. The ferry leaves Key West early; it needs a Key West night before, and ideally after.

When is Key West at its worst?
Midday in cruise season and during Fantasy Fest in late October, when prices and crowds both peak. Mornings and evenings are the fix.
