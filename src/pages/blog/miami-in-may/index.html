Miami in May: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. May opens with the Miami Grand Prix, the loudest weekend of the spring, then exhales into the start of summer: hotter, wetter, and cheaper by the week. Thread it right and May is two trips in one: a grand prix weekend at full Miami volume, or a value beach week once the racing leaves.

The May events that matter

Miami Grand Prix. Formula 1's Miami round runs at the Miami International Autodrome around Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens; recent editions took the first weekend of May, and the 2027 date is confirmed on f1miamigp.com. It is a citywide event: yacht parties, brand takeovers, and hotel pricing that rivals Art Basel week near the circuit and on the beach. Our events hub tracks the weekend's public events.

Memorial Day weekend, May 29 to 31, 2027. The summer season opener, with the Hyundai Air and Sea Show over Miami Beach (the show's Salute to American Heroes runs Memorial Day weekend in recent editions; confirm on usasalute.com) and the beach at full summer strength.

Miami Swim Week and Haitian Heritage Month. Swim Week's shows cluster in late May on the beach (organizers confirm dates each spring), and May is Haitian Heritage Month in Miami Dade, with the Haitian Compas Festival among its anchors in recent editions. Both reward a check of the event announcements post as the month approaches.

May weather in Miami

Long term averages: highs around 86 degrees, lows around 72, and rainfall jumping to five inches or more as the rainy season establishes itself around mid May. The pattern matters more than the totals: bright mornings, towering afternoon build up, a hard short storm, and a golden evening. The ocean is warm, the sand is hot by noon, and shade becomes a planning item.

Hotel prices and availability in May

Two markets in one month: Grand Prix weekend prices like a peak event, then rates fall through the month into the summer value tier, with a Memorial Day bump at the end. If the Grand Prix is not your reason, arrive after it and May is one of the year's pricing bargains, especially midweek. The family hotels guide matters from here on, as school holidays begin.

Best May trip shapes

Grand Prix trip (3 to 4 days). Book the circuit days first, base on the beach or downtown, and treat the city's party layer as the evening program. Value beach week (5 to 7 days, mid or late May). Morning swims, museum afternoons through the storms from the museums guide, and long late dinners. Pre cruise add on (2 days). May is heavy cruise season from PortMiami; the pre cruise guide turns a sailing into a Miami trip.

One perfect May day, after the Grand Prix leaves

Take the beach early, when the summer pattern gives you glassy water and the whole morning's light, and be off the sand by the time the heat stands up. Late morning is for the Redland run: fruit stands at full early summer load, a shake at Robert Is Here, and the cool green of Fruit and Spice Park if the family votes for one more stop. Come back through Coral Gables for a late lunch, then surrender the storm hour to Frost Science or PAMM, where the afternoon rain becomes a show through the glass instead of a problem. The evening is May's reward: the clouds clear, the light goes gold over the bay, and dinner can be the spontaneous kind that peak season never allows, in Brickell or the Grove, at a table you did not have to book in March. That day, at May's post Grand Prix rates, is the value argument for the whole month in one itinerary.

Frequently asked questions

Is May too rainy for Miami?
No. Rain arrives as short afternoon storms, not lost days. Plan water and outdoor time for mornings.

How expensive is Grand Prix weekend?
The year's spring peak: circuit area and beachfront rates multiply, and the good rooms book months ahead.

When do May rates drop?
Right after the Grand Prix, through the month, with Memorial Day weekend the exception.
