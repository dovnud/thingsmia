Frequently asked questions

What does the blog cover that the guides do not?
Stories and comparisons: Miami's food culture, the history behind the Art Deco skyline, hidden corners, and judgment calls like Key West as a day trip versus an overnight. Anything time sensitive, like event dates, lives in the guides and the events hub instead.

How accurate are the month by month posts?
Each monthly post links to the events hub and the guides, where dates are verified against the organizers. The monthly posts describe the pattern of the month; the exact dates for your year are always in the linked guides.

Can I suggest a topic or correct something?
Yes. Email info@thingsmia.com or use the contact page. Corrections go to the top of the pile, per our editorial policy.
