South Beach vs Miami Beach, Explained Simply

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. South Beach is not a separate city or a separate island. It is the southern neighborhood of the City of Miami Beach, roughly the bottom two miles of the barrier island, and "Miami Beach" is the whole city that contains it. Staying in South Beach means the Art Deco District, Ocean Drive, and the scene. Staying elsewhere in Miami Beach means the same sand with less noise and usually less money. Here is the honest breakdown.

The geography, settled

Miami Beach is a city on a barrier island across Biscayne Bay from mainland Miami, running from Government Cut at the south to 87th Terrace at the north, where Surfside begins. South Beach (SoBe) is its southern section, generally understood as everything south of 23rd Street: the Art Deco District, Ocean Drive, Lincoln Road, and the South of Fifth neighborhood. Mid Beach runs from the 20s to about 63rd Street, and North Beach takes the rest. When a hotel markets itself as "Miami Beach" but sits at 60th Street, it is telling the truth and also telling you it is not in South Beach; the distinction is the whole decision.

South Beach: the case for, and against

For: the walkable grid of Deco hotels and cafes, Ocean Drive's neon at night, Lincoln Road's pedestrian mall, the widest and most famous sand, and the densest dining and nightlife in the city. Our South Beach hotels guide and the South Beach beach page cover it in full. Against: the highest rates and resort fees on the island, spring break intensity in March, noise on the Ocean Drive blocks, and parking that punishes cars. South of Fifth, the pocket south of 5th Street, is the exception inside the exception: residential, calm, and home to some of the best restaurants on the island, including Joe's Stone Crab.

Mid Beach and North Beach: the quieter island

Mid Beach holds the grand resort strip: the Fontainebleau and Eden Roc era properties and their modern successors, with bigger pools, bigger lobbies, and a resort rather than street scene. The Faena, Nobu, and Setai profiles show the range. North Beach is the local Miami Beach: the North Beach Bandshell's community concerts, the Normandy and Altos del Mar streets, lower prices, and a residential calm that suits families and longer stays. The sand and water are the same; the trade is scene for space, and walkability for calm.

The real comparison table

Choose South Beach if

You want to walk to dinner, bars, and the Deco District; it is your first trip; you are coming for Art Basel, Pride, or a festival weekend; and the scene is the point.

Choose Mid Beach if

You want a resort with a serious pool deck, a quieter room, and a short rideshare to the scene rather than a bed inside it. Couples and resort first travelers land here.

Choose North Beach if

You want local Miami Beach: family friendly, lower priced, residential, with the bandshell and the beach path, and you do not mind rides to dinner.

The transport truth

The island is about ten miles long and a mile wide at most, and the free Miami Beach trolley plus the beachwalk tie it together better than visitors expect. A South Beach base makes the mainland (downtown, Wynwood, Little Havana) a causeway ride; a north island base makes South Beach the ride instead. Neither is wrong; they are different trips wearing the same beach. The getting around guide lays out the trolleys, causeways, and rideshare math.

The facts that matter

South Beach is the southern portion of the City of Miami Beach, generally south of 23rd Street; the city runs to 87th Terrace (City of Miami Beach). The free Miami Beach trolley serves the island's length (city transit). South of Fifth and the Art Deco District descriptions follow the city's own neighborhood usage, checked October 30, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is South Beach a separate city?
No. It is a neighborhood of the City of Miami Beach, on the same island and under the same city government.

Which is better for families?
North Beach and the mid beach resorts, for calm and space. South Beach works for families who stay off the Ocean Drive blocks; see the family hotels guide.

Where is the party?
South Beach, concentrated on Ocean Drive and the Collins Avenue club blocks. Mid and North Beach sleep earlier.
