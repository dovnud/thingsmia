Miami in August: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. August is Miami's deal month: Miami Spice restaurant season, spa month pricing, the year's lowest hotel rates, and full summer heat with a watching brief on the tropics. If you eat well and book flexibly, August delivers the most Miami per dollar of any month on the calendar.

The August events that matter

Miami Spice, August 1 to September 30. The Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau's restaurant months: hundreds of restaurants across the county offer prix fixe lunch and dinner menus well below their normal check (the bureau posts the year's list and prices each summer on its Miami Spice page). It is the best dining value in the city, and the restaurants hub is built to pair with it.

Miami Spa Months, July and August. The bureau's companion program puts the big hotel spas on promotional menus in the same window. August is when a treatment at a beachfront flagship costs what a neighborhood spa charges in February.

The quiet calendar is the point. August has no mega events, which is its charm: locals reclaim the city, reservations loosen, and the only calendar that matters is the tropical weather outlook. Our Miami Updates posts carry any storm related notices as they arise.

August weather in Miami

Long term averages: highs around 90 degrees, lows around 76, and rainfall around eight inches. August is deep in the wet season and inside the historically busier half of hurricane season, which runs to November 30 (NOAA). Most August days still follow the summer script: bright morning, brewing afternoon, a storm, a fine evening. The difference from June is the background risk, which flexible bookings answer.

Hotel prices and availability in August

The floor of the year. August rates at the big beachfront names can run half or less of their February levels (season described in words deliberately; the exact discount moves with demand). Weekends near the month's start carry some family traffic before school resumes; late August is the softest demand of the year in Miami. This is the month to book the suite, the cabana, and the spa, using the hotels hub comparisons.

Best August trip shapes

Food trip (3 to 4 days). Spice lunches and dinners as the itinerary, a ventanita breakfast each day, and one splurge room service night in a discounted suite. Spa and pool week (5 days). Spa Months menus, pool days, and a single museum outing from the museums guide. Flexible bargain week (7 days). Book refundable, watch the tropics, and enjoy the empty version of Miami that residents get every year.

A Miami Spice strategy

Spice rewards a method. Book the famous rooms in the program's first two weeks, before word of mouth fills them; the Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau's Spice site lists the year's participating restaurants and menus each summer, and the menus, not the names, should choose for you. Lunch menus are the sharpest value in the program and the easiest tables; save dinners for the rooms whose normal check you would never pay. Pace yourself at two Spice meals a day maximum, because prix fixe generosity is still generosity, and leave one dinner for the ventanita and fritanga Miami that never discounts and never needs to, per the food culture post. Weeknights beat weekends, the first seating beats the second, and a Spice reservation made in July for August is the single best dining move on the Miami calendar.

The facts that matter

Miami Spice runs August 1 to September 30 and Miami Spa Months run July and August, both programs of the Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau, with each year's participants and prices posted by the bureau. Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30 (NOAA). August weather figures are long term National Weather Service averages for Miami. Checked October 30, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is August a bad month because of hurricanes?
August sits inside the season's busier half, and most Augusts pass without a Miami landfall. Refundable bookings and insurance convert the risk into a manageable one.

How good is Miami Spice, really?
It is the county's flagship dining promotion, and the top rooms genuinely discount. Book the famous tables early in the program.

Are the beaches still good in August?
The water is at its warmest and the sand is emptier than winter. Mornings are the reliable window, as all summer.
