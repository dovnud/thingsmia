Miami in March: Weather, Events, and Prices

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. March is Miami's loudest month: the Miami Open, Ultra Music Festival and Miami Music Week, Calle Ocho, and spring break, all under warming, still mostly dry skies. Come for the energy and pay for it, or thread the quieter first half of the month before the festival stack lands.

The March events that matter

Miami Open, March 14 to 28, 2027, Hard Rock Stadium (miamiopen.com, presented by Itau). Two weeks of top tier tennis in a stadium campus built for it, with the grounds as much festival as sport. Our events hub carries the ticket and transport notes.

Ultra Music Festival and Miami Music Week. Ultra closes the month at Bayfront Park (the festival's announced 2027 dates are March 26 to 28; confirm on ultramusicfestival.com), and Music Week fills the clubs and pools around it. Downtown hotel rates and noise both peak; the beach hears it across the bay.

Calle Ocho Music Festival, Little Havana. Carnaval Miami's closing street festival takes over Calle Ocho in mid March (the Kiwanis Club of Little Havana confirms the exact Sunday on carnavalmiami.com). It is the largest Hispanic street festival in the country and the heart of our Little Havana page.

Spring break. Miami Beach runs its annual spring break measures in March: expect crowd controls, curfew style rules in recent years, and a heavy official presence on Ocean Drive. The city's rules for the 2027 season will be posted by the City of Miami Beach; our spring break notices post tracks the standing framework.

March weather in Miami

Long term averages: highs around 80 degrees, lows around 65, and rainfall still modest, roughly two and a half inches. The fronts fade, the ocean warms into the mid 70s, and the only weather plot is the first hints of humidity late in the month. It is the last month of the reliably dry season.

Hotel prices and availability in March

Peak pricing continues, with two spikes: the Open's fortnight around the stadium and downtown, and Music Week, which prices like a festival across the whole market. Spring break demand fills the beach independently of the events. The softest March rates sit in the first ten days, before the Open starts.

Best March trip shapes

Tennis trip (3 to 4 days). Day sessions at the Open, evenings on the beach, one Everglades morning while the dry season still keeps the mosquitoes down. Festival trip (4 days). Ultra plus Music Week parties; base downtown or in Brickell and walk. Quiet March (5 days, early month). Arrive before March 10 and you get dry season weather without the festival economy. All three book better through the hotels hub with the resort fees guide in hand.

Getting around in March

March closes streets for a living: the Miami Open runs park and ride and rideshare operations at Hard Rock Stadium because the surrounding roads cannot absorb the fortnight otherwise; Ultra closes the downtown grid around Bayfront Park for its weekend, pushing traffic onto the causeways; and Calle Ocho closes its namesake street for the festival day. The working strategy is the one the December post teaches for Art Week: pick a side of the bay per day, use Metrorail and the free Metromover downtown, and treat the causeways at event let out times as closed for planning purposes. The city's March notices, including any spring break specific rules, are tracked in the spring break notices post, which is the page to check the week you travel.

Frequently asked questions

When is spring break in Miami?
College breaks scatter across March, with the heaviest weeks mid month. Miami Beach publishes its seasonal rules and any curfew in advance.

Is March still dry season?
Mostly, yes. Rain rises later in spring; March is the last reliably dry month.

Where should Ultra visitors stay?
Downtown and Brickell for walking access to Bayfront Park, or the beach if you accept the causeway commute each night.
