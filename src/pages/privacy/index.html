Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 25, 2026

ThingsMIA (thingsmia.com) is a travel guide website. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, in plain language.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages readers use, so we can improve them. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you personally to us.

Advertising

We show advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at Google's partner sites policy pages.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links to partners such as Viator, Expedia, and Amazon. If you click through to a partner site, that partner's own privacy policy governs what happens there.

Cookies

This site itself sets no account or login cookies, because there are no accounts. Analytics and advertising partners may set cookies as described above. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Contact

Privacy questions: info@thingsmia.com.
