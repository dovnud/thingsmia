Everglades in Winter: Shark Valley Tram, Bike or Airboat

A 15 mile loop with no cars: the 2 hour tram, the $27 bike, and the three airboat operators the park actually permits, in dry season.

Checked facts

The National Park Service lists three Everglades entrances: Homestead, Miami (Shark Valley), and Everglades City, and states that the entrances do not interconnect.

Shark Valley is 30 miles west of Miami off the Tamiami Trail (U.S. Highway 41), with a 15 mile loop road used for walking, biking, or a narrated tram ride.

The National Park Service notes that airboat tours are not physically located at Shark Valley. Airboat services operate through concession partners 10 to 15 minutes east of Shark Valley on Route 41.

Brightline lists MiamiCentral and Fort Lauderdale among its stations, which is what makes the Fort Lauderdale day trip in this section work without a car.

Common questions

Can I ride an airboat inside Everglades National Park?

Plan on an airboat outside the park boundary areas served by concession operators. The National Park Service notes that airboat tours are not located at Shark Valley itself, and that airboat services run 10 to 15 minutes east of Shark Valley on Route 41.

Do the Everglades entrances connect inside the park?

No. The National Park Service states that the Homestead, Miami (Shark Valley), and Everglades City entrances do not interconnect, so pick the entrance that matches your plan before you drive.

Is Key West realistic as a day trip from Miami?

Yes, but it is a long day. The Florida Keys tourism site describes the Overseas Highway as a 113 mile drive, and that is only the Keys segment. The Key West guide above lays out bus, tour, and self drive options with honest timing.

Does the Bimini ferry leave from Miami?

Check the departure port before you book. Current Balearia Caribbean listings put Bimini ferry departures at Port Everglades in Fort Lauderdale, north of Miami, and a passport is required for the Bahamas. The Bimini guide above covers the transfer and the documents.

What is the easiest tour to add to a short trip?

A Biscayne Bay sightseeing cruise or a Little Havana food tour. Both leave from central areas, run in a half day or less, and do not need a rental car.
