Star Island and Celebrity Homes Boat Tour

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The celebrity homes cruise is the classic Biscayne Bay sightseeing boat: about 90 minutes from the downtown marinas, past the PortMiami cruise terminals, around Star Island and the Venetian Islands, and back along the Miami Beach shoreline locals call Millionaire's Row. You see the mansions from the water, which is the only way the public sees them at all, because Star Island is a gated, guard patrolled island with no public access. The narration names celebrity owners past and present. Treat those names as entertainment: houses change hands, and no tour operator is checking the property records. The skyline views and the houses themselves are real, and they carry the trip.

What the cruise actually covers

Most departures leave from Bayside Marketplace or the marinas around it in downtown Miami, which makes the tour easy to bolt onto a downtown day. The route heads out past PortMiami, where the cruise ships give you a sense of the port's scale, then works the bay islands: Star Island first, the single loop road of mansions built on a man made island completed in the 1920s, then the Venetian Islands chain toward Miami Beach, and the waterfront estates along the Beach's western shore. The downtown skyline from the bay is the second show, and on the late afternoon departures it comes with the best light of the day. Boats are double deck sightseeing vessels with an open top deck and a shaded lower deck, a bar on board, and recorded or live narration depending on the operator.

The celebrity names, honestly

Star Island and the bay islands really have housed famous names over the decades, and the guides work from a script of the greatest hits. The problem is tense: scripts age slowly and Miami real estate moves fast, so a house narrated as a star's home may have been sold twice since. Ownership also hides behind companies and trusts, which makes dockside certainty impossible. Our advice is to enjoy the narration as local folklore, enjoy the architecture and the lots, which need no celebrity to be astonishing, and do not repeat the names as fact when you get home. The honest version of this tour is a mansion and skyline cruise, and on those terms it delivers.

Logistics and how to get the most from it

Plan on about 90 minutes on the water, plus early arrival, since boarding is first come for the best seats and the top deck fills on weekends and holidays. The top deck is where you want to be for photos; the tradeoff is full Florida sun, so bring sunscreen and a hat even for the winter departures. Boats run in light rain and reschedule for rough weather; summer afternoon thunderstorms are the usual cause of delays. If you are choosing a departure time, late afternoon gives you cooler air, golden light on the skyline, and a natural lead into dinner downtown. Honest downsides

You never set foot on Star Island, and the boat keeps a respectful distance, so bring zoom expectations rather than binocular ones; a phone camera handles the wide shots fine. The narration quality varies by operator and by day, and the celebrity content is the weakest part of the script, for the reasons above. Finally, this is a gentle sightseeing cruise, not a speedboat: visitors expecting the Thriller style ride should book that product instead of this one.

The facts that matter

Star Island is a man made island in Biscayne Bay within Miami Beach, built up in the 1920s, and it is gated with no public access; its waterfront homes are visible only from the water. Celebrity homes cruises from downtown Miami typically run about 90 minutes, departing the Bayside Marketplace marina area, and route past PortMiami, Star Island, the Venetian Islands, and the Miami Beach shoreline. Boats are sightseeing vessels with open and shaded decks. Celebrity ownership claims in tour narration are unverified and frequently out of date. Frequently asked questions

Can you go onto Star Island itself?
No. The island is gated and guarded, with access limited to residents and their guests. The boat tour is the public way to see the homes, from the water.

How long is the celebrity homes cruise?
About 90 minutes for the standard sightseeing product. Arrive early for boarding and top deck seating, especially on weekends.

Are the celebrity names on the tour accurate?
Not reliably. Some are historic, some are current, and some are simply old script. Enjoy them as stories and judge the tour on the houses and the skyline.

Where do the boats leave from?
Most leave from the downtown marinas around Bayside Marketplace, an easy walk from downtown hotels and the Metromover.
