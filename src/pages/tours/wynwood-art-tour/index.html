Wynwood Art and Street Art Tour Guide

Last reviewed: October 26, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Wynwood has two layers: Wynwood Walls, the ticketed outdoor museum at 2516 NW 2nd Ave that started the district, and the surrounding streets, which are covered in murals and free to walk. Wynwood Walls admission is ticketed, with tickets sold through its official site, and the official site listed hours of 10:30 AM to 6:30 PM daily when checked in October 2026. Guided options in the neighborhood include walking tours and a 1 hour golf cart tour listed on Viator from $45. Start with the Walls, then walk the streets.

What is Wynwood Walls, exactly?

An enclosed outdoor museum of large scale murals in the heart of Wynwood, founded in 2009 by Tony Goldman, the developer credited with turning a warehouse district into Miami's street art center. Since Goldman's death in 2012, the Walls have been curated by his daughter, Jessica Goldman Srebnick, CEO of Goldman Properties. The official FAQ is clear that admission is ticketed, that ticket revenue sustains the site, and that a portion supports the Wynwood Walls Foundation. That is why older guides calling it free are out of date: the free era ended, and current third party prices disagree with each other, so buy from the official site and take the price from there.

Guided tour or self guided walk?

A guide adds the artist names, the techniques, and the stories behind walls you would otherwise photograph and forget. On Viator in October 2026, the Wynwood Graffiti Golf Cart Small-Group Tour ran 1 hour from $45 and covers more ground than walking, which matters in Miami heat, and the Wynwood Graffiti Experience, a hands on spray paint session, ran 1 hour from $42. Walking tours of the neighborhood cover the Walls and the streets together. Self guided works well too: the Walls are ticketed but compact, and the street murals beyond them need nothing but your feet and a charged phone. The honest split: take a guide for your first visit if street art genuinely interests you, and self guide if you mainly want the photo walk and the neighborhood's cafes and breweries.

How much time do you need in Wynwood?

Plan half a day for the neighborhood, not just the museum. The Walls themselves take an unhurried hour or so, the surrounding blocks keep unfolding murals for as long as you care to walk, and Wynwood is built for grazing, with coffee, food halls, and taprooms between the walls. Go in the morning for softer light on the murals and thinner crowds, and treat a midday start in summer as a heat decision, not just a scheduling one.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wynwood Walls free?
No. Admission is ticketed, and the official site sells the tickets. Some older articles still describe free entry; that is legacy information. The murals on the public streets around the Walls are free to view.

Should I book tickets in advance?
Buy through the official site ahead of your visit, especially for weekends and Art Basel week, when Wynwood is at its busiest. It also locks in the current price instead of a third party's stale one.

Is Wynwood only about the Walls?
No. The Walls are the anchor, but the wider arts district is the experience: blocks of murals, galleries, and food and drink spots. Budget time for both layers.

When is Wynwood busiest?
Weekends, and above all during Art Basel Miami Beach in early December, when the whole district becomes an open air art week. Weekday mornings are the calmest window.
