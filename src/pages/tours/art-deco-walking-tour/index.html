Art Deco Walking Tour in South Beach

Last reviewed: October 26, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The official Art Deco walking tour is run by the Miami Design Preservation League (MDPL), the nonprofit that has protected the district since 1976. Every MDPL tour starts at the Art Deco Welcome Center at 1001 Ocean Drive, Miami Beach. The flagship Official Art Deco Walking Tour runs daily, and MDPL's own listings describe it as about 2 hours with a 10:30 AM start, including admission to the Art Deco Museum. Book through mdpl.org: third party sellers listed conflicting prices when checked in October 2026, so this page prints no price.

Who runs the official tour?

MDPL, and that is the reason to take this tour over a generic copy. The League is a nonprofit, founded in 1976, that led the fight to preserve the Miami Beach Architectural District and has offered guided walking tours for over 40 years. Its guides are volunteers credentialed through the MDPL Tour Guide Academy, and every tour dollar supports the preservation mission rather than a tour company. All of its tours, walking, private, and self guided, begin at the Art Deco Welcome Center at 1001 Ocean Drive, which also houses the Art Deco Museum and gift shop.

What does the tour cover?

About 2 hours on foot through the Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue hotel district, reading the buildings as you go: the streamlined curves, neon, and pastel facades of the 1930s hotels, why they were built the way they were, and how the district was nearly lost before preservationists saved it. MDPL's listings note that the walking tour ticket includes admission to the Art Deco Museum and the gift shop at the Welcome Center, which makes a good before or after stop. Arrive 15 minutes before departure, per the League's own guidance.

Guided or self guided?

MDPL also offers an app based self guided audio tour in four languages, plus private group tours. The self guided version suits photographers and anyone who wants to move at their own pace down Ocean Drive, and the district's streets are public, so an informal walk costs nothing at all. The guided tour is the better first visit: the architecture rewards explanation, the guides answer questions a recording cannot, and the 10:30 AM start finishes before the worst of the midday heat. Either way, end at the Welcome Center museum to put names and dates to what you just walked past.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the Art Deco tour start?
At the Art Deco Welcome Center, 1001 Ocean Drive in Miami Beach. Every MDPL tour begins there, guided and self guided alike. Arrive about 15 minutes early.

Is the Art Deco Museum included?
MDPL's own listings for the Official Art Deco Walking Tour say the ticket includes admission to the Art Deco Museum and gift shop at the Welcome Center. Confirm at booking, since inclusions can change.

How much does the tour cost?
Check mdpl.org for the current price. Third party sellers listed different prices for the same tour when this page was checked in October 2026, which is exactly why booking with the League directly is the cleaner move.

Can I just walk Ocean Drive myself?
Yes. The district is public streets lined with the facades, and the MDPL app tour adds narration in four languages. The guided walk adds a preservationist's storytelling and a fixed, heat friendly morning schedule.
