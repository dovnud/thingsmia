Best Restaurants in South Beach

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For the classic South Beach dinner, Joe's Stone Crab in season. For a steakhouse night, Prime 112 or Papi Steak, both South of Fifth. For the hard reservation everyone talks about, Carbone. For a chef led tasting room, Stubborn Seed. The pattern to notice: the strongest rooms cluster South of Fifth and along Collins and Washington, not on the Ocean Drive strip itself, and the fact box below explains why that matters for your bill. Every restaurant below was checked as operating on October 30, 2026.

South of Fifth anchors

Joe's Stone Crab, South Beach

Joe's Stone Crab, at 11 Washington Avenue, is the South Beach institution, and its own menu states the Florida stone crab season as October 15 to May 1, with claws served chilled, cracked, and with mustard sauce. Its 114th season opened on October 16, 2026, per current listings. Order claws in season, hash browns, creamed spinach, and key lime pie. It suits a splurge occasion and seafood traditionalists. Read our full Joe's Stone Crab guide for season strategy before you book. Price level: splurge.

Prime 112, South of Fifth

Prime 112, at 112 Ocean Drive, is the steakhouse anchor of South of Fifth, loud, busy, and built for celebrations. Order a bone in cut to share and the classic sides format. It suits groups marking something, expense account dinners, and visitors who want the South Beach steakhouse scene at full volume. Note the address carefully: this Ocean Drive number sits in the residential South of Fifth stretch, a different world from the tourist strip a mile north, which the fact box below covers. Book ahead for any weekend night. Price level: splurge.

Papi Steak, South of Fifth

Papi Steak, at 736 1st Street, is the high energy steakhouse from the Groot Hospitality group, reopened in late 2025 after a redesign, per Miami coverage this fall. Order a tomahawk style cut for the table if your group wants the full production, in a room that treats dinner as nightlife. It suits celebratory groups and diners who want their steakhouse with a DJ era pulse. If Prime 112 is the classic, Papi is the remix, and South of Fifth holds both within a few blocks. Price level: splurge.

Stubborn Seed, South Beach

Stubborn Seed, at 101 Washington Avenue, is chef Jeremy Ford's small tasting format restaurant, the quiet counterpoint to the steakhouse energy around it. Order the tasting menu and let the kitchen sequence the night. It suits food first travelers and couples marking a real occasion. Because the room is small, it books out earlier than the big rooms on this page, so reserve when your flights are set. It is also the strongest answer on this page to the claim that South Beach food is all scene and no cooking. Price level: splurge.

Lincoln Road, Collins, and the north end

Carbone Miami, South Beach

Carbone, at 49 Collins Avenue, serves Major Food Group's Italian American format, spicy rigatoni vodka and a tableside Caesar at the center of the order. It suits scene first diners and celebrations, and it is one of the hardest tables in Miami to land. Book weeks ahead, be flexible on time, and treat a late seating as a win rather than a compromise. If you cannot get in, the same group runs other Miami rooms, but the Collins Avenue original is the one people mean. Price level: splurge.

MILA, Lincoln Road

MILA, at 1636 Meridian Avenue above Lincoln Road, is the rooftop MediterrAsian restaurant covered in full on our rooftop restaurants page. Order across both halves of the menu, maki and grilled meats. It suits groups, celebrations, and Lincoln Road evenings that start with shopping and end above it. Price level: splurge.

Cecconi's Miami Beach, Mid-Beach edge

Cecconi's, at 4385 Collins Avenue in the courtyard of Soho Beach House, serves Italian food in a garden setting, open to the public per its own site, with weekend brunch listed on its booking pages. Order handmade pasta and wood fired plates, and go for a long lunch if dinner reservations are gone. Location honesty matters here: this address sits at the northern edge of what most people call South Beach, up in Mid-Beach, so treat it as a destination rather than a walk from Ocean Drive. It suits a calmer, greener meal than the South of Fifth rooms offer. Price level: splurge leaning.

The honest Ocean Drive note

Ocean Drive's famous strip of sidewalk cafes charges for the view, the people watching, and the Art Deco backdrop, and the food at many strip addresses is priced for tourists who will not return. Specials shouted by hosts, oversized drinks, and menus without clear prices are the tells. Eat on Ocean Drive for the scene if you like, but for the meal itself, walk a few blocks: South of Fifth, Washington Avenue, and Collins hold every restaurant on this page, and locals book those rooms, not the strip.

Frequently asked questions

Where do locals eat in South Beach?
South of Fifth and along Washington and Collins, at rooms like Joe's Stone Crab, Stubborn Seed, and Prime 112, rather than at the Ocean Drive sidewalk strip. This page is built around that pattern.

When is the best time to eat at Joe's Stone Crab?
During stone crab season, October 15 to May 1 per the restaurant's own menu, when claws are fresh. The 114th season opened October 16, 2026. Outside the season, ask whether claws are fresh or frozen before ordering.

What is the hardest reservation in South Beach?
Carbone on Collins Avenue, followed by the small room at Stubborn Seed. Book both as soon as your travel dates are fixed, and stay flexible on seating time.

Is Macchialina still open?
We left it off this page on purpose. Its own site is live, but a July 2026 local report described its longtime Alton Road space as taken over by a cocktail bar, and we could not resolve the contradiction by October 30, 2026. Check its site directly before planning a night around it.
