Best Peruvian Restaurants in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For ceviche downtown, CVI.CHE 105. For a sit down Peruvian dinner with a pisco bar, Pisco y Nazca in Doral or Kendall. For Peruvian Japanese Nikkei food in a casual format, SuViche. For traditional plates at local prices, Aromas del Peru in West Miami. Miami's Peruvian scene is deep because the city's Peruvian community is deep, and every kitchen below was checked as operating on October 30, 2026, at the address given.

Ceviche bars and Nikkei kitchens

CVI.CHE 105, Downtown

CVI.CHE 105, at 105 NE 3rd Avenue in downtown Miami, is the city's best known ceviche name, with several Miami locations listed on its own site. Order a classic ceviche with leche de tigre, and add a causa or a tiradito if your table shares starters. It suits first time Peruvian diners, downtown workers, and anyone who wants the ceviche bar format done at scale. The downtown room is the original style of the experience, busy at lunch and lively at night. Its sibling Pollos y Jarras is not listed on this page, because its downtown location carried a closed until further notice message in listings checked this month. Price level: mid range.

SuViche, Brickell

SuViche, at 49 SW 11th Street in Brickell, with further locations including Wynwood, serves Peruvian Japanese Nikkei food, sushi and ceviche side by side, per its own site. Order a ceviche and a maki roll in the same round, which is the whole point of the format. It suits younger groups, casual dates, and diners who cannot decide between a sushi night and a ceviche night. The Brickell room works as a weeknight regular as much as a destination. Price level: mid range.

Manta, Wynwood

Manta, at 102 NW 25th Street in Wynwood per its own site, serves Peruvian fusion food with a strong Nikkei streak in a design forward room. Order ceviche, tiradito, and a lomo saltado if you want the classic next to the modern plates. It suits Wynwood gallery nights, groups starting an evening out, and diners who want Peruvian food in a nightlife leaning setting. Book for weekend nights, when Wynwood fills. Price level: mid range to splurge.

Full Peruvian dinners

Pisco y Nazca Ceviche Gastrobar, Doral

Pisco y Nazca's Doral house, at 8551 NW 53rd Street, Suite A101, is a Peruvian ceviche gastrobar built around a pisco bar, with a Kendall location also listed on its own site. Order a pisco sour, a ceviche, and a chaufa or lomo saltado for the table. It suits Doral and west side diners, groups, and anyone who wants the full dinner format rather than a ceviche counter. Use the Doral or Kendall addresses from the restaurant site. The Coral Gables location shows as permanently closed in current listings and is not recommended here. Price level: mid range.

Aromas del Peru, West Miami

Aromas del Peru, at 5757 SW 8th Street in West Miami, with further locations including Kendall, serves traditional Peruvian cooking in a family restaurant format, per its own site. Order the plates Peruvian families order: ceviche to start, then seco, ají de gallina, or a parihuela seafood soup for a hungry table. It suits local diners, families, and visitors willing to drive west for the traditional end of the cuisine. This is the least polished room on the page and, for many readers, the most satisfying one. Price level: budget friendly to mid range.

Mayu, Brickell

Mayu, at 177 SW 7th Street in Brickell, is a Peruvian restaurant serving ceviche and classic plates in the middle of the Brickell dining grid. Order ceviche and a pisco sour at the bar, or settle in for lomo saltado and chaufa rice. It suits Brickell residents and hotel guests who want Peruvian food without a cross town drive, and it works as a group dinner before a Brickell night out. Price level: mid range.

Two closures to know about

La Mar by Gastón Acurio, long the splurge Peruvian name in Brickell, closed with the Mandarin Oriental hotel, which shut on May 31, 2025, ahead of the building's demolition. It is not listed above, whatever older guides still say. Pollos y Jarras, from the CVI.CHE 105 family, carried a closed until further notice notice for its downtown room in listings checked in October 2026, so it is also excluded here. Check a kitchen's own site before a dedicated trip.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best ceviche in Miami?
Start at CVI.CHE 105 downtown for the classic ceviche bar format, or SuViche in Brickell if you want ceviche with sushi in the same meal. Both were operating at the addresses above when checked on October 30, 2026.

What is Nikkei food?
Nikkei is the cooking of Peru's Japanese community, ceviche and tiradito techniques meeting sushi formats. SuViche and Manta on this page work in that style, while Aromas del Peru covers the traditional criollo plates.

What should a first timer order at a Peruvian restaurant?
A classic ceviche, a pisco sour if you drink, and lomo saltado or arroz chaufa as a main. That trio covers the cuisine's coastal, bar, and home cooking sides in one meal.

Is La Mar still open?
No. La Mar closed with the Mandarin Oriental Miami in 2025, and the hotel building was demolished in 2026, per reporting checked for this page. Older lists still recommending it are out of date.
