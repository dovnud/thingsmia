Best Brunch in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For a Brickell weekend brunch with a Miami accent, go to B Bistro and Bakery. For a long outdoor morning, GreenStreet Cafe in Coconut Grove. For the chef led Sunday table, Michael's Genuine in the Design District. For an Australian style cafe brunch, Threefold Cafe in Coral Gables. Weekend brunch claims below are only made where the restaurant's own site or menu shows them, and every place was checked as operating on October 30, 2026.

Brickell and the Design District

B Bistro and Bakery, Brickell

B Bistro, at 600 Brickell Avenue, Suite 175, runs brunch on Saturday and Sunday, per its own site, with a bakery case attached to the dining room. Order the Pastelito Pancakes, which fold a Cuban bakery idea into a brunch plate, or the chilaquiles and croissant French toast. It suits Brickell hotel guests, groups splitting sweet and savory, and anyone who wants to leave with pastries for later. The bakery counter is part of the experience, so walk it before you sit down. Book for weekend mornings, when the room fills early. Price level: mid range.

Michael's Genuine Food and Drink, Design District

Michael's Genuine, at 130 NE 40th Street in the Design District, is chef Michael Schwartz's long running restaurant, and its Sunday brunch is a fixture in the neighborhood guides and current listings checked for this page. Order brunch plates built on the kitchen's wood oven and local sourcing approach, and add something from the pastry side. It suits food first brunchers, Design District shoppers, and visitors who want one chef led meal in a relaxed room rather than a scene. Confirm the current Sunday menu on the restaurant site before you go, as menus here rotate with the market. Price level: mid range to splurge.

Grove, Gables, and Wynwood mornings

GreenStreet Cafe, Coconut Grove

GreenStreet Cafe, at 3468 Main Highway in Coconut Grove, is an indoor outdoor cafe serving breakfast and brunch under the Grove's tree canopy, with lounge seating on the sidewalk. Order the classic egg plates, pancakes, or a brunch sandwich, and take coffee outside. It suits slow mornings, families, dog owners using the outdoor seats, and anyone building a Grove day around brunch first. Weekend lines form at peak hours, so arrive early or put your name in and walk the village. Price level: mid range.

Threefold Cafe, Coral Gables

Threefold Cafe, at 141 Giralda Avenue in Coral Gables, is an Australian, Melbourne inspired cafe whose own brunch page describes its weekend offer. Order the Melbourne style plates, avocado toast done properly, and coffee as the main event rather than an afterthought. It suits coffee serious diners, Gables residents, and visitors who want a cafe format brunch instead of a big restaurant production. The Giralda Avenue setting gives you a pedestrian street to stroll after. Price level: mid range.

Zak the Baker, Wynwood

Zak the Baker, at 295 NW 26th Street in Wynwood, is a kosher bakery and cafe serving breakfast and brunch style plates around its own breads and pastries. Order whatever the bakery case is proudest of that morning, plus eggs and salads from the cafe menu. It suits bakery first diners and Wynwood walkers starting a mural day with a real breakfast. One planning note matters here: the bakery closes on Saturday, so this is a Sunday or weekday brunch pick, not a Saturday one. Price level: budget friendly to mid range.

How we checked weekend brunch

Brunch claims are easy to get wrong, so this page only claims weekend brunch service where an official site or menu shows it. B Bistro lists Saturday and Sunday brunch on its own site, Threefold Cafe describes weekend brunch on its own brunch page, and Zak the Baker's Saturday closure is stated plainly in its own materials, which is why it appears here as a Sunday pick. Hours change with seasons and holidays, so confirm on the restaurant site the week you visit.

Frequently asked questions

Which Miami brunch is best on a Saturday?
B Bistro and Bakery in Brickell lists Saturday brunch on its own site, and GreenStreet Cafe serves weekend mornings in Coconut Grove. Zak the Baker is closed on Saturday, so save it for Sunday.

Where should a foodie book brunch?
Michael's Genuine in the Design District, where the Sunday brunch comes out of a chef led kitchen with a long local following. Book ahead and check the current menu on the restaurant site, because dishes rotate.

What is a good brunch before a Wynwood art day?
Zak the Baker on Sunday or a weekday morning, then walk the murals. On Saturday, start in Brickell or the Grove instead and come to Wynwood after, since the bakery is closed.

Is Blue Collar still open for brunch?
No. Blue Collar served its final service on Sunday, May 17, 2026, after 14 years, per owner announcements reported in Miami food press, so it is not included above. Yardbird's Miami Beach room also shows as permanently closed in current listings checked for this page.
