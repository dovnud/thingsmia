Best Steakhouses in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For the classic South Beach steakhouse night, Prime 112. For dry aged beef with a harbor view, Smith and Wollensky in South Pointe. For a hotel splurge in the north, Bourbon Steak by Michael Mina in Aventura. For something different, COTE's Korean steakhouse format in the Design District, and for an Argentinean grill with local roots, Graziano's on Bird Road. Every house below was checked as operating on October 30, 2026, and price levels are given in words, not invented numbers.

South Beach and Brickell classics

Prime 112, South of Fifth, South Beach

Prime 112, at 112 Ocean Drive in Miami Beach, is the Myles Restaurant Group steakhouse that anchors the South of Fifth dining strip. Order a bone in cut to share, or a filet if you are dining solo, and expect the full classic steakhouse sides format. It suits celebration dinners, expense account tables, and visitors who want the South Beach steakhouse scene in its original form. The room runs loud and busy at peak hours, which is part of the appeal here. Book ahead, especially Thursday to Saturday. Price level: splurge.

Smith and Wollensky, South Pointe, Miami Beach

Smith and Wollensky's Miami Beach house, at 1 Washington Avenue in South Pointe Park, is the chain's USDA Prime, dry aged format set on Government Cut, per its own location page. Order a dry aged cut and watch ships move through the channel from the outdoor seating. It suits diners who want the steakhouse ritual with a view, business dinners, and anyone staying at the south end of the beach. It is the most scenic classic steakhouse setting on this page. Price level: splurge.

The Capital Grille, Brickell

The Capital Grille's Miami restaurant, at 444 Brickell Avenue, is the national chain's dry aged, in house format in the middle of the Brickell business district. Order a dry aged porterhouse or the bone in ribeye, and use it when you need a steakhouse that runs on a business schedule. It suits work dinners, downtown hotel guests, and groups who want a predictable, polished room on the mainland side. Lunch service on weekdays makes it the most flexible entry on this page. Price level: splurge.

North, Design District, and Argentinean

Bourbon Steak by Michael Mina, Aventura

Bourbon Steak, at 19999 West Country Club Drive in Aventura inside the JW Marriott Turnberry, is chef Michael Mina's steakhouse, and its own site lists the price level as $$$. Order a cut finished over the wood fire approach the kitchen is known for, and start with the duck fat fries the menu made famous. It suits north Miami and Aventura diners, resort guests, and a splurge night without the South Beach drive. The Turnberry setting makes it a destination dinner rather than a walk in. Price level: splurge.

COTE Miami, Design District

COTE Miami, at 3900 NE 2nd Avenue in the Design District, is the Miami outpost of Simon Kim's Korean steakhouse, built around tabletop grills and a set format the house calls the Butcher's Feast. Order the feast format for a first visit so the kitchen sequences the cuts for you, with Korean sides alongside. It suits groups, adventurous steak lovers, and anyone bored by the classic chophouse script. The smokeless grill format keeps it social without the smoke. Reservations go quickly on weekends. Price level: splurge.

Graziano's, Bird Road, Westchester

Graziano's Bird Road restaurant, at 9227 SW 40th Street, is the Argentinean steakhouse and market operation's full service house, grilling in the Argentine parrilla tradition. Order a parrillada to share, or an entraña with chimichurri, and shop the attached market format for Argentine wines and goods. It suits families, groups, and diners who want a local institution instead of a hotel dining room. Note that the separate Brickell location shows as permanently closed in current listings, so use the Bird Road address on this page. Price level: mid range to splurge.

How to read price on this page

Steakhouse checks vary too much with cut, size, and wine to quote a number responsibly, so this page uses words: splurge means a full classic steakhouse dinner, and mid range to splurge means the format lets you spend less with shared grills and market priced cuts. The one symbol rating shown, $$$ at Bourbon Steak, comes from that restaurant's own site and is recorded in the fact sheet. Check the current menu on each house's site before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Which Miami steakhouse is best for a celebration?
Prime 112 for the South Beach scene, or Bourbon Steak in Aventura for a resort setting. Both run at splurge level, so book ahead and expect a full evening rather than a quick dinner.

Where can I get dry aged steak in Miami?
Smith and Wollensky in South Pointe and The Capital Grille in Brickell both build their formats around dry aged USDA Prime beef, per their own sites.

What is a good steakhouse for a group?
COTE Miami, where the tabletop grill feast format is designed for sharing, or Graziano's on Bird Road for an Argentine parrillada shared across the table.

Is StripSteak at Fontainebleau still open?
We could not confirm it. The Fontainebleau dining page checked for this page points to prime54, which suggests a rebrand, so StripSteak is not listed above. Confirm the current name and menu on the hotel site before planning around it.
