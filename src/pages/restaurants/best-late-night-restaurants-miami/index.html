Best Late Night Restaurants in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami does have real late night food, but the honest list is shorter than the internet claims. The latest kitchens we could verify on the restaurants' own websites are La Sandwicherie in South Beach, listed as open until 5 a.m. every day, and Big Pink, listed by its operator as open until 5:30 a.m. Thursday to Saturday. Bodega Taqueria's taqueria is listed until 3 a.m. Thursday to Saturday, Bakan and Cafe La Trova run to 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights, and Versailles keeps a full Cuban kitchen to 1 a.m. on weekends. Every time below comes from the restaurant's own site, checked in October 2026.

Open latest: the South Beach counters

La Sandwicherie, South Beach

La Sandwicherie, at 229 14th Street in Miami Beach, is an outdoor French sandwich counter that has fed South Beach since 1988. It is listed as open until 5 a.m. every day on its site, checked October 2026, which makes it the most reliable very late meal in the city. Order the SOBE Club or the Frenchie, both finished with the shop's house vinaigrette, its Magic Sauce. It is budget friendly, counter service, and built for the walk back from Washington Avenue. Note the site's own warning that a late night service charge applies after 10 p.m.

Big Pink, South Beach

Big Pink, at 157 Collins Avenue, is the retro diner in the pink building south of Fifth Street, run by the Myles Restaurant Group. Its operator's site lists it as open until 5:30 a.m. Thursday to Saturday, until 2 a.m. on Sunday, and until midnight Monday to Wednesday, checked October 2026. The menu is a diner sprawl, so order accordingly: chicken and waffles, a burger, or breakfast cooked at 3 a.m. It is mid range, casual, and the sit down answer when a counter will not do.

Bodega Taqueria, South Beach

Bodega Taqueria, at 1220 16th Street in Miami Beach, serves tacos from an airstream style counter with a lounge behind it. Its own location page lists the taqueria as open until 1 a.m. Sunday to Wednesday and until 3 a.m. Thursday to Saturday, checked October 2026, and the lounge keeps similar hours. Al pastor tacos and a burrito are the order, budget friendly to mid range. It suits groups coming off Lincoln Road or the beach clubs who want food first and a bar in the same stop.

Full dinners that run late

Bakan, Wynwood

Bakan, at 2801 NW 2nd Avenue in Wynwood, is the rare late night option that is a full restaurant rather than a counter. Its own site lists Friday and Saturday nights running to 2 a.m., with Thursday to midnight, checked October 2026. That means a real dinner of queso fundido, tacos on house made tortillas, and grilled meats at an hour when most Miami kitchens are dark. It is mid range to splurge and suits a Wynwood night that starts with the murals and ends at the table.

Cafe La Trova, Little Havana

Cafe La Trova, at 971 SW 8th Street, is listed as open until 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights on its site, checked October 2026, and until midnight the rest of the week. The listing covers the whole venue, bar and stage included, so treat it as a full evening: eat dinner from chef Michelle Bernstein's Cuban menu earlier in the night, then stay for the cantineros and the live trova band. It is a splurge by neighborhood standards and the classiest late night on this page.

Versailles, Little Havana

Versailles, at 3555 SW 8th Street, is the late night institution for a complete meal. Its own site lists it as open until 1 a.m. on Friday and Saturday and until midnight Monday to Thursday and Sunday, checked October 2026. Unlike the bar led entries, the full Cuban menu is the point here: ropa vieja, a Cuban sandwich, and a cafecito from the window at hours when Little Havana is otherwise asleep. It is budget friendly to mid range and suits families, night shift eaters, and arrivals from a late flight.

How to read these times

Every closing time on this page was read on the restaurant's own website on October 30, 2026, and is phrased that way on purpose. A venue's listed closing time is when the doors close; a kitchen can stop seating or trim its menu earlier, especially on quiet weeknights. Places whose own sites showed no late hours, including some famous names, were left off rather than copied from review sites. Check the restaurant's site again on the day you go.

Frequently asked questions

Where can you eat at 3 a.m. in Miami?
La Sandwicherie in South Beach, listed as open until 5 a.m. every day on its site, and Big Pink, listed until 5:30 a.m. Thursday to Saturday by its operator. Bodega Taqueria's 3 a.m. Thursday to Saturday listing covers the earlier part of that window.

Is there a sit down restaurant open late, not just counters?
Yes. Bakan in Wynwood runs a full dining room to 2 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights per its site, and Versailles serves its full Cuban menu to 1 a.m. on weekends. Cafe La Trova keeps its room open to 2 a.m. on weekends, with dinner best ordered earlier in the evening.

Why are some famous late night names missing?
Because their own websites did not list a closing time at midnight or later when we checked in October 2026. This page only prints closing times that appear on the restaurant's own site, so a few well known diners and taco spots did not qualify this time.

Do late night places in Miami take reservations?
The counters on this page, La Sandwicherie, Big Pink, and Bodega Taqueria, are walk in. Bakan and Cafe La Trova take reservations for dinner hours, which is the smart way to start a late night at either one.
