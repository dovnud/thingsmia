Best Desserts in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For ice cream with a Little Havana story, go to Azucar Ice Cream on Calle Ocho and order the Abuela Maria. For donuts worth a detour, go to The Salty Donut. For cakes and pies by the slice, go to Fireman Derek's Bake Shop. For pastry chef desserts, go to Bachour in Coral Gables. And for late night cookies, go to Night Owl Cookies. Miami dessert works best as its own outing, after dinner or as an afternoon stop, and this list is built for exactly that, one sweet destination at a time.

How to plan a Miami dessert stop

Heat shapes dessert here. Ice cream and gelato are evening foods in Miami, best after the sun drops, while bakeries and donut shops are morning and afternoon stops before the case thins out. Portions also run generous, especially pies and cakes by the slice, so sharing is normal. If you want the full survey, pair one frozen stop with one bakery stop on the same day, Azucar in Little Havana in the afternoon, for example, and a slice from Fireman Derek's after dinner. For the pie specialists, our best key lime pie in Miami guide goes deep on the city signature slice.

The dessert stops

Azucar Ice Cream, Little Havana

Azucar, at 1503 SW 8th Street in Little Havana, states on its own site that it was founded in July 2011 as an artisanal ice cream and sorbet boutique inspired by the owner abuela and the neighborhood. The Abuela Maria flavor, guava, cream cheese, and Maria cookies, is the signature order, with cafe con leche and platano maduro flavors keeping the Cuban theme. Best for a Calle Ocho afternoon and a first taste of Miami flavors in ice cream form. Price level: budget friendly.

The Salty Donut, Wynwood and beyond

The Salty Donut, with its site at saltydonut.com and a Wynwood flagship among its locations, is the donut destination, a rotating menu of raised and cake donuts in flavors that change with the season, plus coffee to match. Go early for the full case, expect the popular flavors to sell out, and buy a mixed box rather than a single donut, the variety is the point. Best for a morning treat, an office box, and donut completists. Price level: mid range for donuts.

Fireman Derek's Bake Shop, Wynwood and Coconut Grove

Fireman Derek's, with its site at firemandereks.com, is known for pies, cakes, cheesecakes, and cookies sold whole and by the slice, in generous, old fashioned portions. Key lime pie is a natural order in Miami, and the shop rotating cakes suit a take home dessert for a group better than a single plated sweet. Best for slice by slice indulgence and whole pies for a gathering. Price level: mid range.

Bachour, Coral Gables

Bachour, at 2020 Salzedo Street in Coral Gables, appears in our bakery guide too, and it earns a second listing here because the dessert case, petits gateaux, macarons, bonbons style chocolates, and plated level pastry, is a dessert destination on its own, separate from brunch. The restaurant describes itself as a Michelin Guide Bib Gourmand bakery, and the pastry work is the most technically refined on this list. Best for a special occasion dessert and gifts. Price level: splurge leaning.

Night Owl Cookies, multiple locations

Night Owl Cookies, with its site at nightowlcookieco.com, is built for the late night sweet tooth, large warm cookies in rotating flavors, with a model aimed at evening and delivery hours rather than the morning bakery run. It suits a post dinner order in, a hotel room treat, and anyone whose dessert hour starts at ten at night. Best for night owls, as the name promises, and for sharing a warm box among friends. Price level: budget friendly to mid range.

Key lime, the Miami signature

Key lime pie is the dessert most tied to South Florida, tart, creamy, and served chilled, which suits the climate. Fireman Derek's is the slice stop on this list, and our dedicated key lime guide compares bakeries, restaurants, and whole pies to take home across the city.

Frequently asked questions

What dessert is Miami known for?
Key lime pie is the signature, followed by Cuban influenced sweets, guava pastries, flan, and tropical ice cream flavors like the ones at Azucar. A first visit should include at least one of each family.

Where should I go after dinner?
Azucar for an evening scoop in Little Havana, or Night Owl Cookies for a warm late night box. Bachour and Fireman Derek's are better as daytime or early evening stops, so plan those before dinner ends the day.

What is a good dessert to take to a group?
A whole pie from Fireman Derek's, a mixed donut box from The Salty Donut, or a pastry selection from Bachour, depending on whether the group leans classic, casual, or refined.

Are there budget friendly options?
Yes. Azucar scoops and Night Owl cookies are budget friendly, and a single slice at Fireman Derek's is a mid range treat that is large enough to share.
