Best Key Lime Pie in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Key lime pie is a Florida Keys tradition, and Miami is the nearest big city to its home turf, so the pie here ranges from the classic slice at Joe's Stone Crab to bakery specialists who build their whole menu around it. The five stops below all have key lime pie confirmed as a named menu item or a documented specialty on the shop's own site or menu, checked October 30, 2026. Styles vary on purpose: whipped cream or meringue, shallow classic or deep dish, slice at a white tablecloth table or a whole pie in a box.

The classic slice

Joe's Stone Crab, Miami Beach

Joe's Stone Crab, at 11 Washington Avenue in Miami Beach, lists Joe's Key Lime Pie on the desserts section of its own menu, and its classic combination plate even builds a slice into the stone crab order. This is the formal version: a chilled slice in a dining room that has served Miami Beach for generations, ordered after claws and hashed browns. It is a splurge setting, and the pie suits anyone who wants the city's most traditional seafood dinner to end the traditional way. Our full guide to the restaurant is linked below.

Truluck's, Brickell

Truluck's, at 777 Brickell Avenue, makes its key lime pie in house, according to the restaurant's own site, which describes a tart custard in an almond graham crust topped with fresh whipped cream, and notes that its chefs measure the pie against the well known bakeries of Key West. That Keys benchmark is the right spirit for a Miami list. Order it as the end of a seafood dinner in Brickell, where it holds its own next to the carrot cake the room is also known for. Splurge pricing, dinner only context.

The bakery specialists

Fireman Derek's Bake Shop, Wynwood and Coconut Grove

Fireman Derek's Bake Shop is a pie first bakery founded by a former Miami firefighter, with shops at 2545 North Miami Avenue in Wynwood and 3435 Main Highway in Coconut Grove, per its own site. Key lime is the shop's headline pie, sold by the slice and as whole pies, with the tart, hand squeezed style that local readers' polls keep rewarding. This is the pick for pie people: budget friendly to mid range, casual, and built for taking a whole pie home. Go to the Grove shop if you are pairing it with the Fookem's stop below.

Fookem's Fabulous, Coconut Grove

Fookem's Fabulous, at 3606 Grand Avenue in Coconut Grove, is a small bakery whose own site describes its trade plainly: key lime pies, classic and in flavors like coconut and guava, on a signature sea salt graham cracker crust. The mini pie format is the move, because it lets a table taste more than one version. It is budget friendly and suits dessert collectors and anyone who prefers a specialist doing one thing carefully over a big menu doing everything.

Sweet Delights Key Lime Pies, Florida City

Sweet Delights Key Lime Pies is exactly what the name promises, a shop devoted to key lime pies in flavors that run from the original to mamey, guava, coconut, and passion fruit, per its current menu listing. Be honest about the geography: the shop now sits at 1485 NE 1st Avenue in Florida City, south of Miami proper, on the road toward the Keys. That makes it a road trip stop rather than a city dessert run, and a natural first taste on a drive down to Key Largo. Budget friendly, pies by the slice and whole.

What makes it key lime pie

The pie is named for the small, tart Key limes of the Florida Keys, set into a filling traditionally made with sweetened condensed milk and egg yolks over a graham cracker crust. Toppings split the state: some bakers finish with whipped cream, others with meringue, and Miami versions vary from shallow and sharp to deep dish and mild. No place on this page is credited with inventing the pie. Its roots are in the Keys, and every shop here is working in that tradition, not claiming it.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best key lime pie near South Beach?
Joe's Stone Crab on Washington Avenue, where the pie is a named dessert on the restaurant's own menu and part of its classic combination plate. It is a dinner reservation setting, so plan the slice as the end of a meal.

Can you buy a whole key lime pie to take home in Miami?
Yes. Fireman Derek's and Fookem's Fabulous are bakeries, so whole pies are the normal order, and Sweet Delights sells whole pies in many flavors from its Florida City shop. Call ahead on weekends, when popular flavors sell out.

Whipped cream or meringue on top?
Both are traditional, and Miami splits the difference. Truluck's own site describes whipped cream on its pie, while other bakers lean toward meringue or a plain finish. Order the version that sounds right and know the other camp exists.

Is Miami key lime pie as good as Key West's?
The Keys are the pie's home, and a Keys road trip is still the pilgrimage. Miami's advantage is range: a white tablecloth slice at Joe's, two Coconut Grove specialists a short walk apart, and a Brickell dining room that openly benchmarks itself against Key West bakers.
