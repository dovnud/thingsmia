Best Restaurants in Brickell

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Brickell is Miami's business district, and its restaurants split into scene dinners, business safe classics, and one very good daytime bakery. Komodo and Sexy Fish Miami are the big nights out, Quinto brings wood fire cooking on a terrace above Brickell City Centre, Truluck's and The Capital Grille are the polished seafood and steakhouse answers, and B Bistro and Bakery covers breakfast, brunch, and lunch. Every place below was confirmed as operating on October 30, 2026, from its own website or a current listing.

The big nights out

Komodo

Komodo, at 801 Brickell Avenue, is a three story Southeast Asian restaurant and lounge from the team behind several of Miami's nightlife rooms. Its own site describes bold Southeast Asian cooking with a South Florida twist, a full sushi bar, and signature dishes that include Peking duck and lobster dynamite. Order the duck for the table and fill in around it with sushi and small plates. It is a splurge, and it suits groups and celebratory dinners more than quiet ones.

Sexy Fish Miami

Sexy Fish Miami, at 1001 South Miami Avenue, is the Miami outpost of the London restaurant, serving Japanese inspired sushi, sashimi, seafood, and robata grilled dishes, per its own site, in a room built around underwater themed art and late night energy. The caramelized black cod and the raw fish plates are the kitchen's calling cards. It is a splurge and a scene, so dress for the room and book ahead. Its site lists opening to midnight Sunday to Wednesday, and until late Thursday to Saturday.

Quinto at EAST Miami

Quinto, at 788 Brickell Plaza inside EAST Miami, is the Miami version of Uruguay's Parador La Huella, built around a wood fire parrilla, a wood oven, and a big outdoor terrace. The grilled octopus and the fire cooked meats are the orders, and the terrace makes it the Brickell dinner that feels least like a business meal. It is a splurge, suits dates and visitors staying nearby, and also runs one of the neighborhood's better weekend brunches.

The business safe classics

Truluck's

Truluck's, at 777 Brickell Avenue, is the seafood and steak answer for a dinner that has to land. Its own site highlights Florida stone crab served in season, responsibly sourced seafood, and prime steaks, in a sleek room with live piano music. Order stone crab claws when they are in season and the miso glazed sea bass when they are not. It is a splurge that suits business dinners and occasions, and its kitchen also makes the key lime pie on our dessert list, in house.

The Capital Grille

The Capital Grille, at 444 Brickell Avenue, is the classic American steakhouse of the neighborhood: steaks dry aged in house and cut by the restaurant's own butcher, per the chain's site, plus a deep wine list. The dry aged strip and the lobster mac and cheese are the standard orders. It is a splurge, formal without being stiff, and the safest pick on this page for a client dinner or a family occasion where nobody wants a surprise.

B Bistro and Bakery

B Bistro and Bakery, at 600 Brickell Avenue, is Brickell's daytime counterweight, a bistro and bakery by chef Henry Hane serving breakfast, brunch, and lunch. Its own menu names pastelito pancakes, a lump crab eggs benedict on a charcoal croissant, and a case of baked goods as the draws. It is mid range, bright, and the place to send anyone whose Brickell day starts before the steakhouses wake up. Go on a weekend morning for the full brunch menu.

Stone crab season, briefly

Truluck's serves Florida stone crab in season. The Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission sets the stone crab harvest season in state waters as October 15 to May 1, and only the claws may be harvested, which is why Brickell menus list claws rather than whole crabs. Outside the season, order the rest of the seafood menu and save the claws for a return trip.

Frequently asked questions

Which Brickell restaurant is best for a business dinner?
Truluck's and The Capital Grille, in that order. Both are polished, take reservations, and run rooms where a conversation carries. Komodo is the pick when the business dinner is meant to feel like a night out instead.

What is the most affordable restaurant on this Brickell list?
B Bistro and Bakery, by a wide margin. It is a daytime bistro and bakery, so plan it for breakfast, brunch, or lunch rather than dinner.

Do Brickell restaurants need reservations?
For dinner at Komodo, Sexy Fish Miami, Quinto, and Truluck's, yes, especially Thursday to Saturday. Lunch is easier, and B Bistro is walk in.

Where can you eat late in Brickell?
Sexy Fish Miami lists the latest hours in the neighborhood, to midnight Sunday to Wednesday and until late Thursday to Saturday on its site. For later than that, see our late night page, which is built on listed closing times only.
