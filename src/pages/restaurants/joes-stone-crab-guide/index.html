Joe's Stone Crab Guide (Season, Lines, Alternatives)

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Joe's Stone Crab is at 11 Washington Avenue in South Beach, Miami Beach. It was established in 1913. Florida stone crab season runs October 15 to May 1. The main restaurant takes reservations through its own site, and Joe's Take Away next door offers the same signature items in a casual, no reservation format. Go in season, book the dining room if your timing is fixed, and use Take Away if the line or the formality does not suit your day.

The place and its history

Joe's Stone Crab occupies 11 Washington Avenue at the southern end of Miami Beach, in the South of Fifth area of South Beach. Its own site describes it as established in 1913, which makes a meal here part restaurant visit and part Miami history stop. The dining room is large and formal by Miami Beach standards, with a pace built around the season. If you only have one splurge seafood dinner in Miami, this is the address most first time visitors choose, which is why planning matters more here than at any other restaurant in our Miami seafood guide.

Season: when the claws are fresh

The Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission sets the stone crab harvest season in state waters as October 15 to May 1, closed on May 2. Joe's own menu states the same season, October 15 to May 1, and adds that stone crabs are available year round. Read those two facts together: in season you can ask for fresh claws with confidence, and out of season the kitchen can still serve claws, but you should ask how they were stored if freshness is the point of your visit. Florida rules allow only claws to be harvested and the crab is returned to the water, which is why the menu is built around claws by size rather than whole crabs. Claw sizes and pricing change through the season, so this page does not publish prices.

Season at a glance

Harvest season: October 15 to May 1 (Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, state waters). Closed from May 2. What is served: claws only, chilled, cracked, with mustard sauce, per Joe's own menu description. Address: 11 Washington Avenue, Miami Beach, FL 33139, per Joe's own location page.

Reservations, lines, and the Take Away answer

Joe's official site runs reservations for the main restaurant, and its location page directs diners there. Book through the official site as early as your date is offered, especially for weekend dinners in the heart of the season, and treat any third party promise of a guaranteed table with caution. Historically the room was famous for waits, and demand still peaks on season opening, holidays, and weekend evenings, so a reservation is the difference between a planned dinner and a hopeful one. The practical alternative is Joe's Take Away, right next door at the same Washington Avenue address. Joe's describes Take Away as a casual market style space with counter service and cafe seating, no reservation needed, serving signature items including stone crabs, hash browns, cole slaw, creamed spinach, grilled tomatoes, and key lime pie, plus dishes of its own such as a lobster roll and fried chicken. For many visitors, Take Away is the faster, more casual way to taste the same kitchen.

What to order

Start with stone crab claws, chilled and cracked, with the mustard sauce the house serves them with. Add hash browns and creamed spinach, the two sides most associated with the room, and consider the fried chicken, which Joe's menus list alongside the seafood and which regulars order for the table. Finish with key lime pie. If you are ordering at Take Away, the same logic applies in smaller form: claws if they are in season, a lobster roll or fried chicken if they are not, and pie either way. Ask your server what claw sizes are running that day, because portions are sold by size, not by a fixed plate.

Alternatives if Joe's does not work

If the reservation book is full, your timing is outside the season, or the splurge does not fit the trip, Miami gives you good options. Garcia's Seafood Grille on the Miami River serves stone crab in season in a working waterfront setting. Monty's Raw Bar in Coconut Grove offers a casual marina version of a raw bar afternoon. Casablanca, also on the Miami River, pairs a fish market with a more dressed up dining room. All three are covered, with addresses, in our best seafood restaurants in Miami guide, and several also appear in the waterfront restaurants guide. None of them is Joe's, and that is the point: they trade ceremony for ease.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a reservation for Joe's Stone Crab?
For the main dining room, use the reservations route on the official site, joesstonecrab.com, and book as early as your date allows. For Joe's Take Away next door, the official site says no reservation is needed and the format is counter service with cafe seating.

When does Joe's serve fresh stone crab?
Florida stone crab season is October 15 to May 1. Joe's menu notes that season and says stone crabs are available year round, so outside the season, ask whether the claws served are fresh or previously frozen.

What is the difference between the restaurant and Take Away?
The restaurant is the full service, formal dining room. Take Away, at the same 11 Washington Avenue address, is casual: counter service, cafe seating, and a menu built from Joe's signature items plus a few dishes of its own, per the official site.

Where do I park or arrive?
Joe's is at the southern tip of Miami Beach in the South of Fifth neighborhood. Allow extra time for Miami Beach traffic and parking on weekend evenings in season, and consider arriving early enough to walk South Pointe after or before your meal.
