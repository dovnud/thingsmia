Best Food Halls in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. For downtown, choose Julia and Henry's on Flagler Street. For South Beach, The Lincoln Eatery by Lincoln Road. For Wynwood, 1-800-Lucky for Asian food halls and Smorgasburg Miami on weekends for an open air market. For Doral, the hall at 9420 NW 41st Street now operates as City Food Hall Doral, formerly Shoma Bazaar. Note that Time Out Market Miami closed in 2023 and is not an option. Every place below was checked as operating on October 30, 2026.

The halls, by neighborhood

Julia and Henry's, Downtown

Julia and Henry's, at 200 East Flagler Street in downtown Miami, is a multilevel food hall in a historic 1936 building, named for Miami founders Julia Tuttle and Henry Flagler. Its own site describes it as a multilevel culinary and entertainment destination in the heart of downtown. Order by floor and appetite: use it as a lunch crawl, giving each person one stall and sharing a dessert upstairs. It suits downtown workers, visitors based in Brickell or downtown, and groups who want many cuisines plus bars and entertainment in one building. Price level: mid range.

The Lincoln Eatery, Miami Beach

The Lincoln Eatery, at 723 Lincoln Lane North in Miami Beach, sits just off Lincoln Road and gathers fast casual and grab and go concepts under one roof, with seating for groups. Order whatever stall is cooking to order rather than sitting ready made, and use the hall as your flexible meal around Lincoln Road shopping and the beach. It suits South Beach visitors who want one easy answer for a mixed group, families who need speed, and anyone priced out of full service Ocean Drive dining. Vendor lineups rotate, so treat the current stall list on its own site as the source of truth. Price level: budget friendly to mid range.

1-800-Lucky, Wynwood

1-800-Lucky, at 143 NW 23rd Street in Wynwood, is an Asian food hall with multiple stalls, bars, and a record shop and karaoke element in its own description of the space. Order across stalls: something fried, something noodle based, and a taiyaki style ice cream to finish. It suits Wynwood evenings, groups who all want Asian food but different dishes, and visitors who want dinner to turn into a night out without changing venues. The energy rises later in the evening, so families may prefer an earlier visit. Price level: mid range.

City Food Hall Doral, formerly Shoma Bazaar, Doral

The food hall at 9420 NW 41st Street in Doral now lists itself as City Food Hall Doral, formerly Shoma Bazaar, with multiple culinary partners plus a bar and biergarten format carried over from the Shoma era. Order as a group crawl and use the communal seating and event programming as the evening's entertainment. It suits Doral residents, airport area stays, and anyone west of the city who does not want to drive downtown for variety. Because the name has changed, search for the current name when you navigate. Price level: mid range.

Smorgasburg Miami, Wynwood (weekend market)

Smorgasburg Miami, at 2600 NW 2nd Avenue in Wynwood, is an open air weekend food market rather than an indoor hall. Coverage of its return reported a reopening for the 2025 to 2026 season on the weekend of October 4 and 5, with Saturday and Sunday daytime hours and free entry. Order small items from several vendors rather than one large plate, because discovery is the point. It suits weekend visitors to Wynwood, families, and grazers. Because it is seasonal and weekend only, check its official site or social channels for the current weekend before you go. Price level: budget friendly to mid range, set by how many stalls you visit.

Closed, and a name change

Time Out Market Miami, once the best known food hall in Miami Beach, closed in 2023. Do not plan around it, whatever older guides say. Separately, the Doral hall at 9420 NW 41st Street that many guides list as Shoma Bazaar now operates as City Food Hall Doral. Both facts were checked on October 30, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best food hall for a group that cannot agree?
Julia and Henry's downtown, for the widest spread of cuisines in one building, or 1-800-Lucky if the group leans Asian. In both cases, claim a shared table first, then order in rounds so hot food does not wait while others queue.

Is Time Out Market Miami still open?
No. Time Out Market Miami closed in 2023. If a guide lists it as a current option, that guide is out of date. Use The Lincoln Eatery for a Miami Beach food hall instead.

Are food halls good for a budget?
They can be. Each person orders and pays separately, so there is no bill splitting problem, and counter formats keep prices below full service rooms. Set a per person limit first, because small add ons across stalls add up. See also cheap eats in Miami.

Do food halls take reservations?
The format is generally walk in and counter based, with communal seating. For a large group or an event space, check the hall's own site, because some offer bookable areas even though everyday dining is first come.
