Checked facts

The Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission sets the stone crab harvest season in state waters as October 15 to May 1, closed on May 2.

Under the same Florida rules, only stone crab claws may be harvested, which is why Miami menus list claws rather than whole crabs.

Common questions

When is stone crab season in Miami?

Florida's stone crab harvest season in state waters runs October 15 to May 1, per the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. Restaurants serve claws in season, and frozen product outside it, so ask if the season matters to you.

Why do menus sell stone crab claws instead of whole crabs?

Florida rules allow only the claws to be harvested. The Joe's Stone Crab guide above covers ordering, sizes, and what to expect at the table.

Where should I eat Cuban food in Miami?

Start in Little Havana, where the Versailles guide and the Little Havana restaurant list above concentrate. The Cuban restaurant guide widens the net across the city.

How do I eat well in Miami without overspending?

Use the cheap eats and food hall guides above, order Cuban coffee at a ventanita rather than a cafe markup, and save the splurge for one seafood or steakhouse dinner.
