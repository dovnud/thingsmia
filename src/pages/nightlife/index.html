Frequently asked questions

What is the legal drinking age in Miami?
21, the same as the rest of the United States. Bars and clubs check ID at the door, and visitors should carry a passport or other accepted identification rather than a photo of one.

What time do Miami clubs actually get going?
Late. Headline clubs typically fill after midnight and peak in the small hours, which is later than most US cities. Rooftop bars and Wynwood patios run on an earlier clock, which is why many locals do both in one night.

Do I need a table reservation or a guest list spot?
It depends on the room and the night. The biggest clubs are easiest with a reservation on busy nights; smaller bars and speakeasies are often first come. Each venue guide above explains how that specific door works.

What should I wear?
Miami's big rooms enforce dress codes, typically no beachwear, athletic wear, or flip flops at night. Smart going out clothes are the safe default; beach clubs by day are the exception, and their guide explains the difference.
