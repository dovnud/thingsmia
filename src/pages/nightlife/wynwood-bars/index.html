Best Bars in Wynwood

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Wynwood's bar scene grew out of its warehouses, and it still runs on three registers: big brewery gardens, beloved dives, and small serious cocktail rooms, all within a few mural covered blocks of NW 24th Street and NW 2nd Avenue. Start at a brewery in the late afternoon light, eat from the district's kitchens, then pick your register for the night. Everything here is walkable once you are parked, and the murals are the street lighting's competition.

The brewery gardens

Cerveceria La Tropical is the anchor: the Cuban heritage brand, revived in a big Wynwood brewery with a tropical garden, live music programming and a full kitchen, and the most family friendly of the district's drinking addresses in the early evening. Around it, the district's taprooms and beer gardens rotate taps and food trucks through the week. This is the soft start to a Wynwood night, and the reason to arrive before dark: the same streets give you the murals at golden hour and the first round in one walk.

Gramps and the dive register

Gramps, on NW 24th Street, is Wynwood's institution: a no pretense bar and venue with a big patio, cheap drinks by district standards, comedy, karaoke and live bills that have made it the neighborhood's living room for a decade. Every district needs one bar that belongs to the people who live there; in Wynwood it is this one. If your night wants one honest room rather than a circuit, Gramps and a pizza is a complete and correct Wynwood evening.

The cocktail rooms and listening bars

The polished register: The Sylvester, a mid century styled cocktail lounge on NW 26th Street with pool tables and a serious drinks program in a relaxed room, and Dante's HiFi, the Japanese style listening bar where the vinyl and the sound system are the point and reservations pace the room. Both prove Wynwood can do quiet as well as loud. Add the hotel free, warehouse small bars along 24th and 25th as you find them; the district's churn means the newest room is rarely in any guide, including this one.

How a Wynwood night flows

Park once, in the garages off NW 2nd Avenue, and walk. The district's night geography is compact: breweries toward NE 24th, bars along NW 24th and 25th, restaurants threaded through. Weekends bring the crowds and the rideshare surge at closing; weeknights belong to the neighborhood and are better for it. Wynwood pairs with the Design District for a culture heavy day into night, planned in our Wynwood guide. For the city's other bar dialects, the cocktail guide and speakeasies complete the map.

Getting home and the late night logistics

Wynwood's night ends the way it began, on foot, and the logistics are simple if you plan the exit. Rideshare pickups run cleanest from the avenue edges rather than the bar blocks themselves; walking a block or two to NW 2nd Avenue or the 29th Street side cuts the surge shuffle. The district is well lit and busy until late on weekends, quieter and shorter on weeknights. Drivers should use the garages and stay off the mural streets' curbs, which are ticketed enthusiastically. The final local touch: several kitchens run late windows on weekends, and a Wynwood night that ends with food ends better.

The facts that matter

Anchors on this page: Cerveceria La Tropical (Cuban brand founded 1888, revived with a Wynwood brewery and garden), Gramps (NW 24th Street), The Sylvester (NW 26th Street), Dante's HiFi (listening bar, Wynwood). Wynwood's bar core runs along NW 24th and 25th Streets around NW 2nd Avenue. Venues in this district open, close and rebrand faster than any guide cycle; every pick here was a going concern at the October 30, 2026 check, and a venue's own page is the tiebreaker.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wynwood good for bar hopping?
It is built for it: breweries, dives and cocktail rooms inside a few walkable blocks, with the murals as the walk between.

What is the one Wynwood bar for a first timer?
Gramps for the neighborhood as it is, La Tropical for the polished version. Locals would send you to both.

Do Wynwood bars have covers?
Mostly no at the bars and breweries; special event nights are the exception. Clubs and ticketed shows price separately.

Is Wynwood lively on weeknights?
Yes, more locally. The brewery and bar core runs all week, with weekends bringing the regional crowd.
