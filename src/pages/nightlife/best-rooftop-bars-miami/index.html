Best Rooftop Bars in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami's rooftops divide by view. For skyline drama, Brickell's high decks lead, above all Sugar on the 40th floor of the EAST hotel. For bay sunsets, downtown's hotel terraces face west over Biscayne Bay. For ocean air, South Beach's hotel rooftops sit lower and closer to the sand. All of them run on the same clock: golden hour is the product, reservations or early arrival secure it, and the summer rain shower at 6 p.m. is a cast member, not a surprise.

Brickell: the skyline decks

Sugar, the garden rooftop on the 40th floor of EAST Miami at Brickell City Centre, is the city's signature rooftop: Asian accented small plates, a jungle of planting, and the Brickell skyline at eye level. It books out on weekends; reserve or arrive before the after work wave. A few blocks north, Area 31 on the 16th floor of the Kimpton EPIC trades Sugar's garden for a clean bay and river panorama and a seafood kitchen, and it is the downtown sunset seat proper. Both sit inside the Brickell grid, which makes dinner downstairs and drinks upstairs a one elevator evening.

South Beach: ocean level rooftops

Beach rooftops run lower and breezier. Watr, the rooftop at 1 Hotel South Beach, pairs its pool deck with bay to ocean views and a Japanese inflected menu, and it is the special occasion pick on the sand. The rooftops of the Collins Avenue hotels, the Clevelander's deck over Ocean Drive among them, sell the classic postcard: Art Deco below, ocean in front. In the South of Fifth, the hotel decks run calmer and more local. Our South Beach guide places each of them on the map.

The neighborhood rooftops

Two inland decks deserve the trip. Terras, the rooftop at Life House in Little Havana, looks back at the downtown skyline over the neighborhood's rooftops, with a tropical planting scheme and a Latin leaning kitchen; sunset there is a local's recommendation that has not yet been ruined. In South Beach's west side, the Moxy's Serena rooftop does the colorful, courtyard version. Both prove the rooftop rule: the view does not need the ocean if the skyline performs.

Timing, weather and dress

Rooftops are weather businesses. In summer, the afternoon thunderstorm is a near daily appointment; decks close in lightning and reopen after, and the post rain hour is often the clearest of the day. In winter, the decks are the best seats in the city and the hardest to get. Dress codes run smart casual at the hotel decks, stricter at the Brickell flagships on weekend nights. Prices sit at hotel levels across the board; the view is the cover charge. For the opposite of all this altitude and polish, our speakeasies guide goes downstairs and dark.

What it costs and what to order, honestly

Rooftop pricing is hotel pricing: cocktails sit in the city's upper band and small plates carry a view premium, and this page quotes no figures because menus reprint faster than guides. The value strategy is golden hour itself: one or two drinks at sunset deliver the whole product, and dinner can happen at street level prices afterward. Order the house's signature rather than your usual; the decks compete on one or two drinks each, often built on rum, citrus and tropical bitters that suit the climate. If a deck is running a private event, and the Brickell flagships often are, the concierge desk downstairs can usually say where the same view is open tonight.

The facts that matter

The picks on this page are editorial, chosen for view, consistency and setting: Sugar (EAST Miami, Brickell City Centre, 40th floor), Area 31 (Kimpton EPIC, downtown, 16th floor), Watr (1 Hotel South Beach rooftop), Terras (Life House, Little Havana), Serena (Moxy South Beach). Rooftop hours, menus and reservation policies change by season and event; each venue's own page is the final word, checked when you book.

Frequently asked questions

Which rooftop is best for sunset?
For the bay and skyline, Area 31 and Terras face the sunset. Sugar's garden and skyline work at any hour after dark.

Do Miami rooftops take reservations?
The flagship decks do and should be booked on weekends. Smaller hotel decks are walk in with a wait at golden hour.

What happens to rooftops in the rain?
They pause. Lightning closes open decks; most reopen after the cell passes, which in summer is often within the hour.

Are rooftops 21 plus?
Hotel decks generally welcome hotel guests and diners of all ages at restaurant hours and run adult at late hours; check the venue for your timing.
