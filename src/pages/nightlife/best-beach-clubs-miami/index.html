Best Beach Clubs in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. A Miami beach club day is a booking, not a walk in: the famous names run on reservations, daybed minimums and door lists, above all on weekends and through the winter season. Pick by intent. Nikki Beach is the party institution at the tip of South Beach. The hotel clubs, Hyde Beach at the SLS and Strawberry Moon at The Goodtime Hotel among them, sell the polished pool party. The Standard, on Belle Isle, sells the opposite: a garden, a pool and quiet. All three are good days; they are not the same day.

The institutions: Nikki Beach and the party circuit

Nikki Beach, at 1 Washington Avenue where South Beach ends, has run the barefoot luxury day party since the late 1990s: white daybeds, a beach restaurant, DJs building through the afternoon and a Sunday session that is a Miami ritual. It is the default answer for birthdays, bachelorettes and anyone who wants the scene with sand under it. Book the daybed or table before you fly; weekend walk ins are a queue and a hope. The party circuit's other big rooms, the SLS's Hyde Beach in season among them, run on the same reservation logic and peak at the same hours, 2 to 6 p.m.

The hotel day clubs

The Collins Avenue hotels have turned their pool decks into day clubs with a cover or a minimum, and the best of them work for non guests who book properly. Strawberry Moon, the pool club at The Goodtime Hotel in mid South Beach, does the pastel, Mediterranean, DJ at brunch version. The beachfront decks of the big Mid Beach resorts sell cabana days with full hotel service behind them. Our hotel guides, the Fontainebleau among them, flag which decks open to outside bookings; policies change by season, so confirm at booking, not at the pool gate.

The calm register: The Standard and Joia Beach

For the anti party, two addresses. The Standard Spa on Belle Isle runs an adults oriented garden and pool day built around its spa and hydrotherapy circuit, day passes when available, and a crowd reading books rather than bottle menus. Joia Beach, on Watson Island by Jungle Island, puts tables and daybeds on a private feeling stretch of sand facing the downtown skyline, more restaurant with a beach than club with a kitchen. Both prove the beach club idea stretches from a whisper to a shout in this city.

Booking reality: minimums, timing, weather

The economics: prime weekend daybeds and cabanas carry food and drink minimums that climb with the season, and the honest price is whatever the venue quotes for your date, which is why this page prints none. The day peaks mid afternoon; arrive by noon to own your spot. Summer storms clear decks fast and usually briefly. Dress is swimwear that reads intentional, cover ups for the restaurant parts, and sunscreen applied before the first round, a rule our sunscreen guide exists to support.

The facts that matter

Anchors: Nikki Beach is at 1 Washington Avenue, South Beach, operating since the late 1990s. Hyde Beach is the SLS South Beach's pool venue. Strawberry Moon is the pool club at The Goodtime Hotel. The Standard Spa, Miami Beach is on Belle Isle (Island Avenue) and is adults oriented. Joia Beach is on Watson Island. Day access, minimums and outside guest policies are set per venue and per date and are not quoted here; each venue's booking page governs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a beach club in advance?
On weekends and all winter season, yes for the famous names. Weekday and off season walk ins fare better but the reservation is still the guarantee.

Can I go to a hotel beach club without staying there?
Several sell day access or cabana bookings to non guests; policies change by season. Confirm when you book, not on arrival.

What is the best beach club for a quiet day?
The Standard on Belle Isle, with Joia Beach as the lunch led alternative. Both are the opposite of the day party circuit.

What should I budget?
Treat a prime weekend daybed as a group expense with a food and drink minimum, quoted per date by the venue. Weekdays and calm clubs cost a fraction of the peak.
