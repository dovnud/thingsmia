LIV Miami Guide (Fontainebleau)

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. LIV is the nightclub inside the Fontainebleau Miami Beach at 4441 Collins Avenue, and it remains the name brand of Miami nightlife: a vast, high production room of headline DJs, table service and confetti, running late nights at the end of the week. The venue's own calendar is the only reliable source for which nights and which artists; recent seasons have run Thursday through Sunday with doors at 11:30 p.m. Expect a door policy that rewards dressing up and arriving with a plan, tickets or a table booked ahead rather than hope.

The room and the night

LIV occupies the Fontainebleau's old La Ronde Room footprint, the space where Sinatra era Miami performed, rebuilt as a multi level club of dance floor, mezzanine and skyboxes under a kinetic ceiling of lights. The production is the product: headline EDM and hip hop names, festival grade sound and lighting, and a crowd that dresses for the cameras. Nights build late; the room's real hours are 1 to 4 a.m. whatever the door time says, and the headline set lands later still. If your taste runs to small rooms and conversation, this is not your club, and Miami has better answers for you in our speakeasies guide.

Tickets, tables and the door

There are three ways in: advance tickets for specific event nights sold through the venue's official site, table reservations booked with the venue's VIP team, and the door itself, which is the least reliable of the three on big nights. The venue enforces a dress code and a 21 plus ID rule, and door discretion is a real feature of Miami clubs, not a myth: groups that read as a problem wait, and solo arrivals in beach clothes wait forever. We quote no prices here on purpose: covers and table minimums swing wildly by artist and season, and any number printed in October would be wrong by your weekend. The venue's site is the price source, checked the week you go.

Which night, and the holiday factor

The club's themed nights have historically given the week a rhythm, with Sundays carrying a particular reputation among locals, and holiday weeks, above all New Year's week and Art Basel week, running extended calendars with the biggest bookings of the year. If your Miami dates include a holiday weekend, LIV is likely to be both the best and the hardest door of your trip; book the ticket the day your travel is fixed. Our New Year's Eve guide covers the season's biggest night across the city.

Staying over and getting home

The Fontainebleau itself is the zero logistics option, and our Fontainebleau guide covers the resort around the club. Mid Beach and South Beach hotels are a short rideshare down Collins; at 5 a.m. closing, rideshare surge is a fact of life, and the walk to the taxi line is part of the night. Do not drive: parking at the resort is priced for guests, and Miami Beach enforcement on club nights is enthusiastic.

Is LIV worth it?

For the traveler who wants the definitive Miami mega club once, yes: there is one LIV, it is in the Fontainebleau, and the production justifies the pilgrimage. For a whole weekend of nights out, mix it: LIV for the spectacle, a Wynwood bar night and a proper cocktail bar for the craft. The rest of this section plans exactly that mix.

Frequently asked questions

What nights is LIV open?
Recent seasons have run Thursday through Sunday, with special calendars on holiday weeks. The venue's official calendar is the authority for your dates; some nights are private events or dark.

How do I guarantee entry?
Buy the advance ticket for your event night from the venue's official site, or book a table with its VIP team. The walk up door is discretionary, especially on headline nights.

What is the dress code?
Upscale and enforced. Beachwear, shorts and athletic shoes are the classic refusals for men; the venue publishes its current code with each event.

What time should I arrive?
For ticketed entry, around doors or shortly after is painless. The room peaks after 1 a.m.; the headline set is later still.
