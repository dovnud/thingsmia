The room as it was

STORY opened in 2012 as the second act from the creators of LIV, scaled differently: 27,000 square feet by the venue's own description, a single vast floor wrapped with VIP tables, five bars, and the Infinite Hybrid sound system and concert scale lighting that made it the techno and big room room of Miami Beach, in deliberate contrast to LIV's glamour. Its circus style production transformed the room nightly, and its bookings ran from EDM headliners to the underground leaning Link Miami Rebels nights that gave it credibility with the serious crowd. For a decade it was the answer to a specific question: where is the biggest room in South Beach tonight.

Why the status matters, and what we know

Clubs of this scale live on leases and licenses, and South Beach real estate has consumed famous rooms before. What we can state from the public record as of October 30, 2026 is narrow: the venue's address is 136 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL 33139; its published phone is (305) 479-4426; listing services currently mark it temporarily closed; and its own website is the only source that can confirm a reopening, a relocation or a farewell. This page will be updated when the venue itself publishes its future. Until then, treat any third party selling STORY tickets for near dates with suspicion, and buy nothing you cannot refund.

If it reopens: how a STORY night worked

The pattern was the mega club standard, executed at scale: doors late, around 11 p.m., the room building past 1 a.m., headline sets in the small hours, close at 5. Tickets through the venue's channels for event nights, tables through its VIP line, dress code enforced, 21 plus. The South of Fifth location put it a short walk from the Ocean Drive hotels and a quick rideshare from everywhere else on the beach. Keep this paragraph as the template if the lights come back on.

Where to go instead, tonight

The direct substitute is LIV, same creators, same scale, running at the Fontainebleau; our LIV guide plans that night properly. For the underground leaning crowd STORY's Rebels nights served, the Warehouse and club spaces of downtown and Little River carry the techno calendar now, and Wynwood's rooms handle the middle ground. The nightlife hub maps all of it, and the tracked link below carries Miami's bookable nightlife, tours and event tickets for your dates.

The neighborhood around it: South of Fifth

Whatever STORY's own future, its neighborhood remains a complete night out. South of Fifth is the beach's calm, expensive end: a cluster of serious restaurants, the park at South Pointe with its sunset over the cruise channel, and the marina promenade. The classic STORY era evening ran dinner in SoFi, the club after midnight, and the walk along the water to recover; the dinner and the walk still work perfectly, and they pair with LIV or the downtown rooms as the late chapter. Plan the neighborhood, and the night survives any single venue's dark doors.
