Halloween in Miami 2026

Halloween falls on a Saturday in 2026, and Miami treats a Saturday Halloween as a civic holiday. The center of it all is Miami Beach, where Lincoln Road closes its pedestrian promenade for the annual Halloween Block Party on Saturday October 31: family trick or treating in the early evening, then one of the great costume parades in America until the early hours. Wynwood answers with its HalloWYN block party, Coconut Grove keeps it family sized, and every club and rooftop in the city programs the night. This hub covers the confirmed 2026 events first, then the evergreen guidance for doing Halloween in a city where it is 80 degrees at midnight.

Halloween 2026: the confirmed facts

  • The date: Saturday, October 31, 2026.
  • Lincoln Road Halloween Block Party, Miami Beach: free. Family trick or treating with candy stations and face painting from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.; the main block party runs from 8 p.m. into the early hours, with live music including the Wonderama Band and DJs. More than 17 Lincoln Road restaurants and bars run specials along the route.
  • Parking: Miami Beach holds parking at $1 per hour through October 31, per the Lincoln Road Business Improvement District.
  • HalloWYN, Wynwood: the arts district's Halloween block party takes over Wynwood on Halloween night, October 31.
  • Coconut Grove: a family Halloween celebration on Fuller Street on October 31, with storytelling at Books & Books Coconut Grove at 6 p.m.
  • Earlier in the week: Zoo Miami's family event Zoo Boo ran October 24 and 25, 2026.

Sources: Lincoln Road BID event announcements and the Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau, checked October 30, 2026.

Lincoln Road: the main event

If you do one thing on Halloween in Miami, do Lincoln Road. The evening runs in two acts. From 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. it belongs to families: trick or treating along the promenade, candy stations, crafts, and costumed characters, with the restaurants' terraces full of parents watching it unfold. Then the light changes. From 8 p.m. the block party takes over all eight blocks of the pedestrian mall, and Lincoln Road becomes an open air catwalk: tens of thousands of people, costumes that took months, live bands and DJs, and a crowd whose real activity is looking at each other. There is no ticket, no gate, and no program to keep; you walk it end to end, eat and drink at the promenade restaurants (more than 17 of them run Halloween specials, per the BID), and let the parade come to you. Arrive by trolley, bus, or rideshare: Miami Beach traffic on Halloween night is a costume of its own, and the city's $1 per hour parking rate through October 31 still requires finding a space. Wynwood, downtown, and the club night

The mainland's answer is HalloWYN, Wynwood's Halloween block party, where the arts district closes streets for stages, costume crowds, and the warehouse bars' biggest night of the fall. It skews younger and louder than Lincoln Road, and the murals make the best costume backdrop in the city. Downtown and Brickell split the difference with rooftop parties and hotel events, and the big clubs across the city program Halloween headline sets; those lineups sell through the venues in the final weeks of October, so check the rooms you care about directly. A word of logistics for all of it: rideshare surge on Halloween night is the worst of the Miami year after New Year's Eve. Groups do better pre booking a car, walking a few blocks out of the closed zones before ordering, or simply staying late enough that the first wave home has passed.

Family Halloween

Miami is genuinely good at the children's version of this holiday. Zoo Boo at Zoo Miami, the season's family anchor, ran its 2026 edition on October 24 and 25 with costumes, trick or treat stations, and the zoo's animals in their Halloween programming. On the night itself, Coconut Grove's Fuller Street celebration is the neighborhood scale option: a street party with storytelling at Books & Books at 6 p.m., trick or treating, and a crowd of Grove families rather than a mega event. Lincoln Road's early shift (5:30 to 7:30 p.m.) is the third good answer, and the rule everywhere is the same: do the family hours, enjoy them fully, and be home before the city's adult Halloween clocks in at nine. Costumes for children in Miami need one adjustment: plan for heat and a possible rain shower, because October 31 in South Florida is a summer night wearing a costume.

Costumes and comfort, Miami rules

Everything about Halloween costuming changes at 80 degrees with humidity. Choose breathable builds over full suits, test any makeup against sweat before the night (it will run), and treat footwear as the real costume decision: Lincoln Road's promenade and Wynwood's streets mean miles of standing and walking, and the best costume in Miami is the one whose owner can still walk at midnight. Replica weapons and realistic props are a hard no at venues and a bad idea in street crowds; security across the city's events will take them, and should. Keep a phone charged and a meeting point set, because the block parties are exactly the scale where groups separate. Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest Halloween event in Miami?
The Lincoln Road Halloween Block Party in Miami Beach: free, all evening on Saturday October 31, 2026, family trick or treating from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. and the main party from 8 p.m. into the early hours.

Is there a family friendly option on the night itself?
Yes: Lincoln Road's early hours, and Coconut Grove's Fuller Street celebration with storytelling at Books & Books at 6 p.m. Zoo Boo at Zoo Miami ran earlier in the week, October 24 and 25.

What should I know about costumes in Miami?
It will be hot and humid. Light, breathable costumes, sweat proof makeup, and walking shoes beat elaborate builds. Leave realistic prop weapons at home; venues will confiscate them.
