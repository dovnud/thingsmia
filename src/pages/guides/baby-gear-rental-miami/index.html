Baby Gear Rental in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Flying to Miami with a baby does not have to mean flying with a nursery. Miami's baby gear rental market runs on local providers, most visibly through BabyQuip, who deliver and set up cribs, strollers, car seats, and toy packages at hotels, Airbnbs, the airport, and even cruise ports, often before you arrive. You pack the baby, the diapers, and the one lovey that cannot be replaced; the furniture meets you there.

What you can rent, and how it arrives

The core rental list from Miami providers covers the heavy and bulky end of baby logistics: full size cribs, pack and plays and sleep pods, strollers including full travel systems, and car seats, plus the supporting cast of toys and beach gear. The model, as BabyQuip's Miami providers describe it, is delivery and setup: your gear is delivered and set up when and where you need it, coordinated with your provider for a delivery window, including hotel delivery arranged before your arrival, so the crib is standing in the room when you walk in with a tired baby.

One Miami provider's stated service area covers Miami and the surrounding areas including Brickell, Doral, Key Biscayne, and MIA airport, and South Florida listings explicitly offer delivery to hotels, Airbnbs and vacation rentals, airports, and the cruise ports at PortMiami and Port Everglades. Local pickup and drop off is also offered on Miami listings if you would rather collect gear yourself, with the address provided after you reserve.

The decisions that matter

Crib versus pack and play is the first call: a full size crib matches home for a baby who sleeps in one, travels worst, and is exactly why this service exists; a pack and play or sleep pod is the lighter footprint option for a small hotel room. Car seat is the second call, and the honest framing is convenience versus familiarity: rental seats from established providers are cleaned and inspected per the provider's stated process, but some families prefer their own seat for a baby with strong opinions, in which case rent everything else and check the seat as luggage.

Stroller choice should follow your Miami, not a generic list. A full travel system stroller handles the boardwalk, the zoo, and the airport; an umbrella stroller is the right second stroller for a toddler who mostly walks. Beach days add one more rental thought: shade and a wagon beat carrying a hot baby across hot sand, and several providers list beach packages.

Booking pattern and cruise notes

Book when you book the trip, not the week of travel: individual providers hold limited inventories of the popular items, and holiday weeks in Miami (Christmas, spring break, Art Week) drain them. Prices, delivery fees, and minimum rental periods are set by each individual provider and vary, so this page does not quote figures; compare two or three provider shops for your dates and read the delivery terms before paying.

Cruise families: PortMiami and Port Everglades delivery means the gear can meet you at the port or your pre cruise hotel and be collected after the sailing, a pattern that pairs with a pre cruise night in Miami. Frequently asked questions

What baby gear can I rent in Miami?
Full size cribs, pack and plays and sleep pods, strollers and travel systems, car seats, toys, and beach gear, through local providers on platforms such as BabyQuip.

Can gear be delivered before we arrive?
Yes. Providers coordinate a delivery window and can deliver and set up at your hotel before arrival, as well as to Airbnbs, the airport, and cruise ports.

How much does baby gear rental cost in Miami?
Prices, delivery fees, and minimum rental periods are set by each provider and vary by item and dates. Compare provider listings for your exact dates rather than relying on quoted averages.

Is a rental car seat a good idea?
It is the convenience choice most visiting families make, from providers who state their cleaning and inspection process. If your baby strongly prefers their own seat, rent the crib and stroller and check your seat as luggage.
