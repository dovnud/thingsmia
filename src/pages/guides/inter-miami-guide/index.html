Inter Miami Visitor Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The single most important update for any visitor: Inter Miami no longer plays in Fort Lauderdale. The club's permanent home, Nu Stadium at Miami Freedom Park, opened on April 4, 2026, with a match against Austin FC, after the club's first six seasons at Chase Stadium in Fort Lauderdale. The new stadium holds 26,700 seats and sits inside the 131 acre Miami Freedom Park district in Miami itself, which changes every piece of match day logistics older guides describe.

The stadium move, stated plainly

Inter Miami CF played its first six seasons at its Fort Lauderdale stadium, known most recently as Chase Stadium, roughly 30 miles north of Miami. In April 2026 the first team moved home: Nu Stadium, the centerpiece of the Miami Freedom Park district, opened with Inter Miami hosting Austin FC on April 4, 2026. The Fort Lauderdale ground remains in the club's system for its reserve side, but if you are buying a ticket to see the first team, you are going to Miami, not Fort Lauderdale, and any guide that still routes you to Chase Stadium is out of date.

The club describes Nu Stadium as a 26,700 seat home in the heart of Miami. Premium spaces include the Nu Club, a 770 person lounge with a glass tunnel view of players entering the pitch, and Nu Plaza, the fan hub at the ground. The wider Miami Freedom Park district covers 131 acres, so the area around the stadium is being built out as an entertainment district rather than a stadium in a parking lot.

Tickets: how the club sells them

The club's anchor product is the Season Ticket Membership, which covers all 17 MLS regular season home games and was positioned by the club as the best pricing route for the inaugural Nu Stadium season. Single match tickets are sold through the club's official ticketing channels, and single match parking passes are sold separately, a detail worth knowing before you assume parking is bundled. Demand for the Messi era club has kept secondary prices high for marquee matches; for a one off visit, compare the club's single match inventory first, check the visiting team and day of week (weeknight matches price softer than Saturdays), and treat too cheap resale listings with suspicion.

Shift4 powers ticketing and concession payments at Nu Stadium, so expect a mobile first, card based match day: ticket on your phone, concessions by card or phone tap, and no reliance on cash anywhere in the building.

Getting there and the match day shape

For the club's opening match, fans arrived by both public transportation and car, and the district is designed around a mix of the two. The honest planning advice for a first visit is to decide your exit before you decide your entrance: post match, 26,700 people leave at once, and the first rideshare wave is the slowest and most expensive. Walk to a nearby spot for a drink, or use the transit and shuttle options the club publishes for your specific match, which are listed on the club's match day pages and can change by opponent and kickoff time.

Kickoff times in MLS vary by broadcast window, and Miami evenings run hot and humid from May through September. A night match in the open air is the Florida experience in full: hydrate before you go, expect a possible summer rain delay pattern, and dress for weather that can change between the anthems and halftime.

Where to stay for a match

Because the stadium is now in Miami, the whole city is a reasonable base, which was never true of the Fort Lauderdale years. Staying near the airport corridor and west Miami shortens the stadium trip; Brickell and downtown add the full city weekend around the match; Miami Beach adds a causeway each way. For a one night match trip, proximity wins; for a long weekend, stay where you want to spend the other days and treat the match as an evening excursion. Frequently asked questions

Where does Inter Miami play now?
At Nu Stadium at Miami Freedom Park in Miami, since April 4, 2026. The club played its first six seasons in Fort Lauderdale at Chase Stadium, which is now the reserve team's ground.

How many seats does Nu Stadium have?
26,700, per the club. Premium spaces include the Nu Club lounge with a glass tunnel view of the players entering the pitch.

Is parking included with a match ticket?
No. The club sells single match parking passes separately. Check the club's match day page for your fixture before you go.

When is the MLS season?
MLS runs from late winter into autumn, with playoffs after. Summer home matches are evening events in serious heat and humidity; plan water and rain flexibility.
