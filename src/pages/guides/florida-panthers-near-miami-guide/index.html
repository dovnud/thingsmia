Florida Panthers Guide for Miami Visitors

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Say it first, because it decides your evening: the Florida Panthers do not play in Miami. Their home is Amerant Bank Arena at One Panther Parkway in Sunrise, Florida, about 34 road miles northwest of downtown Miami, roughly a 40 minute drive with no traffic and an hour or more on a game night. From Miami Beach, plan on about an hour. A Panthers game is a half day commitment from a Miami base, and for hockey fans it is worth it; for everyone else it needs to be a deliberate choice, not a surprise.

The geography, honestly

Sunrise is a Broward County suburb west of Fort Lauderdale, and Amerant Bank Arena anchors its entertainment district next to the Sawgrass Mills outlet mall. From downtown Miami the drive is about 34 miles, mostly I-95 or Florida's Turnpike north and then west; free flowing, that is around 40 minutes, and on game nights the arena approaches slow down well before the lots. From Miami Beach you cross a causeway first and should plan on about an hour each way.

There is no practical transit option for a visitor: no train drops you at the arena, and a rideshare each way costs real money and runs into the same postgame surge as every arena in America, with a longer ride attached. The realistic patterns are a rental car, a rideshare pair you budget for honestly, or staying the night of the game in the Fort Lauderdale or Sunrise area and making the Miami days separate.

Arena rules that differ from Miami's other venues

Amerant Bank Arena is completely cashless, with no ATMs inside, so bring a card or a phone wallet. Its bag policy is more welcoming than Kaseya Center's: bags up to 12 by 12 inches are permitted and do not need to be clear, small bags 4 by 6 inches and under skip the X-ray lines, larger permitted bags go through X-ray bag check lines, and medical and diaper bags are allowed; oversize bags can be locker checked at the Publix Plaza stairs for a fee. Children under 2 do not need a ticket if they sit on a lap.

The arena box office runs through SeatGeek, open weekdays 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. and extended on event days, and single game tickets are sold on SeatGeek as well. Doors open 60 minutes before puck drop, and Panthers parking lots open two hours before the game through Gates 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7, with garage access via Gates 1, 5, and 6, and lots closing an hour after the game ends. Bus parking runs $75 through Gate 7 if your group is arriving that way.

Is it worth the trip from Miami?

For a hockey fan, yes, and the last few years of Panthers hockey have made the building one of the loudest in the league. The arena is big, modern, and built for the sport, the crowd is a genuine local crowd rather than a tourist one, and Sawgrass Mills next door gives the day a second act: outlet shopping in the afternoon, dinner, then the game. That combination is the correct shape of a Panthers day from Miami, leave after lunch, shop, eat, watch hockey, drive back late.

For a casual visitor with three nights in Miami and no particular love of hockey, the honest answer is that the same evening buys you a Heat game downtown with a free Metromover ride home. Frequently asked questions

Do the Florida Panthers play in Miami?
No. They play at Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise, Florida, about 34 miles northwest of downtown Miami. Budget 40 minutes to over an hour of driving each way.

What is the bag policy at Amerant Bank Arena?
Bags up to 12 by 12 inches are allowed and do not need to be clear. Small bags 4 by 6 inches and under skip the X-ray lines. The arena is fully cashless.

When do Panthers parking lots open?
Two hours before puck drop, through Gates 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7, and they close an hour after the game. Doors open 60 minutes before puck drop.

How do I make a Panthers game work on a Miami trip?
Pair it with Sawgrass Mills outlet shopping next door in the afternoon, eat before the game, and drive back after. Or stay that night near Fort Lauderdale and keep the Miami nights separate.
