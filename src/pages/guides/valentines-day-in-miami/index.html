Valentine's Day in Miami Guide

Last reviewed: November 2, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Valentine's Day in 2027 is Sunday, February 14, and in Miami it lands in the middle of the busiest February weekend of the year: the Presidents Day long weekend, the closing day of the Miami International Boat Show, and the Coconut Grove Arts Festival weekend. That collision is the whole planning problem, and it has one solution: book in the order this page sets out. The sunset cruise and the hotel go first, in November and December. The dinner reservation goes when restaurants open their February books. The day itself, set out hour by hour below, is the easy part.

Why this Valentine's Day is a booking problem

Three calendars stack on the same weekend. Valentine's Day itself is Sunday, February 14, 2027. Monday, February 15 is Presidents Day, a federal holiday, which turns the weekend into a three day trip for out of town couples. And Miami's own February peak is running at full strength: the Discover Boating Miami International Boat Show closes that Sunday after its February 10 to 14 run, and the Coconut Grove Arts Festival fills the Grove from February 13 to 15. Hotels price the weekend as a peak weekend, the sunset departures sell to couples and boat show visitors at the same time, and the good dinner seatings go to whoever reserved first. None of this makes the weekend a bad idea. It is, weather wise, close to Miami's best weekend of the year. It just punishes anyone who starts planning in February.

The booking order

First, the room. If you are coming from out of town, or upgrading to a hotel night in town, book it now and treat everything else as flexible. South Beach puts you on the beach and inside the restaurant map; Brickell and downtown put you on the bay, closer to the cruise docks and with the skyline as the backdrop. Second, the sunset cruise. The single most Valentine's shaped hour in Miami is the last hour of daylight from the water: the skyline behind you, the bay going gold, and dinner still ahead of you. Sunset departures on February 14 are a fixed, small inventory, and they are the first thing on this page that can genuinely sell out. The Sunday itself, hour by hour

Late morning: the Grove or the beach. The Coconut Grove Arts Festival is in full swing on its second day, a genuinely good daylight date: outdoor art, food stalls, and the bay at the end of the street. Our festival guide covers arrival. The alternative is the classic one, a slow beach morning in South Beach or Mid Beach, saving your energy for the evening.

Afternoon: leave it empty. The amateur mistake is a packed day that arrives at the cruise dock tired and rushed. Check in, dress properly, and be at the dock early. February evenings on the water are cool by Miami standards once the boat is moving; bring a layer, a note our Miami in February guide makes about the whole month.

Sunset: on the water. The cruise is the day's headline. Biscayne Bay at sunset, the downtown skyline lighting up on the way back, and the city dressed for the evening by the time you dock.

Evening: dinner, then one more stop. Dinner after the cruise, at the restaurant you reserved in January. If the night has one more chapter in it, make it a rooftop bar or a live music room rather than a club line; our Miami for couples guide maps the slower evening options, and the nightlife hub covers the rest.

Monday, February 15: the bonus day. Presidents Day is yours to spend unhurried: a late checkout, one museum or garden from the couples guide, and the trip home after the weekend traffic has thinned. If you are staying the week, the season keeps rolling toward the South Beach Wine and Food Festival on February 25 to 28.

Frequently asked questions

When is Valentine's Day in 2027?
Sunday, February 14, 2027. It falls inside the Presidents Day long weekend, with the federal holiday on Monday, February 15.

What else is on in Miami that weekend?
The Miami International Boat Show closes on February 14 after running February 10 to 14, and the Coconut Grove Arts Festival runs February 13 to 15. Expect peak weekend hotel pricing and book the room and the sunset cruise early.

What is the one thing to book first?
The sunset cruise for February 14, right after the hotel. Departures are a small, fixed inventory and it is the most date specific plan on this page.

When do Valentine's dinner reservations open?
Most Miami restaurants open books roughly a month ahead, so mid January is the moment. No 2027 Valentine's menus had been published as of November 2, 2026; pick the restaurant from the guides above now and reserve when its book opens.
