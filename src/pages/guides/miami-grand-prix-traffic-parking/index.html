Miami Grand Prix Traffic and Parking Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Race weekend transport at the Miami International Autodrome runs on one principle: everything is pre booked, assigned, and spread around Miami Gardens and beyond. The promoter runs 26 numbered parking lots, free shuttles from three compass points including Brightline's Aventura station, and an all off site rideshare system. The drive yourself and figure it out approach is the one plan the event is designed to punish.

Parking: 26 lots, assigned, booked in advance

The official parking system spreads 26 numbered lots around the stadium district, and your pass assigns you a lot chosen to minimize your walk to your gate, with listed lot to gate distances running from 0.1 to 1.4 miles. Formula 1's own ticket store states the rule plainly: on site parking is limited and must be booked in advance. Some passes are physical and mailed, others are digital through Ticketmaster Account Manager in April, so check which kind you hold before race week, and screenshot or save the pass offline, because 60,000 people hitting the same cell towers is not the moment to discover a login screen.

There is no meaningful cash at the gate, turn up and pay layer to this system for the Grand Prix. If you do not hold a parking pass, your plan is a shuttle lot or a train, not a hopeful drive toward the stadium hoping a nearby business sells spots; the surrounding streets are controlled on race weekend.

The shuttle system: the promoter's preferred answer

The promoter runs free shuttles from three directions, and calls the eastern one highly recommended. EAST: Brightline's Aventura Station at 19796 West Dixie Highway runs shuttles every 10 minutes to Entry Gate 3, and there is an east walk option from 695 NW 199th Street, a 1.4 mile walk to Gate 14B. SOUTH: Lot 95 at 16000 NW 7th Avenue shuttles via Lots 15 and 16 to Gate 3, with a walk option from Lot 43 in Miami Gardens, 1.3 miles to Gate 5 or Gate 3. NORTH: Lot 70 at 1 Seminole Way in Hollywood, at the Seminole Hard Rock, shuttles to Gate 14, with a walk option from Lot 35 on NW 27th Avenue to Gate 8.

Brightline is the premium version of the same idea: trains badged Hard Rock Stadium Connect include complimentary round trip shuttles, which leave Aventura station 10 minutes after train arrivals, with return shuttles timed to depart the race an hour before trains. If your hotel is anywhere near a Brightline station, downtown Miami, Fort Lauderdale, or beyond, the train plus shuttle is the fastest, calmest route the event offers, and it skips the parking market entirely.

Rideshare: off site, surged, and officially discouraged

Every rideshare lot for the Grand Prix is off site. You must set your drop off and pick up point to a specific rideshare lot, and the promoter warns plainly to expect surge pricing and extended waits, to the point that its parking pages actively steer fans away from rideshare as a first choice. If you use it anyway, walk to your lot early for the return, keep your phone charged, and agree the lot letter with your driver before the race ends, because the post race hour is the worst communications environment of the weekend.

Road closures follow the same philosophy. No official 2027 closure schedule has been published yet, and this page will not invent one. In past years, per Miami Gardens Police and Turnpike reporting around the 2025 race, the Florida's Turnpike Exit 2X ramps at NW 199th Street closed for the weekend and NW 199th Street itself closed between NW 27th Avenue and NW 14th Court during event hours. Treat that as the shape of what is coming, and check the promoter's transport pages in April for the confirmed 2027 plan.

The facts that matter

Promoter official transport pages, checked October 30, 2026: 26 numbered parking lots (including lots 1 to 4, 8, 11, 12, 17, 18, 20 to 22, 30 to 35, 40, 41, 44, 45, 50, 60, 70, 95); lot to gate distances 0.1 to 1.4 miles; parking must be booked in advance. Free shuttles: East from Brightline Aventura Station (19796 W Dixie Hwy) every 10 minutes to Gate 3; South from Lot 95 (16000 NW 7th Ave); North from Lot 70 (1 Seminole Way, Hollywood). All rideshare lots are off site. No official 2027 road closure schedule published as of this check. Official source: f1miamigp.com.

Frequently asked questions

Can I just drive to the Miami Grand Prix and park?
Only with a pre booked pass for one of the 26 official lots. On site parking is limited and must be booked in advance; there is no turn up and pay layer.

What is the best way to get to the race?
Brightline to Aventura plus the free shuttle to Gate 3, which the promoter highly recommends, or the free shuttle lots to the south and north. Both skip the parking market and the worst road traffic.

Are rideshares a good idea for the Grand Prix?
They are the weakest option: all rideshare lots are off site, and the promoter warns of surge pricing and long waits, especially after the race.

Which roads close for the Grand Prix?
The 2027 closure list is not published yet. In past years the Turnpike's Exit 2X ramps and NW 199th Street by the stadium closed during event hours; expect a similar pattern and confirm in April.
