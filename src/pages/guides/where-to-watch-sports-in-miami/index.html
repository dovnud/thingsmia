Where to Watch Sports in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. This page does one job: where to sit in front of a screen in Miami when the game matters. It lists viewing venues only, with no betting content of any kind, because watching is the activity. The short version: Wynwood and Brickell hold the city's best dedicated sports bars, Miami Beach covers the casual end, and every neighborhood in the metro has a reliable chain option.

The dedicated sports bars

Grails Miami, Wynwood. At 2800 North Miami Avenue, Grails describes itself as Wynwood's first sports bar, open since 2019, and it is built like a sneaker museum that happens to show games: 75 plus TVs, a Sneaker Lounge, a covered Miami Vice Patio, and The Yard outside with a giant projector. The sound is on for major games and there is no cover, which makes it the default answer for a big fight night or a national game in the middle of the city.

American Social Bar and Kitchen, Brickell. On the Miami River at 690 Southwest 1st Court, American Social calls itself Miami's premier sports bar and restaurant, with HD TVs positioned for viewing from effectively any seat inside and out. It is the polished end of the category: a full kitchen, a riverfront patio, and a crowd that treats game day as a dinner reservation with a screen.

Batch Gastropub, Brickell. At 30 Southwest 12th Street, Batch is the neighborhood pub version: big screens and projectors, a serious kitchen for a sports bar, and a location directly by a Metromover and Metrorail station, which makes it the easiest sports bar in the city to reach without a car.

The reliable chain layer

Duffy's Sports Grill is the Florida chain that fills the map: locations across the metro including North Miami Beach at 3969 Northeast 163rd Street and Kendall at 8575 Southwest 124th Avenue, running its Our Game Is Always On format of wall to wall screens and a full grill menu. Chains earn their place on this page for one reason: wherever you are staying in greater Miami, there is one within a short drive, and the game you want will be on with sound in at least one corner of the room.

Hotel bars and beach bars fill the rest of the need. The honest rule for Miami Beach: most Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue venues will happily put a game on, but few are built around it, so for a match you truly care about, cross to Wynwood or Brickell, and treat the beach bars as the background option for games you are only half watching.

How to choose by game

For soccer, start with the neighborhood bars in Little Havana, Wynwood, and the Latin corridors of the city, where match culture is the house culture, and see our Inter Miami guide for the live version. For NFL Sundays, Grails and the big rooms earn their crowds. For a fight or a pay per view event, call ahead: venues buy events selectively, and the one you want may require a reservation or a cover on the night even where the house rule is usually no cover.

Two practical notes that apply everywhere on this page. Hours, happy hours, and watch party programming change constantly and are not printed here as facts; check the venue's own site on the day. And on the biggest nights of the year, the Super Bowl, a World Cup final, a Canes or Dolphins playoff game, arrive early or book, because the best rooms fill by reputation alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best sports bar in Miami?
For a dedicated sports bar experience, Grails in Wynwood and American Social in Brickell lead most local lists. For convenience near your hotel, Duffy's Sports Grill has locations across the metro.

Where can I watch soccer in Miami?
Little Havana, Wynwood, and the city's Latin neighborhood bars are the match culture centers. Grails shows major soccer with sound on.

Do Miami sports bars charge cover?
Usually not for regular games. Marquee pay per view events and championship nights can carry a cover or a reservation requirement, so call the venue ahead for those.

Can I watch a game in Miami Beach?
Yes, most beach bars will show games, but few are built around them. For a game that matters, Wynwood and Brickell have the dedicated rooms.
