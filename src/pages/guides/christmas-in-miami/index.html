Christmas in Miami Guide

Last reviewed: November 3, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Christmas in Miami means holiday lights after dark and the beach in the daytime, sometimes on the same day. The season's anchor events for 2026 are Zoo Lights at Zoo Miami (17 select nights from November 27 to December 30), Christmas Wonderland at Tropical Park (November 12, 2026 to January 3, 2027), Luminosa at Jungle Island (November 13 to January 10), the NightGarden at Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden in Coral Gables (from November 12), and Miami City Ballet's Nutcracker at the Arsht Center (December 11 to 27). One famous name is missing on purpose: Santa's Enchanted Forest is closed permanently and is not running in 2026. Details, dates, and the honest alternatives are below.

What does Christmas in Miami actually feel like?

Warm, mostly. December is one of Miami's driest months: the long term averages for the month are daytime highs around 78 degrees and overnight lows in the low 60s, with about 2 inches of rain across the whole month. Those are averages, not promises: a cold front can bring a genuinely chilly day or two, so pack one layer alongside the swimwear. Christmas Day at the beach is a normal Miami plan, and December 24, Nochebuena, is the big family night in Miami's Cuban community, when much of the local city is at a dinner table rather than out on the town. Plan your own Christmas Eve accordingly: restaurants that are open book out early.

Which light displays are worth your evenings?

Zoo Lights Miami at Zoo Miami. The zoo's night event runs on 17 select nights in 2026: November 27 and 28, then December 4, 5, 11, 12, 18 to 23, and 26 to 30, from 6:30 to 10 p.m. Expect more than a million lights, a 26 foot LED holiday tree (the opening night tree lighting is at 7 p.m. on November 27), and Santa appearances through December 23. Two practical notes: Zoo Lights is separately ticketed from daytime zoo admission, and tickets are sold online only through the zoo's own shop, so buy before you drive out to 12400 SW 152nd Street. Note the gap in the date list: the event is dark on December 24 and 25.

Christmas Wonderland at Tropical Park. The big fair style celebration at Tropical Park on Bird Road (SW 40th Street) runs from November 12, 2026 to January 3, 2027. Admission includes unlimited rides, the Bavarian Marketplace around the park tree, and the 230 foot America 250 Wheel; parking is free, and games, food, and Santa photos cost extra. If you remember the holiday fair that used to fill this park, see the Santa's Enchanted Forest section below: Christmas Wonderland now carries a tribute lane of the original displays.

Luminosa at Jungle Island. A lantern festival on Watson Island, November 13, 2026 to January 10, 2027, open Sunday to Thursday 5:30 to 9:30 p.m. and Friday and Saturday until 10:30 p.m. The 1.35 mile path runs through seven zones themed on Miami neighborhoods, lit by more than 43,600 LEDs. The organizer lists tickets from $25.90 for adults and $17.90 for children, checked October 30, 2026; expect date based pricing at checkout. Jungle Island is at 1111 Parrot Jungle Trail, minutes from downtown.

NightGarden at Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden. The illuminated night walk through the garden opens its eighth season on November 12, 2026, on select evenings into early January, and adds a real ice rink under the palms this year. Fairchild sits at 10901 Old Cutler Road; check the event's ticket page for the exact night calendar before you go, because not every evening is a NightGarden evening.

Nights of Lights at Pinecrest Gardens. The smaller, local favorite: select nights December 4 to 27, 2026, 6:30 to 10 p.m., rain or shine, at 11000 Red Road in Pinecrest. It suits families who want lights, a festival atmosphere, and an early night rather than a mega production.

What happened to Santa's Enchanted Forest?

It closed, permanently, and it is not happening in 2026. The fair ran for decades at Tropical Park, moved to Hialeah Park in 2021, sat out 2024, and its permanent closure was confirmed in November 2025. Any website still selling it as a 2026 event is out of date. The good news: Christmas Wonderland, which now occupies Tropical Park, includes SEF: Memory Lane, a tribute built from original Santa's Enchanted Forest displays, so the nostalgia stop is inside the new fair.

What is on besides the lights?

The Nutcracker. Miami City Ballet dances George Balanchine's The Nutcracker at the Ziff Opera House, Adrienne Arsht Center, 1300 Biscayne Boulevard, from December 11 to 27, 2026. It is the season's one essential indoor ticket and the performances closest to Christmas Day sell first.

The Winterfest Boat Parade, in Fort Lauderdale. On Saturday December 12, 2026, a fleet of decorated boats (the parade is in its 55th year) leaves downtown Fort Lauderdale at 6 p.m. and run a 12 mile route north. Be clear about the geography: this is Fort Lauderdale, roughly a 40 minute drive from Miami in normal traffic and slower on parade night. It is worth the drive as a day trip, and nobody should promise you a Miami version of it.

The Junior Orange Bowl Parade, in Coral Gables. On Sunday December 6, 2026, the Miracle on the Mile Holiday Parade brings floats, marching bands, and performers down Miracle Mile in Coral Gables, presented by the Junior Orange Bowl, which calls the parade its founding event and counts 2026 as its 78th year. The route begins at Alhambra Circle and Ponce de Leon Boulevard, travels south on Ponce to Miracle Mile, then west to Biltmore Way by City Hall. Watching from the sidewalk along the route is free; the organizer sells an optional VIP hospitality and grandstand experience for visitors who want a seat, food, and drinks with their parade. It is the one classic holiday parade inside Miami Dade itself, and it pairs with dinner on Miracle Mile the same night.

Art Week spillover. If your trip starts early in the month, Art Basel runs December 4 to 6 and the whole first week of December is exhibition season; the Art Basel hub covers it. The events hub tracks the rest of the month.

What is open on Christmas Day?

More than in most American cities. The beaches are open and busy. Zoo Miami is open in the daytime (it operates daily) even though Zoo Lights takes December 24 and 25 off. Many hotel restaurants serve Christmas Day seatings, and the big attractions largely run holiday hours, but hours genuinely vary by venue and by year, so confirm the specific place on its own site before you build the day around it. What to book ahead: Christmas Day dinner, the Nutcracker performances nearest the 25th, and any tour you care about, because holiday week departures fill.

Where should you stay for a Christmas trip?

December is peak season and the Christmas to New Year week is the most expensive stretch of the Miami year, so book as early as you can and treat any flexible rate as worth having. For the classic beach Christmas, our hotel guides cover the Fontainebleau, the Faena, The Setai, 1 Hotel South Beach, The Betsy, and the Loews Miami Beach. For the light displays, which sit on the mainland and in the southern suburbs, a downtown or Coral Gables base shortens every evening drive.

Frequently asked questions

Is Santa's Enchanted Forest happening in 2026?
No. It closed permanently; its last seasons were at Hialeah Park and the closure was confirmed in November 2025. Christmas Wonderland at Tropical Park is the successor event and includes a tribute lane of the original displays.

What are the Zoo Lights dates for 2026?
November 27 and 28, and December 4, 5, 11, 12, 18 to 23, and 26 to 30, from 6:30 to 10 p.m. Tickets are sold online only, and the event is separately ticketed from daytime zoo admission.

Can you go to the beach on Christmas Day in Miami?
Yes. December averages put daytime highs around 78 degrees, and Christmas Day beach time is a Miami tradition. A cold front can bring a cooler day, so keep one layer in the bag.

What is the best area to stay in for the light displays?
Zoo Lights, Pinecrest Gardens, and Fairchild Garden all sit on the mainland or in the southern suburbs, and Christmas Wonderland is at Tropical Park. A downtown, Brickell, or Coral Gables hotel makes those evenings short drives; a beach hotel adds a causeway crossing to each one.

Is the Winterfest Boat Parade in Miami?
No. It is in Fort Lauderdale, on Saturday December 12, 2026. Plan it as a day trip, about 40 minutes each way in normal traffic.
