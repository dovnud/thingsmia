Group Trips to Miami: The Logistics Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Group trips to Miami fail on one fact more than any other: the big house rental. Miami Beach prohibits short term rentals in all single family homes and in many apartment buildings, so the classic group villa inside Miami Beach is usually an illegal listing, and the city's enforcement is aimed at exactly that product. Build the trip on hotels and authorized buildings instead, use the free trolley as your group vehicle, and put the celebration budget into a boat day.

The rental rule that decides your lodging

The City of Miami Beach defines a vacation or short term rental as a stay of less than six months and one day, and states that such rentals are prohibited in all single family homes and in many multifamily buildings in certain zoning districts. Where short term rental is allowed, the operator needs a city Vacation and Short Term Rental Business Tax Receipt and a Resort Tax account, and both numbers must appear in the listing, which gives you a practical test: a Miami Beach listing that does not show those numbers is a listing to walk away from.

The consequence for groups is structural. A whole house in a residential Miami Beach neighborhood is the highest risk lodging in the city, for the guests as much as the owner. The legal defaults are hotels, which handle groups professionally, and authorized apartment buildings in permitted districts. Outside Miami Beach the rules change by city and by building, and condo and HOA minimum stay rules layer on top; the verification habit, tax receipt numbers in Miami Beach, building rules confirmed in writing elsewhere, is the same everywhere.

Moving a group without a fleet of cars

Miami Beach hands groups a gift: the free trolley. The South Beach loops run 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily, about every 20 minutes, covering the convention center, Lincoln Road, and City Hall area, which is most of a group's daily geography. Downtown and Brickell groups get the same gift in the free Metromover; the county's paid backbone, Metrorail and Metrobus, runs at a $2.25 base fare for cross city moves.

For the rest, rideshare splitting beats renting in the beach zones: parking a large vehicle in Miami Beach costs more goodwill and money than the rides do. Groups flying in together should also know the airport pattern: MIA to Miami Beach is a fixed geography every rideshare and taxi handles constantly, and a pre booked van for arrival day is the one vehicle hire that reliably earns its cost.

The group day that justifies the trip

Put the money where the memory is: a group boat day. Miami Beach Marina at 300 Alton Road hosts charter operations including catamarans listed for groups up to 49 and 42 guests, and a private charter scales from a family reunion to a full corporate offsite without changing format. Book it early, confirm the alcohol and swimming rules with the operator, and schedule it mid trip, not on departure day.

Beach days for groups run on the city's rules, which are stricter than most groups expect: no tents or tables, no coolers, no glass, no loud music, and no public alcohol consumption on the public beach. A group's beach base is therefore a hotel beach concession or a beach club arrangement, where chairs, shade, and service are the product, with the public sand for swimming and walking. Frequently asked questions

Can our group rent a house in Miami Beach?
In a single family home, no: the city prohibits short term rentals in all single family homes and many apartment buildings. Legal listings show a city Business Tax Receipt and Resort Tax account number. Hotels and authorized buildings are the safe default.

How do we move a group around Miami Beach?
The free trolley covers South Beach 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. Downtown groups get the free Metromover. For everything else, split rideshares rather than renting and parking in the beach zones.

What is the best group activity in Miami?
A private boat charter on Biscayne Bay. Catamarans from Miami Beach Marina are listed for groups up to 49 guests. Book early and confirm the operator's alcohol and swimming rules.

Can we set up a tent and coolers on the beach for the group?
No. Miami Beach prohibits tents, tables, coolers, glass, loud music, and public alcohol on the public beach. Use a hotel beach concession or beach club as the group base instead.
