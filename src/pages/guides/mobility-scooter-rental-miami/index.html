Mobility Scooter Rental in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami model for mobility scooter rental is delivery, not a shop counter. The national rental service with a dedicated Miami operation, Scootaround, delivers a charged, ready to ride scooter directly to your hotel, Airbnb, the cruise port, or even your cruise ship cabin, and collects it the same way at the end. For a visitor, that delivery model is the whole product: no van rental, no hauling equipment across the airport.

How rental works in Miami

Scootaround's Miami page lays out the visitor pattern. You choose the equipment, they deliver it to your hotel, Airbnb, cruise port, or cruise ship, fully charged and ready to use, and you do not even need to be present for hotel or cruise delivery. At the end of the rental, hotel returns are simply left at the bell desk for pickup. The equipment range covers transportable scooters that break down for a car trunk, standard scooters, heavy duty and XL models, plus power wheelchairs and manual wheelchairs for companions or mixed needs.

Book before you travel, not on arrival: cruise sailings and big event weekends pull delivery slots, and the delivery window is coordinated with your check in. Tell the service your hotel and arrival time, confirm where the scooter will be waiting (front desk and bell desks handle these deliveries daily in Miami), and ask about the charging routine for your model; every hotel room has an outlet, but knowing the battery range for a full day at Zoo Miami or on the Miami Beach boardwalk shapes your itinerary.

The cruise connection

Miami is the busiest cruise port in the world, and scooter rental is built around it. Scootaround delivers directly to ship cabins at PortMiami, and states it is the exclusive mobility provider for Norwegian Cruise Line, Oceania Cruises, and Regent Seven Seas, and a preferred provider for Carnival. If you are sailing on another line, the port and hotel delivery options still apply; the practical pattern many cruisers use is delivery to a pre cruise hotel the night before, ride it onto the ship yourself, and keep the same scooter for the whole sailing.

Our cruise hub covers PortMiami logistics end to end. The key planning fact is timing: book the scooter when you book the cruise extras, not the week of sailing, and confirm the delivery point, cabin versus port terminal, with the rental service in writing.

The sand rule and where scooters work in Miami

One rule from the rental service itself: its scooters are not designed for sand and should not be used on the beach. That is not a company quirk; powered scooters and soft sand do not mix anywhere. Miami Beach answers the problem differently, with city provided beach wheelchairs designed for sand, covered in our accessible Miami travel guide.

Everywhere else, Miami is scooter friendly in the ways that matter: the Miami Beach boardwalk and the Baywalk are flat and paved, the big attractions are step free in their main routes, Metromover is free and accessible in downtown and Brickell, and the convention center and the arenas publish their accessibility setups. Prices for scooter rental vary by equipment type and rental length and are confirmed at booking; the service does not publish a single Miami daily rate, so treat any specific dollar figure quoted elsewhere as unverified and get your quote in the booking flow.

Frequently asked questions

How does scooter rental work in Miami?
By delivery. The rental service brings a charged scooter to your hotel, Airbnb, cruise port, or ship, and picks it up the same way. You do not need to be present for hotel or cruise delivery.

Can I take a rental scooter on a cruise?
Yes. Delivery can be made directly to your cabin at PortMiami, and the major rental service is the exclusive or preferred mobility provider for several cruise lines. Book early in the cruise planning process.

Can I ride a mobility scooter on the beach?
No. Rental scooters are not designed for sand. Miami Beach provides beach wheelchairs built for sand at no stated charge on the city's programme page; see our accessible travel guide.

How much does a scooter rental cost in Miami?
It varies by scooter type and rental length and is quoted at booking. No single official Miami daily rate is published, so get the quote in the booking flow rather than trusting figures on other sites.
