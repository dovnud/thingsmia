MIA Airport Guide for Visitors

Last reviewed: October 26, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Arriving at Miami International Airport, follow the signs up to the MIA Mover on Level 3: the free automated people mover carries you to the Rental Car Center and the Miami Intermodal Center, where Metrorail trains run into downtown for $2.25. Taxis and ride app pickups wait on the arrivals level outside baggage claim, and PortMiami sits about 9 miles away for cruise passengers. Everything below is the detail, with links to the airport's official pages.

How do you get from MIA to downtown and Brickell?

The train is the value play. Ride the MIA Mover from the terminal to the Miami Intermodal Center and step onto Metrorail at Miami Airport Station: the Orange Line runs through downtown's Government Center and on to Brickell and the southern suburbs for the standard $2.25 Miami-Dade Transit fare, checked October 26, 2026. If your hotel is downtown or in Brickell, this is usually the fastest cheap option in traffic, and the free Metromover loops take over for the last stretch inside downtown. The full system is explained in our Getting Around Miami guide.

How do you get from MIA to Miami Beach?

There is no train to the Beach, so the choice is bus or car. Metrobus Route 150, the Miami Beach Airport Express, runs between the airport station at the Intermodal Center and Miami Beach; once on the island, the city's free trolley covers local trips. Otherwise, taxis and ride app services pick up on the arrivals level and cross the causeway directly, which is the simplest door to door option with luggage or a late arrival.

How do you get from MIA to PortMiami for a cruise?

The airport's own ground transportation page puts PortMiami about 9 miles from MIA, and lists the working options: pre arranged cruise line transportation from the airport bus stations, taxis and ride share services, public transportation via the Intermodal Center, and rental cars from the Rental Car Center. Most cruisers with luggage take a taxi or rideshare and are at the terminal area quickly; budget travelers can ride the MIA Mover to the Intermodal Center and connect onward, as the airport page describes. If you are staying the night before your sailing, the best hotels for cruise passengers page and the cruise hub take it from there.

Where do taxis, rideshare, and rental cars pick up?

Per the airport's official ground transportation page: taxicab and ride app pickup zones are on the arrival (ground) level, outside the baggage claim areas. Rental cars live in the MIA Rental Car Center, reached by the MIA Mover, whose station sits on the 3rd level between the Dolphin and Flamingo garages. For parking rates, terminal maps, and anything that changes by season, use the airport's site rather than any guide's memory: start at the official MIA ground transportation page and the MIA homepage, which also links lost and found.

The facts that matter

From the airport's official site, checked October 26, 2026: the MIA Mover station is on the 3rd level between the Dolphin and Flamingo garages, connecting the terminal to the Rental Car Center and the Miami Intermodal Center; taxis and ride app pickups are on the arrivals level outside baggage claim; PortMiami is about 9 miles from MIA. From Miami-Dade Transit: Metrorail fare from the airport station is $2.25, and the MIA Mover ride itself is free. Airline terminal and concourse assignments change, so this page does not print a static list; check yours on the airport's official site before you travel.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MIA Mover free?
Yes. The automated people mover between the terminal, the Rental Car Center, and the Intermodal Center charges no fare. It is the link that makes the train and the rental cars practical.

Can I ride transit straight to South Beach?
Not by train. Metrobus Route 150, the Miami Beach Airport Express, is the transit route from the airport station to Miami Beach; after that, the free Miami Beach trolley handles trips on the island.

What if my flight lands late at night?
Taxis and ride app services at the arrivals level are the dependable late night option; transit runs long hours but thins out overnight. If you land very late before a cruise, an airport area or downtown hotel and a fresh start in the morning beats a complicated transfer.

Where do I check my airline's terminal?
On the airport's official site or your airline's app, close to your travel date. Assignments move between concourses over time, which is why this guide links the source instead of printing a table that would quietly go stale.
