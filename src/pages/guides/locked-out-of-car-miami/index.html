Locked Out of Your Car in Miami

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The order of calls matters more than the speed of any locksmith. If it is a rental car, call the rental company's roadside line first, the number on your rental agreement and key fob, because an outside locksmith can cost you money the rental terms will not cover. If you are an AAA member, AAA's lockout service covers you as a driver or a passenger in any car. And never agree to work from a search ad quote that changes when the van arrives.

Rental cars: the company is your first call

Your rental agreement is the rulebook, and the roadside number on it and on the key fob is the only number that keeps you inside it. Hertz, to take the documented example from its own support pages, includes Basic Emergency Roadside Service with every rental, but that basic cover handles mechanical defects only; locked in or lost keys are covered only under its optional Premium Emergency Roadside Service, and without it Hertz roadside may charge a fee, $98 or more by its own page, or you can use your own auto club. Hertz's published US and Canada roadside number is 800-654-5060, and its guidance is explicit: do not have the vehicle towed before contacting its roadside service.

Other rental brands run their own versions of the same structure, basic cover for breakdowns, key problems as an extra or a fee, so read your own agreement rather than assuming. The universal rule survives every brand: the rental company must be the first call, because keys, replacements, and liability all live with them.

AAA members: covered in any car

AAA's lockout service is one of the quiet superpowers of the membership. Per AAA's own pages, lockout service is included in all membership plans, a technician is dispatched to unlock the car, membership follows the person rather than the vehicle, so you are covered as a driver or a passenger, in your car or a rental, and if the technician cannot open it, AAA will tow the car or you can hire a locksmith and request reimbursement up to your membership tier's limit. AAA's published roadside number is 800-222-4357, and service can also be requested online or in the app.

If a traveling companion has AAA and you do not, their membership covers the situation when they are present as your passenger. It is the cheapest rescue on this page by a wide margin.

Independent locksmiths: use them last, and price first

Miami has plenty of legitimate locksmiths and a well known fringe of search ad operators whose business model is a low phone quote followed by a much higher bill at the car, in cash. The defenses are simple and they work: get the full price, including the service call, before anyone is dispatched; pay by card, never cash, so the charge can be disputed; ask for the business name and check it against the invoice; and if anyone is pressuring you, walk away and call the rental company, AAA, or your hotel for a referral instead. Hotel front desks and condo security desks in Miami deal with this weekly and keep lists of the locksmiths they trust.

If a child or a pet is locked in the car, skip this whole page: call 911 immediately. Heat inside a parked Miami car becomes dangerous in minutes, and emergency services will open the car as an emergency, not an errand.

Frequently asked questions

I locked the keys in my rental car. Who do I call?
The rental company's roadside line, on your agreement and key fob, before anyone else. Key cover varies: Hertz, for example, covers locked keys only under its optional premium roadside product, otherwise a fee applies.

Does AAA cover lockouts in a rental car?
Yes. AAA membership follows the person, and lockout service is included in all plans for members as driver or passenger. If the technician cannot open the car, AAA tows it or reimburses a locksmith up to your tier's limit.

How do I avoid a locksmith scam?
Agree the full price before dispatch, pay by card, get the business name on an invoice, and use rental company, AAA, or hotel referrals first. A quote that changes at the car is the scam working as designed.

A child or pet is locked in the car. What do I do?
Call 911 immediately. Miami heat makes a parked car dangerous in minutes, and emergency services treat it as an emergency.
