Best Hotels for the Miami Grand Prix

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami International Autodrome sits at 347 Don Shula Drive in Miami Gardens, roughly 15 miles north of downtown Miami, and that geography decides your hotel choice before price does. The closest real hotel cluster is Aventura, about 6 miles east, which also holds the Brightline station with the free shuttle to Gate 3. Everything else is a trade: beach, nightlife, and price against the race weekend drive.

Closest in: Miami Gardens and Aventura

Miami Gardens itself has a small set of hotels near the stadium, zero commute on race day and very little else around them; they sell out earliest and price like it. For most visitors the correct answer is Aventura, about 6 miles and a normal traffic drive of 15 to 20 minutes east of the stadium. Aventura carries a full stack of business and resort hotels around Aventura Mall, and it owns race weekend's killer feature: Brightline's Aventura station, an official free shuttle point to Gate 3, which turns the worst transport problem of the weekend into a train ride.

Hallandale Beach, about 7 miles away on the Aventura border, is the value extension of the same idea: slightly older stock, lower prices, same road logic. If your group is splitting costs, this corridor, Aventura to Hallandale, is where the math works best per minute of commute.

Beach bases: Sunny Isles and Hollywood

Sunny Isles Beach, about 9 to 10 miles east of the stadium and 20 to 25 minutes in normal traffic, is the closest real beach resort strip: high rise full service hotels on the sand, a quieter, more residential feel than South Beach, and a straight shot west to the circuit. Hollywood, Florida, about 9 miles northeast, adds the Seminole Hard Rock's guitar hotel and casino scene, plus the Broadwalk beach strip, and it also holds the northern shuttle lot at Lot 70 with free service to Gate 14.

From either, race weekend drives run far longer than the normal traffic times quoted here; build your morning around the shuttle lots or an early departure rather than the map's optimistic number. What these areas buy you is a genuine beach holiday wrapped around the race, which for most visitors is the better trip than three nights beside a stadium.

The city option: Brickell, downtown, and Miami Beach

Brickell and downtown Miami sit 15 to 16 miles south, 25 to 35 minutes in normal traffic and much longer on race days. You stay there for the city: the restaurants, the nightlife, the bayfront, and Brightline's MiamiCentral station for the train north. Miami Beach and South Beach, 17 to 20 miles away, are the maximal version of the same trade, the full beach scene at the price of the longest race day commute of any area on this page.

The honest framing: if the race is 80 percent of your trip, stay north. If the race is one day of a Miami week, stay where you want to live for the week and treat race day as an expedition, with the Brightline plus shuttle route doing the heavy lifting. Either way, book early and book refundable where you can: the promoter notes the first five editions sold out, and hotel inventory in the northern corridor reprices the moment grandstand tickets move.

The facts that matter

Geography from the circuit address and promoter transport pages, checked October 30, 2026: Miami International Autodrome, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens, about 15 miles north of downtown Miami. Approximate road distances: Aventura about 6 miles (Aventura Mall is 5.75 miles from the stadium), Hallandale Beach about 7 miles, Hollywood about 9 miles, Sunny Isles Beach about 9 to 10 miles, Brickell and downtown about 15 to 16 miles, Miami Beach about 17 to 20 miles. Drive times quoted on this page are normal traffic approximations; race weekend takes far longer. Official shuttle points: Brightline Aventura Station and Lot 70, Seminole Hard Rock, Hollywood.

Frequently asked questions

What is the closest hotel area to the Miami Grand Prix?
Aventura, about 6 miles east, with a full hotel cluster and the Brightline station that runs free shuttles to Gate 3. Miami Gardens has a few stadium adjacent hotels that sell out first.

Can I stay in South Beach for the Grand Prix?
Yes, but it is the longest commute on this page, 17 to 20 miles, and slow on race days. Do it if the beach week matters more than the race day drive, and use Brightline plus the shuttle.

Where do I stay for the beach and the race?
Sunny Isles Beach: real resort hotels on the sand about 9 to 10 miles from the circuit, or Hollywood for the casino scene and the northern shuttle lot.

When should I book Grand Prix hotels?
Early. The first five editions sold out and the northern corridor reprices fast. Refundable rates are worth a premium until your tickets are certain.
