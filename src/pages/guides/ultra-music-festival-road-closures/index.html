Ultra Weekend Road Closures and Getting Around Downtown

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Ultra Music Festival returns to Bayfront Park in downtown Miami on March 26 to 28, 2027, with gates Friday 4 p.m. to midnight, Saturday noon to midnight, and Sunday noon to 10 p.m. The festival closes the heart of Biscayne Boulevard for the better part of four days. The 2027 closure list is not published yet, and nobody honest can give it to you; what follows is the documented pattern the City of Miami has run in recent years, labeled as exactly that, plus the transit plan that makes the closures irrelevant.

The typical closure pattern, from the city's own 2026 guide

The City of Miami Police downtown resident traffic guide for Ultra 2026 ran traffic operations from 9 p.m. on the Thursday before the festival to 7 a.m. on the Monday after, and the same basic pattern was used in 2024. Southbound Biscayne Boulevard traffic is diverted west at Northeast 6th Street, with Northeast 2nd Avenue and Miami Avenue as the alternate routes. Northbound traffic is diverted into the southbound lanes at Southeast 1st Street and returns to normal flow at Northeast 4th Street, and no vehicles are allowed on Biscayne Boulevard south of Northeast 5th Street. PortMiami access stays open via Northeast 5th Street, which matters if you are cruising that weekend.

Expect the 2027 plan to rhyme with this, because the festival footprint does not move, but confirm the final map when the city publishes it in March. The practical translations are evergreen: do not plan a drive down Biscayne Boulevard at any point that weekend; Brickell to points north runs on Miami Avenue and the side streets; and any hotel booking on Biscayne itself comes with a walk the last blocks built in.

The transit plan that beats the closures

Ultra's own transport page and Miami-Dade County's festival releases agree on the winning move: rail. In 2026 the county ran Metrorail and Metromover from 5 a.m. to 2 a.m. the following morning on Friday and Saturday, and until midnight Sunday, with Metrorail at $2.25 one way or $5.65 for a one day pass and the Metromover free. The route is Metrorail to Government Center, transfer to the Metromover Inner Loop, and exit at College/Bayside, First Street, or Bayfront Park, each a short walk from the gates. Extended 2027 hours have not been announced yet; the county has run them in recent years and publishes them in March.

There is no parking at the festival site, full stop, and the surrounding garages price for the weekend. Metrobus routes through downtown are detoured during the closure window (in 2026, routes 3, 7, 9, 11, 100, 101, and 203, with Metrorail station parking at $4.50 for the day), and rideshare pickups are pushed outside the restricted zone, so the walk to your car is part of the fare. Brightline and Tri-Rail both serve MiamiCentral, about a mile on foot from Bayfront Park, and Tri-Rail has run special late post festival trains with $5 roundtrip weekend fares.

Festival basics and where to stay

Ultra is 18 plus for general admission and 21 plus for VIP and alcohol, with valid photo ID required, no re entry, and no camping; it is a city festival, and downtown is the campground. Stay downtown or in Brickell and the closures become scenery: you walk or Metromover to the gates, and the whole weekend runs on foot. Stay in Miami Beach and you are crossing a causeway into a closure zone twice a day, which is survivable but is the worst version of the weekend. Stay near a Metrorail station anywhere on the line and the train does the work.

If you are a downtown resident or a non festival visitor that weekend, the mirror image advice applies: move your car before Thursday night if you will need it, schedule around the Friday and Saturday midnight exits, and treat Bayfront Park's neighborhood as a pedestrian zone until Monday morning.

The facts that matter

Ultra official site and City of Miami and Miami-Dade County sources, checked October 30, 2026: venue Bayfront Park, 301 N Biscayne Blvd, Miami, FL 33132. 2027 dates March 26 to 28; gates Fri 4 p.m. to midnight, Sat noon to midnight, Sun noon to 10 p.m.; 18 plus GA, 21 plus VIP; no re entry, no camping, no parking at the site. Closure pattern quoted is the city's documented 2026 plan (Thu 9 p.m. to Mon 7 a.m.; southbound diverted at NE 6th St; northbound into southbound lanes at SE 1st St; no vehicles on Biscayne south of NE 5th St), also used in 2024; the 2027 list is not yet published. 2026 transit: Metrorail and Metromover 5 a.m. to 2 a.m. Fri and Sat, Metrorail $2.25, Metromover free. Official sources: ultramusicfestival.com and miamidade.gov.

Frequently asked questions

When is Ultra Music Festival 2027?
March 26 to 28, 2027, at Bayfront Park in downtown Miami. Friday gates run 4 p.m. to midnight, Saturday noon to midnight, Sunday noon to 10 p.m.

Which roads close for Ultra?
The 2027 list is not published yet. In the city's 2026 plan, Biscayne Boulevard closed in stages from Thursday 9 p.m. to Monday 7 a.m., with southbound traffic diverted at NE 6th Street and no vehicles allowed south of NE 5th Street. Expect a similar pattern.

How do I get to Ultra without a car?
Metrorail to Government Center, then the free Metromover to College/Bayside, First Street, or Bayfront Park. There is no parking at the festival site.

Where should I stay for Ultra?
Downtown or Brickell, within walking or Metromover distance of Bayfront Park. Miami Beach means crossing into the closure zone twice a day.
