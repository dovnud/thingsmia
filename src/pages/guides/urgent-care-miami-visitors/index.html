Urgent Care in Miami for Visitors

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. If it is an emergency, call 911. That is the universal emergency number in the United States, and the City of Miami Beach's own visitor guidance says the same. Everything else on this page is about the far more common visitor problems, the ear infection, the sprained ankle, the fever that will not break, the cut that needs stitches but not an ambulance. For those, Miami's walk in urgent care networks are the right tool: no appointment, on site labs and X-rays, and hours that cover your trip.

Urgent care, emergency room, or 911: the decision

Call 911 for anything that threatens life, limb, or sight: chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding, a head injury, a suspected overdose, or a serious accident. Miami Beach's visitor page gives the same instruction, and US emergency dispatch works from any phone, including a foreign mobile. Do not drive yourself to an emergency room in that state; the ambulance is the treatment starting early.

Urgent care is for the next tier down: fevers, flu, ear and sinus infections, sprains and strains, minor cuts, sunburn that blisters, stomach bugs, urinary infections, and the classic Miami special, stepping on something in the sand or the water. The walk in networks below handle all of that with on site lab testing and digital X-ray, which means one visit instead of a referral chain. If the urgent care clinician decides you need the emergency room, they will tell you, and that triage is itself worth the visit.

The networks visitors actually use

MD Now Urgent Care runs multiple Miami clinics and states its model plainly: walk in, no appointment necessary, open daily 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., with services covering illness and injury care, lab testing, digital X-rays, and EKGs. Its Miami locations include Coral Way at 2750 Coral Way, Midtown Miami at 2310 Biscayne Boulevard, Brickell at 1250 South Miami Avenue, and Dadeland at 6605 South Dixie Highway, which between them cover downtown, Brickell, Midtown, and the Coral Gables side of the city.

Baptist Health Urgent Care, part of South Florida's largest hospital system, runs walk in centers staffed by board certified physicians with on site X-ray and labs, including Brickell at 2660 Brickell Avenue, Suite 100, and a Miami Beach location at 709 Alton Road, which is the most useful address on this page for beach based visitors. Both networks say they accept most major insurances and offer self pay options; neither publishes a simple citable price for a visit, so ask for the self pay rate when you check in if you are uninsured, before treatment, not at the desk on the way out.

Practical notes for visitors

Bring your ID and your insurance card, US or travel insurance, and understand that acceptance of a specific foreign travel policy is decided clinic by clinic; many visitors pay by card and claim back from their travel insurer, so keep every receipt and ask for an itemized bill. Pharmacy runs are easy in Miami: the big chains fill prescriptions near every neighborhood on this page, and your hotel front desk can point you to the nearest one at any hour.

Two Miami specific warnings. First, heat and sun are medical issues here, not comfort issues: dehydration and heat exhaustion send more visitors to clinics than almost anything else, and the treatment starts with shade, water, and stopping. Second, ocean injuries have their own logic: jellyfish stings and stingray shuffles are beach realities, and lifeguards are your first stop for anything that happens in the water; they treat minor stings on the spot and will tell you if it needs a clinic. Frequently asked questions

What is the emergency number in Miami?
911, from any phone, including foreign mobiles. Use it for anything that threatens life, limb, or sight. For non emergency visitor problems, urgent care is usually the faster and cheaper route.

Can I just walk into urgent care in Miami?
Yes. Both MD Now and Baptist Health Urgent Care run walk in models with no appointment needed. MD Now publishes daily hours of 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Where is urgent care in Miami Beach?
Baptist Health has a Miami Beach urgent care location at 709 Alton Road, on the west side of South Beach, convenient from most beach hotels.

Will urgent care accept my travel insurance?
It varies by clinic and policy. Many visitors pay by card and reclaim from their travel insurer afterward. Bring your policy details, keep receipts, and ask for an itemized bill.
