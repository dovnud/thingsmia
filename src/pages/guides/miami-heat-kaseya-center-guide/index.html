Miami Heat Game Guide: Kaseya Center

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Kaseya Center sits at 601 Biscayne Boulevard in downtown Miami, on the bay, ringed by Bayside Marketplace, Bayfront Park, and the Brickell skyline. It seats about 19,600 for basketball and it is the easiest major venue in Miami to reach without a car: the free Metromover loops past it. The one rule that catches visitors out is the bag policy, which is close to no bags at all.

The bag policy: plan on bringing almost nothing

Kaseya Center encourages guests to bring no bag at all. The exceptions are narrow: small bags and purses smaller than 10 by 6 by 2 inches, plus medical bags and infant bags. Anything bigger has to go to the Binbox lockers the venue provides for oversize bags, which open an hour before doors and close an hour after the show, start at about $10, and sit at the South Plaza above the Gate 7 steps and by the Box Office near Gates 4 and 5. Entry is mobile only and the building runs cashless, so the full kit for a Heat game is a phone, a card, and pockets.

This is the arena to visit on a night you are already downtown with nothing to carry. If you are coming straight from the beach or the airport with a day bag, route past your hotel first, or budget the locker stop into your arrival time.

Getting there: Metromover first, car last

The venue's own directions point fans to public transit, and for once the transit is genuinely the best option. Ride Metrorail to Government Center and transfer to the free Metromover, or ride the Metromover from anywhere in the downtown and Brickell loop; the Park West and Freedom Tower area stops are a short walk from the arena doors. The Metromover is free, frequent, and immune to the game night gridlock that locks up Biscayne Boulevard.

On site parking is limited: the arena's P2 Garage sells a limited number of first come passes for individual games, and most drivers end up in nearby downtown garages such as the Bayside Marketplace garage. If you drive, arrive early enough to make a parking search part of the plan rather than a crisis, and expect downtown event pricing. Rideshare is plentiful downtown, with the usual postgame surge in the first twenty minutes after the buzzer.

Making a night of it

A Heat game is the rare Miami sports night that slots into a full evening. Bayside Marketplace sits next door for a casual pregame meal on the water, Bayfront Park gives you the bayfront walk, and Brickell, a short Metromover ride south, holds the denser restaurant and bar scene for after the game. The arena district is also the Ultra Music Festival's neighborhood in March, so a late season Heat game can collide with festival road closures; our Ultra weekend closures guide explains the pattern to check.

Inside, the building's food program leans local more than most arenas, and the lower bowl corners give the best value view in the house for basketball. Doors typically open well before tipoff; arrive in time to watch warmups from the rail if you have lower seats, which is the closest free upgrade in the NBA.

Seats and tickets, briefly

Basketball sightlines at Kaseya Center are honest from most of the building. The 100 level corners and the first rows of the 300 level center sections are the value sweet spots; seats behind the baskets are the cheapest and the most obstructed during play at the far end. Suites and club spaces exist for groups, sold through the team's official channels. Buy through the team's official seller or the venue box office where you can, and treat any street level ticket offer around the arena as the scam it almost always is.

Where to stay

Downtown and Brickell hotels put you on the Metromover loop and within a walk or one stop of the arena, and they are the correct base for a game night trip. Miami Beach is a causeway drive away, fine on a quiet night and slow on a festival or weekend night. Frequently asked questions

Can I bring a bag to a Heat game?
Plan on no. Only small bags under 10 by 6 by 2 inches, medical bags, and infant bags are admitted. Bigger bags go to the venue's rental lockers near Gates 7 and 4/5.

How do I get to Kaseya Center without driving?
Metrorail to Government Center, then the free Metromover to the Park West or Freedom Tower stops, a short walk from the doors. It is the fastest route on game night.

Is parking available at the arena?
Limited. The P2 Garage sells a limited number of single game passes; most drivers use nearby downtown garages such as Bayside Marketplace. Arrive early if you drive.

What else is near Kaseya Center?
Bayside Marketplace next door, Bayfront Park on the bayfront, and Brickell a short Metromover ride south for dinner and drinks after the game.
