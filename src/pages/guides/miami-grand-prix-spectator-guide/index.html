Miami Grand Prix Spectator Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. First, two updates every older guide gets wrong. The race is now the Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix, with Hard Rock as title partner, and the 2027 dates are confirmed as April 30 to May 2, 2027, round six of the championship. The Miami International Autodrome is a temporary 5.412 km, 19 turn circuit built across the Hard Rock Stadium grounds in Miami Gardens, the race runs 57 laps, and every one of the first five editions sold out. Plan like it.

The ticket ladder, from Campus Pass to Paddock Club

The entry ticket is the Campus Pass, the promoter's three day general admission ticket: it opens the whole circuit campus, the fan zones, the marina and beach club viewing areas, and the general admission sightlines, plus a perk every ticket holder gets, access to the 300 level of Hard Rock Stadium for panoramic views over the campus and the Team Village. It is the right ticket if your race weekend is about atmosphere and exploring, and the wrong one if you need a guaranteed seat for the start.

Above it sit the reserved grandstands, including team grandstands for eight of the F1 teams, and the Grandstand Pass, which returns for 2027 and rotates you through a different grandstand on each of the three days, the connoisseur's way to learn the circuit. Grandstand tickets include the wider campus access too. At the top, hospitality runs from the Hard Rock Beach Club and the MSC Yacht Club to the expanded Paddock Club, which for 2027 stretches toward Turn 1, hosts more than 9,000 guests across 305,000 square feet, and went on sale to the general public for the first time. Every ticket type is a three day product in practice; this is a weekend event, not a Sunday errand.

The circuit and what you actually watch

The Autodrome is 5.412 km with 19 turns and a long back straight where the cars earn their overtaking zones; the lap record stands at 1 minute 29.708 seconds, set by Max Verstappen in 2023. The marina section, complete with yachts on dry land, and the beach club are the circuit's signature made for television corners, while the stadium section wraps the track around the football bowl so parts of the campus feel like a festival built inside a sports complex.

Because the circuit is temporary and flat, sightlines are a grandstand game: elevation is everything. General admission viewing banks exist, but a first timer who cares about following the race should buy a seat, bring a radio or the F1 app for commentary, and accept that following positions on a road course means screens plus the cars in front of you. The support program and the campus entertainment fill the gaps between sessions across all three days.

Heat, sun, and the Florida factor

Late April and early May in Miami Gardens means temperatures in the high 80s, humidity building, and sun that punishes the unprepared by lap ten. The campus has limited natural shade. Sunscreen applied before you arrive, a hat, water discipline, and a plan for the midday lull are not soft advice here; they are the difference between a great Sunday and a medical tent visit. Afternoon thunderstorms are a standing Florida possibility in this window, and sessions can be delayed for lightning; a light rain layer in the bag and a flexible post race plan cover it.

Arrive through the gate nearest your seat, because the campus is large and walking it in the heat at midday is a real cost. Our traffic and parking guide covers the lots, the Brightline shuttle play, and why rideshare is the option the promoter itself warns you about.

The facts that matter

Formula 1 and promoter official sources, checked October 30, 2026: event name Formula 1 Hard Rock Miami Grand Prix; 2027 dates April 30 to May 2, 2027, confirmed by the promoter; venue Miami International Autodrome, 347 Don Shula Drive, Miami Gardens, FL 33056. Circuit 5.412 km, 19 turns, 57 laps, 308.326 km race distance; lap record 1:29.708, Verstappen, 2023. Ticket types: Campus Pass (three day general admission), reserved and team grandstands, Grandstand Pass, hospitality including Paddock Club, MSC Yacht Club, and Hard Rock Beach Club. Every ticket includes 300 level stadium access. Official sources: f1miamigp.com and formula1.com.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Miami Grand Prix in 2027?
April 30 to May 2, 2027, confirmed by the promoter. Note that older pages and last year's listings say May 1 to 3; those were the 2026 dates.

What is the Campus Pass?
The three day general admission ticket: full campus access and general admission viewing, plus 300 level access inside Hard Rock Stadium. It does not include a reserved grandstand seat.

Is the Miami Grand Prix a sellout risk?
Yes. The promoter states the first five editions all sold out, and 2027 tickets went on general sale in September 2026. Buy early through official channels.

What should I bring for the heat?
Sunscreen, a hat, a refillable plan for water, and a light rain layer. The campus has limited shade and late spring Miami sun is the event's toughest opponent.
