Miami Open Tennis Visitor Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami Open presented by Itau takes over Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens every March for one of the biggest stops in tennis: an ATP Masters 1000 and a WTA 1000 played over about two weeks. The tournament has published March 14 to 28, 2027 as its next dates, marked subject to change. The ticket decision that matters is simple: a Grounds Pass gets you the whole campus and every outer court, but not a reserved seat in Stadium Court.

Grounds Pass versus Stadium Court: the core decision

The Grounds Pass is the tournament's value ticket and, for many fans, its best one. Per the tournament, it gives all day campus access from open to close, first come first served general admission seating at the Grandstand and the outer courts, and access to the campus restaurants, bars, and experiences. What it does not include is a reserved seat inside Stadium Court, where the top seeds play their singles matches. If the player you came for is scheduled on Stadium Court, a Grounds Pass lets you watch everything except that match from a seat.

Stadium Court access comes through Single Session tickets, a reserved seat for the two to three matches of one session, or through premium and luxury seating. A Reserved Grandstand ticket splits the difference: a reserved seat in the Grandstand, the second largest court, which hosts high quality matches all tournament long. The classic local strategy is a Grounds Pass for an early round day, when the outer courts are stacked with top 20 players at arm's length, and a Stadium Court session for a quarterfinal or semifinal night.

Planning the day

The campus is the Hard Rock Stadium grounds converted into a tennis village, and it rewards a full day rather than a match sprint. Early rounds are the connoisseur's rounds: practice courts put you within a few feet of the best players in the world, the outer courts run matches from morning to late evening, and the food and drink campus is a destination in itself. By the second week the grounds thin out, the sessions concentrate in Stadium Court, and the Grounds Pass value fades accordingly.

March in Miami Gardens is warm and can be wet. Sessions run day and night, the day session is a sun management exercise, and the night session is the comfortable ticket. Sunscreen, a hat for the day session, and a layer for the evening cover most of it. Tournament logistics like parking and bag rules run on the stadium's systems; check the tournament's Know Before You Go page for your session, because event specific rules are set per edition.

Tickets timing and the March collision

Grounds passes have historically gone on sale about a month before the tournament, while Stadium Court and package products sell earlier, so the buying rhythm is: book your trip and your marquee session early, and add grounds days closer in when the schedule shape is clearer. The Miami Open lands in the same March window as spring break in Miami Beach and, in some years, close to Ultra weekend downtown, so hotels price the month as a block. Staying north, in Aventura or Sunny Isles, cuts both the stadium drive and the worst of the beach pricing, and our spring break guide explains the city rules your beach days will run into.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Miami Open in 2027?
The tournament has published March 14 to 28, 2027, marked subject to change. It is played at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens.

Is a Grounds Pass worth it?
In the first week, emphatically yes: outer courts and practice courts put top players within a few feet, all day. It does not include a reserved Stadium Court seat, so add a session ticket for a marquee match.

Where is the Miami Open played?
At Hard Rock Stadium, the Dolphins' stadium in Miami Gardens, converted into a tennis campus each March.

When do Grounds Passes go on sale?
Historically about a month before the tournament, while Stadium Court sessions and packages sell earlier. Check miamiopen.com for the current edition's sale dates.
