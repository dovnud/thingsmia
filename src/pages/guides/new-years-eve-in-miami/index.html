New Year's Eve in Miami Guide

Last reviewed: November 9, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Miami gives you three New Year's Eves: the free public countdown at Bayfront Park in downtown, the hotel and club parties in Miami Beach and Brickell, and the night from the water on a Biscayne Bay cruise. One honest status note before anything else: as of November 9, 2026, the Bayfront Park Management Trust has not published the December 31, 2026 edition's lineup or format, so this guide describes the event's established shape, flags last year's details as last year's, and tells you exactly where the announcement will land. Hotels, cruises, and parties, meanwhile, are bookable now and sell out; December 31 is the single most expensive night of the Miami year.

Where is the free public countdown?

Bayfront Park, 301 Biscayne Boulevard in downtown Miami, is the traditional home of the city's big free countdown, and its signature is pure Miami: the Big Orange, a 35 foot neon orange that climbs the side of the InterContinental Miami through the evening, with fireworks over Biscayne Bay at midnight. The current festival format, presented by the Bayfront Park Management Trust, debuted on December 31, 2025 under the name The Countdown Miami: free general admission with an RSVP, doors at 5 p.m., a concert lineup through the evening, and the fireworks finale. That is last year's edition described accurately, not a promise about this one. For December 31, 2026, check bayfrontparkmiami.com for the Trust's announcement before you lock plans, and expect the RSVP to matter if the free format returns. Arrive by Metromover (it is free) rather than driving; downtown parking on the 31st is the worst of the year.

What about Miami Beach and the hotels?

The other pole of the night is ticketed: beachfront hotels run galas, rooftops run parties, and Ocean Drive fills with people watching the sky at midnight. The big properties announce their New Year's Eve lineups through October and November, which means the right move in late October is to book the room first and the party second, once the lineup for your hotel posts. Our hotel guides cover the classic addresses: the Fontainebleau, the Faena, The Setai, W South Beach, Eden Roc, and The Betsy on Ocean Drive itself. If the party is the point of the trip, the Miami nightlife hub covers the club side of the city year round.

Can you watch the fireworks from the water?

Yes, and it is the version of the night many locals pick: a bay cruise puts the skyline in front of you and the fireworks overhead, away from the downtown crowds. New Year's Eve departures are a distinct, limited inventory on the bay cruise calendar and they sell out ahead of ordinary December dates, so book early and read the boarding time carefully; missing a 10 p.m. boarding because of causeway traffic is the classic Miami mistake. How do you get home after midnight?

Plan the exit before the first drink. The Metromover loops downtown and Brickell for free and is the natural way out of Bayfront Park; the Metrorail's Government Center station connects there for the ride out to the mainland neighborhoods. Miami-Dade Transit publishes holiday service details for the night, so check its site in December for the 2026 schedule rather than assuming last year's. Rideshare works but surges hard in the first hour after midnight, and causeway traffic back to Miami Beach crawls; walking fifteen minutes away from the park before ordering a car is the veteran move. If you are staying in Miami Beach and celebrating downtown, or the reverse, one clean rule: celebrate on the side of the bay where your bed is.

Where should you stay?

December 31 rates are the peak of the peak season, and the best located rooms go early. Stay downtown or in Brickell for the Bayfront Park countdown, and in Miami Beach for the hotel party circuit; the guide above explains why crossing the bay at 1 a.m. is the thing to avoid. Frequently asked questions

Is the Bayfront Park New Year's Eve event confirmed for 2026?
The park is Miami's traditional public countdown home and the Trust presented the current format's first edition on December 31, 2025. As of November 9, 2026, the lineup and format for December 31, 2026 had not been published; check bayfrontparkmiami.com for the announcement before finalizing plans.

Where are the fireworks?
The downtown show fires over Biscayne Bay at midnight, best watched from Bayfront Park and the downtown waterfront. Hotel and beach celebrations run their own displays and parties; confirm your hotel's own program when you book.

What is the Big Orange?
A 35 foot neon orange that climbs the side of the InterContinental Miami beside Bayfront Park during the evening. It is Miami's answer to the ball drop, and it is exactly as serious as it sounds.

When should you book for New Year's Eve in Miami?
Now. Rooms, bay cruises, and the headline hotel parties all sell against the same fixed date, and late October is when the sensible inventory is still there.

How late does transit run?
Check Miami-Dade Transit's published holiday schedule for the night in December; do not rely on a previous year's hours. The Metromover itself is free every day of the year.
