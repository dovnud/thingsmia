What the Miami Grand Prix Really Costs

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. Start with what can be verified. For the 2027 race (April 30 to May 2), the promoter's own published three day grandstand prices start at $695 for the Beach Grandstand and climb past $1,100 for Start/Finish. Everything below separates the official numbers, which are quoted with their source, from the budget lines that have no official price, which are labeled as the estimates they are. Anyone who quotes you an exact all in weekend cost to the dollar is inventing at least half of it.

Tickets: the official 2027 ladder

The promoter's published three day grandstand prices for 2027, checked October 30, 2026, run like this: Beach Grandstand from $695, Marina Grandstand $730, Turn 18 Grandstand $935, Turn 1 North Grandstand $945, and the Start/Finish Grandstand $1,125. The Grandstand Pass, which rotates you through different grandstands across the three days, is sold in packages at $845 (Marina package), $920 (Turn 18 package), and $1,105 (Turn 1 package). These are the promoter's listed prices before fees, and grandstands can sell through tiers, so treat them as the floor of the range, not a guarantee at checkout.

Two honest gaps in the official picture. First, the 2027 Campus Pass, the general admission ticket, had no published price on the promoter's site at the time of checking; for scale, the promoter's own release for the 2025 race put the three day Campus Pass from $430, and general admission prices have climbed since, so budget above that line and verify on the promoter's ticket pages before you commit. Second, hospitality, Paddock Club, the MSC Yacht Club, and the Hard Rock Beach Club carry no public prices; they are quote based products, and any exact figure you see for them is a resale or an invention.

Transport and parking: the quiet budget line

Parking can cost zero, if you play it right. The promoter's Grandstand+ membership includes complimentary parking for the 2027 race for three day grandstand holders who enroll in auto renewal by April 1, 2027. Beyond that, no official general parking pass price was published in the promoter's pages at checking time, and the free options are real: the shuttle lots and the Brightline Aventura shuttle cost nothing beyond your train fare if you come by rail. The expensive transport choices are the ones made by default: a rideshare pair on race day, priced under surge, or a rental car plus parking plus the hours of your life in the lot exit queue.

Hotels are the swing item of the whole budget, and no honest guide can fix their number: race weekend reprices the northern corridor (Aventura, Sunny Isles, Hollywood) hard, and the same room can double between an early refundable booking and a late one. The budget move with the highest return is simply booking refundable rooms the week you buy tickets. Our Grand Prix hotels guide ranks the areas by distance so you can price the trade yourself.

A realistic weekend stack, labeled as estimates

Build your own stack from the verified anchors. A mid range weekend for one person, with a Beach or Marina grandstand seat bought early ($695 to $730 before fees), three nights in the northern corridor booked refundable months ahead, Brightline and shuttles for transport, and campus food and drink across three days, lands in the low thousands of dollars before flights; the same weekend assembled late, with a Start/Finish seat, surge rideshares, and a repriced hotel, can run two to three times that. The variables that move the total most, in order, are the hotel booking date, the grandstand tier, and whether your airport is MIA or Fort Lauderdale.

What the race does not charge you for is worth naming too: every ticket includes access to the 300 level of Hard Rock Stadium for the panoramic and Team Village views, the campus fan zones are inside your ticket, and the support program fills the days between F1 sessions. The fans who overspend at Miami are usually buying their way out of logistics they could have solved with a shuttle lot and an early alarm.

The facts that matter

Grandstand Pass packages: $845, $920, $1,105. Grandstand+ membership includes complimentary 2027 parking with auto renewal enrollment by April 1, 2027. Historical anchor, labeled: the 2025 three day Campus Pass started at $430 per the promoter's 2025 release. Not published at check time: 2027 Campus Pass price, general parking pass price, hospitality prices.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest official ticket for the 2027 Miami Grand Prix?
The cheapest published price is the Beach Grandstand from $695 for three days. The Campus Pass general admission price for 2027 was not published on the promoter's site at our October 2026 check; in 2025 it started at $430.

How much is parking at the Miami Grand Prix?
There was no published general parking price at check time. Grandstand+ members get complimentary 2027 parking, and the shuttle lots plus Brightline shuttles are free to use.

What does a full Grand Prix weekend cost?
There is no single honest number. With an early bought mid tier grandstand seat, a refundable northern corridor hotel, and shuttle transport, budget in the low thousands per person before flights; late assembly can multiply it.

Are hospitality tickets priced publicly?
No. Paddock Club, MSC Yacht Club, and Hard Rock Beach Club prices are not published by the promoter. Treat any exact figure elsewhere as unverified.
