Miami Hurricanes Game Day Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami Hurricanes play their home football at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, the same building as the Dolphins, but a Canes Saturday has its own rhythm: earlier tailgating, a younger crowd, themed home games across the season, and one piece of logistics the Dolphins do not offer, the HRS Express shuttle from Lot 70 with $10 parking and a free ride to the stadium. If you are visiting for one college game, that shuttle is the smartest seat in the transport plan.

The season has themes, and they change the crowd

The university builds its home schedule around themed games, and the themes are worth checking when you pick your weekend. The 2026 home slate, as published by the university, opened with Florida A&M as the home opener, put Central Michigan on Family Weekend, circled Florida State as an Orange Out game where fans are asked to wear orange, and closed the home year with Duke for Homecoming, Virginia Tech on Salute to Service day, and Boston College on Senior Day. The Florida State game is the season's emotional peak and its most expensive ticket; Homecoming is the alumni heavy weekend; the early season nonconference games are the value dates.

Home games are all at Hard Rock Stadium, confirmed on the university's official schedule. Coral Gables, where the university actually sits, is about an hour south in game day traffic, which is why the stadium experience and the campus experience are two separate trips for most visitors.

Tickets: groups, suites, and the student system

The university's Ticket Central sells season tickets, individual game tickets, and suites, and groups of 10 or more get discounted block seating, the easiest group discount in Miami sports. Terrace Suites hold up to 40 guests and Social Club options cover groups of 50 to 100, both sold through the same office at miamihurricanes.com or 1-800-GO-CANES. Student tickets run through the university's own student system, with availability announced at the start of the season, so visiting students should not count on walking up as a student.

For a single game visit, the value pattern mirrors most of college football: September nonconference games are the cheapest way into the building, rivalry games price like a different sport, and the secondary market softens in the final week for all but the biggest finales. Buy through the university or a major resale platform, never from a parking lot seller.

HRS Express and the parking pattern

The transport gem of a Canes Saturday is the HRS Express: park at Lot 70 for $10 while supplies last and ride a free, climate controlled shuttle to the stadium, with return service after the game. It runs for Hurricanes home games and removes the stadium lot pricing and the postgame lot exit from your day entirely. Confirm it for your specific game on the university's Ticket Central page, where it is listed per game.

If you drive into the main lots, college games run directive parking in every lot from opening, stricter than the Dolphins pattern: attendants fill rows in order, so groups in multiple cars must arrive together to tailgate together. The stadium's tailgating rules apply in full: one space per vehicle, your setup in the marked 8 by 10 foot space behind the car, gas and charcoal grills welcome, open fires and kegs banned. The clear bag policy is the stadium's standard one: clear bags up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, small bags up to 4.5 by 6.5 inches.

How a Canes game differs from a Dolphins game

The crowd skews younger and louder in the student sections, the band and the pregame traditions carry the day, and the tailgate scene starts earlier relative to kickoff. Themed games change the color palette of the whole stadium, an Orange Out looks and photographs completely different from a standard Saturday, and Homecoming weekend fills the Coral Gables campus with alumni events worth a separate morning. Visitors pairing the game with a campus visit should do the campus on Friday or Sunday, not on game day itself, when all roads point at Miami Gardens.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the Miami Hurricanes play?
At Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, the same stadium as the Dolphins. The university campus in Coral Gables is about an hour away in game day traffic.

What is the HRS Express?
The university's park and ride for home games: park at Lot 70 for $10 while supplies last and take a free shuttle to the stadium, with return service after the game.

Do group discounts exist for Canes games?
Yes. Groups of 10 or more get discounted block seating through the university's Ticket Central, and Terrace Suite and Social Club options cover groups of 40 to 100.

Which home game should a visitor pick?
Florida State is the peak rivalry game, Homecoming is the alumni weekend, and the early season nonconference games are the best value tickets of the year.
