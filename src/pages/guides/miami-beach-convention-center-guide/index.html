Miami Beach Convention Center Guide

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026. Sources for this page are listed in its fact sheet.

Short answer first. The Miami Beach Convention Center, at 1901 Convention Center Drive in the heart of South Beach, is the rebuilt anchor of Miami Beach's meetings economy: a 60,000 square foot Grand Ballroom, nearly half a million square feet of contiguous exhibit halls, up to 84 breakout rooms, and a campus that adds Pride Park's six acres and the on site Rum Room restaurant. It is 12 miles from Miami International Airport and six miles from downtown Miami and PortMiami, per the center itself.

Parking: the $20 garage and the backups

The center's own parking garage holds 800 spaces at a $20 flat fee, with decks on the 4th and 5th (rooftop, uncovered) levels, entrances from 1901 Convention Center Drive on the west and 2000 Washington Avenue on the east, and hours of 6 a.m. to 1 a.m., seven days a week. Valet service is also available. Accessible parking is on the rooftop level of the garage, and EV charging stations are on the 5th floor, two details worth knowing before you circle.

When the center's garage fills, and on Art Basel and boat show weeks it fills, the center itself points drivers to the city's nearby municipal garages: G7 at 581 17th Street, G9 at 1661 Pennsylvania Avenue, and G12 at 1909 Meridian Avenue, all a short walk from the entrances. Special event pricing can differ from the standard $20 flat fee, so treat that number as the everyday rate and check the center's directions page for your specific event week.

The building and the campus

The renovation that reshaped the center cost about $640 million and produced the current shape: a 60,000 square foot Grand Ballroom, the largest in South Florida, four junior ballrooms, an Exhibit Hall ABCD of roughly 491,651 contiguous square feet, and up to 84 breakout rooms. In practice that means the center hosts the city's biggest weeks, Art Basel's Miami Beach show, the Miami International Boat Show's indoor arm, and citywide conventions, often with the whole district moving at convention tempo.

The campus is the surprise for first timers. Pride Park, six acres on the west side, brings lawn, sculptures, and an outdoor gym into the middle of a convention district, and Collins Canal Park adds three acres to the north. The center's own restaurant, Rum Room, sits on campus at 2100 Washington Avenue. Two cultural landmarks frame the neighborhood: The Fillmore Miami Beach at the Jackie Gleason Theater, 1700 Washington Avenue, directly adjacent to the center, and the New World Center, home of the New World Symphony, at 500 17th Street a few blocks west. An evening at either is the classic convention week upgrade, and our restaurants near the convention center guide covers the eating side of the same blocks.

Getting there and the attendee pattern

Most attendees fly into MIA and ride the 12 miles to the beach, then stop driving: the center sits inside Miami Beach's free trolley network, Lincoln Road is a short walk south, and the beach itself is under a mile east. The attendee playbook is in our companion convention attendee guide: trolley loops, the walk times to Lincoln Road and the sand, and the evening run to Wynwood across the causeway. If you are exhibiting or speaking, load in logistics live on the center's own planner pages; if you are attending, everything you need is within a mile and most of it is within four blocks.

Frequently asked questions

How much is parking at the Miami Beach Convention Center?
The center's garage charges a $20 flat fee with 800 spaces on levels 4 and 5, open 6 a.m. to 1 a.m. Event pricing can differ. Backup city garages sit at 17th Street, Pennsylvania Avenue, and Meridian Avenue.

How far is the convention center from the airport?
Twelve miles from Miami International Airport, per the center. It is also six miles from downtown Miami and PortMiami.

What is near the convention center?
Lincoln Road a short walk south, the beach under a mile east, The Fillmore Miami Beach next door, the New World Center a few blocks west, and the on campus Rum Room restaurant.

How big is the Miami Beach Convention Center?
A 60,000 square foot Grand Ballroom, four junior ballrooms, roughly 491,651 square feet of contiguous exhibit space, and up to 84 breakout rooms.
